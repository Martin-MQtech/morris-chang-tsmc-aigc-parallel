【音效：远处传来低沉的防空警报，渐渐拉近，又渐渐远去】

[SFX: a low air-raid siren in the distance, drawing closer, then fading]

1937年，广州。

Guangzhou, 1937.

五岁的男孩被母亲紧紧牵着手，随着人流往码头跑。

A five-year-old boy, his hand tightly held by his mother, runs with the crowd toward the pier.

他不太明白发生了什么。他只知道，天上最近常常出现奇怪的声音——像一只巨大的铁鸟，在云层里嗡嗡作响。然后，是爆炸。

He doesn't quite understand what is happening. He only knows that lately, strange sounds have been coming from the sky — like a giant iron bird, droning in the clouds. And then, explosions.

大人们说，那叫轰炸。

The adults call it bombing.

男孩回头看了一眼身后的城市，又看了一眼母亲。母亲没有说话，只是把他抱得更紧了些。

The boy glances back at the city, then up at his mother. She says nothing. She only holds him tighter.

【主叙述者】

[Main narrator]

很多年后，男孩长成了老人，执掌着全球最大的半导体公司。

Many years later, the boy is an old man, running the largest semiconductor company on earth.

他早已记不清那次离开的许多细节。但他记得，那天的码头上，到处都是和他一样被大人牵着走的孩子。

He no longer remembers many details of that departure. But he remembers this: that day at the pier, the docks were crowded with children just like him — led by the hand, heading for the boats.

他说，18岁以前，他逃了三次难，住过六个城市，换了十个学校。

He would later write that before he turned eighteen, he fled three times, lived in six cities, and changed ten schools.

而这一切的起点，就是1937年，广州，码头边，那个五岁孩子手里的那张船票。

And it all began here — 1937, Guangzhou, at the pier, with a boat ticket in the hand of a five-year-old.

【主叙述者】

[Main narrator]

故事要从更早说起。

The story begins earlier.

1931年，男孩出生在浙江宁波。父亲在银行界做事——那是中国近代最有名的商帮之一，宁波帮，世代以经营钱庄、航运和实业闻名，足迹遍布上海、香港乃至海外。

In 1931, the boy was born in Ningbo, Zhejiang province. His father worked in banking — the family belonged to the Ningbo Guild, one of modern China's most famous merchant clans, a people built for centuries on money shops, shipping, and enterprise, with networks stretching across Shanghai, Hong Kong, and overseas.

男孩五岁那年，父亲升职了，被派到广州做银行经理，全家便迁往广州。

When the boy was five, his father was promoted — sent to Guangzhou as a bank manager. The whole family moved south.

那时的广州，是南中国最繁华的港口城市。骑楼下人来人往，珠江上船帆相接，茶楼里一盅两件，从早到晚都有人声。男孩对这个城市的记忆很模糊——他太小了。他只记得，那是一个很热闹的地方。

Guangzhou in those years was the most bustling port city in southern China. Life moved under the arcaded shopfronts, junks crowded the Pearl River, and teahouses hummed from morning till night. The boy's memories of the city are vague — he was very young. He only remembers that it was a very lively place.

但男孩在广州只住了半年。

But the boy lived in Guangzhou for only six months.

【主叙述者 · 放缓】

[Main narrator, slower]

1937年7月，卢沟桥事变，全面抗战爆发。

In July 1937, the Marco Polo Bridge Incident ignited full-scale war between China and Japan.
