他这一年 His Year世界这一年 The World's Year
第 01 期逃难的孩子1937–42
他这一年 · His Year

随父母从广州迁往香港，在警报声里度过童年。He moved from Guangzhou to Hong Kong with his parents, spending his childhood to the sound of air-raid sirens.

世界这一年 · The World's Year

广州大轰炸、香港沦陷，东亚卷进全面战争。The bombing of Guangzhou and the fall of Hong Kong dragged East Asia into all-out war.

一个孩子学会的第一件事，是收拾行李。The first thing a child learned was to pack.
第 02 期考不进去的南开 & 作家梦1943–48
他这一年 · His Year

重庆南开、上海南洋模范，作家梦被一句「写作会饿肚子」按下。At Nankai in Chongqing and Nanyang Model in Shanghai, his dream of writing was shelved by one line: "Writing will leave you hungry."

世界这一年 · The World's Year

陪都重庆、1948 金圆券恶性通胀，一代人财富蒸发。Wartime capital Chongqing, then the 1948 gold-yuan hyperinflation that evaporated a generation's wealth.

时代用通胀，替他改写了职业志愿。Inflation rewrote his career plans for him.
第 03 期从黄浦江到查尔斯河1949–50
他这一年 · His Year

1949 赴美入哈佛，1950 转学 MIT。In 1949 he left for Harvard; in 1950 he transferred to MIT.

世界这一年 · The World's Year

国共易手、铁幕落下，马歇尔计划重建欧洲。China changed hands, the Iron Curtain fell, and the Marshall Plan rebuilt Europe.

他以为的「暂时离开」，成了「不归路」。The "temporary leave" he imagined became a road of no return.
第 04 期四十封求职信1954–58
他这一年 · His Year

两次博士落第，40 封求职信只换来几封回音，因一美元之差入了半导体。Twice he failed the doctorate; forty applications drew a few replies, and a one-dollar difference steered him into semiconductors.

世界这一年 · The World's Year

晶体管刚走出贝尔实验室，斯普特尼克震动美国。The transistor had just left Bell Labs; Sputnik jolted America.

被拒绝，是命运在指另一条路。Rejection is fate pointing out another road.
第 05 期隔岸观火的叛乱1957–68
他这一年 · His Year

在达拉斯，隔着半个美国看硅谷那场叛乱。From Dallas, he watched Silicon Valley's rebellion from half a country away.

世界这一年 · The World's Year

叛逆八帮创立仙童，洛克式创投与英特尔相继诞生。The Traitorous Eight founded Fairchild; Rock-style venture capital and Intel followed.

别人的剧本，后来成了他自己的。Someone else's script later became his own.
第 06 期德仪的太空竞赛岁月1958–64
他这一年 · His Year

1958 入德州仪器，在职读博把自己清零。He joined Texas Instruments in 1958, then reset himself by earning a doctorate on the job.

世界这一年 · The World's Year

基尔比点亮第一块集成电路，肯尼迪押注登月。Kilby lit the first integrated circuit; Kennedy bet on the moon.

最强的强者，敢在上升期归零。The strongest dare to reset on the way up.
第 07 期半导体之巅的十年1964–78
他这一年 · His Year

一路升到半导体集团总经理，却在会议室里输掉棋局。He rose to head the semiconductor group, yet lost the boardroom game.

世界这一年 · The World's Year

微处理器开启个人计算时代，石油危机与日本军团压境。The microprocessor opened the personal-computing age; the oil crisis and the Japanese juggernaut pressed in.

最难的仗，不在市场，在会议室。The hardest battles are not in the market, but the boardroom.
第 08 期离开德州 & 受邀回台1978–87
他这一年 · His Year

1983 辞 TI，1985 赴台任工研院院长，1987 创台积电。He left TI in 1983, took over ITRI in 1985, and founded TSMC in 1987.

世界这一年 · The World's Year

美日半导体贸易战正酣，台湾经济奇迹前夜。The US–Japan semiconductor trade war raged; Taiwan's economic miracle was dawning.

离开一个错误的位置，是最重要的一步棋。Leaving the wrong position is the most important move.
第 09 期纯代工的革命1987–95
他这一年 · His Year

创办台积电，只造别人的芯片。He founded TSMC and built only other people's chips.

世界这一年 · The World's Year

全球化分工成形，高通、英伟达等无厂公司崛起。Global division of labor took shape; fabless firms like Qualcomm and Nvidia rose.

不做主角，做让所有人成为主角的平台。Take no lead role — build the platform that makes everyone the lead.
第 10 期从台湾到世界1995–98
他这一年 · His Year

1997 纽交所上市，1998 登最佳经理人榜。Listed on the NYSE in 1997; named a top manager in 1998.

世界这一年 · The World's Year

亚洲金融风暴席卷，互联网黎明初现。The Asian financial crisis swept through; the internet dawned.

风暴来时，扎实的企业反而被看见。When the storm comes, solid companies get seen.
