# 台积电篇二·第二十七章｜自「技术自主」到立志「技术领先」

九十年代的台积电，在技术层面的最大变化，是从「技术自主」的底线意识蜕变为「技术领先」的宏伟志愿。这个转变不仅牵动了公司未来数十年的研发方向，也直接拒绝了IBM诱惑性的合作提案——因为我们已经立下志愿，要做就要做世界的领头羊。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">The most significant technological shift at TSMC in the 1990s was the transformation from the底线 consciousness of "technological self-sufficiency" into the grand aspiration of "technology leadership." This shift not only shaped the company's R&D direction for decades to come, but also led directly to the rejection of IBM's tempting collaboration proposal—for we had already committed to being the world's leader.</p>
</div>

与别的半导体公司比，一九八七年台积电的「节点」技术大约落后一般半导体公司二至三代。别的公司已量产1.25微米或1.0微米，台积电只能量产2.0微米。但到了一九九九年，台积电已赶上九○年代的半导体公司，大家都能做0.18微米。而且相较于其他业界，台积电不做自有产品而仅做专业代工，并且良率比别人好。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Compared with other semiconductor companies in 1987, TSMC's technology nodes lagged roughly two to three generations behind. While other companies had begun mass production at 1.25 microns or 1.0 micron, TSMC could only produce 2.0 microns. But by 1999, TSMC had caught up with the rest of the industry—all could produce 0.18 microns. And compared to others, TSMC produced no proprietary products, focusing solely on professional foundry services, with yields superior to competitors.</p>
</div>

与之前在工研院时的技术开发速度比，工研院在一九七六年自RCA移转7.0微米「节点」，开发了十一年——从7.0到5.0微米，再到3.5微米，再到一九八七年的2.0微米——十一年中仅仅三代而已！
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Compared with the technology development pace during my ITRI days, ITRI transferred the 7.0-micron node from RCA in 1976 and spent eleven years developing it—from 7.0 to 5.0 microns, then to 3.5 microns, and finally to 2.0 microns in 1987. Only three generations in eleven years!</p>
</div>

为什么台积电能做得比工研院快？其实答案很简单。第一，台积电的研发经费比工研院充裕。工研院VLSI计划、「次微米制程技术发展计划」扣除建厂及购置设备的资本支出后，平均每年研发经费不超过新台币四亿元。而台积电随着业务发展，研发费用在一九九五年已达七亿五千万元，在二○○○年更到达五十一亿元！有了充裕经费就能吸引人才。第二，工研院的研发是在没有竞争压力下进行的，而台积电的研发不但有压力，还有诱因——分红、股票升值等等。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Why could TSMC advance faster than ITRI? The answer is straightforward. First, TSMC's R&D budget was far more generous. After deducting capital expenditures for building fabrication plants and purchasing equipment from the ITRI VLSI program and the Sub-micron Process Technology Development Plan, average annual R&D spending never exceeded NT$400 million. In contrast, as TSMC's business grew, R&D expenses reached NT$750 million in 1995 and NT$5.1 billion in 2000. Ample funding attracted talent. Second, ITRI's R&D proceeded without competitive pressure, whereas TSMC's R&D faced both pressure and incentives—profit sharing, stock appreciation, and more.</p>
</div>
