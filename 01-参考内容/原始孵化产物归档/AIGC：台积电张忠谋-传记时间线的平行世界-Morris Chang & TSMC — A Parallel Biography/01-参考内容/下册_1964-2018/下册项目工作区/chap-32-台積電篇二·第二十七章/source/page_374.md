## 自德仪学来的「技术自主」

在德仪，我学会了技术是一个半导体公司的灵魂，所以必须自主技术。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">At Texas Instruments, I learned that technology is the soul of a semiconductor company—and therefore technology must be self-sufficient.</p>
</div>

当初我们（工研院）良率占优势，但节点技术落后于国外先进公司，所以特地创造了「专业晶圆代工」这个崭新的商业模式。在向飞利浦募资时，我们拒绝了飞利浦以「它们技术为基础」的诱惑，坚持了「技术自主」。十三年后，我们面临另一次技术的诱惑，并再一次坚持了「技术自主」。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">At that time, we (at ITRI) held an advantage in yield, but our node technology lagged behind advanced foreign companies. We therefore created the entirely new business model of "professional foundry services." When raising capital from Philips, we resisted the temptation to base our technology on theirs, insisting instead on technological self-sufficiency. Thirteen years later, we faced another technological temptation—and again we held firm to the principle of self-sufficiency.</p>
</div>

## 九○年代台积电技术进步神速

虽然台积电的商业模式避开了设计技术，也让落后的「节点」技术继续有销路。但是半导体业的「节点」技术进步很快——这就是所谓的「摩尔定律」。摩尔定律的大意是：可置于硅芯片上同一面积的晶体管数目每两年增加一倍。然而，许多旧的IC设计仍适用旧的「节点」技术，这也正是旧的「节点」技术继续有销路的原因。但是，新的设计绝大部分都需要与时俱进的「节点」制造技术。所以如果台积电不赶上「节点」技术，我们的业务不会成长很快。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Although TSMC's business model sidestepped design technology and allowed older node technologies to remain viable, semiconductor node advancements proceeded rapidly—this is what is known as Moore's Law. Moore's Law essentially states that the number of transistors that can be placed in a given area on a silicon chip doubles approximately every two years. Nevertheless, many legacy IC designs continued to rely on older node technologies, which is precisely why those older nodes retained market demand. However, the vast majority of new designs required cutting-edge node manufacturing technology. If TSMC failed to keep pace with node advancements, our business would not grow rapidly.</p>
</div>

在这方面，我一点也不需要担忧，因为台积电的进步神速！在成立后的十四年内（一九八七至二○○○年），台积电的「节点」从一九八七年自工研院移转的2.0微米开始，逐步进步到一九八八年的1.5微米，一九八九年的1.2微米，一九九○年的1.0微米，一九九二年的0.八微米，一九九三年的0.六微米，一九九五年的0.五微米，一九九六年的0.三五微米，一九九八年的0.二五微米，一九九九年的0.一八微米——十四年内「节点」进步了九代！
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">On this front, I had no cause for concern, for TSMC's progress was remarkably rapid. Within fourteen years of establishment (1987 to 2000), TSMC's technology nodes advanced from the 2.0 microns transferred from ITRI in 1987, progressing stepwise to 1.5 microns in 1988, 1.2 microns in 1989, 1.0 micron in 1990, 0.8 microns in 1992, 0.6 microns in 1993, 0.5 microns in 1995, 0.35 microns in 1996, 0.25 microns in 1998, and 0.18 microns in 1999—nine generations of node advancement in fourteen years!</p>
</div>
