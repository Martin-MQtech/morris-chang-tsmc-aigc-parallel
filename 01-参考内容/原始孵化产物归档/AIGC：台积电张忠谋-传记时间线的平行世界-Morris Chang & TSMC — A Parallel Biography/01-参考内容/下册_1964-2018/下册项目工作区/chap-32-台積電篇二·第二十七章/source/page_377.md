## 「节点」技术注释

「节点」技术的「代」，与摩尔定律的「代」呼应。摩尔定律预测每两年晶体管密度加倍为一「代」。「节点」技术的「代」，大约是闸极长度（gate length）的平方减半为一代。例：2的平方是4，1.5的平方是2.25。大约是4的一半，所以1.5微米被视为2微米后的「一代」。

> **rebook-translation**
>
> **From "Technology Self-Sufficiency" to "Technology Leadership"**
>
> The primary technological transformation at TSMC during the 1990s was a shift from a defensive posture of "technology self-sufficiency" to an ambitious commitment to "technology leadership." This transformation shaped not only the company's R&D direction for decades to come but also provided the rationale for rejecting a tempting joint-development proposal from IBM. We had made our resolve: if we were going to do this, we would be the world leader.
>
> Compared to other semiconductor companies, TSMC's technology node at founding in 1987 lagged some two to three generations behind industry norms. While competitors were mass-producing at 1.25 µm or 1.0 µm, TSMC could only manufacture at 2.0 µm. By 1999, however, TSMC had caught up with the industry at large—all were capable of producing at 0.18 µm. What differentiated TSMC was its pure-play foundry model (no proprietary products of its own) and its consistently superior yields.
>
> The contrast with the earlier ITRI era was striking. Starting from the 7.0 µm node transferred from RCA in 1976, ITRI's VLSI program took eleven years—from 7.0 to 5.0, then 3.5, then 2.0 µm in 1987—just three generations in eleven years!
>
> Why was TSMC so much faster? The answer is simple. First, R&D budgets. After subtracting capital expenditures for fab construction and equipment, ITRI's Submicron Process Development Program averaged less than NT$400 million annually. By 1995, TSMC's R&D spending reached NT$750 million; by 2000, it had surged to NT$5.1 billion. Money attracts talent. Second, ITRI operated without competitive pressure. TSMC's R&D, by contrast, had both pressure and powerful incentives: profit-sharing and stock appreciation.
