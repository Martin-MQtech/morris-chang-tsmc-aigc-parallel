## IBM的诱惑

在此承接上章「高通」节。我与市场及营销人员历经多次看似毫无进展的拜访后，高通终于在九八年决定要与我们合作。对台积电来说，这是非常振奋的消息——我们知道高通会是高技术、重量级的客户。但对当时的IBM，这当然是非常坏的消息。IBM微电子部面临即将失去一个大客户，而他们最大的凭借——其制程技术，看起来也已被台积电追上。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Picking up where the previous chapter left off with Qualcomm: after numerous visits by our marketing and sales teams that seemed to go nowhere, Qualcomm finally decided to partner with us in 1998. For TSMC, this was extremely encouraging news—we knew Qualcomm would be a high-technology, heavyweight client. But for IBM at the time, this was certainly very bad news. IBM's Microelectronics Division faced the imminent loss of a major client, and their greatest asset—their process technology—appeared to have been caught up by TSMC.</p>
</div>

怎么办？客户的丧失看起来已无法避免，但技术也许还可以挽救。挽救的办法就是与「后起之秀」台积电合作开发下一代的制程技术（0.13微米）。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">What to do? The loss of the client appeared unavoidable, but the technology might still be salvaged. The solution was to collaborate with the "rising star" TSMC to co-develop the next generation of process technology (0.13 microns).</p>
</div>

九九年，IBM提出他们的合作方案给台积电总经理曾繁城及研发副总蒋尚义。曾和蒋不需要、也没有问我就坚决拒绝了。毕竟，我们已立了「领先世界」的志愿，这志愿与「合作开发」格格不入。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">In 1999, IBM presented their collaboration proposal to TSMC general manager Zeng Fan-cheng and VP of R&D Chiang Shang-yi. Zeng and Chiang resolutely rejected it without even consulting me. After all, we had already committed to "leading the world," a志愿 that was fundamentally incompatible with "co-development."</p>
</div>

其实，即使我们没有「领先世界」的志愿，「合作开发制程」也极难成功。要有好的结果，两个研发团队要在同一屋檐下工作。以九九年IBM与台积电的相对实力与「名气」而言，IBM一定会要求我们送研发团队去IBM。那么，我们已经没有力量做自己的开发，一切都要靠远在万里外的合作。如果因任何原因合作成果不佳——例如团队在异国人生地不熟、两团队间沟通不良、或缺乏彼此尊重等等——我们的下一代节点开发也就完了。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">In truth, even without our "world-leading"志volition, "co-developing processes" would have been extremely difficult to succeed. To achieve good results, both R&D teams would need to work under the same roof. Given the relative strength and "prestige" of IBM and TSMC in 1999, IBM would almost certainly have demanded that we send our R&D team to IBM. We would then have had no capacity to conduct our own development, and everything would depend on a collaboration thousands of miles away. If the collaboration had faltered for any reason—such as the team being unfamiliar with the foreign environment, poor communication between the two teams, or a lack of mutual respect—our next-generation node development would have been finished.</p>
</div>

后来联电找了IBM合作。但三年半后，《工商时报》报道中，联电董事长曹兴诚表示：三年半前与IBM结盟转移制程技术，让联电栽了一个大跟头。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Later, UMC sought collaboration with IBM. But three and a half years later, the Industrial Times reported that UMC chairman Tsao Hsing-cheng stated: the alliance with IBM to transfer process technology had been a major setback for UMC.</p>
</div>
