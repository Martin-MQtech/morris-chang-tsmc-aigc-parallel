德仪篇第八章

张忠谋

德仪篇·第八章｜德仪迷途

一九七二年一片光明——在我晋升为德仪全球半导体集团总经理时（一九七二年春），公司正在兴奋和庆祝心情中。一九七〇至七一年的美国经济不景气看起来已过去，德仪所有业务都看好。董事长海格底在一九六〇年为公司定的长期营收目标——十亿美元——看来在一九七二或七三年就可达成。现在已在酝酿新目标——三十亿美元，预备在十年内达成。随着自己定的目标即将达成，海格底抱着「功成身退，让下一代年轻人接棒」的态度，已告诉董事会他预备六十二岁（一九七五年）退休，交棒予夏伯特。

我接管半导体业务不过是全部人事布局的一部分：夏伯特在三年后就要接董事长位，他要在三年内训练彪希成为总裁——那幺，我就顺理成章地接了彪希的职务。

夏伯特的野心——夏伯特追随海格底多年，深威海格底的贡献在于为德仪开启新业。的确，当海格底进入德仪前身「地质测验公司」时（一九四五年），德仪的生意只有地质测验一小块。此后二十几年内，海格底先后把德仪导入国防系统、半导体、材料与控制。现在这些新事业——尤其半导体的规模——都已远远超过德仪起家的地质测验。

这些是海格底的「大有为」。现在，夏伯特认为他的「大有为」的时间和机会到了。夏伯特怎幺「大有为」？

ReadShift 双语翻译

A bright 1972 —

When I was promoted to GM of TI's Worldwide Semiconductor Group in the spring of 1972, the company was in a mood of excitement and celebration. The 1970–71 recession appeared to be over, and all of TI's businesses looked promising. Chairman Haggerty's long-term revenue target he had set in 1960 — $1 billion — appeared achievable by 1972 or 1973. Now a new target was being discussed: $30 billion, to be reached within a decade. With his self-imposed target within reach, Haggerty, embracing the attitude of "retire after success, let the next generation take over," had informed the board that he planned to retire at sixty-two (1975) and pass the baton to Shepherd.

My taking over the semiconductor business was merely part of the broader personnel blueprint: Shepherd would take the chairmanship in three years; he had three years to groom Shep as President — and I would then step seamlessly into Shep's role.

Shepherd's ambition —

Shepherd had followed Haggerty for many years and deeply admired Haggerty's contribution in opening new businesses for TI. Indeed, when Haggerty entered TI's predecessor — Geophysical Service Inc. — in 1945, TI's business consisted of only the tiny seismic-testing piece. Over the next twenty-plus years, Haggerty successively led TI into defense systems, semiconductors, and materials and controls. Now these new businesses — especially semiconductors — had far outgrown the seismic-testing origins.

These were Haggerty's "great achievements." Now, Shepherd believed his time and opportunity for "great achievements" had arrived. How would Shepherd achieve "great things"?

Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇
Succession blueprint—接班人蓝图，指最高管理层对职位继承的系统规划；在德仪的案例中，这个蓝图（夏伯特接董、彪希接总、张忠谋接彪希）因夏伯特的战略失误而最终破灭。
"Great achievements" narrative—「大有为」叙事，指接班人试图超越前任的心态；在组织行为学中，「超越前任的野心」既是驱动创新的力量，也是导致偏离核心业务的根源。
Passing the baton (交棒)—交棒，指最高领导者将职位传承给接班人；在企业管理中，「交棒」是创始人或长期任职CEO最困难也最关键的决策——它要求的不只是选择正确的人，还要有时机和节奏的精准判断。
修辞赏析 · Language & Logic Appreciation

「我接管半导体业务不过是全部人事布局的一部分」——张忠谋用「不过是」三个字，把一次大升级（集团副总裁、全球半导体总经理）说成了一部宏大机器中的一个零件。这种低调的表达背后，是一种对组织体系的深刻理解：个人的升迁从来不是孤立事件，而是最高层权力布局的一环。但历史最终证明：这部精密的「人事布局」却因夏伯特的一着错棋（消费者产品）而满盘皆输——张忠谋最终接到的，不是他预期中的总裁职位，而是一张来自台湾的邀请函。 "My taking over the semiconductor business was merely part of the broader personnel blueprint." — Chang uses "不过是" (merely) to frame a major promotion (Group VP, Worldwide Semiconductor GM) as one part in a grand machine. Behind this understatement lies a deep understanding of organizational systems: an individual's promotion is never an isolated event but a piece of the top-level power arrangement. But history ultimately proved: this sophisticated "personnel blueprint" was undone by Shepherd's single wrong move (consumer products) — what Chang ultimately received was not the presidency he had anticipated, but an invitation from Taiwan.

消费者产品的诱惑

张忠谋在自传中精确地分析了德仪踏入「消费者产品」的心理根源：

"消费者产品是销售给社会大众的产品。一直到七〇年代初期，德仪不做消费者产品。但在晶体管开发初期，海格底曾与一家小型袖珍收音机公司密切合作以推广晶体管市场——这是一场很成功的合作，参与的德仪高层经理人（包括夏伯特）都感受到消费者市场规模的威力。」「后来，集成电路功能快速进步，成为许多电子产品的核心。见到这种消费者产品，有些人就不禁要想：为什幺要让包装商赚这幺多钱？最重要的元件是我们做的集成电路，我们只要买些别的零件、再把整个东西包装起来，就可以多赚很多钱。」这种思想在一九七一、七二年计算器刚开发时达到颠峰。计算器只有三个重要元件：电路、显示器和键盘——德仪通通生产。德仪卖这些元件给计算器公司的售价约几十美元，而计算器零售价在七〇年代初期高达两百多美元！

「为什幺德仪只赚几十美元，不赚两百多美元的德仪？」「当然，当德仪真的一股脑投入计算器生意时，马上发觉消费者产品生意与德仪习惯的国防及半导体生意完全不同——而且并不那幺容易做。更糟的是，两百多美元零售价只是一个泡沫——很快地跌到几十美元，后来跌到十几美元。」【注释】

[1] 接班人计画延后：后来一九七五年景气不好，原定接班人夏伯特认为海格底如此显赫功绩不应在景気黯淡时退休，因此把海格底退休及自己的接班延后了一年。

德仪篇·第八章｜德仪迷途

野心与诱惑的致命交集——一九七二年，几乎与我接任半导体集团总经理同时，夏伯特「大有为」的野心与在德仪潜伏多年的「消费者产品」诱惑做了一个致命的交集。

夏伯特宣布德仪进入「消费者产品」事业，先以普通计算机开始，接下来会有「科学计算机」、电子表、家庭电脑等。他同时宣布：德仪要利用它在半导体的优势，全力支援消费者产品的发展。

一九五〇年代以成功经营半导体事业崛起的夏伯特，在一九七二年已与半导体业脱节。他并不充分了解一九六五年提出的「摩尔定律」，也不了解半导体还有多年快速成长。事实上，从一九七二到二〇〇〇年，世界半导体市场每年平均成长率一五%。德仪只要专心做半导体、保持它的半导体市占率（十几%），它的半导体集团就可以在二〇〇〇年达到两百二十亿美元营收——已远远超过全公司二〇〇〇年营收目标：一百亿美元。

夏伯特想「大有为」没有错，错的是他的方向。他只要专心继续耕耘半导体，德仪就可以超出他的长期目标。但他开启了德仪不熟悉的新战场。夏伯特不但分散了德仪的焦点、没有给半导体足够资源——他更分散了德仪半导体集团的焦点。本来德仪半导体集团的焦点是外部市场，现在夏伯特却钦定「服务内部客户」为半导体集团首要任务；而且，他把半导体集团当作消费者产品集团的人才库，调走了不少经理及技术人才。

ReadShift 双语翻译

The fatal intersection of ambition and temptation —

In 1972, almost simultaneously with my taking over as GM of the Semiconductor Group, Shepherd's ambition to "achieve great things" and the long-simmering temptation of "consumer products" converged in a fatal intersection.

Shepherd announced that TI would enter the "consumer products" business, beginning with basic calculators and followed by "scientific calculators," electronic watches, home computers, and more. He also announced that TI would leverage its semiconductor strength to fully support the development of consumer products.

Shepherd, who had risen to prominence by successfully running the semiconductor business in the 1950s, had by 1972 grown disconnected from the semiconductor industry. He did not fully understand Moore's Law, proposed in 1965, nor did he appreciate how many years of rapid growth still lay ahead for semiconductors. In fact, from 1972 to 2000, the world semiconductor market grew at an average annual rate of 15%. Had TI simply focused on semiconductors and maintained its market share (in the mid-teens), its Semiconductor Group alone could have reached $22 billion in revenue by 2000 — far exceeding the company's total 2000 revenue target of $10 billion.

Shepherd was not wrong to want to "achieve great things" — he was wrong in his direction. Had he simply continued to focus on semiconductors, TI could have exceeded his long-term targets. But instead he opened a new battlefield unfamiliar to TI. Shepherd not only分散了 TI's focus and denied semiconductors sufficient resources — he further scattered the Semiconductor Group's own focus. The Semiconductor Group's original focus had been external markets; now Shepherd decreed "serving internal customers" as its primary mission. Moreover, he treated the Semiconductor Group as a talent pool for the Consumer Products Group, transferring away numerous managers and technical staff.

Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇
Fatal intersection—致命交集，指两个独立趋势（野心与诱惑）在错误时机的碰撞；在战略管理中，「致命交集」往往导致资源分散和核心能力侵蚀。
Moore's Law (摩尔定律)—摩尔定律，由英特尔联合创始人戈登·摩尔于一九六五年提出，指集成电路上晶体管数目约每十八至二十四个月增加一倍；这一定律是半导体产业最具预测力的经验法则——忽视它的管理者将付出高昂代价。
Serving internal customers—服务内部客户，指将原本面向外部市场的生产能力转为服务内部需求；在组织行为学中，「内部客户优先」往往会削弱企业对外部市场的响应能力。
修辞赏析 · Language & Logic Appreciation

「两百二十亿美元营收 vs. 一百亿美元全公司目标」——张忠谋时隔近三十年后仍然精准地算出了德仪在「专注半导体」和「分心消费者产品」之间的机会成本。这个数字对比（仅半导体一项潜力即超过全公司目标一倍）是整章最有力的论证——它证明夏伯特的选择不是「做不做消费者产品」的问题，而是「要不要放弃一个确定性巨大的未来」的问题。这种「事后精算」的读法，不是「后见之明」，而是张忠谋对产业发展规律的深刻洞察。 "$22 billion in semiconductor revenue vs. $10 billion total company target." — After nearly thirty years, Chang still precisely calculated the opportunity cost between "focusing on semiconductors" and "distracting with consumer products." This numerical contrast (semiconductor potential alone exceeding the total corporate target by more than double) is the chapter's most devastating argument: it proves that Shepherd's choice was not about "whether to enter consumer products" but about "whether to forfeit a staggeringly certain future." This "retrospective precision" is not hindsight — it is Chang's profound insight into the industry's developmental laws.

第八章小结：德仪迷途的代价

一九七二年的消费者产品决定，是德仪从半导体霸主走向漫长衰退的起点。张忠谋在自传中精确诊断了这场战略失误的三个根源：

第一，夏伯特的「大有为」野心——他想成为海格底第二，却选择了错误的方向。

第二，半导体技术脱节——夏伯特自五〇年代后不再懂技术，也不知摩尔定律，低估了半导体业的成长潜力。

第三，人才掠夺——把半导体集团的焦点从「服务外部市场」改为「服务内部客户」，并把半导体集团的优秀经理和技术人员调往消费者产品事业。

这三重打击叠加在一起，使德仪在最需要专注半导体的时候分散了焦点。张忠谋在自传中写道：「一九七二年的『大升级』只带来暂时的兴奋和喜悦——即将来临的是六年的奋斗，尤其是对公司内部环境的奋斗。」

ReadShift 双语翻译

The 1972 consumer products decision marked the beginning of TI's long decline from semiconductor dominance. Chang precisely diagnosed three roots of this strategic failure: Shepherd's "great achievements" ambition directed at the wrong vector; Shepherd's disconnection from semiconductor technology and ignorance of Moore's Law; and talent diversion — refocusing the group from external to internal markets and transferring its best people to Consumer Products.

"The 1972 'great promotion' brought only temporary excitement and joy — what lay ahead was six years of struggle, especially against the corporate internal environment."
