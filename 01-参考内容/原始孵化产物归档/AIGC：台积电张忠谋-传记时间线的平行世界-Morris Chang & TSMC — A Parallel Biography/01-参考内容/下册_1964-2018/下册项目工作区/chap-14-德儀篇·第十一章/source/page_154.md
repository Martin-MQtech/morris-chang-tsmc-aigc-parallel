## 瀑布式品质训练

虽然我在工程师、工程经理、业务总经理时期，都很注意品质，但我从未做过品质专业；现在我是品质专业人了。头三个月，我要密集地学习。

首先，我打听了谁最具权威性，决定到三位「大师」的教室上课。他们是克罗斯比（Philip Crosby）、裘兰（Joseph Juran）和戴明。

每一位大师的课都是两天或三天。在这些课里，我学到了不少技术方面的知识；但与品质总监职务直接相关，我学到最重要的一课就是：「品质不是一个人的事，也不是几个人的事；品质观念必须渗透整个组织，到每一个员工。」

凭着我这一课，我在德仪创立了「瀑布式」（Cascade）品质训练。

所谓瀑布式训练，有以下几个步骤：

（一）所有集团总经理（最高层经理人）都去克罗斯比「品质学校」，上两天观念性的课。

（二）所有Division总经理（次高层经理人）都去上裘兰的三天兼具观念和技术的课；裘兰的课有教材和习题。

（三）每一Division总经理受训回来后，再用裘兰的教材，教他的直接下属（Department Managers）。

（四）每一Department Manager上课后，又教他的直接下属。

（五）如此类推，一直教到管理二、三十作业员的生产线领班。国外工厂，也是一样做法。

<p class="en-para"><b>Cascade Quality Training</b></p>
<p class="en-para">Although I had paid close attention to quality as an engineer, engineering manager, and business GM, I had never worked in quality as a specialty; now I was a quality professional. For the first three months, I studied intensively.</p>
<p class="en-para">First, I found out who was most authoritative and decided to attend classes from three "gurus": Philip Crosby, Joseph Juran, and Deming.</p>
<p class="en-para">Each guru's class lasted two or three days. In these classes, I learned considerable technical knowledge. But the most important lesson directly relevant to the Quality Director role was: "Quality is not the work of one person or a few people; the quality concept must permeate the entire organization, reaching every employee."</p>
<p class="en-para">With that single lesson, I created "Cascade" quality training at TI.</p>
<p class="en-para">The cascade training worked as follows: (1) All Group GMs (top-level managers) attended Crosby's "Quality School" for two days of conceptual training. (2) All Division GMs (next-level managers) attended Juran's three-day course combining concepts and technical skills, with textbooks and exercises. (3) After returning from training, each Division GM used Juran's materials to teach his direct reports (Department Managers). (4) After attending class, each Department Manager then taught his direct reports. (5) And so on, down to production line supervisors managing twenty to thirty operators. Foreign factories followed the same approach.</p>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Cascade training (瀑布式训练)**
**中文解释**：指从最高层开始逐层向下传递的培训模式——每一层受训后即成为下一层的教师；在组织发展中，「瀑布式训练」是「知识内部化」的高效手段——它同时解决了「知识传递」和「教师资格认证」两个问题。
**商业造句">The cascade training model — where each trained level becomes the teacher of the next — is a deeply efficient organizational knowledge transfer mechanism because it simultaneously solves the problems of scaling instruction and certifying competence.*

2. **Quality gurus: Deming, Juran, Crosby (三位品质大师)**
**中文解释**：对七〇至八〇年代美国产业界影响最大的三位品质管理大师。戴明（Deming）侧重统计品管和高层承诺；裘兰（Juran）侧重品质三部曲（规划、改善、控制）；克罗斯比（Crosby）侧重「零缺陷」和「品质免费」。张忠谋在德仪的瀑布式训练以裘兰的教材为基础——是三人中唯一将其方法论系统教材化的一位。
**商业造句">Of the three quality gurus, Chang chose Juran's materials as the backbone of TI's cascade training — Juran being the only one whose methodology had been fully codified into teachable, reusable courseware.*
