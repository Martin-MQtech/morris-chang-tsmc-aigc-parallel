# 德仪篇·第十一章｜品质与生产力总监

「品质与生产力总监」在德仪是一个新的职位；在我之前，没有这个职位，所以也可以说这职位是夏伯特为我而创设。但事实上，在一九八〇年代的美国，创设这个职位是相当「时尚」的举动。在一九六〇、七〇年代，日本企业（尤其电子和半导体）异军突起，美国公司深深感受到他们的威胁。惠普公司一位工程经理在一学术论文刊列日本记忆体品质胜于美国的数据；日本企业内流行的「品质圈」也在美国被摹仿；几位素以品质为业的「品质大师」——戴明（Edwards Deming）、裘兰（Joseph Juran）、克罗斯比（Philip Crosby）——忽然红了起来，每个人都收到无数演讲或顾问邀请。在这节骨眼上，德仪任命一个「品质总监」，也是很自然的事。

我的新职在一九八〇年十二月就宣布，夏伯特在口头宣布时，还加上了一句：「在品质问题上，英理士代表我」，算是十足给了我面子。在新职，我只有一个秘书，没有别的属下，所以要想做事，必须经过我的同事——公司的集团总经理以及他们的属下。我相当自信我有能力经过同事追求公司品质目标，因为我相信绝大部分同事都相当尊敬我——后来的发展，证实了我的自信。

一九八〇年除夕，我寄了一封电子信给德仪所有高级经理（大约百人左右），祝贺他们新年健康、快乐、成功，而且告诉他们我下周就开始与他们合作改善他们产品的品质。这封信得到相当好的反应。最起码的反应是：「这人没灰心，他还是想做事。」

<p class="en-para">"Quality and Productivity Director" was a new position at TI — no one had held it before me, so one could say the position was created for Shepherd for me. But in fact, in 1980s America, creating such a position was quite a "fashionable" move. In the 1960s and 1970s, Japanese companies (especially in electronics and semiconductors) had risen with stunning force, and American corporations deeply felt the threat. An HP engineering manager published a paper in an academic journal listing data showing Japanese memory quality surpassed American. The "quality circles" popular within Japanese companies were being imitated in America. Several "quality gurus" — Edwards Deming, Joseph Juran, Philip Crosby — suddenly became famous, each receiving countless invitations to lecture or consult. At this juncture, TI's appointment of a "Quality Director" was quite natural.</p>
<p class="en-para">My new position was announced in December 1980. When Shepherd made the oral announcement, he added: "On quality matters, Morris represents me" — giving me full face. In my new role, I had only one secretary and no other subordinates, so to get things done I had to work through my colleagues — the company's Group GMs and their people. Quite confident in my ability to pursue company quality goals through colleagues, because I believed the vast majority of colleagues held me in considerable respect — later developments proved my confidence well-founded.</p>
<p class="en-para">On New Year's Eve 1980, I sent an email to all of TI's senior managers (about a hundred or so), wishing them health, happiness, and success in the new year, and telling them I would be working with them next week to improve their product quality. This email received quite a good response. At minimum: "This man hasn't lost heart. He still wants to get things done."</p>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Quality circles / 品质圈**
**中文解释**：日本企业推行的员工自愿参与品质改善活动的小组；「品质圈」是七〇至八〇年代日本制造业崛起的核心武器之一——它将品管责任从专门的品管部门扩散到每一位员工。
**商业造句">Japanese quality circles — small voluntary groups of workers who meet regularly to discuss and solve quality problems — were one of the key weapons behind Japan's Manufacturing miracle in the 1970s and 1980s.*

2. **Staff position without direct reports (没有直接下属的幕僚职位)**
**中文解释**：张忠谋的「品质与生产力总监」只有一个秘书，没有直接下属——这种「幕僚」职位需要通过影响力（influence without authority）来推动工作；在组织行为学中，「没有职权的领导」（leading without authority）是管理中最高难度的技能之一。
**商业造句">Chang's Quality Director role had no direct reports — only a secretary — making it a pure "influence without authority" position that required leading through colleagues rather than directing subordinates.*
