## 成立世界先进半导体公司

九四年为记忆体公司募资，比八五至八六年为台积电募资容易得多了。重要原因是台积电在成立后的七年内已有爆发性的成长（九三年营收新台币一百二十三亿元，营业净利四十四亿元），投资人新的记忆体公司也会同样成功。新公司在几个月内就募集到一百二十二亿元。投资者由台积电领衔（四十二亿元），包括联华、台聚等十三家公司以及工研院与台积电的员工。与八五至八六年的台积电募资相比，台积电当年只募到五十五亿元。

下一步要"投标"，也就是要决定以多少新公司的股票回馈政府。在投标前，业界盛传至少台积电与联电会投标，所以投标的数字必须保守机密。所有投资人都授权我决定，不必告诉他们。想了几天后，我合理的回馈就是政府对"次微米计画"真实的经费付出。这数字是57亿5500万元（5亿7550万股）。

开标了。我们得标。惊奇的是：我们是唯一投标者！联电董事长立刻发布：他成功地抬高了我对政府的回馈。当时我虽稍有被玩弄的不快，但这不快很快地过去，毕竟，更大的挑战还在后面。

得标后，立刻成立新公司，初期资本总额一百八十亿元。新公司的名字，世界先进，是后来卢志远取的。志远也同时带领"次微米计画"同仁共两百余人过来到新公司，成为新公司的核心团队。志远成为新公司的营运副总。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Raising capital for a memory company in 1994 was much easier than it had been for TSMC in 1985-86. The important reason: TSMC had experienced explosive growth within seven years of its founding (1993 revenue NT$12.3 billion, net operating profit NT$4.4 billion). Investors believed the new memory company would succeed similarly. The new company raised NT$12.2 billion within a few months. Investors, led by TSMC (NT$4.2 billion), included thirteen companies such as UMC and Taisu Core, plus ITRI and TSMC employees. Compared to TSMC's fundraising in 1985-86, when TSMC had only raised NT$5.5 billion.</p>
<p class="en-para">Next came the "bidding"—determining how much of the new company's stock to give the government in return. Before bidding, industry rumors said at least TSMC and UMC would bid, so the bid numbers had to be kept strictly confidential. All investors authorized me to decide without informing them. After thinking for a few days, I decided the reasonable return to the government was the actual funding the government had contributed to the "Submicron Program." This figure was NT$575.5 million (57.55 million shares).</p>
<p class="en-para">The bids were opened. We won the bid. Surprisingly: we were the only bidder! UMC's chairman immediately announced that he had successfully driven up our return to the government. Although I felt a slight displeasure at being played, this displeasure quickly passed—after all, the bigger challenges still lay ahead.</p>
<p class="en-para">After winning, the new company was immediately formed, with initial total capital of NT$18 billion. The new company's name, Vanguard International, was later coined by Lu Chih-yuan. Chih-yuan also led over 200 colleagues from the "Submicron Program" to the new company, becoming its core team. Chih-yuan became the new company's COO.</p>
</div>

## 艾凡斯与波折

我自经济部决定要招标时，就开始物色总经理。第一人史钦泰。他是"次微米计画之父"，也是台湾资深半导体研发者，所以是当然人选。史在九三年底也答应接受此职。但九四年初，史忽然被升任为工研院院长，他就告诉我：他宁愿当工研院院长，而不来世界先进做总经理了。

我的第二人选是孔毅（Roger Kung），当时在美国摩托罗拉担任。经过面后，孔同意参加。但当他辞职时，摩托罗拉给了新职位。他宁留摩托罗拉，不来世界先进了。

第三人选是一位德仪旧属，当时在英特尔当副。但考虑了几天后，也回绝了我。

三个人回绝我后，我有点著急了。我想到老朋友艾凡斯。他曾任IBM副总裁多年，后来参加H&Q创业投资基金。我想他虽不曾经营半导体事业，但对半导体不陌生，而且他多年电脑企业高阶经验，应该能领导世界先进的年轻一辈。艾凡斯欣然接受我的聘请，在九五年一月报到。

此后十个月世界先进在兴奋的忙碌中度过——忙碌扩厂，购置制造设备。4M DRAM是世先唯一能量产的产品，市价持续高于十二美元，而制造成本约五、六美元。营收从第一季几乎零，到第二季四亿元，第三季度十五亿元！到八月，公司首次收支平衡。艾凡斯模仿台积电首月收支平衡的做法，宣布员工分红！

但是好景不长。九六年四月的一个周末，艾凡斯约我午餐，告诉我4M DRAM价格迅速下降，因为下一代记忆体：十六M DRAM来了。但世界先进的十六M DRAM还在开发中，离量产还远得很。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">When the Ministry of Economic Affairs decided on bidding, I began scouting for a general manager. My first choice was Shi Qin-tai. He was the "father of the Submicron Program" and Taiwan's senior semiconductor R&amp;D leader—he was the natural choice. Shi had agreed to accept the position at year-end 1993. But in early 1994, Shi was suddenly promoted to ITRI president. He told me: he would rather be ITRI president than come to Vanguard as general manager.</p>
<p class="en-para">My second choice was Roger Kung, then at Motorola in the U.S. After interviews, Kung agreed to join. But when he resigned, Motorola offered him a new position. He chose to stay at Motorola instead.</p>
<p class="en-para">My third choice was a former TI subordinate who was then a vice president at Intel. But after considering for a few days, he also declined.</p>
<p class="en-para">After three people rejected me, I was getting anxious. I thought of an old friend— Evans. He had served as an IBM vice president for many years, later joining H&amp;Q venture capital. I thought that although he had never run a semiconductor business, he was no stranger to semiconductors. Moreover, with his many years of senior computer industry experience, he should be able to lead Vanguard's younger generation. Evans gladly accepted my offer and reported for duty in January 1995.</p>
<p class="en-para">For the next ten months, Vanguard passed in excited busyness—busy expanding the fab, purchasing manufacturing equipment. 4M DRAM was Vanguard's only volume product, with market price consistently above $12 while manufacturing cost was about $5-6. Revenue went from almost zero in Q1 to NT$400 million in Q2, NT$1.5 billion in Q3! By August, the company achieved break-even for the first time. Evans imitated TSMC's practice after its first break-even month and announced an employee bonus!</p>
<p class="en-para">But the good times didn't last. On a weekend in April 1996, Evans asked me to lunch and told me that 4M DRAM prices were falling rapidly because the next generation—16M DRAM—was arriving. But Vanguard's 16M DRAM was still in development, far from volume production.</p>
</div>

## 曾繁城接手与转型

九六年新年假后，4M DRAM市价继续崩跌；第一季平均价格是7.57美元，第二季3.94美元，第三季2.29美元。自第二季起，世界先进进入亏损状态，而且生产愈多，亏损愈大。艾凡斯撑不住了。

我怜悯这位老朋友，但同时也决定他不能继续做世界先进总经理了。刚巧那时台积电营运副总曾繁城我不满他的总经理布鲁克，想从台积电辞职。我对曾说："你不必辞职，暂时去世界先进当总经理吧。"曾欣然同意。五月八日我就对世界先进干部宣布艾凡斯辞职，繁城（大家都称他为FC）任总经理。

再一年，世界先进亏损更大，FC更急著要回台积电。九八年五月，我宣布FC回台积电任总经理职，卢志远暂代世界先进总经理。同时我竭力鼓励当时台积电营运副总蔡力行去世界先进当总经理。蔡很不愿意，但经我力劝后，他终于同意。

蔡力行只做了十三个月（一九九九年二月至二零零零年三月）世界先进总经理，但他做了一件很重要的事——他对我说："世界先进若记忆体没有希望，把它转型成专业晶圆代工！与台积电一样。那么，台积电可以帮助它慢慢再站起来。"

我同意。自二零零年起，我们把世界先进转型。二零零一至二零零二年全球半导体业不景气，台积电和世界先进都不能幸免，但二零零三年后，世界先进的晶圆代工业务就慢慢茁壮。这十几年来，世界先进已是一个很健康的专业晶圆代工公司。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">After the 1996 New Year holiday, 4M DRAM market prices continued to crash. Q1 average price: $7.57; Q2: $3.94; Q3: $2.29. From Q2 onward, Vanguard entered a loss position—the more they produced, the more they lost. Evans couldn't hold out anymore.</p>
<p class="en-para">I pitied this old friend, but also decided he couldn't continue as Vanguard's general manager. As it happened, TSMC's COO Zeng Fan-cheng was dissatisfied with his general manager Brook and wanted to resign from TSMC. I said to Zeng: "You needn't resign—go to Vanguard as general manager for now." Zeng gladly agreed. On May 8, I announced to Vanguard executives that Evans had resigned and Fan-cheng (everyone called him FC) was now general manager.</p>
<p class="en-para">One year later, Vanguard's losses grew even larger, and FC was even more anxious to return to TSMC. In May 1998, I announced FC's return to TSMC as general manager, with Lu Chih-yuan acting as Vanguard general manager. At the same time, I strongly encouraged TSMC COO Cai Lixing to go to Vanguard as general manager. Cai was very reluctant, but after my earnest urging, he finally agreed.</p>
<p class="en-para">Cai Lixing served as Vanguard general manager for only thirteen months (February 1999 to March 2000), but he did one very important thing—he said to me: "If Vanguard has no future in memory, transform it into a dedicated foundry! Like TSMC. Then TSMC can help it slowly stand up again."</p>
<p class="en-para">I agreed. From 2000, we transformed Vanguard. The 2001-2002 global semiconductor downturn hit both TSMC and Vanguard, but after 2003, Vanguard's foundry business gradually grew stronger. For over a decade now, Vanguard has been a healthy dedicated foundry company.</p>
</div>

## 记忆体梦碎

德碁比世界先进早五年开始，但它的命运与世界先进相似。世界先进虽有自主记忆体技术，但技术跟不上竞争者，所以失败。德碁没有自主记忆体技术，它的技术来源——德仪，在九零年代决定退出。幸而在九九年台积电因为自己来不急扩充产能，决定为增加产能而购并德碁。自此德碁的招牌消失，它的工厂变成台积电的晶圆七厂。

九零年代德碁和世界先进在记忆体上的努力，是台湾企图强力进入记忆体业的勇敢一搏。但"爱拼才会赢"，世界先进和德碁拼了，而没有赢。幸而有台积电这一支救兵，才不致"死伤惨重"。

当年李健熙的"忠"告："台湾出不起记忆体业需要的资本，也没有足够人才……要是真的要做记忆体，还是与三星合作"，至少有一半是对的。的确，德碁和世界先进人才、资本都不够，但是李健熙漏了另一个重点："学习曲线"！在九零年代德碁和世界先进开始做记忆体时，三星已远在"学习曲线"的前面。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Tepro started five years earlier than Vanguard, but its fate was similar to Vanguard's. Although Vanguard had independent memory technology, the technology couldn't keep up with competitors, so it failed. Tepro had no independent memory technology at all, and its technology source—TI—decided to exit the memory business in the 1990s. Fortunately, in 1999, TSMC, unable to expand its own capacity fast enough, decided to acquire Tepro to increase capacity. From then on, Tepro's brand disappeared, and its fabs became TSMC's Fab 7.</p>
<p class="en-para">The efforts of Tepro and Vanguard in memory in the 1990s were a bold attempt by Taiwan to forcefully enter the memory industry. But "only those who fight will win"—Vanguard and Tepro fought, but didn't win. Fortunately TSMC existed as a rescue force, preventing "heavy casualties."</p>
<p class="en-para">Lee Kun-hee's "sincere" advice—"Taiwan cannot afford the capital needed for the memory industry, nor does it have enough talent... if you really want to do memory, better to cooperate with Samsung"—was at least half right. Indeed, Tepro and Vanguard lacked both talent and capital. But Lee Kun-hee missed another critical point: the "learning curve"! When Tepro and Vanguard started making memory in the 1990s, Samsung was already far ahead on the learning curve.</p>
</div>
