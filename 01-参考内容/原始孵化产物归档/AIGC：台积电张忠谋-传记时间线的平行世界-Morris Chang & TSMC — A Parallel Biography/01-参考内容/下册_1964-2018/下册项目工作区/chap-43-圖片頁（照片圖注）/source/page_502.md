2009年5月全球经济刚从金融危机中走出来时，我与诺贝尔经济学奖得主克鲁格曼有一段对谈。克鲁格曼说他对全球短期景气审慎乐观，对中期相当悲观，但对十五到二十年后的长期则是非常乐观。克鲁格曼那时的"长期"我已经是九十几岁，于是我忍不住引用另一位著名经济学家凯恩斯的说法"长期，大家都死了"，引得哄堂大笑。（《商业周刊》提供）

<p class="en-para">In May 2009, when the global economy was just emerging from the financial crisis, I had a dialogue with Nobel economics laureate Paul Krugman. Krugman said he was cautiously optimistic about the short-term outlook, quite pessimistic about the medium term, but very optimistic about the long term of fifteen to twenty years out. At that time, Krugman's "long term" would find me in my nineties, so I couldn't help but quote another famous economist, John Maynard Keynes: "In the long run, we are all dead." The audience burst into laughter. (Business Weekly)</p>

## 修辞赏析

引用凯恩斯名言"长期，大家都死了"堪称神来之笔——以幽默的哲学智慧回应经济学家的长期预测，制造出人意料的喜剧效果。
