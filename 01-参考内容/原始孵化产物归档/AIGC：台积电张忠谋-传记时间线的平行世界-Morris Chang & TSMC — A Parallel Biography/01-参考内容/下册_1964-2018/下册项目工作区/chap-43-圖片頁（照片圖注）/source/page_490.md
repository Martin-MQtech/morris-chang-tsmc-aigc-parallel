1994年9月3日，台积电股票以每股订新台币90元在台湾证券交易所上市，但却是"有价无市"，此后六个交易日都"无量涨停"，直到涨到142.5元才开始有交易。

1997年7月台积电租用40公顷土地开始兴建晶圆六厂，是第一家进驻南科的企业。直到2024年，台积电在南科共租用180公顷土地，员工人数则超过23,000人。

<p class="en-para">On September 3, 1994, TSMC stock listed on the Taiwan Stock Exchange at NT$90 per share, but it was a market "with price but no volume" — for the next six trading days, price rose to the daily limit with zero turnover, until reaching NT$142.5 when trading finally began.</p>
<p class="en-para">In July 1997, TSMC leased 40 hectares of land to build Fab 6, becoming the first company to enter the Southern Taiwan Science Park. By 2024, TSMC has leased a total of 180 hectares in the park with over 23,000 employees.</p>
