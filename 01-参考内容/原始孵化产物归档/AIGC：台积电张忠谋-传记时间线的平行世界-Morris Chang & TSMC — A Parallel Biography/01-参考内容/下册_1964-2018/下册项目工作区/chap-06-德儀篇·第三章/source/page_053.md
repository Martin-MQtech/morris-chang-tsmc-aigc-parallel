# 德仪篇·第三章｜总经理的学习

掌握技术——

我认为半导体业总经理，一定要深懂半导体技术。我在德仪从「锗部」总经理，做到「矽部」总经理、「集成电路部」总经理、「全球半导体」总经理，而且半导体技术又是与日俱进，所以掌握技术进步绝对是我总经理任务的重项。在这重项上，我拥有过去半导体工程师的经验，又加上史丹佛的博士教育，再加上在每一总经理任内，我至少每星期有两三小时与技术人员会谈——不懂必问，对我的计划有疑虑也必质疑，有时也会做技术专家们没有没有想到的建议。在这样的环境中，我相信我这个总经理追赶上了半导体技术。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Mastering technology —</p>
<p class="en-para">I believe that a semiconductor GM must deeply understand semiconductor technology. At TI, I progressed from GM of the Germanium Division to GM of the Silicon Division, then GM of the Integrated Circuit Division, and finally GM of Worldwide Semiconductors. And semiconductor technology advanced daily. So keeping pace with technological progress was absolutely a top priority for me as GM. For this, I had my earlier experience as a semiconductor engineer, plus my Stanford PhD education. Moreover, in every GM role, I spent at least two or three hours each week meeting with technical staff. When I did not understand something, I asked. When I had doubts about their plans, I challenged them. Sometimes I made suggestions the technical experts had not thought of. In this environment, I believe this GM kept pace with semiconductor technology.</p>
</div>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Keep pace with technological progress**
**中文解释**：追赶技术进步，指管理者持续更新技术知识以避免决策脱节；在半导体产业中，技术迭代速度极快，「追赶」而非「保持」是更诚实的描述。
**商业造句">The CEO of a photonics startup must keep pace with technological progress — not by running the lab, but by asking questions sharp enough to expose what the team does not yet understand.*

2. **Challenge plans**
**中文质疑计划，指管理者对技术团队的提议提出建设性质疑；在高效组织中，「质疑」被视为一种尊重而非否定。
**商业造句">A board that challenges plans without demoralizing the team is worth more than one that rubber-stamps every proposal.*

### 修辞赏析

<span class="rhetoric-note">
<span class="zh">「不懂必问」四个字，是张忠谋最反直觉的管理习惯。大多数管理者——尤其是那些从技术岗位升上来的管理者——本能地回避「不懂」这个词。张忠谋却把它变成了自己的竞争优势。一个总经理敢于在技术人员面前说「我不懂，请你解释」，不仅展现了罕见的谦逊，更重要的是：它迫使技术人员把复杂的技术问题翻译成管理者能理解的商业语言。这个过程本身就是一种组织学习。</span>
<span class="en">Four characters: "When I did not understand, I asked." This is Chang's most counterintuitive management habit. Most managers — especially those promoted from technical roles — instinctively avoid the phrase "I don't understand." Chang turned it into a competitive advantage. A GM who dares to say "I don't understand, please explain" to technical staff not only displays rare humility but, more importantly: it forces technical staff to translate complex technical problems into business language the manager can grasp. This process itself is a form of organizational learning.</span>
</span>

### 背景知识延伸

**技术型总经理 vs. 商业型总经理**：张忠谋坚持「半导体业总经理一定要深懂半导体技术」，这与现代MBA教育中「总经理应该是通才」的理念形成鲜明对比。他的理由是：半导体是一个技术驱动产业，技术路线决策往往决定了企业的生死。如果总经理不懂技术，就无法判断技术团队的建议是否合理、无法在关键技术转折点上做出正确选择。这一认知后来被张忠谋完整地带入台积电——他本人深度参与技术路线讨论，台积电的技术决策也因此比竞争对手更少犯战略性错误。
