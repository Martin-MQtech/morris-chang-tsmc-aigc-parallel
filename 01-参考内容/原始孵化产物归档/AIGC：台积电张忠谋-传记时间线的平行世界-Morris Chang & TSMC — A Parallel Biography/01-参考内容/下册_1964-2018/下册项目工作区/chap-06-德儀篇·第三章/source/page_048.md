# 德仪篇·第三章｜总经理的学习

一九六四年被任命为「锗部」总经理时，正如我就职时对主要下属的谈话——我对「锗部」的研发、工程、生产部门都已相当熟悉，但对领导、行销、客户、市场、定价、会计，还有许多要学习。这以后几年，我全力投入学习如何做「总经理」。

首先学习的是行销。卖不出货，哪来利润？德仪半导体部门有「总行销部」，为半导体部门所有「业务单位」共用，但是各业务单位——例如「锗部」——有它自己的「市场司」。「市场司」规划新产品，帮助「总行销部」的行销工程师争取订单，而且还有一个很重要的权力：定价。

<p class="en-para">When I was appointed General Manager of the Germanium Division in 1964, as I had said to my key subordinates in my inaugural address — I was already quite familiar with the division's R&D, engineering, and production functions. But leadership, marketing, customers, markets, pricing, financial accounting — I still had much to learn. In the years that followed, I devoted myself entirely to learning how to be a general manager.</p>
<p class="en-para">The first thing I learned was marketing. If you can't sell the product, where does profit come from? TI's semiconductor division had a "Central Marketing Group" shared by all business units, but each business unit — such as the Germanium Division — had its own "Marketing Department." The Marketing Department planned new products, helped the Central Marketing Group's field sales engineers win orders, and held one critical power: pricing.</p>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Field sales engineer**
**中文解释**：行销工程师（现场销售工程师），指兼具技术知识和销售能力的一线人员；在半导体产业中，行销工程师是客户与工厂之间的关键桥梁。
**商业造句">The field sales engineer who could translate a customer's thermal requirements into a spec sheet was worth ten pure salespeople in the semiconductor business.*

2. **Central Marketing Group**
**中文解释**：总行销部（中央营销部门），指为所有业务单位提供 shared marketing 服务的组织；这种矩阵式结构既保证了规模化效率，也保留了各业务单位的自主性。
**商业造句">The Central Marketing Group set the pricing guidelines, but each business unit's Marketing Department had the final say on specific deals — a balance of scale and autonomy.*

3. **Pricing power**
**中文解释**：定价权，指企业对产品售价的控制能力；拥有定价权的企业通常具备技术壁垒、品牌信任、或供应短缺中的至少一项。
**商业造句">Patents gave the company temporary pricing power — but temporary was enough to fund three years of next-generation R&D.*

### 修辞赏析

<span class="rhetoric-note">
<span class="zh">「卖不出货，哪来利润？」——这句大白话，是张忠谋独特的教学式叙事风格。他不断用这种反问句把复杂的管理问题拉回到最朴素的商业常识。这种风格在「自传下册」中反复出现，成为他作为企业思想家最易辨识的修辞标志。它不仅仅是一种写作技巧，更是一种世界观：所有的管理学问，最终都要回答「卖不出货，哪来利润」这个最根本的问题。</span>
<span class="en">"If you can't sell the product, where does profit come from?" — This plain-spoken question is Chang's distinctive pedagogical narrative style. He repeatedly uses such rhetorical questions to pull complex management problems back to the most fundamental business common sense. This style recurs throughout the second volume, becoming his most recognizable rhetorical signature as a business thinker. It is not merely a writing technique but a worldview: all management knowledge must ultimately answer the most fundamental question — "If you can't sell the product, where does profit come from?"</span>

### 背景知识延伸

**德仪的业务单位（Business Unit）体制**：一九六〇至七〇年代，德仪率先在半导体产业中推行「业务单位」制。每个业务单位相当于一家小型公司，拥有独立的研发、生产、营销和定价权。这种分权式组织结构在当时是一种管理创新——它让每个业务单位像创业公司一样敏捷，同时又共享德仪的品牌、资金和制造能力。张忠谋是这个体制最大的受益者之一：三十二岁就被授予总经理头衔，掌管一个营收数千万美元的完整事业体——这在层级分明的日本或欧洲企业中几乎不可能发生。
