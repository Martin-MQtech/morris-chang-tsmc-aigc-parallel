# 德仪篇·第三章｜总经理的学习

学习领导——

三十三岁当「锗部」总经理时，我第一次领导一个人数众多（三千人）的组织。此后几十年中，除了几个短时期外，我领导的人数从几千人到几万人。当然每个时刻，经常只有十来个人对我直接报告，但我总认出他们下面一层、甚至再下面一层里积极做事、能力不错的人员，所以我日常接触几十个人。

当时我只凭我的直觉领导。五十几年后的现在分析领导和能力，觉得领导力有两部分：第一部分是凝聚团队，第二部分是带领团队朝对的方向走。而领导是否成功，却只有一个标准：你的团队有没有在赢？

如何凝聚团队？以诚信对待。我相当严格地为每一个单位主管设目标，我也赏罚分明。在德仪，赏、罚的幅度相当大——赏可以加薪、发奖金、送股票选择权；罚可以调职、降职甚至解雇。德仪的企业文化不但允许，甚至鼓励主管把不适任的下属调职、降职甚至解雇。

<p class="en-para">Learning leadership —</p>
<p class="en-para">When at thirty-three I became GM of the Germanium Division, it was the first time I had led a large organization — three thousand people. In the decades that followed, except for a few brief periods, I led groups ranging from a few thousand to tens of thousands. Of course, at any given moment, only about ten people reported directly to me. But I always made a point of identifying the energetic, capable people one or two levels below my direct reports, so my daily contacts numbered in the dozens.</p>
<p class="en-para">At the time, I led purely by instinct. Analyzing leadership and ability fifty-odd years later, I believe leadership has two components: the first is bonding the team, and the second is leading the team in the right direction. And there is only one standard for whether leadership is successful: is your team winning?</p>
<p class="en-para">How to bond a team? Treat people with integrity. I set goals rigorously for every unit head, and I rewarded and punished clearly. At TI, the magnitude of rewards and punishments was considerable — rewards could be salary increases, bonuses, stock options; punishments could be transfers, demotions, even termination. TI's corporate culture not only permitted but actively encouraged managers to transfer, demote, or even terminate subordinates who were not performing.</p>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Bond the team**
**中文解释**：凝聚团队，指建立团队成员之间的信任和归属感；在组织行为学中，「bonding」与「alignment」是两个互补但不同的领导力维度。
**商业造句">The new CTO bonded the team not through offsites and trust falls, but by consistently backing her engineering leaders in public and coaching them in private.*

2. **Winning as the only standard**
**中文解释」：赢作为唯一标准，指以结果而非过程评价领导力；在竞技性强的商业环境中，这种标准虽然残酷，但往往最为公正。
**商业造句">She rejected "good effort" as a leadership metric — in her view, winning was the only standard, because the market did not award points for effort.*

3. **Stock options**
**中文解释**：股票选择权，指在未来以约定价格购买公司股票的权利；在六〇年代的美国企业中，股票选择权是吸引和留住高层人才的关键工具。
**商业造句">Stock options turned a handful of Atari programmers into millionaires in 1982 — and convinced a generation of engineers that joining a startup was not irresponsible.*

### 修辞赏析

<span class="rhetoric-note">
<span class="zh">「你的团队有没有在赢？」——张忠谋用这一个反问句，给出了领导力最冷酷也最清晰的定义。他不谈「愿景」、不谈「使命」、不谈「文化」，只谈一个字：「赢」。这种极简主义的领导力定义，与他五十年前凭直觉领导的经验一脉相承。它也解释了张忠谋在台积电的管理风格为什么如此「铁血」：因为在他的体系中，「赢」不是可选项，而是唯一合法的存在理由。</span>
<span class="en">"Is your team winning?" — With a single rhetorical question, Chang delivers the coldest and clearest definition of leadership. No "vision," no "mission," no "culture" — just one word: "winning." This minimalist definition of leadership is consistent with his fifty years of instinctive leadership. It also explains why Chang's management style at TSMC was so "iron-blooded": in his system, "winning" is not optional — it is the only legitimate reason for existence.</span>
</span>

### 背景知识延伸

**以诚相交、保持距离**：张忠谋在自传中罕见地分享了自己的管理细节：「以诚恳态度对待部下：不说假话，但也保持相当距离，很少与部下交际。」德仪常有团体同事餐会，大家都携眷参加，张忠谋当然也携眷参加。此外——每次升迁请他和妻子到家里晚餐，他也尽量应邀，因为「这是礼貌之事」——但「一次单独邀请，认识家眷以后，以后也只有团体活动才见面」。张忠谋认为这样的保持距离，「可以在我这里做出客观的决策」。这种「亲密有间」的管理风格，在他此后五十年的领导生涯中从未改变。
