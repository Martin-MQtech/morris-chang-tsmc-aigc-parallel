## 寂寞的七十寿辰

一九七九年农历正月，是母亲农历七十寿辰。那时我正在赶「家庭电脑」的开发，孤单地住在拉吧。母亲寿辰前的几个礼拜，我打电话给住在纽约的父母亲，告诉他们我工作忙得很，不能去纽约庆祝母亲的七十寿辰了。父母亲说，那就让他们到拉吧来过母亲的七十岁生日吧，顺便看看我在拉吧的生活情形。

我的拉吧公寓虽然够大，但除了我卧室和客厅有几件必要的家具外，没有别的家具；所以我把父母亲安顿在一个汽车旅馆里。父母亲来了几天，我还是每天照常上班；母亲寿辰那天，我们三人去附近一家中国餐馆点了几道菜，算是庆祝母亲寿辰。

我记得母亲四十寿辰，是在香港过的，那时我们刚从上海逃难到香港，但在母亲生日那天，还是摆了好几桌麻将，晚上有好几桌宴席；母亲五十寿辰在纽约过，那时我在德仪已有初期的成功，我请假到纽约，买了一件貂皮短大衣送给母亲，参加了母亲热闹的寿宴，母亲好高兴，穿了新大衣赴宴，告诉朋友这是她儿子送给她的；母亲六十寿辰，我刚升德仪副总裁不久，请父母同去迈阿密及牙买加度几天愉快的假期。

现在母亲七十寿辰，我在德仪的下山路上，父母亲知道，也痛惜我在下山路上。在陈旧的中餐厅用了晚餐后，三人在冷寂的拉吧街道上，暗淡欲熄的路灯下，无言步回父母亲的旅馆。母亲在餐馆里强颜为笑，但在路灯下已凄然欲泣了。

<p class="en-para"><b>A Lonely Seventieth Birthday</b></p>
<p class="en-para">In the first lunar month of 1979, it was my mother's seventieth lunar birthday. At the time, I was racing to develop the home computer, living alone in Lubbock. A few weeks before her birthday, I called my parents in New York and told them I was too busy with work to travel to New York to celebrate my mother's seventieth birthday. My parents said they would come to Lubbock instead to celebrate her seventieth birthday and see how I was living.</p>
<p class="en-para">My Lubbock apartment was spacious enough, but beyond a few essential pieces of furniture in my bedroom and living room, it was unfurnished. So I settled my parents in a motel. They stayed for several days; I still went to work every day as usual. On my mother birthday, the three of us went to a nearby Chinese restaurant and ordered a few dishes to mark the occasion.</p>
<p class="en-para">I remember my mother's fortieth birthday was celebrated in Hong Kong. We had just fled Shanghai for Hong Kong, but on her birthday, we still set up several tables of mahjong and hosted multiple dinner parties in the evening. Her fiftieth birthday was in New York. By then I had achieved initial success at TI. I took time off to go to New York, bought a mink jacket for my mother, and attended her lively birthday banquet. Mother was so happy — she wore the new jacket to the party and told her friends it was a gift from her son. Her sixtieth birthday came shortly after I was promoted to TI Vice President. I took my parents to Miami and Jamaica for a pleasant vacation.</p>
<p class="en-para">Now, my mother's seventieth birthday — I was on the downhill path at TI. My parents knew it and grieved for me on that downhill path. After dinner at a dated Chinese restaurant, the three of us walked in silence along the desolate Lubbock streets, under the dim, flickering streetlights, back to my parents' motel. My mother had forced a smile at the restaurant, but under the streetlights she was already on the verge of tears.</p>

### 修辞赏析

<span class="rhetoric-note">
<span class="zh">「母亲在餐馆里强颜为笑，但在路灯下已凄然欲泣了。」——这是张忠谋自传中最为沉痛的个人场景。整部自传以理性和克制著称，但这一段关于母亲七十寿辰的叙述，几乎打破了这种克制。从四十寿辰的热闹、五十寿辰的貂皮大衣、六十寿辰的迈阿密假期，到七十寿辰在拉吧汽车旅馆前的无声漫步——张忠谋用一组递减的「寿辰蒙太奇」，把一个职业经理人的「下山」与一个家庭的情感代价并置，让读者看到：在那些宏大的商业决策背后，是具体的人、具体的牺牲、具体的无法弥补的遗憾。</span>
<span class="en">"My mother had forced a smile at the restaurant, but under the streetlights she was already on the verge of tears." This is one of the most devastating personal scenes in Chang's entire autobiography. The entire memoir is known for its rationality and restraint, but this passage about his mother's seventieth birthday nearly breaks that restraint. From the liveliness of the fortieth, the mink jacket of the fiftieth, the Miami vacation of the sixtieth, to the silent walk in front of a Lubbock motel at the seventieth — Chang uses a descending "birthday montage" to juxtapose a professional manager's "descent" with a family's emotional cost, showing the reader: behind those grand commercial decisions are real people, real sacrifices, real irreparable regrets.</span>
</span>
