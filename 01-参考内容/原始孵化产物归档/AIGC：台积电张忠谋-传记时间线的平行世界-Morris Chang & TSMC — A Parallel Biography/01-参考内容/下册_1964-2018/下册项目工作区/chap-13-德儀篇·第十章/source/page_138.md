## 消费者计算机

消费者计算机的问题是成本。在日本及台湾厂商激烈竞争下，德仪已毫无订价力，盈亏全靠成本。成本又分两类：元件（半导体和液晶显示器），及封装。元件都是德仪自己半导体集团供应的。供应消费者集团是我任半导体总经理时的大头痛，我也知道在这宗生意上半导体的利润很低，现在我易位而处，无颜也无立场要他们再让价。

封装成本呢？的确有机会。一九七八年我们已把大部分封装工作外包给日本东芝公司，但东芝既是竞争者，而且为我们封装的价格也不低。那么为什么不找一家更便宜的台湾厂商呢？

简单的答案是：德仪不够信任台湾厂商的品质。那时德仪台湾已封装测试集成电路近十年，也获得总部的信任，但那信任是老师（美国德仪）对徒弟（德仪台湾）的信任；在计算机封装上，美国德仪既无资格做任何厂商的老师，也不轻易信任任何代工商。但对日本厂商的一般品质相当佩服，所以就让东芝代工。

假使海格底没有在一九七六年退休，那么计算机的封装成本问题，恐怕在一九七八年就解决了。那一年，海格底（虽已从董事长职位退休，但仍为董事）受孙运璿院长、李国鼎政务委员之邀，来台湾访问一周。孙、李给了他「红地毯」待遇：每晚必宴，每演讲必满座；孙、李讨教不尽，而且请海格底组织「行政院科技顾问团」，每年来台湾开会一周。

<p class="en-para"><b>Consumer Calculators</b></p>
<p class="en-para">The consumer calculator problem was cost. With fierce competition from Japanese and Taiwanese manufacturers, TI had no pricing power at all — profit or loss depended entirely on cost. Costs broke down into two categories: components (semiconductors and LCD displays), and packaging. Components were all supplied by TI's own Semiconductor Group. Supplying the Consumer Products Group had been a major headache when I was Semiconductor GM, and I knew semiconductor margins on this business were razor-thin. Now that I had switched seats, I had neither the face nor the standing to ask them to lower prices further.</p>
<p class="en-para">Packaging costs? There was indeed an opportunity. In 1978, we had already outsourced most packaging work to Japan's Toshiba, but Toshiba was both a competitor and their packaging prices weren't low. So why not find a cheaper Taiwanese manufacturer?</p>
<p class="en-para">The simple answer: TI didn't trust Taiwanese manufacturers' quality enough. By then, TI Taiwan had been packaging and testing ICs for nearly a decade and had earned headquarters' trust — but that trust was a teacher's (American TI) trust in its apprentice (TI Taiwan). In calculator packaging, American TI had no qualification to be anyone's teacher, and was not inclined to trust any contract manufacturer lightly. But TI held Japanese manufacturers' general quality in high regard, so they let Toshiba do the contract work.</p>
<p class="en-para">Had Haggerty not retired in 1976, the calculator packaging cost problem would likely have been resolved by 1978. That year, Haggerty (though retired from the Chairman role, still a Director) was invited by Premier Sun Yun-suan and Minister Lee Kuo-ting to visit Taiwan for a week. Sun and Lee gave him the "red-carpet" treatment: dinner every night, packed auditoriums for every lecture. They sought his counsel extensively and asked Haggerty to organize the "Executive Yuan Science and Technology Advisory Group," which would meet in Taiwan for one week each year.</p>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Pricing power vs. cost control (订价力 vs. 成本控制)**
**中文解释**：在激烈竞争的市场中，企业失去「订价力」（pricing power）后，唯一的生存之道就是成本控制；德仪的消费者计算机业务正是这种「无订价力、只能拼成本」的典型处境。
**商业造句">With no pricing power in the calculator market, TI's only path to profitability was ruthless cost reduction — a game where Japanese and Taiwanese competitors held structural advantages.*

2. **Trust gradient in outsourcing (外包中的信任梯度)**
**中文解释**：指企业在选择代工商时的信任层级——「老师对徒弟的信任」>「对日本厂商的佩服」>「对台湾厂商的不够信任」；这种信任梯度直接决定了外包决策，即使成本差异明显。
**商业造句">TI's trust gradient — teacher-apprecice trust in TI Taiwan, admiration for Japanese quality, but insufficient trust in independent Taiwanese contractors — overrode pure cost logic in outsourcing decisions.*
