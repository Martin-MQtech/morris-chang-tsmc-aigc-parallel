## 机器人之旅

在我们正为计算机装配成本困扰、又不能决定是否继续外包东芝之时，忽然夏伯特引进一位「机器人专家」。此人来源我已记不清楚，花了约三年时间（正与我任消费者集团总经理时间重叠），他是德仪公司相当「红」的人。夏伯特让他成立了一个机器人开发组。公司高级主管包括我，几乎每月都被召去听他的进展报告。老实说，当时我认为他的研发进度距离真正应用在计算机或电子表的封装上，至少十年、二十年（事实上，四十年后的现在，还没有大规模的机器人封装），但夏伯特似乎认为机器人的应用已近在眉睫。

在这位专家报告时，我坐在第二排想打瞌睡。我最深的印象是，他常伸缩、弯曲他的左右手臂来模拟机器人。姿势倒很好。

## 电子表和巴黎行

德仪的电子表业务以高价（两百美元以上）的LED表开始，后来发现高价表需要「品牌」，而「德仪」品牌显然不足。再后来，德仪开发了低成本的液晶显示器（LCD），就开始进攻低价电子表市场。在我接任消费者集团时，大部分电子表营收已属低价电子表类，而低价电子表又有东南亚厂商的激烈竞争——德仪的整个电子表业务已在亏损状态。

高价表倒引起了董事会的兴趣。我发现，几乎每一位董事（都男性）都戴了一只名牌手表。董事会的兴趣集中在手表的图形设计上，他们要求：每一个新设计样本都要先让他们审视。结果，没有一个设计能让大家都喜欢。

多年戴中阶Seiko手表的我，一开始就被董事会认为是手表外行人，并没有与他们讨论的资格。讲到高价手表需要「品牌」，有一位拥有法国葡萄园的德仪董事，运用了他在时尚界的关系，替我去巴黎与皮尔·卡登（Pierre Cardin）见面。卡登的名字就是名牌，我见他的目的，就是奉董事会命要求他授权德仪把名字放在我们高价表上。

<p class="en-para"><b>The Robot Venture</b></p>
<p class="en-para">Just as we were mired in calculator assembly costs and couldn't decide whether to continue outsourcing to Toshiba, Shepherd suddenly brought in a "robotics expert." I can no longer remember this person's background. He spent about three years on this (overlapping precisely with my tenure as Consumer Products GM) and was a highly "famous" figure within TI. Shepherd had him set up a robotics development group. Senior executives, including me, were summoned nearly monthly to hear his progress reports. Frankly, I believed his R&D progress was at least ten to twenty years away from real application in calculator or watch packaging (in fact, forty years later, there is still no large-scale robotic packaging). But Shepherd seemed to think robotics application was imminent.</p>
<p class="en-para">During this expert's presentations, I sat in the second row fighting sleep. My deepest impression was how he frequently stretched and bent his left and right arms to simulate a robot. The gestures were quite good.</p>
<p class="en-para"><b>Electronic Watches and the Paris Trip</b></p>
<p class="en-para">TI's electronic watch business began with high-end (over $200) LED watches. Later, it was discovered that high-end watches required "brand," and the "TI" brand was clearly insufficient. Still later, TI developed low-cost LCD displays and began attacking the low-end electronic watch market. By the time I took over the Consumer Products Group, most watch revenue came from low-end electronic watches, and the low-end market saw fierce competition from Southeast Asian manufacturers — TI's entire watch business was already in a loss position.</p>
<p class="en-para">The high-end watches, however, captured the Board's interest. I discovered that nearly every director (all male) wore a luxury brand watch. The Board's interest focused on the watch's graphic design. They demanded that every new design sample be submitted to them for review first. The result: no design pleased everyone.</p>
<p class="en-para">Having worn a mid-range Seiko for many years, I was immediately dismissed by the Board as a watch outsider with no standing to participate in their discussions. When it came to luxury watches requiring "brand," one TI director who owned a French vineyard used his connections in the fashion world to arrange for me to meet Pierre Cardin in Paris. Cardin's name was the brand. My purpose in meeting him was, at the Board's behest, to request that he license his name for our high-end watches.</p>

### 修辞赏析

<span class="rhetoric-note">
<span class="zh">「姿势倒很好」——这是张忠谋式的冷幽默。在整段关于机器人专家冗长而无望的叙述中，他以这四个字做结。没有愤怒，没有批评，只有一个旁观者对一场荒诞剧的简短评语。这种「冷幽默」是张忠谋自传中反复出现的修辞策略：面对不合理的人事和决策，他不直接批评，而是以一个细节（伸缩手臂模拟机器人）让读者自行判断其荒谬。</span>
<span class="en">"The gestures were quite good." This is classic Chang deadpan humor. After an entire passage about the robotics expert's interminable and hopeless presentations, he closes with these four words. No anger, no criticism — just a brief reviewer's note on an absurd performance. This "deadpan humor" is a recurring rhetorical device in Chang's autobiography: faced with unreasonable people and decisions, he doesn't criticize directly but offers a single detail (stretching arms to simulate a robot) and lets the reader draw their own conclusions about its absurdity.</span>
</span>
