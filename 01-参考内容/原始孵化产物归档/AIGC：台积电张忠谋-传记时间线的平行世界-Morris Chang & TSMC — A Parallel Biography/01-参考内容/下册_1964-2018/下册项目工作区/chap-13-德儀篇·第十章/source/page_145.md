我在半导体总经理任内，总是在烦恼消费者集团种种（我认为）不合理的要求，并没太注意所谓「家庭电脑」到底多么有用。到了消费者集团，很快就发现正在如火如荼开发的家庭电脑，实在用途不大；而且限制它的因素相当基本：集成电路的功能还不够成熟，成本也还太高。

怎么办？假使我那时（一九七八年）有现在的勇气，我会说：取消家庭电脑案！等集成电路和电脑软体技术更成熟（也就是说：做出来的家庭电脑更有用）后再议。

但是我那时没有这勇气。事实上，即使我有勇气对夏伯特及彪希建议取消家庭电脑，我也一定当场被严厉驳回。只要看后来家庭电脑彻底失败后，他们还坚持继续做，一直到一九八三年，搞得整个德仪公司几乎被拖垮，他们才停止家庭电脑业务就可以知道。

夏伯特早已宣告了他「到处都有计算」的理念。一九七八年九月美国《商业周刊》的封面故事，虽被彪希抢去了光采，但在「高科技创新」理念方面，却更固化夏伯特对「家庭电脑」的期待。

在我的岗位上，既没有勇气取消家庭电脑，还是尽力做吧！我把我手下最懂技术、最能干的经理人——彼得·邦菲（原任科学计算机总经理）调任为家庭电脑总经理；我给他一切他要求的人力经费。彼得的希望不见得比我的高，但我们要幹一番——也许奇迹会出现。

一九七九年六月，我们正式宣布德仪「家庭电脑」（TI99/4）问世，零售价一千一百五十美元。

宣布后两、三个月内，彼得和我就知道奇迹没有出现，通路和初期买主的反应已纷纷进来：应用有限，售价太高。最具讥笑意义的评论来自当时最红的半导体分析师罗森（他曾在一九七五、七六、七七年邀请我参加他举办的「三巨头座谈」）：

「巨山咆哮，出来的却是一只老鼠。」

<p class="en-para">As Semiconductor GM, I had always fretted about the Consumer Products Group's various (in my view) unreasonable demands and hadn't paid much attention to how useful the "home computer" really was. Once I moved to the Consumer Products Group, I quickly discovered that the home computer being developed so fervently was of quite limited use. And the constraints were quite fundamental: IC functionality wasn't mature enough yet, and costs were still too high.</p>
<p class="en-para">What to do? Had I possessed the courage then (1978) that I have now, I would have said: cancel the home computer program! Revisit the topic when IC and computer software technology matured (that is: when a home computer could actually be made useful).</p>
<p class="en-para">But I didn't have that courage then. In fact, even if I'd had the courage to suggest canceling the home computer to Shepherd and Shep, I would have been soundly rejected on the spot. One only needs to look at how, after the home computer's thorough failure, they still insisted on continuing until 1983 — nearly dragging the entire company down with them — before finally stopping the home computer business.</p>
<p class="en-para">Shepherd had already proclaimed his "Ubiquitous Computation" vision. The September 1978 Business Week cover story, though its spotlight was stolen by Shep, further cemented Shepherd's expectations for the "home computer" in terms of "high-tech innovation" ideology.</p>
<p class="en-para">In my position, having neither the courage to cancel the home computer nor the standing to challenge the program, I gave it my best. I assigned my most technically savvy and capable manager — Peter Bonfield (the former Scientific Calculator GM) — to be Home Computer GM. I gave him all the headcount and budget he requested. Bonfield's hopes were no higher than mine, but we were going to give it our all — perhaps a miracle would occur.</p>
<p class="en-para">In June 1979, we officially announced the TI Home Computer (TI99/4), with a retail price of $1,150.</p>

<p class="en-para">"The mountain roared, and out came a mouse."</p>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **"Ubiquitous Computing" premature by 12–14 years (提前十二年的普适计算愿景)**
**中文解释**：夏伯特的「到处都有计算」与学术界在九〇年代提出的「普适计算」（Ubiquitous Computing, Weiser 1988）概念高度吻合——但超前了不止一代；在创新管理中，「早一代」是先驱，「早三代」则是先烈。
**商业造句">Shepherd's vision aligned remarkably with what Mark Weiser at Xerox PARC would formally articulate as "Ubiquitous Computing" a decade later — but being right too early is the same as being wrong in business.*

2. **"The mountain roared, and out came a mouse" (巨山咆哮，出来一只老鼠)**
**中文解释**：分析师罗森对TI99/4的尖锐评语，也成为科技史上最著名的嘲讽之一；在营销中，「产品期待」和「产品现实」之间的差距直接决定口碑——越是大力造势的产品，一旦不如预期，反噬越强。
**商业造句">Ben Rosen's "The mountain roared, and out came a mouse" entered tech lore as one of the most devastating analyst quips — it crystallized the gap between the $1,150 price and the meager utility delivered.*
