# 德仪篇·第十二章｜最后的吶喊与离去

就在我还在德仪做「最后的吶喊」时，我和前妻决定分居及办理离婚手续。我们两人同年，在二十岁时（她在波士顿大学，我在麻省理工）相恋，二十二岁就结婚——结婚得太早了。两人的爱大约继续了十几年，但当我史丹佛读博士时，就已渐渐消散；到四、五十岁时，两人几乎已成陌路人了。

我们都没有外遇，但三十年时光，人总会变的。幸运的夫妻一起变，我和前妻没有这么幸运；至少有十年时间，「分居」、「离婚」就常在心上和口中，但女儿还小，为了她，还是一起过日子吧。我们五十岁时，女儿已去波士顿念大学（她母亲的母校），而且即将毕业自立，我们分手的时间也已到了。

我早已有相当积蓄，但从未享受过物质上舒服的家。我找了一个宽敞（七十坪）的公寓：客厅、饭厅、两个卧房，再加一个很大（三十坪）的起居室兼书房。找室内设计师布置，买了舒服的现代化家具，装了一流音响……一九八一年底，我满五十岁，独自搬入新寓。

华丽的周围，不能补偿内心的寂寞；孤独的夜晚里，萦绕在我心的是三十年的恋爱和婚姻：

记得那日花底相遇，我问你心中有何希望？
你向我轻轻私语：「要你，要你，要你！」

记得那夜月色迷离，你问我心中有何秘密？
我向你轻轻私语：「爱你，爱你，爱你！」

<p class="en-para">Even as I was making my "final outcry" at TI, my ex-wife and I decided to separate and begin divorce proceedings. We were the same age — we fell in love at twenty (she at Boston University, I at MIT) and married at twenty-two. Too young. Our love lasted perhaps a dozen years or so. But by the time I was at Stanford pursuing my Ph.D., it had already begun to fade. By our forties and fifties, we were practically strangers.</p>
<p class="en-para">Neither of us had affairs. But thirty years — people always change. Fortunate couples change together. My ex-wife and I were not so lucky. For at least ten years, "separation" and "divorce" were constantly on our minds and lips. But our daughter was still young, so for her sake, we stayed together. By the time we were fifty, our daughter was at Boston University (her mother's alma mater) and about to graduate and stand on her own. The time for us to part had come.</p>
<p class="en-para">I already had substantial savings but had never enjoyed a materially comfortable home. I found a spacious (roughly 230 square meters) apartment: living room, dining room, two bedrooms, plus an expansive (roughly 100 square meters) living room-cum-study. I hired an interior designer, bought comfortable modern furniture, installed top-tier audio... At the end of 1981, at fifty years old, I moved into my new apartment alone.</p>
<p class="en-para">The splendid surroundings could not compensate for the loneliness within. In the lonely nights, what lingered in my heart was thirty years of love and marriage:</p>
<p class="en-para">I remember that day meeting beneath the flowers — I asked you, what hope was in your heart?</p>
<p class="en-para">You whispered softly to me: "Want you, want you, want you!"</p>
<p class="en-para">I remember that night under the hazy moonlight — you asked me, what secret was in my heart?</p>
<p class="en-para">I whispered softly to you: "Love you, love you, love you!"</p>
