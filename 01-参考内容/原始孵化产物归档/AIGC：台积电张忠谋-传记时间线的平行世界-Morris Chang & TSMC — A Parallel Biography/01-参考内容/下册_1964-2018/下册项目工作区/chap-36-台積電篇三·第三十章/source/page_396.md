## 陆续增加独立董事

飞利浦最初持有台积电近七成股份，董事会十席中占七席。直至二○○○年，飞利浦持股降至约二成，空出一席董事席位，我才有机会提名一席董事——立刻提名施振荣。施振荣是台湾电脑界巨子，也办过半导体事业（德碁）。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Philips initially held nearly 70% of TSMC's shares, occupying seven of ten board seats. It was not until 2000, when Philips' holdings dropped to roughly 20% and a board seat became vacant, that I had the opportunity to nominate one director—I immediately nominated Schindler. Schindler is a titan of Taiwan's computer industry and had also founded a semiconductor business (Tatung).</p>
</div>

再等两年，到了二○○二年，开发基金持股已降至9.7%，三席监察人也空出一席，再加上公司章程可设十名董事，那时只有八人。所以我又有机会提名两位董事、一位监察人。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Two more years passed, and by 2002, the Development Fund's holdings had dropped to 9.7%, creating a vacancy among the three supervisors. Additionally, the company charter allowed for ten directors, but only eight sat at the time. So I had another opportunity to nominate two directors and one supervisor.</p>
</div>

二○○一年底，我在法国巴黎参加世界杯桥牌比赛，每天早上看欧洲版《华尔街日报》。有一天忽然看到彼得·邦菲（Peter Bonfield）即将自英国电信公司退休的消息。邦菲是英国人，也是我在德仪的旧属，与我有「共患难」之谊（见本书第十章）。他在德仪兵荒马乱时期离开德仪，回到英国后事业顺利，做到英国电信的总裁，还被英国女皇封为爵士。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">At the end of 2001, I was in Paris, France, competing in the World Bridge Championship, reading the European edition of The Wall Street Journal each morning. One day I suddenly saw news that Peter Bonfield was about to retire from British Telecom. Bonfield is British and was also my former subordinate at TI, with whom I shared "hardship during difficult times" (see Chapter 10). He left TI during its chaotic period, returned to the UK where his career prospered, rising to president of British Telecom, and was knighted by the British Queen.</p>
</div>

邦菲完全符合我理想中台积电董事的条件。见到他即将退休新闻的当天，我就从巴黎打电话给他，问他愿否担任台积电董事。当时他很高兴接到我的电话，但还未决定退休后做什么，要我等他正式退休后再找他。几个月后，我从台湾再打电话给他，他欣然答应任台积电董事。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Bonfield perfectly matched my ideal criteria for a TSMC director. On the very day I saw the news of his impending retirement, I called him from Paris and asked whether he would serve on TSMC's board. He was delighted to receive my call but had not yet decided what to do after retirement, asking me to contact him again after his formal retirement. A few months later, I called him from Taiwan, and he gladly accepted the position of TSMC director.</p>
</div>
