二○○二年我还提名了另一位董事：雷斯特·梭罗（Lester Thurow）。他是MIT史隆管理学院教授及前院长，也是ADI的董事。我在当ADI董事时认识他，后来又在台湾时代基金会与他更为熟悉。梭罗是管理学「大师」级人物，而且因长期担任ADI董事，对公司治理有丰富经验。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">In 2002, I also nominated another director: Lester Thurow. He was a professor and former dean of MIT's Sloan School of Management, and also a director of ADI. I had met him when I served on the ADI board, and later became more familiar with him through the Taiwan Times Foundation. Thurow was a "master" figure in management studies, and through his long service on the ADI board, he had rich experience in corporate governance.</p>
</div>

二○○二年我提名的监察人是迈克·波特（Michael Porter）。波特是哈佛商学院教授，竞争学大师，在竞争策略领域著作等身。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">In 2002, the supervisor I nominated was Michael Porter. Porter is a professor at Harvard Business School, a master of competition studies, and has authored numerous works in the field of competitive strategy.</p>
</div>

**关于波特的小故事**
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
</div>

一九九九年四月，波特应《天下杂志》之约来台湾演说，我也去听了。波特的演说一向很精彩，这次也不例外。但他讲英语，而且讲的内容对没有管理学理论背景的听众而言稍嫌生硬，听众的反应不很热烈。波特似乎也察觉了。演讲后《天下杂志》还安排了我和另外两位台湾企业家与波特对谈。波特更显得没精打采，似乎已认定台湾是管理学蛮荒之地，他在这里完全浪费时间。对谈一结束，他就拔脚想走。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">In April 1999, Porter came to Taiwan at the invitation of *Global Management* magazine, and I attended his lecture as well. Porter's lectures are always excellent, and this was no exception. But he spoke in English, and the content was somewhat stiff for an audience without a background in management theory; the audience's response was not very enthusiastic. Porter also seemed to notice this. After the lecture, *Global Management* arranged a dialogue between Porter, myself, and two other Taiwanese entrepreneurs. Porter appeared even more listless, as if he had already concluded that Taiwan was a managerial wilderness and that he was wasting his time here. As soon as the dialogue ended, he was ready to leave.</p>
</div>

我喊：「请留步。」他转过头来，我问：「您认识布鲁斯·汉德森（Bruce Henderson）吗？」
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">I called out, "Please stay." He turned around, and I asked, "Do you know Bruce Henderson?"</p>
</div>

波特的眼睛突然亮了——居然在此「管理学蛮荒之地」，有人问他认不认识竞争学的鼻祖、学习曲线的创始者、波士顿顾问集团（BCG）的创办人。波特转过身来，以惊异的口气反问我：「你知道他吗？」
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Porter's eyes suddenly lit up—to have someone ask him in this "managerial wilderness" whether he knew the founder of competitive strategy, the creator of the learning curve, and the founder of the Boston Consulting Group (BCG). Porter turned around and asked me in an astonished tone, "Do you know him?"</p>
</div>

我不但知道汉德森，他还是我「竞争学」的启蒙者。我一直运用他所发明的「学习曲线」（见本书第三章）。我与波特有了十五分钟愉快的谈话。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Not only did I know Henderson, but he was also my启蒙 in "competition studies." I have continuously applied the "learning curve" he invented (see Chapter 3 of this book). I had a pleasant fifteen-minute conversation with Porter.</p>
</div>
