## 董事会运作实务

董事常会每季召开一次，虽然好几位董事要从英国、美国来，但在我董事长任内，所有常会都是面对面。除常会外，我们很少有特别会议，要开特别会议时就用视讯方式开。

每次常会需时一晚加两整天，通常开始于星期日我与独立董事的「会前晚餐」。由于董事们在两星期前已收到详细议程，如果他们对议程或任何议案有意见，在晚餐时就可提出；即使对议案没有意见，也可以随意聊聊与公司有关的事。这些与独董的「会前晚餐」是建立信任、凝聚共识的重要场合。

星期一上午审计委员会开整个半天的会。下午薪酬委员会开会两小时。下午四时全体董事会开始，先是高阶经理人的业务或研发报告。当天晚餐算是社交场合，好几位高阶经理人与董事们共进晚餐。

星期二继续开董事会，上午通常是多项议案的表决。既然已有「会前晚餐」达成的共识，有问题的议案已被修改或撤回，所以议案的通过通常非常顺利。下午是「策略讨论」——所有研发、竞争、员工福利、购并等议题均在台面上讨论。

## 董事会对台积电的贡献

在我任董事长的三十一年内，有二十七年（一九八七至二○○五年，及二○○九至二○一八年）我也担任CEO。从外面看起来，台积电一帆风顺；事实上，我们也度过、经历不少危机，抓住许多机会，避免了许多陷阱。当然我是责无旁贷的掌舵者，但是董事会对我的支持及建言，着实功不可没。

<p class="en-para">Board regular meetings were held quarterly. Although several directors traveled from the United Kingdom and the United States, all regular meetings during my tenure as chairman were held in person. Aside from regular meetings, we rarely held special ones; when we did, they were conducted by video conference.</p>
<p class="en-para">Each regular meeting lasted one evening plus two full days, typically beginning on Sunday with a pre-meeting dinner that I shared with the independent directors. Because directors had received a detailed agenda two weeks in advance, they could raise any concerns about the agenda or proposals during the dinner; even without objections, they could discuss company matters informally. These pre-meeting dinners with the independent directors were important occasions for building trust and forging consensus.</p>
<p class="en-para">On Monday morning, the audit committee met for half a day. In the afternoon, the compensation committee met for two hours. At four o'clock, the full board began, starting with business or research and development reports from senior management. The dinner that evening was a social occasion, with several senior managers joining the directors.</p>
<p class="en-para">The board continued on Tuesday. The morning was typically devoted to voting on multiple proposals. Since consensus had been reached at the pre-meeting dinner, proposals with problems had already been revised or withdrawn, so passage was usually smooth. The afternoon was reserved for strategy discussions, on which all matters—including research and development, competition, employee benefits, and mergers and acquisitions—were discussed openly.</p>
<p class="en-para">In my thirty-one years as chairman, I also served as CEO for twenty-seven of them (1987 to 2005, and 2009 to 2018). To an outside observer, TSMC appeared to sail smooth seas. In fact, we navigated numerous crises, seized many opportunities, and avoided many pitfalls. I was of course the helmsman with undeniable responsibility, but the board's support and counsel were truly indispensable.</p>
