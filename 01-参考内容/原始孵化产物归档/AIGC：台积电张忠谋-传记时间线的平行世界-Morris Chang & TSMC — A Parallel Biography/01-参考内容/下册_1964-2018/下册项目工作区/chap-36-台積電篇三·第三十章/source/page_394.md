# 台积电篇三·第三十章｜建构理想的董事会

## 我心目中理想的董事会

理论上，一个公司的最高权力机构是股东大会，但股东大会通常一年只开一次，所以把权力交给它选出来的董事会。在英美制度下，董事会选任有能者为总裁（CEO），主理公司业务；也选出董事领导董事会，监督总裁，扮演公司重要决定最后把关者的角色。董事会既然是公司最高权力单位，每一位董对股东、对员工、对社会都负有相当责任。董事应以公司经营的长期方向和策略为己任。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">In theory, the highest authority of a company is the shareholders' meeting, but since shareholders' meetings are typically held only once a year, power is delegated to the board of directors elected by them. Under the Anglo-American system, the board appoints a capable president (CEO) to manage company business and also elects directors to lead the board, oversee the CEO, and serve as the ultimate gatekeepers of important company decisions. Since the board is the company's highest governing body, each director bears considerable responsibility to shareholders, employees, and society. Directors should take as their duty the company's long-term direction and strategy.</p>
</div>

但大部分台湾的董事会并不如此。大部分董事会都被大股东控制，董事人选自然倾向大股东能控制的一类。开会议程都由大股东或大股东所派的董事长决定。每次开会行礼如仪，绝少讨论，更没有真正的集思广益。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">But most Taiwanese boards do not operate this way. Most boards are controlled by major shareholders, and director candidates naturally tend to be those whom major shareholders can control. Meeting agendas are determined by major shareholders or the chairman they appoint. Meetings proceed with ritualistic formality, with little discussion and certainly no genuine deliberation.</p>
</div>

我在创办台积电前已有不少董事会经验。在德仪，我虽不是董事，但因主持最重要的业务单位，多年来经常列席董事会。后来任通用器材以及亚德诺半导体（ADI）的董事，又以天使投资人身份任好几个初创公司的董事。在台湾我也当过远东水泥的董事，后来，我又当了美国高盛银行总公司的董事。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">I had considerable board experience before founding TSMC. At TI, although I was not a director, I frequently attended board meetings for many years because I headed the most important business unit. Later, I served as a director of General Instrument and Analog Devices (ADI), and as an angel investor, I served on the boards of several startups. In Taiwan, I was also a director of Far East Cement, and later, I became a director of Goldman Sachs' global headquarters.</p>
</div>
