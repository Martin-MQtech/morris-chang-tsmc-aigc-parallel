他回到波士顿后还派两位助理到新竹花了两星期时间，为撰写台积电「个案研究」而搜集资料。在个案首次在哈佛商学院授课时，波特邀请我去哈佛现身说法。学生在那堂课前已预读「个案研究」。在两个小时的时间中，第一个小时由波特讲授台积电个案，休息十分钟后，第二个小时由学生对我提问。波特的讲授很精彩，学生提问的水准也很高，当然我也不是省油的灯。总之，那天我度过了愉快的两个小时。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">After returning to Boston, he still dispatched two assistants to Hsinchu for two weeks to gather materials for writing a TSMC "case study." When the case was first taught at Harvard Business School, Porter invited me to come to Harvard to share my experience. Students had pre-read the "case study" before the class. In the two-hour session, the first hour was spent by Porter lecturing on the TSMC case; after a ten-minute break, the second hour consisted of students asking me questions. Porter's lecture was excellent, and the students' questions were of high caliber. Naturally, I was no pushover either. In short, I spent a pleasant two hours that day.</p>
</div>

## 持续扩充独立董事阵容
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
</div>

随着飞利浦继续降低台积电持股，我更有机会提名董事。二○○三年，我提名当时负责台积电营运及营销的执行副总蔡力行。九席董事中，三席属经理人（我、总经理曾繁城及蔡力行），三席独董（邦菲、梭罗及施振荣），开发基金仍占一席，飞利浦仍占两席。三席监察人中，开发基金及飞利浦各占一席，波特占一席。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">As Philips continued to reduce its TSMC holdings, I had more opportunities to nominate directors. In 2003, I nominated Tsai Li-hsing, who was then the executive vice president responsible for TSMC's operations and marketing. Among the nine board seats, three belonged to management (myself, General Manager Zeng Fan-cheng, and Tsai Li-hsing), three to independent directors (Bemfund, Thurow, and Schindler), one to the Development Fund, and two to Philips. Among the three supervisors, the Development Fund and Philips each held one seat, and Porter held one.</p>
</div>

二○○六年，飞利浦又减少了一席董事，我们增加了一席独董：卡莉·菲奥莉娜（Carly Fiorina）——前惠普董事长暨总裁。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">In 2006, Philips reduced its board seat by one, and we added one independent director: Carly Fiorina—former chairman and CEO of HP.</p>
</div>

二○○八年，飞利浦出清台积电股票并辞去最后一席董事职位，台积电董事会自成立后第一次没有飞利浦的踪影。前后二十年——从当初一心想要控制台积电，到后来见厚利出售所有台积电股票——飞利浦对台积电有恩亦有怨。当然从我的立场看，要经营一家公司还是一开始就独立自主最好。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">In 2008, Philips liquidated its TSMC shares and resigned its last board seat. For the first time since its founding, the TSMC board had no Philips presence. Over roughly twenty years—from initially seeking to control TSMC to later selling all TSMC shares at a substantial profit—Philips was both a benefactor and a source of friction for TSMC. Of course, from my standpoint, it is always best for a company to be independent from the start.</p>
</div>

随着飞利浦的全部搬离，我建构董事会的工作也接近完成。后来加入的几位独立董事一样都是杰出人选——卡莉非常精明认真，有话必说。之后我们又加入了汤马斯·恩吉布斯（Thomas Engibous），前德仪董事长兼总裁，将德仪半导体事业从亏损中挽救出来；邹至庄，著名经济学家；陈国慈，前台积电法务长，离开台积电做社会工作，八年后回来做独立董事；麥克·斯宾林特（Michael Splinter），前应用材料（Applied Materials）董事长兼总裁及前英特尔执行副总裁。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">With Philips' complete departure, my work of building the board was nearly complete. The subsequent independent directors who joined were equally outstanding choices—Carly was shrewd and serious, always speaking her mind. Later we added Thomas Engibous, former TI chairman and CEO, who rescued TI's semiconductor business from losses; Tsou Chih-chuang, a renowned economist; Chen Kuo-tzsu, former TSMC general counsel, who left TSMC for social work and returned eight years later as an independent director; and Michael Splinter, former Applied Materials chairman and CEO and former Intel executive vice president.</p>
</div>
