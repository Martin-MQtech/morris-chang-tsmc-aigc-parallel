## 三大改革

一九八六、八七年是我勤于改革工研院的时期。我同时推动三个改革。

### 改革一：十年内一半经费来自民间企业

那时工研院的研究计划只有极少数根据工业界需要，绝大多数都在院内各所长领导下拟就，向经济部申请经费。几乎全部经费都由经济部拨给（有一部分名义上来自国营企业，例如中油、台电，但他们受经济部指示，给工研院钱，并没委托工研院做什么事）。

孙运璿先生在任经济部长时，要工研院做IC计划；后来他任行政院长时，又指示工研院做「自动化」研究；但他只给了题目，这两个大计划的细节，仍由电子所和机械所规划，然后经济部同意。

我认为政府经费的专案有必要，但应该做比较长期——五年、十年后台湾工业界用得到的研究。同时工研院应该出去问工业界他们的近期需要，工研院也应该对工业界推销工研院的研究实力——替民营企业做他们需要的研究，但是也要他们出经费。

工研院的确有这研究实力。一九八五年时，工研院有四千多员工，大部分都有理工学士或硕士学位。大的研究所有一千多员工，小的研究所也有几百员工。而且，照我的想法，长期的政府专案与中短期的工业委托应该有互相滋养作用。

我设了数量目标：十年内（一九九六年）一半经费来自民间企业！我的真正目的是要连接工研院和台湾工业，让工研院做台湾工业界需要的研究。我不愁经费。我任院长时，工研院申请政府经费并不难。我只是要连接工研院和台湾工业界的研究需要，而钱（经费）是最有效的连结方式。

我天真地认为我的目的一定会被大家认同，事实却完全相反。无论院内、院外，都没有接受我的想法。在院内，一般同仁的反应是：我们是研究人，不是营销员，也不是生意人，为什么要我们去推销我们的研究？政府官员一般的反应是：好好的政府出钱的研究不做，为什么要去募民间的钱？工业界的反应是：本来就应政府出经费，做出来的成果如果我们要，应该免费给我们。

我的「改革一」在我任院长时没有被接受，后来几十年也鲜有人提起。倒是最近几年，居然有人重提三十年前工研院旧事，认为当时我的想法不无道理。三十余年事过境迁，我当时失败的痛苦，早已麻木了。

<p class="en-para"><b>The Three Reforms</b></p>
<p class="en-para">1986 and 1987 were the years I was most active in reforming ITRI. I simultaneously pushed three reforms.</p>
<p class="en-para"><b>Reform 1: 50% of Funding from Industry within Ten Years</b></p>
<p class="en-para">At that time, very few ITRI research projects were based on industry needs. The vast majority were conceived by the institute directors within ITRI and submitted to the MOEA for funding. Nearly all funding came from the MOEA. (A portion was nominally from state-owned enterprises like CPC and Taipower, but they contributed at the MOEA's direction without commissioning any actual research.)</p>
<p class="en-para">When Sun Yun-suan was Minister of Economic Affairs, he directed ITRI to undertake the IC program. Later as Premier, he instructed ITRI to pursue "automation" research. But he only provided the topics; the details of both major programs were planned by the Electronics and Mechanical Institutes and then approved by the MOEA.</p>
<p class="en-para">I believed government-funded projects were necessary, but should focus on longer-term research — work that Taiwan's industry would need five or ten years out. Meanwhile, ITRI should go out and ask industry what their near-term needs were. ITRI should also market its research capabilities to industry — doing research that private companies needed, but having them fund it.</p>
<p class="en-para">ITRI did have such research capability. In 1985, ITRI had over 4,000 employees, most holding bachelor's or master's degrees in science or engineering. The major institutes had over 1,000 staff; even the smaller ones had several hundred. Moreover, in my view, long-term government projects and short-to-medium-term industrial contracts should have a mutually nourishing relationship.</p>
<p class="en-para">I set a quantitative target: 50% of funding from private industry within ten years (by 1996)! My true purpose was to connect ITRI with Taiwan's industry, so ITRI would do the research Taiwan's industry needed. I was not worried about funding. During my presidency, ITRI had no difficulty securing government funding. I simply wanted to link ITRI with Taiwan's industrial research needs, and money was the most effective linking mechanism.</p>
<p class="en-para">I naively assumed my goal would be universally embraced. The truth was the exact opposite. Neither inside nor outside ITRI did anyone accept my idea. Inside, the typical reaction from colleagues was: We are researchers, not salesmen or businessmen — why should we have to market our research? Government officials generally reacted: The government is generously funding your research — why go chasing private money? Industry's reaction: The government should be paying for this. If they produce results we want, they should give them to us for free.</p>
<p class="en-para">"Reform 1" was not accepted during my presidency, and for decades afterward, few people brought it up. Only in recent years have people revisited the thirty-year-old ITRI episode and conceded that my thinking had some merit. More than thirty years have passed; the pain of that failure has long since numbed.</p>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Research funding model (研究经费模式)**
**中文解释**：张忠谋试图将工研院从「政府全额拨款」转向「政府与民间各半」的经费结构；这一改革虽在当时失败，但方向与后来全球研究机构的趋势一致。
**商业造句**：*Chang's push for 50% industry funding was decades ahead of its time — today, the most resilient research institutions worldwide maintain diversified funding from both government and private sector.*

2. **Mutually nourishing relationship (互相滋养作用)**
**中文解释**：长期基础研究与应用开发之间的正向互动——长期研究提供技术储备，短期应用为长期研究提供方向和经费。
**商业造句**：*The best research organizations balance long-term exploratory work with short-term applied projects — each nourishing the other in a virtuous cycle of discovery and commercialization.*

3. **"We are researchers, not salesmen" (我们是研究人，不是营销员)**
**中文解释**：工研院同仁对张忠谋改革的典型反应，体现了研究机构文化与企业需求之间的根本张力。
**商业造句**：*The "researchers, not salesmen" objection reflected a deep cultural resistance to market orientation — a challenge that every research institution faces when trying to bridge the lab-to-market gap.*
