## 调整降价速度（二○一○年及以后）

经验和「学习曲线」理论都告诉我们：同一节点技术晶圆的单位成本会随累积产量而下降。再加上「抢单」竞争激烈，半导体的降价已成惯例。

只是二○○五至二○○九年蔡力行任CEO期间，台积电降价的幅度超过了成本降低的幅度，以致毛利率降低。在这期间，管理团队又提出了一个营收大幅增加、但利润不增加、无法接受的五年计划。我只得把这五年计划原封退还，要求管理团队再提。

二○○九年六月我回任CEO后的首要工作之一就是降低降价速度，使得我们的毛利率能逐步上升。通常台积电每年的价格在前一年底就和客户商定，所以我在二○○九年六月中已无能为力，我只能致力于二○一○年的价格。经过半年我自己、总部「订价」幕僚单位以及全球营销组织的努力，使得二○一○年的降幅缩小到二○○九年降幅的三分之二；后一年的降幅再进一步降低到二○○九年降幅的一半。

随着售价比较稳定，「减低成本」运动又每时每刻都在推行，我们的毛利率也有显著的进步。二○○九至二○一八年都在四十五％至五十％间。五年内毛利率有五个百分点的进步！

## 多位「派遣人力」为「正职同仁」（二○一○年）

在同一地区内，如果雇主候用的人员有「同工不同酬」现象，这个雇主是自找麻烦——小则士气不振，大则工会干涉、群体抗议。

我在返任CEO后发现台积电约有两万两千位「正职员工」，却有三千两百位「派遣人力」。其中约五百位「派遣人力」是属于一些不同于一般「正职员工」的专业（保全、医护、团膳等），但绝大部分约两千七百位「派遣人力」的原因是为了降低成本。

在二○○九年底发现这问题后，我找了五位与此问题有关的副总成立「专案小组」，要求他们拟出办法以「正职员工」取代「派遣人力」。二○一○年三月我们对全体同仁公布此办法，并在六月至九月执行。办法是在原本两千七百多位工作与既有「正职员工」相似的「派遣人力」中，遴选两千四百位新任「正职同仁」。未经遴选的三百多位，台积电也经中介公司为他们找到新工作，或领取我们提供的「关怀金」离去。

此「替代案」还有一个我未预料但也不惊讶的后果——两千四百位自「派遣人力」改为「正职同仁」的生产力，竟与原来两千七百位「派遣人力」的生产力一样。我们不需要多添人。

若对的事，常有好报。

<p class="en-para">Both experience and the 'learning curve' theory tell us that the unit cost of wafers at the same node technology declines as cumulative output increases. Coupled with fierce competition for orders, price reductions in semiconductors had become customary.</p>
<p class="en-para">However, during Ming-Lieh Tsai's tenure as CEO from 2005 to 2009, TSMC's price cuts exceeded the rate of cost reduction, causing gross margins to decline. During this period, the management team also proposed an unacceptable five-year plan that would significantly increase revenue but not profit. I had no choice but to return the plan unopened and ask the team to resubmit.</p>
<p class="en-para">One of my first tasks after resuming the CEO role in June 2009 was to slow the pace of price cuts so that our gross margins could gradually recover. Because TSMC's prices for the coming year were typically agreed upon with customers at the end of the preceding year, I could do nothing for 2009 by mid-June; I could only focus on 2010 pricing. After six months of effort by myself, the headquarters pricing staff, and the global marketing organization, the price decline for 2010 was reduced to two-thirds of the 2009 decline; the following year's decline was reduced further to half of the 2009 decline.</p>
<p class="en-para">As selling prices stabilized and the campaign to reduce costs continued around the clock, our gross margin also improved significantly. From 2009 to 2018, it remained between forty-five and fifty percent—a five percentage point improvement over five years!</p>
<p class="en-para">Within the same workplace, if employees doing the same work receive different pay, the employer is inviting trouble—on the small scale, demoralization; on the large scale, union interference and collective protest.</p>
<p class="en-para">After resuming the CEO role, I discovered that TSMC had approximately 22,000 'regular employees' but 3,200 'dispatched workers.' Of these, about 500 dispatched workers were in specialized roles different from regular employees (security, medical, catering, etc.), but the vast majority—approximately 2,700—were dispatched workers hired primarily to reduce costs.</p>
<p class="en-para">After discovering this issue at the end of 2009, I assembled five vice presidents associated with this matter into a 'special project team' and asked them to devise a plan to replace dispatched workers with regular employees. We announced this plan to all employees in March 2010 and implemented it from June to September. From the original 2,700+ dispatched workers whose jobs were similar to existing regular employees, we selected 2,400 to become new 'regular colleagues.' For the remaining 300+ not selected, TSMC also helped them find new jobs through intermediary companies or provided them with 'care payments' to leave.</p>
<p class="en-para">This 'substitution plan' had one consequence I had not anticipated but was not surprised by—the productivity of the 2,400 who were converted from dispatched workers to regular employees was the same as that of the original 2,700 dispatched workers. We did not need to add any headcount.</p>
