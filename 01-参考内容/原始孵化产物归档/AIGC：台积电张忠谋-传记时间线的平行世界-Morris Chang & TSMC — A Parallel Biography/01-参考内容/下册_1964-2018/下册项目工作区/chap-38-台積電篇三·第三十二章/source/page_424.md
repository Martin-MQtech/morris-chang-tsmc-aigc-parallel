## 解决四十纳米良率问题（二○○九年）

就在我重返CEO职前的几个月，台积电的最新节点技术——四十纳米——已开始量产，但并不顺利：良率过低，且有品质问题。我似乎又回到了一九六八年的德仪（见第六章），但四十一年前的经验也给了二○○九年的我可用的教训。

二○○九年的我已远离生产技术的细节，所以绝不一九六八年我的上司彪希所为——「帮忙」远过于「帮忙」。我把信心寄托在生产团队主管秦永沛副总上，从不参加他的技术会议，只是随时都抽出时间听取他主动给我的报告。

我还做了一件「公关」事。在我重返CEO职的二○○九年六月时，台积电的「四十纳米生产问题」已广为投资人所知。二○○九年七月底，在我返任CEO后的第一次「法人说明会」上，财务长做了每季的例行报告后，我站起来宣布：

「大家都很关心四十纳米的生产问题，我们也很努力要解决这问题。现在我想请负责营运的资深副总刘德音博士报告生产线现状——但他在生产线工作太忙，不能亲自来此，所以我们以网络直播联机方式，请他做此报告。」

接着，会场的屏幕上出现德音穿着白色实验室长袍，站在洁净室门前，开始做他的专业性报告。我看一百多听众的脸色：又惊异又感动。我想：这是一个吉兆。

果然，几个月后，四十纳米良率问题解决，一切回归正常。我对刘德音、秦永沛以及他们生产团队的信心没有白费。

<p class="en-para">Several months before I resumed the CEO post, TSMC's newest node technology—forty nanometers—had begun mass production, but not smoothly: yield was too low, and there were quality issues. I seemed to have returned to TI in 1968 (see Chapter 6), but the experience from forty-one years earlier also provided lessons I could apply in 2009.</p>
<p class="en-para">In 2009, I was far removed from the details of production technology, so I would never repeat what my boss Bush did in 1968—'helping' far beyond what was helpful. I placed my confidence in Vice President Yong-pei Chin, who led the production team, and never attended his technical meetings; I simply made time whenever he主动 brought me reports.</p>
<p class="en-para">I also did something of a 'public relations' nature. By June 2009, when I resumed the CEO role, TSMC's 'forty-nanometer production problem' was well known to investors. In late July 2009, at the first investor conference after my return, after the CFO had delivered the quarterly report, I stood up and announced:</p>
<p class="en-para">'Everyone is concerned about the forty-nanometer production problem, and we are working hard to resolve it. I would now like to ask Dr. Aric Liu, senior vice president in charge of operations, to report on the production line status—but he is too busy on the production floor to come here in person, so we will connect via live video.'</p>
<p class="en-para">Next, Dr. Liu appeared on the screen at the venue, wearing a white lab coat and standing before the cleanroom door, beginning his technical report. I watched the expressions of the more than one hundred listeners: a mixture of surprise and being moved. I thought: this was a favorable omen.</p>
<p class="en-para">As it turned out, a few months later, the forty-nanometer yield problem was resolved and everything returned to normal. My confidence in Aric Liu, Yong-pei Chin, and their production team was not misplaced.</p>
