## 赶上高潮：加大投资（二○一○至二○一八年）

怎么才能跨上这高潮？我们必须立刻投资四十及二十八纳米的产能！

在「滚滚新世纪」内，自二○○一至二○○九年，台积电的资本投资相当稳定，每年大致在二十至二十五亿美元范围内。二○一○年，我发狠了。我要跨上我想象中的、新节点技术给我们的高潮。我加倍产能投资到五十九亿美元，比二○○九年的两倍还多！

我的发狠是因为我看到了台积电的机会。但当我发出此一讯息——二○一○年第一季董事会议程——好几位独立董事担心了，要在会前与我通电话。在他们心目中，市场景气还未完全恢复，怎么就投资那么多产能？二○一○年一月二十六日，台湾的晚上八点、美国的清晨、英国的下仗，我和三位在台湾、美国和英国的独立董电话会议。他们质问我要投资那么多，我告诉他们四十及二十八纳米技术的机会。他们半信半疑。最后我说：「这件事让我做吧，毕竟我是公司的负责人。」

他们这才不再反对。

二○○九至二○一○年我看到的是四十及二十八纳米的机会。但随着台积电研发经费继续增加，研发能力继续增强，新技术层出不穷，以后营收及获利成长机会愈来愈多、也愈大。在二○一○至二○一八年八年内，台积电的资本投资自前述二○一○年的五十九亿美元成长到二○一八年的一百零五亿美元。营收自二○一○年的一百三十三亿美元成长到二○一八年三百四十二亿美元；获利也自二○一○年的五十一亿美元成长到二○一八年的一百一十六亿美元。

<p class="en-para">How do we seize this climax? We must invest immediately in capacity for forty- and twenty-eight-nanometer technology!</p>
<p class="en-para">During the 'surging new century,' from 2001 to 2009, TSMC's capital investment was relatively stable, averaging between 2 and 2.5 billion dollars annually. In 2010, I went all in. I wanted to ride the wave that I saw coming from the new node technologies. I doubled capacity investment to 5.9 billion dollars—more than double the 2009 level.</p>
<p class="en-para">My determination came from seeing TSMC's opportunity. But when I put this on the agenda for the first-quarter 2010 board meeting, several independent directors grew concerned and called me before the meeting. In their view, the market had not fully recovered—why invest so much capacity? On January 26, 2010, at 8 p.m. in Taiwan, early morning in the United States, and the evening in the United Kingdom, I held a teleconference with three independent directors in Taiwan, the United States, and the United Kingdom. They questioned why I wanted to invest so much. I told them about the forty- and twenty-eight-nanometer technology opportunities. They were half-convinced, half-doubtful. In the end, I said, 'Let me make this decision. After all, I am the person in charge.'</p>
<p class="en-para">Only then did they cease their opposition.
In 2009 and 2010, I saw the opportunity in forty- and twenty-eight-nanometer technologies. But as TSMC's R&amp;D funding continued to increase and R&amp;D capabilities strengthened, with new technologies emerging one after another, the opportunities for revenue and earnings growth became ever greater. Over the eight years from 2010 to 2018, TSMC's capital investment grew from the aforementioned 5.9 billion dollars in 2010 to 10.5 billion dollars in 2018. Revenue grew from 13.3 billion dollars in 2010 to 34.2 billion dollars in 2018; earnings grew from 5.1 billion dollars in 2010 to 11.6 billion dollars in 2018.</p>
