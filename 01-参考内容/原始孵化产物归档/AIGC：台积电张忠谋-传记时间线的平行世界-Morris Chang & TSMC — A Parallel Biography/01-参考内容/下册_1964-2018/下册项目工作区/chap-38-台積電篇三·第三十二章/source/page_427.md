## 合并先进技术事业组织及主流技术事业组织（二○○九年十月）

在蔡力行任CEO期间，他成立了先进技术事业组织及主流技术事业组织。这两个单位的主力都是生产线（在台积电，我们亦称生产为「营运」），先进组织负责十二吋厂，主流组织负责六吋及八吋厂。

力行的事业组织其实也是一九九六年布鲁克主意的重演（见第二十四章）。一九九六年我否决了布鲁克的主意，十几年后没有否决力行。我想是因为布鲁克只是「总经理」不是CEO，而力行是CEO，应该给CEO更多空间。

但现在我回来做CEO，这个错误也应该改掉了。所以在处理最急迫问题（调整降价速度、解决与辉达的悬案）后，我就把先进组织和主流组织的业务发展单位抽出来，再把这两个组织合并为全公司的营运组织。

接着，合并两个抽出来的业务发展单位，并且赋予它新的使命——台积电的Marketing Department。

欧美大公司通常把行销（Sales）和Marketing放在一起。台湾企业界了解行销，但常忽视Marketing，甚至连好的译名都没有。我查英汉辞典，它对Marketing的译名是：市场学。其实Marketing岂止是一门学问！对以「客制」产品为主的台积电，它是每天都要执行的专业。

既然Marketing没有好的译名，我们就称它为Business Development吧。这名字译成中文容易，就是「业务发展」。

业务发展组织的使命是：

（一）了解并预测世界晶圆代工市场。

（二）了解台积电前三十大客户（约占台积电总营收的八十％）。

（三）了解台积电自己的研发及制造能力，因而配合客户的新产品要求，提高台积电的市占率。

（四）因为要做到（一）、（二）、（三），所以要常与客户接触。

我对CEO（包括自己）的期许是：把外面的世界带进公司里面，动员公司的人力、财力资源来应付外来的挑战及机会。其实，业务发展组织的负责人就是一个小型的CEO。

<p class="en-para">During Ming-Lieh Tsai's tenure as CEO, he established an Advanced Technology Business Organization and a Mainstream Technology Business Organization. Both units were centered on production lines (at TSMC, we also call production 'operations'); the Advanced unit was responsible for twelve-inch fabs, and the Mainstream unit for six-inch and eight-inch fabs.</p>
<p class="en-para">Tsai's business organization was essentially a replay of Brook's idea from 1996 (see Chapter 24). In 1996 I had rejected Brook's proposal, but more than a decade later I did not reject Tsai's. I suppose it was because Brook was only a 'general manager,' not CEO, whereas Tsai was CEO, and a CEO should be given more latitude.</p>
<p class="en-para">But now that I had returned as CEO, this mistake也应该 be corrected. So after addressing the most urgent issues (adjusting the pace of price cuts and resolving the outstanding dispute with NVIDIA), I pulled out the business development units from both the Advanced and Mainstream organizations and merged the two into a single company-wide operations organization.</p>
<p class="en-para">Next, I merged the two extracted business development units and endowed them with a new mission—TSMC's Marketing Department.</p>
<p class="en-para">Large companies in Europe and the United States typically group Sales and Marketing together. The Taiwanese business community understands 'sales' but often neglects 'marketing,' to the point that there is not even a good translation. I looked up marketing in an English-Chinese dictionary, and the translation given was 'market studies.' But marketing is far more than an academic discipline! For TSMC, which is primarily a custom-product company, it is a professional function practiced every day.</p>
<p class="en-para">Since marketing had no good Chinese translation, we called it Business Development. Translating that name into Chinese was easy: 'business development.'</p>
<p class="en-para">The mission of the business development organization was:</p>
<p class="en-para">(1) Understand and forecast the global foundry market.</p>
<p class="en-para">(2) Understand TSMC's top thirty customers (accounting for approximately eighty percent of TSMC's total revenue).</p>
<p class="en-para">(3) Understand TSMC's own research and development and manufacturing capabilities, thereby aligning with customers' new product requirements to increase TSMC's market share.</p>
<p class="en-para">(4) To accomplish (1), (2), and (3), it was necessary to maintain frequent contact with customers.</p>
<p class="en-para">My expectation for a CEO (including myself) was to bring the outside world into the company and mobilize the company's human and financial resources to meet external challenges and opportunities. In essence, the head of the business development organization was a small CEO.</p>
