## 二○一○至二○一八年

二○○八年的不景气来得急，但也恢复得快。与二○○一年的不景气相比，台积电营收自二○○一年第一季急降，一直到二○○三年第三季才回到二○○○年第四季的水准——不景气时期共十一季。

二○○八年台积电营收自第四季急降，但二○○九年第四季就已几乎回到二○○八年第三季水准——不景气时期只有四季。事后看起来，二○○八年的半导体市场不景气完全因为金融危机——投资工具过于氾滥而使得金融机构的槓桿太高，其实与半导体的基本需求无关。一旦各国政府出手拯救金融业，半导体需求不但复原，而且比「不景气」前成长更快。

这就是我在二○○九年六月复任CEO后看到的。幸运地，同时我们逐渐解决了四十纳米的良率问题。更好的消息：研发单位报告在下一个节点技术上（二十八纳米），他们有很快的进展。二十八纳米是一个关键技术，如果能在两年内开始量产，正在迅速发展的智慧型手机内的半导体将是我们的天下！

这是一个莎士比亚的「高潮」时刻：

> There is a tide in the affairs of men,
> Which, taken at the flood, leads on to fortune;
> Omitted, all the voyage of their life
> Is bound in shallows and in miseries.

世事的起伏本来是波浪式的，人们要是能够趁著高潮一往直前，一定可以功成名就；要是不能把握时机，就要终身蹭蹬，一事无成。

<p class="en-para">The 2008 downturn arrived quickly and also recovered quickly. Compared with the 2001 downturn, TSMC's revenue plummeted in the first quarter of 2001 and did not return to the fourth-quarter 2000 level until the third quarter of 2003—a downturn period of eleven quarters.</p>
<p class="en-para">In 2008, TSMC's revenue dropped sharply in the fourth quarter, but by the fourth quarter of 2009 it had almost recovered to the third-quarter 2008 level—a downturn of only four quarters. Looking back, the 2008 semiconductor downturn was entirely caused by the financial crisis—the excessive proliferation of investment vehicles had pushed financial institutions' leverage too high—which had nothing to do with the fundamental demand for semiconductors. Once governments around the world moved to rescue the financial sector, semiconductor demand not only recovered but grew faster than before the 'downturn.'</p>
<p class="en-para">This was what I saw after resuming the CEO role in June 2009. Fortuitously, we were simultaneously resolving the forty-nanometer yield problem. Even better news: the R&amp;D organization reported rapid progress on the next node technology (twenty-eight nanometers). Twenty-eight nanometers is a critical node; if we could begin mass production within two years, the semiconductors in rapidly growing smartphones would be ours for the taking.</p>
<p class="en-para">This was a Shakespearean 'climax' moment:</p>
<p class="en-para">'The course of true affairs is wave-like; if people can press forward with the tide, they will surely achieve success; but if they fail to seize the moment, they will stumble through life and accomplish nothing.'</p>
