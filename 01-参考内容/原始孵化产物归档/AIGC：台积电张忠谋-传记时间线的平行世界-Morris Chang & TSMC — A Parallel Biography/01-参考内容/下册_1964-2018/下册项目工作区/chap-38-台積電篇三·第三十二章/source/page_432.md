## 二十八纳米的汗与泪

我们当时以为大胆对二十八纳米产能力的投资，事后看来还嫌太少。但二十八纳米的大客户——高通，也拥有相当大的责任。当我们试产成功准备二○一二年量产时，高通起初估计他们二○一二年全年的需求是四万片晶圆。几个月后（二○一一年八月）他们发现给客户的样品受到超过预期的欢迎，就修改需求估计到十二万片。对台积电而言，一下子增加一个新产品三倍已是很艰难的任务，但高通的需求还继续增加——最后达到三十万片。

二○一二年二月九日，当高通的需求与我们的供应能力已有相当落差时，高通董事长及总裁联袂来访，他们的开场白就是：「我们只是要多点芯片，我们愿意付较高的价钱。」

我了好几位副总帮忙：涨价无补于事，我们也不会做，我们已正在尽全力增加产能及产量。

一小时后，他们恨恨而去，临走前撂下一句狠话：为了对他们股东负责，他们必须再找另一个供应来源。我回答：「等到你们新的来源可以供应时，我们也可以供应你们完全的需要了。」

高通的确找了另一个来源——格罗方德（GlobalFoundries），但我的预言是准确的。二○一二年九月十三日，我去高通的美国加州圣地牙哥总部访问高通总裁，他微微叹息似地说：「你说的话是对的，现在我们的新来源可以供应我们的边际需要，但你们也可以供应了。」

结果二十八纳米产能过剩了。我们另一位客户也跑来对我说：「现在二十八纳米有新的来源，而且价钱比你们便宜，所以我们要在这边减单了。」

我又气又惊，因为这位客户是我们一直认为忠诚的伙伴。他倒振振有辞：「这个新的来源不是我们建立的，而是高通建立的。既然高通建立了这个来源，你总不能说他不能用吧？」

<p class="en-para">What we had considered a bold investment in twenty-eight-nanometer capacity later appeared, in retrospect, to have been insufficient. But Qualcomm, TSMC's major customer at the twenty-eight-nanometer node, also bore considerable responsibility. When we successfully completed trial production and prepared for 2012 mass production, Qualcomm initially estimated their full-year 2012 demand at 40,000 wafers. A few months later (August 2011), they found that the samples provided to their customers were more popular than expected, and they revised their demand estimate to 120,000 wafers. For TSMC, tripling output for a new product at once was already a formidable task, but Qualcomm's demand continued to grow—eventually reaching 300,000 wafers.</p>
<p class="en-para">On February 9, 2012, when Qualcomm's demand had diverged considerably from our supply capacity, Qualcomm's chairman and president visited together. Their opening words were: 'We just need more chips. We are willing to pay a higher price.'</p>
<p class="en-para">I asked several vice presidents to assist: raising prices would not help, and we would not do it. We were already doing our utmost to increase capacity and output.</p>
<p class="en-para">An hour later, they left in frustration, but not before delivering a parting threat: to be responsible to their shareholders, they had to find another supply source. I replied: 'By the time your new source can supply you, we will also be able to supply your complete needs.'</p>
<p class="en-para">Qualcomm did find another source—GlobalFoundries—but my prediction proved accurate. On September 13, 2012, I visited Qualcomm's headquarters in San Diego, California, and spoke with the president. He sighed slightly and said, 'What you said was correct. Now our new source can supply our marginal needs, but so can you.'</p>
<p class="en-para">The result was that twenty-eight-nanometer capacity became oversupplied. Another one of our customers came to me and said: 'Now there is a new source for twenty-eight nanometers, and the price is cheaper than yours, so we are reducing our orders here.'</p>
<p class="en-para">I was both angry and startled, because this customer had always been considered a loyal partner. He replied calmly: 'This new source was not established by us, but by Qualcomm. Since Qualcomm established it, can you really say they should not be allowed to use it?'</p>
