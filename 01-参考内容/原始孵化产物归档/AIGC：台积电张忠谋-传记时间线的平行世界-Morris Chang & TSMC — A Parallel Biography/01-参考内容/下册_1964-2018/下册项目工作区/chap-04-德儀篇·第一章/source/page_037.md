## 本节注释汇总

### [1] 小布希（George W. Bush）

乔治·W·布希（一九四六—），美国第四十三任总统（二〇〇一—二〇〇九）。生于德州，一九七五年毕业于耶鲁大学，后入哈佛商学院。一九九五至二〇〇〇年任德州州长，二〇〇〇年以极具争议的选举人团优势当选总统。任内遭遇九一一事件，发动反恐战争及伊拉克战争，是美国历史上最受争议的总统之一。卸任后回归德州，远离政治中心，专注于绘画与慈善。

<p class="en-para"><b>[1] George W. Bush</b></p>
<p class="en-para">George W. Bush (1946–), the forty-third president of the United States (2001–2009). Born in Texas, he graduated from Yale University in 1975 and later attended Harvard Business School. He served as governor of Texas from 1995 to 2000 and was elected president in 2000, winning by a narrow margin in the Electoral College amid considerable controversy. His presidency was marked by the September 11 attacks, the War on Terror, and the Iraq War, making him one of the most controversial presidents in American history. After leaving office, he returned to Texas, distanced himself from the political center, and devoted himself to painting and philanthropy.</p>

### [2] 德州仪器（Texas Instruments, TI）

德州仪器成立于一九三〇年，总部位于德州达拉斯。公司早期从事石油勘探设备的地震探测，一九五一年进入半导体行业。一九五四年，TI 的工程师 Gordon Teal 制造出全球第一个商用硅晶体管。TI 是集成电路的共同发明者之一（与 Fairchild 的 Robert Noyce 分别从不同路径发明了集成电路）。TI 在半导体业的黄金年代（一九六〇—一九八〇年代）是全球最大的半导体公司之一。张忠谋于一九五八年加入 TI，在此度过了二十五年职业生涯。

<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para"><b>[2] Texas Instruments (TI)</b></p>
<p class="en-para">Texas Instruments was founded in 1930 and headquartered in Dallas, Texas. The company originally manufactured seismic equipment for oil exploration before entering the semiconductor industry in 1951. In 1954, TI engineer Gordon Teal produced the world's first commercial silicon transistor. TI was one of the joint inventors of the integrated circuit (alongside Fairchild's Robert Noyce, who arrived at the invention through a different path). During the golden age of the semiconductor industry—the 1960s through the 1980s—TI was one of the largest semiconductor companies in the world. Morris Chang joined TI in 1958 and spent twenty-five years of his career there.</p>
</div>

### [3] 邱吉尔《同代人》（Great Contemporaries）

《同代人》是邱吉尔于一九三七年出版的传记散文集，收录了他对同时代二十一位政治人物和军事领袖的评论。Curzon 篇写于一九二七年，其中「黄金—黄铜—铅」一段是英语文学中最著名的传记修辞之一。邱吉尔之引用，并非对 Curzon 的讽刺，而是对一位「少年得志、中年煊赫、晚年失势」的政治家的公允评价。张忠谋引用此段，同样不带讽刺——他以 Curzon 自比，坦然接受事业有其黄昏的事实，但选择在黄昏之后重新出发。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para"><b>[3] Churchill's Great Contemporaries</b></p>
<p class="en-para">Great Contemporaries, published by Churchill in 1937, is a collection of biographical essays in which he commented on twenty-one political figures and military leaders of his era. The essay on Curzon was written in 1927, and the passage about "gold—bronze—lead" is among the most famous pieces of biographical rhetoric in the English language. Churchill's reference was not satirical but rather an even-handed assessment of a politician who rose early, peaked in middle age, and declined in his later years. Chang's use of the passage carries no irony either: he identifies with Curzon, accepts soberly that every career has its twilight, and chooses to set out anew once the evening falls.</p>
</div>
