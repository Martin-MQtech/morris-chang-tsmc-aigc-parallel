# 德仪篇·第六章｜实现「美国梦」

我的双极集成电路策略（三）

我在第三章〈总经理的学习〉说过，半导体产品可分为两类：「标准式商品」和「客制品」。「标准式商品」卖给许多客户，竞争者也多，利润往往不高。「客制品」只为客户而製，规格往往由客户定，竞争者少或没有，利润通常较高。

我任「锗」、「矽」部总经理时就有过许多「客制品」。集成电路部当然也竭力争取「客制品」。在六〇年代后期及七〇年代早期，德仪「集成电路部」有三个大「客制品」客户——两个是我争取的。

其中之一是 IBM。当年 IBM 在资讯界的地位，比今日苹果在消费者的地位还高。我争取到的客製品每年销售金额也在几千万美元之谱，利润也不错。我尤其记得，一九七〇年集成电路市场萧条，但这个「客制品」的需求没有大波动，为我们一九七〇年的业绩增色不少。

另一个原来就有的大客户是国防商北美航空（North American Aviation）。我们的「客制品」为国防部所用，非常重视品质及可靠度。

第三个「客制品」客户是拍立得相机公司（Polaroid）——也是我争取到。我们的「客制品」专为拍立得最先进的相机 SX70 设计。在数码相机还相当遥远的七〇年代，拍立得「即刻洗出照片」的技术深受社会大众欢迎，新的 SX70 尤有重要创新。为这个「客制品」计画，我多次去波士顿拍立得总部与他们协商，深深感受到拍立得全体员工对 SX70 的兴奋和期待，也借此机会认识拍立得董事长兰德（Edwin Land）——一位当代的科技怪杰。

这几个「客制品」都为德仪带来不少营收和高于「标准式商品」的获利率。更重要的，它们赋予德仪同仁一种特别使命感——因为这几个「客制品」的终端产品——IBM 的新电脑、北美航空的导弹、拍立得的 SX70——都是当时社会闻名的产品。每次在报纸杂志看到这些产品的报导，我们「与有荣焉」！

<p class="en-para">My bipolar IC strategy (3)</p>
<p class="en-para">As I discussed in Chapter 3, semiconductor products fall into two categories: "standard commodities" and "custom products." Standard commodities were sold to many customers with numerous competitors, often yielding thin margins. Custom products were made for specific customers, with specs usually defined by the customer, fewer or no competitors, and typically higher margins.</p>
<p class="en-para">As GM of the Germanium and Silicon divisions, I had managed many custom products. The IC division certainly pursued them aggressively. In the late 1960s and early 1970s, TI's IC Division had three major custom-product customers — two of which I had won myself.</p>
<p class="en-para">One was IBM. IBM's position in the information industry then was analogous to Apple's in consumer electronics today. The custom product I had won generated tens of millions of dollars annually in revenue with healthy margins. I especially remember that during the IC market recession of 1970, demand for this custom product barely wavered — it bolstered our 1970 results considerably.</p>
<p class="en-para">Another, pre-existing major customer was North American Aviation, a defense contractor. Our custom products went into Defense Department systems, where quality and reliability were paramount.</p>
<p class="en-para">The third custom-product customer was Polaroid, which I also won. Our custom product was designed specifically for Polaroid's most advanced camera, the SX70. In the 1970s, when digital cameras were still a distant prospect, Polaroid's "instant photo" technology was enormously popular with the public, and the new SX70 represented a significant innovation. For this project, I traveled repeatedly to Polaroid's Boston headquarters to negotiate. I felt firsthand the excitement and anticipation that pervaded Polaroid for the SX70, and had the opportunity to meet Polaroid's chairman, Edwin Land — a technological wunderkind of his era.</p>
<p class="en-para">These custom products brought TI substantial revenue and margins well above standard commodities. More importantly, they gave TI employees a special sense of mission — because the end products — IBM's new computers, North American Aviation's missiles, Polaroid's SX70 — were all celebrated products in their day. Every time we read about them in a newspaper or magazine, we felt a shared pride!</p>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Custom product mission**
**中文解释**：客製品使命感，指员工因参与知名终端产品而产生的归属感和荣誉感；在 B2B 企业中，这种「使命感」是比薪酬更有效的留任因素。
**商业造句**: *The custom chip for the Boeing 777 gave every TI engineer a reason to brag at dinner parties — and harder for a competitor to lure them away with a 20% salary bump.*

2. **Edwin Land (兰德)**
**中文解释**：Edwin Land（一九〇九—一九七一），拍立得公司创始人，即时成像技术的发明者；他是乔布斯之前矽谷最受崇拜的「科技怪杰」之一——兼具科学家、发明家和企业家三重身份。
**商业造句**: *Edwin Land was to the 1960s what Steve Jobs would become to the 2000s — a technologist who made consumers feel the future in their hands.*

3. **Shared pride**
**中文解释**：与有荣焉，指因参与而闻名产品而产生的间接荣誉感；在组织行为学中，这种「间接荣誉」是企业文化的重要组成部分——它让员工觉得自己的工作「有意义」。
**商业造句**: *The "Designed in California" sticker gave even the lowest-ranking assembly technician a shared pride that no bonus check could replicate.*

### 修辞赏析

<span class="rhetoric-note">
<span class="zh">「每次在报纸杂志看到这些产品的报导，我们『与有荣焉』！」——张忠谋用一句古语，精准地捕捉了 B2B 企业员工最珍贵的情感资产：间接荣誉感。德仪的员工不是终端消费者直接看到的品牌，但他们知道 IBM 的电脑里有他们的芯片、拍立得的相机里有他们的电路。这种「藏在幕后的荣耀」，是 B2B 企业文化的核心价值一一它告诉员工：你不不需要做你需要做的事，你的产品使那些产品成为可能。</span>
<span class="en">"Every time we read about them in a newspaper or magazine, we felt a shared pride!" — Chang uses a classical Chinese phrase to capture the most precious emotional asset of B2B employees: indirect pride. TI employees were not the brand that end consumers saw directly, but they knew their chips were inside IBM's computers and their circuits inside Polaroid's cameras. This "behind-the-scenes glory" is the core value of B2B corporate culture — it tells employees: you do not need to be visible; your product makes those products possible.</span>

### 背景知识延伸

**定期减价策略（一九七二年）**：张忠谋在第三章中提到与BCG的合作后，最具冲击力的应用是德仪在一九七二年公开宣布的双极集成电路「定期每季减价」政策。德仪在双极集成电路的市场占有率已领先所有竞争者，製造成本也是同业中最低。公开「定期减价」让竞争对手疲于奔命：「他们眼前与德仪竞争，已因为成本较高而疲于奔命，德仪的定期减价更使他们连将来都没有指望。他们唯一的出路是退出这个市场。」张忠谋保存了一份一九七四年《电子新闻》的报导，标题大意是「市场已供不应求，英理士·张仍宣布德仪继续定期减价」。照片中他一脸凶悍。
