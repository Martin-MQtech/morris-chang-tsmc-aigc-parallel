# 德仪篇·第六章｜实现「美国梦」

计算器 MOS IC：两全其美的生意——

旧的 MOS 研发团队出走后，德仪新组建 MOS 研发。这时忽然有客户找上门来——他们要的不是旧 MOS 团队潜心研究的记忆体，而是一个新花样：计算器 IC。

一九七〇年代初期，计算器是非常珍贵的新产品，零售价一百美元以上。实际元件成本只有二三十美元。计算机商人只要装上塑胶外壳就可卖一百多美元——多么好的生意！许多商人成立计算器公司，大家都疯了，急着要买计算器用 IC。

德仪新的 MOS 团队非常乐意应付计算器 IC 生意，因为计算器 IC 的设计和製造都较记忆体简单。旧团队在一九六八年前辛苦耕耘的记忆体，早已被新团队抛在脑後，新团队现在的工作是为公司立刻赚钱的工作！

就在这一九七一年，MOS 归我管。过去我虽有「集成电路总经理」头衔，但事实上只有双极集成电路，没有 MOS。现在齐全，名符其实了。我接手的 MOS 团队已扩充到二三十名工程师，而且因为计算器 IC 的成功，都处在得意洋洋的状态中。

<p class="en-para">Calculator MOS IC: The win-win business —</p>
<p class="en-para">After the old MOS R&D team had departed, TI built a new MOS R&D effort. Then customers suddenly appeared — not seeking the memories the old team had been painstakingly developing, but something new: calculator ICs.</p>
<p class="en-para">In the early 1970s, calculators were a precious new product retailing for over $100. The actual component cost was only twenty or thirty dollars. Calculator merchants could slap on a plastic case and sell for over a hundred dollars — what a business! Many merchants founded calculator companies. Everyone was frantic, scrambling to buy the ICs that calculators required.</p>
<p class="en-para">TI's new MOS team was delighted to serve the calculator IC business, because designing and manufacturing calculator ICs was simpler than memories. The memory work the old team had painstakingly cultivated before 1968 was already forgotten by the new team. The new team's work was about making money for the company right now!</p>
<p class="en-para">It was in this context, in 1971, that MOS came under my jurisdiction. In the past I had held the title of "IC GM," but in practice I had only managed bipolar ICs, not MOS. Now the portfolio was complete — the title matched the reality. The MOS team I inherited had grown to twenty or thirty engineers and was riding high on the success of calculator ICs.</p>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **MOS portfolio catch-up**
**中文解释**：MOS 产品组合补全，指张忠谋的管辖范围从双极扩展到 MOS；在半导体产业中，「双极 + MOS」的组合覆盖了当时最先进的技术路线——但这个组合也让他的责任和风险同时倍增。
**商业造句">Taking over the MOS portfolio gave Chang the full IC spectrum to manage — and the full spectrum of competitors to battle, from Intel in memory to Motorola in logic.*

2. **Calculator gold rush**
**中文解释**：计算器淘金热，指七〇年代初期计算器市场爆发带来的 IC 需求狂欢；在半导体产业史中，计算器 IC 是第一批「量大、价低、技术门槛适中」的消费类产品——它开启了半导体从军品到民品的时代。
**商业造句">The calculator gold rush of the early 1970s was the moment semiconductor companies learned that consumer electronics could generate volumes that defense contracts could only dream of.*

### 第六章小结：美国梦的转折

一九七二年是张忠谋人生的「瞻前顾后点」。四十一岁，集团副总裁，掌管全球半导体业领袖——他用了二十三年走完了从一个穷留学生到行业霸主的路程。父母亲终于可以退休了。他的「美国梦」已经超额实现。

但张忠谋知道——前途不只是光明的。计算器 IC 带来的得意洋洋的笑容背后，是 MOS 在记忆体和微处理器领域的全面落后。英特尔的 110三记忆体正在席卷市场，微处理器已经发明——而德仪连影子都没有。彪希不懂半导体技术，夏伯特刚愎自用。下一步的总裁职位看起来理所当然——但德仪已经在「消费者产品」的歧途上越走越远。

一九七二年，是张忠谋「美国梦」的顶点，也是他在德仪黄金时代的终点。接下来的十年，将为他的离职埋下伏笔。

<p class="en-para">1972 was Chang's "looking-back-and-forward point." At forty-one, Group VP of the world's leading semiconductor operation — he had spent twenty-three years traveling from penniless student to industry titan. His parents could finally retire. His "American Dream" had been exceeded.</p>
<p class="en-para">But Chang knew — the road ahead was not entirely bright. Behind the smug smiles generated by calculator IC success lay MOS's comprehensive deficiency in memory and microprocessors. Intel's 1103 memory was sweeping the market; microprocessors had already been invented — and TI did not even have a trace. Shep did not understand semiconductor technology; Shepherd was rigidly self-opinionated. The presidency looked like the natural next step — but TI was already far down the wrong road of "consumer products."</p>
<p class="en-para">1972 was the apex of Chang's American Dream and the end of his golden era at TI. The next decade would plant the seeds of his departure.</p>
