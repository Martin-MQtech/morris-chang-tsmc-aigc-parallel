<p class="en-para">TSMC's core values—integrity, commitment, innovation, and customer trust—were tested in our relationship with Apple. At the same time, we tested Apple's values. In the end, both companies passed the test.</p>
<p class="en-para">After I retired from TSMC in 2018, I had little contact with Apple. But in February 2024, Jeff Williams came specifically to visit me. More than thirteen years had passed since he first appeared unannounced at my home in 2010. He was still handsome and erect. We shared Cantonese dim sum served at the restaurant upstairs from my office. He said something that warmed my heart: 'TSMC is one of Apple's very few best partners.'</p>
