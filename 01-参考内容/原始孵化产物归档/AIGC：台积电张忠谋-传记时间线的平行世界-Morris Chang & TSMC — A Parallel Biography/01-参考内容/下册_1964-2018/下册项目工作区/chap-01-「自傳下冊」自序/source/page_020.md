「下册」的第一篇「德仪篇」占全书三分之一篇幅，叙述我自二十七岁至五十二岁黄金年龄的崛起及衰退。德仪时期也是我最热情澎湃的年代。德仪让我「独上高楼，望尽天涯路」；为此，我无比感激。但是它对我的培植也激发了我登上德仪巅峰的雄心。然而这雄心是无法实现的——不说别的，只看一九七〇年代美国德克萨斯州的客观环境，就可断定我的雄心很难很难实现。但是我的痴心，居然使我在「明知不可为」的情况下，还在德仪多滞留了五、六年，后来连婚姻都赔上了。现在回想，那五、六年是多么荒谬的五、六年！

台积电成功后，常常浮现在我心头的是：假如我在一九七〇年代真的登上德仪的巅峰，我会怎样？半导体业又会怎样？我想我会自德仪退休，现在会是德克萨斯州老人（请参阅本书第一章）。至于半导体业呢？美国历史学者及畅销书《晶片战争》作者克里斯·米勒在二〇二三年访问台湾与我对谈时说：恐怕台积电会变成「德仪电」；如果真的这样，那可是要影响地缘政治的。

<p class="en-para">The opening section of Volume II, "The TI Episodes," takes up one-third of the book's length, recounting my rise and decline during the prime of my life from twenty-seven to fifty-two. The TI years were also my period of greatest passion. TI had let me "ascend the high tower alone, gazing to where the sky meets the earth" — for which I was profoundly grateful. Yet its cultivation of me had also ignited the ambition to reach TI's very summit. That ambition, however, could not be realized — one need only look at the objective environment facing a Chinese-American in 1970s Texas to know how improbable it was. But my infatuation kept me lingering at TI for five or six years longer than I should have, in the face of what I knew to be unattainable — and I eventually paid for it with my marriage. Looking back now, those five or six years were utterly absurd.</p>
<p class="en-para">After TSMC's success, a reflection often drifts across my mind: had I truly reached TI's summit in the 1970s, what would have become of me? What would have become of the semiconductor industry? I suppose I would have retired from TI and now be an old man in Texas (see Chapter 1 of this book). And the semiconductor industry? Chris Miller, the American historian and bestselling author of Chip War, said when he visited Taiwan to speak with me in 2023: TSMC might well have become "TI Semiconductor" — and if that had happened, the geopolitical consequences would have been immense.</p>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Prime of my life**
**中文解释**：黄金年龄，指一个人体力、智力和创造力最旺盛的人生阶段。
**商业造句**：*He left Goldman Sachs in the prime of his life to build a climate-tech startup — a decision that puzzled his mentors.*

2. **Ascend the high tower alone, gazing to where the sky meets the earth**
**中文解释**：独上高楼，望尽天涯路，语出晏殊《蝶恋花》，形容一个人在事业上达到一定高度后感受到的孤独与辽阔。
**商业造句**：*After taking the company public, she felt she had ascended the high tower alone — visible to all, understood by few.*

3. **Objective environment**
**中文解释**：客观环境，指不由个人意志转移的外部条件总和，包括制度、文化、社会偏见等。
**商业造句**：*Entrepreneurial ambition alone cannot overcome an objective environment devoid of risk capital and second chances.*

4. **Geopolitical consequences**
**中文解释**：地缘政治后果，指一个产业或企业的战略选择对国家间政治格局产生的影响。
**商业造句**：*TSMC's decision to build fabs in Arizona carries geopolitical consequences that extend far beyond semiconductors.*

5. **Chip War**
**中文解释**：《晶片战争》，克里斯·米勒著，2022 年出版，获《金融时报》2022 年度商业图书奖；被译作近二十种语言。
**商业造句**：*After reading Chip War, investors began treating semiconductor supply chains as a geopolitical risk factor, not just an industry trend.*

### 修辞赏析

<span class="rhetoric-note">
<span class="zh">张忠谋用「痴心」概括自己在德仪最后五年的滞留——「痴」字妙在既含有「痴迷」又有「痴傻」的双重意味。痴迷于登顶，痴傻于现实——一个「痴」字让读者同时感受到人物的执迷与天真，是极高密度的修辞压缩。</span>
<span class="en">Chang summarizes his final five years at TI with the word 痴心 ("infatuation") — a character that carries twin resonances: obsession and folly. Obsessed with the summit, foolish about reality — a single character lets the reader feel both the fixation and the naiveté, an extraordinarily dense rhetorical compression.</span>
</span>

<span class="rhetoric-note">
<span class="zh">「台积电恐变成德仪电」——米勒的这个假说，被张忠谋用作自序中最有力的「替代历史」（counterfactual）推演。这种写法让读者意识到：今天被视为「理所当然」的台积电，其实是某个「差一点」就消失的脆弱历史产物。悬念感立刻拉满。</span>
<span class="en">Miller's hypothesis — that TSMC might have become "TI Semiconductor" — is wielded by Chang as the preface's most powerful counterfactual. The reader is made acutely aware that TSMC, today's "inevitability," is in fact a fragile historical artifact that nearly wasn't. The suspense is immediate.</span>
</span>

### 背景知识延伸

**「独上高楼，望尽天涯路」出处**：出自北宋晏殊《蝶恋花·槛菊愁烟兰泣露》。王国维在《人间词话》中将此句列为「古今之成大事业、大学问者」三种境界之第一境：「昨夜西风凋碧树，独上高楼，望尽天涯路。」张忠谋化用此句概括自己在德仪时期孤独探索的心境，暗合王国维「第一境界」的本义。

**「德仪电」假说的影响**：米勒「TSMC 可能变成 TI Semiconductor」的说法，在地缘政治学者和半导体产业分析师中引起了广泛讨论。如果台积电从未独立存在，全球芯片供应链的格局可能完全不同。张忠谋在自序中引用此说，既表达了对历史的坦诚，也暗示了台积电成功的偶然性——以及这份偶然对今日世界的深远意义。
