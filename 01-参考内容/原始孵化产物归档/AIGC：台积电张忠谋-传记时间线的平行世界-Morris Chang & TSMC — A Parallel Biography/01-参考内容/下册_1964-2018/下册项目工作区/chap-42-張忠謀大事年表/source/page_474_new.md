<p class="en-para">## TSMC After the Succession (2018–2024)</p>
<p class="en-para">After Morris Chang retired in 2018, TSMC continued to flourish under the dual-leadership of Tim Liu and Morris Wei:</p>
<p class="en-para">- 2019: TSMC led the industry in mass-producing enhanced seven-nanometer (7+) technology</p>
<p class="en-para">- 2020: TSMC announced an investment of approximately twelve billion dollars to build an advanced fab in Arizona, USA</p>
<p class="en-para">- 2021: TSMC and Japan's Sony established a joint-venture fab in Kumamoto Prefecture (JASM)</p>
<p class="en-para">- 2022: TSMC led the industry in mass-producing three-nanometer technology</p>
<p class="en-para">- 2024: Tim Liu announced his retirement; Morris Wei succeeded him as both chairman and president, and TSMC entered a single-leadership system</p>
<p class="en-para">In the second volume of his autobiography, written in 2024, Morris Chang wrote: seeing TSMC reach new heights under the leadership of his successors was his greatest comfort.</p>
