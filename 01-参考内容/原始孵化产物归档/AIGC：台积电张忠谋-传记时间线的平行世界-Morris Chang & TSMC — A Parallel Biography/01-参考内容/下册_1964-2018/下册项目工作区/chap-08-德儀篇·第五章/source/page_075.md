# 德仪篇·第五章｜四个上司

一九七〇年代德仪半导体部的两个最大技术挑战是：如何追上（日本及美国）竞争者的 MOS 记忆体，以及如何追上英特尔的微处理器。这两个挑战都是技术问题。当我在与这两个技术问题挣扎时，夏伯特始终不了解我的需要。他不但不给我资源，反而为公司设立了新焦点——消费者产品（计算机、电子表、家庭电脑等）——把公司的资源都给了新焦点。德仪自此失焦迷途。

除了这两个缺点外，夏伯特还有一个迷思：他认为一个好的经理人可以经营任何行业。在一九七二年，虽然德仪没有任何拥有消费者产品经验的经理人，夏伯特认为别的行业的经理人也可以成功经营消费者产品事业。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">The two greatest technical challenges facing TI's semiconductor division in the 1970s were: how to catch up with competitors' (both Japanese and American) MOS memory, and how to catch up with Intel's microprocessors. Both were technical problems. As I struggled with these two challenges, Shepherd never understood my needs. Not only did he deny me resources, he established a new corporate focus — consumer products (calculators, electronic watches, home computers, and so on) — and diverted corporate resources to this new focus. From that point on, TI lost its way.</p>
<p class="en-para">Beyond these two flaws, Shepherd had another myth: he believed a good manager could run any industry. In 1972, even though TI had zero managers with consumer product experience, Shepherd believed managers from other industries could successfully run a consumer products business.</p>
</div>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Lost its way**
**中文解释**：失焦迷途，指企业因战略摇摆而失去了原有的核心竞争优势；在商业叙事中，「失焦」往往比「失败」更常见也更危险——因为它意味着企业在「做很多事」中逐渐忘记了「什么事最重要」。
**商业造句">TI lost its way not because it stopped innovating, but because it chased consumer electronics while the world's most valuable silicon real estate — memory and microprocessors — was being claimed by competitors.*

2. **Good manager can run any industry**
**中文解释**：好经理人可以经营任何行业，这是一种「管理普世主义」的迷思；在张忠谋看来，这种信念忽视了行业专业知识的壁垒——尤其是在技术驱动型产业中，「通用型管理者」往往不如「行业专家型管理者」。
**商业造句">The myth that a good manager can run any industry dies hard in boardrooms — and is always revived the quarter before a diversification disaster.*

3. **MOS memory vs. Microprocessor**
**中文解释**：MOS 记忆体 vs. 微处理器，一九七〇年代半导体产业最核心的两个战场；英特尔凭借在这两个领域的领先奠定了「矽谷之王」的地位，而德仪因「失焦」失去了这个机会窗口。
**商业造句">TheMOS memory and microprocessor battles of the 1970s were the original "platform wars" — whoever won them would define the industry's architecture for decades.*

### 第五章小结

张忠谋在德仪的二十年间经历了四位上司，每一位都在他身上留下了印记。他从吕斯学会了「非正式沟通」；从彪希学会了「如何在层级中生存」；从海格底学会了「远见与勇气」；从夏伯特学会了「刚愎自用」的危险。这四种遗产，构成了张忠谋日后管理台积电的「隐性基因」——他此后在台积电的管理风格，既包含吕斯的亲和力、彪希的组织力、海格底的远见，也刻意规避了夏伯特的刚愎自用。

一个伟大的管理者，不是天生的，而是从他的上司——无论是好是坏——身上「选择性地继承」的结果。

<p class="en-para">Over twenty years at TI, Chang had four bosses, each leaving a mark. From Lewis, he learned informal communication. From Shep, he learned how to survive in a hierarchy. These four legacies became the "hidden DNA" of Chang's management of TSMC — his later style incorporated Lewis's warmth, Shep's organizational discipline, Haggerty's farsightedness, and deliberately steered clear of Shepherd's inflexibility.</p>
<p class="en-para">A great manager is not born — he is the result of "selective inheritance" from his bosses, both good and bad.</p>
