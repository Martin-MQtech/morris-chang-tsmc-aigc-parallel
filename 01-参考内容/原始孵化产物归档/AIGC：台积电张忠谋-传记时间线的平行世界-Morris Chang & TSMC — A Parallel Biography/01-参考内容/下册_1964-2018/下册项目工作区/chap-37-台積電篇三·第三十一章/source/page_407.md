## 几个政经论坛

我自少年时期就留意时事，每天读报纸。十八岁到了美国后，受了「国破家亡」的刺激，更加强了这个习惯。开始参加政经论坛时，我其实是先专心听政经学者、国会议员、政府官员，以及少数企业家夸夸而谈。几次之后，觉得自己也有意见可以贡献，发言就愈来愈多——这是我参加论坛「有趣的、有益的」经验。在这过程中，也认识了好几位政经人物。

在二○○○年代中期，我分别参加了好几次三边委员会（Trilateral Commission）年会、美国企业研究院（American Enterprise Institute）年会、以及「美国艾森豪奖金会」（Eisenhower Fellowships）董事会；我也参加了其他几个「一次性」的政经会议。

**三边委员会（Trilateral Commission）**

三边委员会在七○年代早期成立，「三边」指的是美国、欧盟和日本——那时全球经济最强的三边。受邀参加的都是美、欧、日的政治、学术、经济精英。当时德仪董事长海格底是起始时的会员。大约一九七三或一九七四年吧，海格底去东京参加此会，我恰巧也在东京，海格底就携我同到会场。

三十年后，原来的「三边」已不是「唯三」经济强权，我也被邀请成为会员，也能够无畏地与「要人」们寒暄。在会议中，我也相当踊跃地发表意见或质疑别人的意见。会议长达三天半、议程丰富。

<p class="en-para">I had paid attention to current affairs since my youth, reading the newspaper every day. After arriving in the United States at eighteen, the trauma of national crisis only reinforced the habit. When I first began attending policy and economics forums, I listened intently to the sweeping pronouncements of scholars, congressmen, government officials, and a handful of entrepreneurs. After a few sessions, I felt I too had opinions to contribute, and I spoke more and more—it was an interesting and worthwhile experience. During this process, I also got to know several prominent figures in politics and economics.</p>
<p class="en-para">In the mid-2000s, I attended several annual meetings of the Trilateral Commission, the annual meeting of the American Enterprise Institute, and the board of Eisenhower Fellowships. I also participated in a few one-off policy and economics conferences.</p>
<p class="en-para">**The Trilateral Commission**</p>
<p class="en-para">The Trilateral Commission was founded in the early 1970s. 'Trilateral' referred to the United States, the European Union, and Japan—the three strongest economic powers in the global economy at the time. Those invited were the political, academic, and economic elites of the three regions. At that time, Howard Morris, chairman of TI, was a founding member. Around 1973 or 1974, Howard went to Tokyo for a meeting, and I happened to be in Tokyo as well. He brought me along to the venue.</p>
<p class="en-para">Thirty years later, the original 'trilateral' was no longer the sole economic powerhouse, and I had been invited to become a member myself. I could now converse fearlessly with these dignitaries. At the meetings, I spoke and questioned others' views quite actively. The sessions lasted three and a half days and were rich in agenda.</p>
