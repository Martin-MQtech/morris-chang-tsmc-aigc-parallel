**背景知识延伸｜蔡力行与联发科**

蔡力行离开台积电后，二○一七年转接任联发科CEO。联发科是台湾另一家重要的IC设计公司，以手机芯片和智能设备平台闻名。蔡力行在联发科推动了「真旗舰」策略，使联发科重返高端手机芯片市场。这段「台积电CEO→联发科CEO」的轨迹，既是个人职业生涯的延续，也是台湾半导体产业链上下游人才流动的缩影。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">After leaving TSMC, Tsai Li-hsing became CEO of MediaTek in 2017. MediaTek is another important Taiwanese IC design company, known for its smartphone chips and smart-device platforms. At MediaTek, Tsai drove a "true flagship" strategy that brought the company back into the high-end smartphone chip market. This trajectory—from TSMC CEO to MediaTek CEO—was both a continuation of his personal career and a microcosm of talent flow across the upstream and downstream segments of Taiwan's semiconductor industry.</p>

**修辞赏析｜「山雨欲来风满楼」**

张忠谋引用唐诗「山雨欲来风满楼」描述他在华府演讲后的危机感。这首诗出自许浑《咸阳城东楼》，原意是暴风雨来临前的寂静时刻。张忠谋在国会演讲时听众问的「不是台湾经济，而是美国金融现状」，让他意识到一场比二○○一年互联网泡沫更大的风暴正在酝酿。后来真的有人听了他的话出清股票、躲过了二○○八年金融海啸——从文学隐喻到现实影响，这首诗的功能超出了修辞本身。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Chang invoked the Tang dynasty line "before the mountain rain, the wind fills the tower" to describe the sense of foreboding he felt after his Washington address. The poem, by Xu Hun in "Clouds over the East Tower of Xianyang," originally evokes the stillness before a storm. When attendees at his Congressional testimony asked not about Taiwan's economy but about America's financial condition, Chang recognized that a storm larger than the 2001 dot-com bubble was gathering. Later, some who heeded his warning sold their positions and weathered the 2008 financial crisis. The poem's function, in this case, extended well beyond rhetoric into lived consequence.</p>
</div>
