## 蔡力行的结局

蔡力行离开CEO岗位后，转任台积电「新事业」总经理。新事业包括太阳能和LED两个方向。当时外界都看好这两个产业的发展前景，太阳能尤其在全球气候变迁的大背景下受到高度关注。

然而事与愿违。大陆在太阳能产业大力投资，造成全球产能过剩，台积电的太阳能业务很快陷入苦战。LED则是另一个困境——少数几家大厂把持知识产权，后进者很难突围。力行了几年，始终无法打开局面。

二○一四年，蔡力行离开了台积电，转任中华电信公司董事长。二○一七年，他又转任联发科（MediaTek）CEO。从我个人的角度，看到自己一手培养的人才最终为别家公司所用，是非常可惜的事。但从另一方面想，台积电栽培的人才能在更广泛的领域发光发热，对台湾的整体产业也不是坏事。

如今蔡力行在联发科表现优异，也算延续了他在半导体产业的贡献。只是在我交出CEO职位之前，我始终希望继承者是在台积电体系内成长的人。这个愿望在二○一八年我最终退休时实现了——由刘德音和魏哲家接任。

<p class="en-para">After leaving the CEO post, Ming-Lieh Tsai transferred to become general manager of TSMC's 'New Business' division, which encompassed solar energy and LEDs. At the time, the outside world was optimistic about the prospects of both industries, with solar energy receiving particularly high attention against the backdrop of global climate change.</p>
<p class="en-para">However, events did not go as planned. Mainland China's heavy investment in solar energy created a global oversupply, and TSMC's solar business soon fell into a difficult battle. LEDs presented another challenge: a handful of large firms held key patents, making it difficult for late entrants to break through. Tsai worked at it for several years but could never open a viable path.</p>
<p class="en-para">In 2014, Tsai left TSMC to become chairman of Chunghwa Telecom. In 2017, he became CEO of MediaTek. From my personal perspective, it was regrettable to see talent I had personally cultivated ultimately benefit another company. But on the other hand, TSMC's cultivated talents shining in broader fields was not a bad thing for Taiwan's overall industry.</p>
<p class="en-para">Tsai has since performed excellently at MediaTek, continuing his contribution to the semiconductor industry. But before I handed over the CEO position, I always hoped that my successor would be someone who had grown within the TSMC system. That wish was realized when I retired in 2018—Aric Liu and CC Wei took over.</p>
