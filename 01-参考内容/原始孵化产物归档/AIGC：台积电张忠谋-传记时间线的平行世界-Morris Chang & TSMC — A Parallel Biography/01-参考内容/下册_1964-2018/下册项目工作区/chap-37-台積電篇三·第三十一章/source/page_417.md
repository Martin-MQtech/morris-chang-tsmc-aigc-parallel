## 收回CEO后的整顿

收回CEO职务后，我立即开始了全方位的整顿。

首先是人事层面：我亲自整顿了人力资源部门，停擺了以「绩效评估」作为裁员依据的机制。这个机制本来是为了帮助员工进步的正面工具，却被蔡力行用作了裁员的武器。我重新强调：绩效评估的目的是帮助员工成长，而不是惩罚或淘汰。如果员工绩效不佳，主管应该提供辅导、调岗机会，只有在所有努力都失败之后，才能考虑最后的手段。

其次是沟通层面：我亲自约见了每一位被资遣的员工代表，听取他们的心声。同时我也向台积电全体员工发出了公开信，承诺今后不会再有「假淘汰、真裁员」的做法。我强调台积电是一家以人为本的公司，员工是公司最珍贵的资产。

再者是股利政策：由于台积电在二○○九年第一季几乎到了亏损边缘，董事会决定不发股利。但我承诺，一旦公司恢复获利，一定会补发。这个承诺在二○一○年实现，台积电的获利回到了历史高档，股利也回到了高水平。

最后是士气危机：经历了裁员风暴，台积电的士气跌到了谷底。我回到CEO岗位后，用了大约一年时间才把士气恢复过来。在那一年里，我不断与员工沟通，重拾他们对公司的信心。

这次事件让我深刻认识到：管理者的权力如果没有适当的监督，即使出发点是好的，也可能造成严重的后果。董事会作为最高治理机构，它的监督功能不应该只是形式上的。

<p class="en-para">After resuming the CEO position, I immediately began a comprehensive overhaul.</p>
<p class="en-para">First, on the personnel front: I personally reorganized the human resources department and suspended the mechanism that had used 'performance evaluation' as the basis for layoffs. This mechanism had originally been a constructive tool designed to help employees improve, but Tsai had turned it into a weapon for layoffs. I reasserted that the purpose of performance evaluation was to help employees grow, not to punish or eliminate them. If an employee's performance was poor, supervisors should provide coaching and transfer opportunities; only after all efforts had failed should the ultimate measure be considered.</p>
<p class="en-para">Second, on the communication front: I personally met with representatives of each dismissed employee to hear their concerns. At the same time, I sent an open letter to all TSMC employees, promising that there would be no more 'fake elimination, real layoffs.' I emphasized that TSMC was a people-oriented company and that employees were its most precious asset.</p>
<p class="en-para">Third, on dividend policy: because TSMC had nearly reached the brink of losses in the first quarter of 2009, the board decided not to pay dividends. But I promised that once the company returned to profitability, dividends would be made up. This promise was fulfilled in 2010, when TSMC's earnings returned to historic highs and dividends returned to their previous level.</p>
<p class="en-para">Finally, there was the morale crisis: after the layoff storm, TSMC's morale had sunk to rock bottom. After I returned to the CEO role, it took approximately one year to restore it. Throughout that year, I communicated constantly with employees to rebuild their confidence in the company.</p>
<p class="en-para">This incident taught me a profound lesson: without proper oversight, even well-intentioned managerial authority can produce serious consequences. As the highest governance body, the board's supervisory function should not be merely formal.</p>
