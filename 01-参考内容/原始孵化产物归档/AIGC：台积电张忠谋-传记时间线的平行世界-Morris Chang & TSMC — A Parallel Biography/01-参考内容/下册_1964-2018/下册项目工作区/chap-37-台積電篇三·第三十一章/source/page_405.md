# 台积电篇三·第三十一章｜释出又收回CEO职

## 释出CEO职（二○○五年）

自二○○一年第一季开始的「互联网泡沫破灭」引发的半导体不景气，在二○○三年第三季结束。二○○三年台积电第三季营收回到泡沫破灭前水平（二○○○年第四季）。二○○四年又是破纪录的一年，无论营收或获利都超出上次纪录（二○○○年）不少。

来到二○○五年前瞻台积电可预见的未来，天上没有一片乌云，而我也七十四岁矣！虽身体健康，但也应该为公司准备「继承」问题。思考后，决定先走第一步：释出CEO职，但仍保留董事长职。

台湾的公司法没有CEO这个职位，一般的译名「执行长」也不正确。事实上，CEO这个职位和这职位的权力，都是美国在十八世纪末期的发明。据说发明者是亚历山大·汉弥尔顿（Alexander Hamilton）。他是美国宪法起草人之一，后来担任华盛顿总统的财政部长。在美国宪法里，总统是国家的Chief Executive，拥有实际大权。汉弥尔顿在他财政部长任内设立美国第一银行时，也要让该银行首长在银行内拥有类似总统在国家的权力。他就沿用了总统在宪法上的头衔，但为了尊重国家总统，所以在Chief Executive后面加了Officer一字，就变成CEO了。

台湾对CEO的译名「执行长」甚不正确。CEO的职权岂止「执行」！他应该把外面的世界（包括国际政经、客户、竞争者）带进公司，动员公司的财力及人力资源，迎接他带进来的机会和挑战。一个公司的CEO，应该是公司的主要对外接触者，也是策略规划者及执行者。

自台积电成立，我一直是董事长兼CEO，也一直有一位总经理（除了一九九七至一九九八年我自兼总经理外），但历任总经理都没有我上述的CEO权力。现在，我要董事会任命一个名符其实的CEO。任命谁？

<p class="en-para">The semiconductor downturn triggered by the dot-com bubble burst, which began in the first quarter of 2001, ended in the third quarter of 2003. TSMC's third-quarter revenue in 2003 returned to the pre-bubble level of the fourth quarter of 2000. 2004 was another record-breaking year, with both revenue and earnings surpassing the previous record set in 2000.</p>
<p class="en-para">Looking ahead at TSMC's foreseeable future in 2005, there was not a cloud in the sky. And I was seventy-four years old. Though in good health, it was time to prepare for the question of succession. After reflection, I decided to take the first step: relinquish the CEO role while retaining the chairmanship.</p>
<p class="en-para">Taiwan's Company Law does not recognize the position of CEO, and the common translation 'Chief Operating Officer' is inaccurate. In fact, the CEO position and the authority it carries are American inventions from the late eighteenth century. The inventor is said to be Alexander Hamilton, one of the drafters of the U.S. Constitution, who later served as Secretary of the Treasury under President Washington. Under the U.S. Constitution, the president is the Chief Executive of the nation, wielding real power. When Hamilton established the First Bank of the United States during his tenure as treasury secretary, he wanted the bank's head to hold within the institution a power analogous to that of the president in the nation. He adopted the president's constitutional title but, out of respect for the national president, added the word 'Officer' after 'Chief Executive,' thus creating the CEO.</p>
<p class="en-para">The Taiwanese translation 'Chief Operating Officer' for CEO is far from accurate. A CEO's authority extends well beyond mere 'operation.' He should bring the outside world—international politics and economics, customers, competitors—into the company and mobilize its financial and human resources to meet the opportunities and challenges he has introduced. A company's CEO should be its primary external interface, as well as its strategist and executor.</p>
<p class="en-para">From TSMC's founding, I had always served as both chairman and CEO, and there had always been a general manager (except during 1997–1998, when I also held that post). Yet none of the successive general managers possessed the CEO authority I have just described. Now I asked the board to appoint a true CEO. Whom should I appoint?</p>
