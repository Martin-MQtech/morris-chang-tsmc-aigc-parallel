## 一九八二年孙运璿院长的聘雇及我的婉谢

一九八二年一月我的日记：

一月十日（星期日）上午十时去Marriott。M受孙运璿「说客」之托，要我去台湾工作，但他对何工作并不明了，讲了好几小时，不得要领。晚六时半去M家，M受李国鼎托，说李要我组织一个高科技对外贸易公司，其他情形也不详细。

一月十一日 早上电话李国鼎告知他已和潘及M谈过，又告诉他下月会飞去台湾和他详谈。

一月十二日 对彪希（BARR ROL）说李国鼎事。

一月十三日 彪希要我打电话回绝李国鼎，我说和李面谈一次吧，彪希虽答应，但似不高兴。

那时我是德仪的「品质总监」，本来就应到全球德仪工厂视察，所以相当容易安排在台湾逗留几天。后来安排在三月二十四日自东京抵台湾，二十五日在德仪台湾办德仪事，二十六至二十九日与孙院长、李政委、方贤齐院长、潘文渊顾问讨论台湾政府要给我的工作，以及待遇。

孙院长亲自对我讲工作。他说，我可以有三个选择：一是做他的科技顾问，二是做工研院院长，三他让李政委说。

李政委说他想要我主持政府要办的创投基金（就是M在达拉斯说的贸易公司），但这完全只在「想」的阶段，资金的来源都还没有。李政委只是要让我知道：创投基金也是一个可能。

待遇方面，只有潘顾问代孙院长问我最近三年待遇多少？我告诉他：二十五万、二十万、二十二万（均美元）。他请示了孙院长后告诉我：「不会让你吃亏。」

<p class="en-para"><b>Premier Sun's 1982 Offer and My Decline</b></p>
<p class="en-para">From my diary, January 1982:</p>
<p class="en-para"><i>January 10 (Sunday)</i> — 10 AM went to the Marriott. M, acting as an emissary for Sun Yun-suan, wanted me to work in Taiwan, but he had no clarity about what the job would be. Talked for hours without getting to the point. 6:30 PM went to M's house. M, entrusted by Li Guoding, said Li wanted me to organize a high-tech foreign trade company. Other details were vague.</p>
<p class="en-para"><i>January 11</i> — Morning, called Li Guoding, told him I had spoken with Pan and M, and that I would fly to Taiwan next month to discuss in detail.</p>
<p class="en-para"><i>January 12</i> — Told Barr about Li Guoding's matter.</p>
<p class="en-para"><i>January 13</i> — Barr asked me to call and decline Li Guoding. I said I would meet Li face-to-face once first. Barr agreed but seemed displeased.</p>
<p class="en-para">At that time I was TI's "Quality Director" and was expected to visit TI factories worldwide, so it was relatively easy to arrange a few days in Taiwan. Eventually, I scheduled my visit: arriving from Tokyo on March 24, handling TI Taiwan business on the 25th, and meeting with Premier Sun, Minister Li, President Fang Xianqi, and consultant Pan Wen-yuan from the 26th to the 29th to discuss the position and compensation the Taiwan government was offering.</p>
<p class="en-para">Premier Sun personally explained the job to me. He said I had three choices: first, be his technology advisor; second, be ITRI President; third, he would let Minister Li explain.</p>
<p class="en-para">Minister Li said he wanted me to run a government-sponsored venture capital fund (the trading company M had mentioned in Dallas), but this was still purely at the "thinking" stage — funding sources had not yet been identified. Minister Li simply wanted me to know that the VC fund was also a possibility.</p>
<p class="en-para">On compensation, only Consultant Pan, on Premier Sun's behalf, asked what my salary had been for the past three years. I told him: $250,000, $200,000, $220,000 (all USD). After consulting Premier Sun, he told me: "We won't let you suffer a loss."</p>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Quality Director (品质总监)**
**中文解释**：德仪全球品质与生产力的最高主管；张忠谋在德仪「下山」途中被安排此职，虽仍有全球视察的便利，但已远离核心决策圈。
**商业造句**：*The Quality Director role gave Chang legitimate reason to visit TI fabs worldwide — a perk that masked his political marginalization within the company.*

2. **Venture capital fund (创投基金)**
**中文解释**：由政府主导、投资于初创科技公司的事业基金；李国鼎的创投构想后来成为台湾科技业资金链的重要一环。
**商业造句**：*Li Guoding's vision for a government-backed venture capital fund was ahead of its time — it would take years for the concept to materialize into the thriving VC ecosystem Taiwan has today.*

3. **"We won't let you suffer a loss" (不会让你吃亏)**
**中文解释**：孙运璿院长对张忠谋的待遇承诺；这句话体现了台湾政府揽才的诚意，但也暗示了公部门薪资与跨国企业高管之间的巨大差距。
**商业造句**：*Premier Sun's assurance that Chang "wouldn't suffer a loss" was a gesture of goodwill — but also an acknowledgment that government salaries could never match corporate executive compensation.*
