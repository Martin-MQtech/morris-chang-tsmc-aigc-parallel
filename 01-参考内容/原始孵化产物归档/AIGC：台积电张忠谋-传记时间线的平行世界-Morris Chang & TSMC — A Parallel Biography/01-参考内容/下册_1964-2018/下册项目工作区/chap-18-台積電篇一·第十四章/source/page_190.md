## 工研院IC计划的开始

一九七五年，在我德仪半导体集团总经理任内，大约是营销部的属下告诉我德仪收到一封台湾工研院的「招标」函——征求IC技术移转，以及相关知识产权的授权。德仪当时政策不卖技术（十几年后，德仪窃此政策改变），所以我毫不犹豫地决定不投标。许多年后，我才知道工研院当年发了不少招标函，但只有少数厂商投标。

一九七六年一月，一位已自美国RCA退休、但仍住在普林斯顿的潘文渊先生要来达拉斯「请教」我，说是为了台湾IC计划招标。我在电话上又对潘先生讲了一遍德仪不卖技术的政策，但潘说他不是要德仪移转技术，而是有别的问题要「请教」，我就与他约了一月二十二日下午在我达拉斯办公室见面。

那天潘与另一位工研院顾问凌君同来。潘的主要问题是：「CMOS是不是最有希望的IC技术？」对这问题，我有一个肯定的答复：「是。」在一九七一年我接手德仪MOS部门后，我历经「PMOS」、「NMOS」，最后「CMOS」。站在一九七六年一月的立足点，CMOS成为最终胜出的技术，在我心中，已没有什么疑问。

潘、凌两位似乎很满意的答复。多年后，我才知道我对潘的肯定答复的确是台湾买RCA CMOS技术的最后关卡（至少是最后关卡之一）。紧接着潘的达拉斯行，四十二天以后（一九七六年三月五日），工研院就与RCA签约，再一个多月后（四月二十六日）工研院就派出第一批工程师去RCA实习。后来几十年IC技术的发展，的确以CMOS为主流，所以我一九七六年给潘者的答复没有错。

<p class="en-para"><b>The Genesis of ITRI's IC Program</b></p>
<p class="en-para">In 1975, during my tenure as TI's Semiconductor Group General Manager, I was informed by someone in marketing that TI had received an "invitation to bid" from Taiwan's Industrial Technology Research Institute (ITRI). The solicitation sought IC technology transfer and related intellectual property licensing. TI's policy at the time was not to sell technology (a policy TI would quietly change a decade later), so I decided without hesitation not to bid. Many years later, I learned that ITRI had sent out numerous bids that year, but only a handful of companies responded.</p>
<p class="en-para">In January 1976, a Mr. Pan Wen-yuan, retired from RCA but still living in Princeton, wanted to come to Dallas to "consult" me, saying it was about Taiwan's IC program bidding. On the phone, I repeated TI's no-technology-sales policy, but Pan said he wasn't asking TI to transfer technology — he had other questions to "consult" me about. We scheduled a meeting for the afternoon of January 22 in my Dallas office.</p>
<p class="en-para">That day Pan came with another ITRI consultant, Mr. Ling. Pan's main question was: "Is CMOS the most promising IC technology?" I gave an affirmative answer: "Yes." After taking over TI's MOS division in 1971, I had journeyed through PMOS, NMOS, and ultimately CMOS. Standing at the vantage point of January 1976, CMOS had emerged as the definitive winning technology — of this I had no doubt.</p>
<p class="en-para">Pan and Ling seemed satisfied with my answer. Years later, I learned that my affirmative response to Pan was indeed the final checkpoint (or at least one of them) for Taiwan's decision to purchase RCA's CMOS technology. Just 42 days after Pan's Dallas visit (March 5, 1976), ITRI signed with RCA, and a little over a month later (April 26), ITRI dispatched its first batch of engineers to RCA for training. The subsequent decades of IC technology development confirmed CMOS as mainstream — so my answer to Pan in 1976 was correct.</p>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Technology transfer (技术移转)**
**中文解释**：指将技术知识、专利、工艺流程从一方转移给另一方的商业行为；在半导体产业中，技术移转通常以授权金或权利金的形式进行。
**商业造句**：*Technology transfer deals in semiconductors typically involve not just patent licensing, but also the tacit knowledge of process integration that cannot be captured in documents alone.*

2. **CMOS (Complementary Metal-Oxide-Semiconductor，互补金属氧化物半导体)**
**中文解释**：一种低功耗的IC制造技术，将成为全球半导体产业的主流制程；张忠谋在1976年即预判CMOS的最终胜出，这是他技术判断力的早期体现。
**商业造句**：*CMOS technology's superior power efficiency made it the inevitable choice for the coming wave of consumer electronics — a conviction Chang held as early as 1976.*

3. **Invitation to bid (招标函)**
**中文解释**：政府或公共机构为特定项目向潜在供应商发出的正式邀请；工研院以招标方式寻求IC技术移转，体现了当时台湾政府推动产业升级的决心。
**商业造句**：*The ITRI's invitation to bid for CMOS technology transfer was Taiwan's opening move in its decades-long project to build a domestic semiconductor industry.*
