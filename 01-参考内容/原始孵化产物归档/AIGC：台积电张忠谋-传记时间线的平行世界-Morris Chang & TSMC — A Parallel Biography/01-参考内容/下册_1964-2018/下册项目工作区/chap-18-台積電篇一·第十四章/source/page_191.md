## 一九七六年近代工程技术讨论会

与潘文渊会面的同月，秘书忽然告诉我有一位自称是台湾行政院秘书长的人从洛杉矶打电话给我，要和我讲话，我满腹怀疑地接起电话。对方自我介绍他是费骅，行政院秘书长，也是中国工程师学会会长，电话目的是邀请我参加当年七月在台北举行的近代工程技术讨论会，并做专题演说。我在电话上答应考虑，挂了电话后就打电报给德仪台湾，嘱他们打听一下费秘书长及近代工程技尤讨论会的来历。德仪台湾很快就给我正面回音，七月会期也正值德仪假期，我就答应了。

那会议是我与台湾关系的真正开始。我利用德仪的假期赴会。记得我在演说前一天早上就到台北，在圆山大饭店登记后就到山下圆山联谊会，坐在游泳池旁，准备我次日的演讲。虽然台北七月相当炎热，但我来自更炎热的达拉斯，并不觉得不舒服。

一九七六年我还在德仪事业的「高原」上，还是总公司「成长委员会」主席，理论上负责全公司的成长发展策略。那时人们正大做价格一半的电子表，我的演讲就以「电子表」为题，阐述德仪如何先从「市场研究」决定「目标电子表」的性能、设计，以及最利推销的价格；然后，如何动员内部研发、工程、生产部门，不但要做到目标的性能和设计，而且要做到使得公司能在二十美元零售价赚到合理利润的成本。

这场演讲相当受欢迎。在一九七六年的台湾，科技产业相当落后。我所讲的世界级科技公司如何有计划地推出新产品的故事，绝大听众是第一次听到。

听众中有一位是李国鼎政务委员。此时离我一九六八年因筹备德仪台湾而来台拜访时任经济部部长的他，已经有八年之久。李政委当时六十六岁，一生服务政府，历任行政院美援运用委员会秘书长、经济部部长、财政部部长等财经要职，但在一九七六年，他刚经历了重大的心脏手术。据他自己告诉我，蒋经国行政院院长为了减轻他的负担，请他担任科技方面的政务委员。国鼎先生虽在大学时修物理，但已离开科技多年，现在做了科技政委，当然想与世界级的科技公司建立关系；德仪正是这样的公司，何况里面还有一个华裔的高阶主管！

我演讲后李政委就趋前称赞，并约我午餐，此后无论我来台湾，或他去美国，他总会约我见面；每次他去美国，他总会要来德仪参观，看看有什么新发展。他也结识了刚自德仪董事长职位退休的海格底。

<p class="en-para"><b>The 1976 Engineering Conference</b></p>
<p class="en-para">In the same month as my meeting with Pan Wen-yuan, my secretary suddenly told me that someone claiming to be the Secretary-General of Taiwan's Executive Yuan was calling from Los Angeles and wished to speak with me. I picked up the phone full of skepticism. The caller introduced himself as Fei Hua, Secretary-General of the Executive Yuan and President of the Chinese Institute of Engineers. His purpose was to invite me to speak at the upcoming Engineering Conference in Taipei that July. I said I would consider it, and after hanging up, I cabled TI Taiwan to investigate the background of Secretary-General Fei and the conference. TI Taiwan quickly gave me a positive assessment. The July timing coincided with TI's holiday period, so I accepted.</p>
<p class="en-para">That conference marked the true beginning of my relationship with Taiwan. I used TI's holiday to attend. I remember arriving in Taipei the morning before my speech, checking into the Grand Hotel, then going down to the Yuan Shan Club by the swimming pool to prepare for the next day's presentation. Although Taipei in July was quite humid and hot, I came from the even more sweltering Dallas and didn't find it uncomfortable.</p>
<p class="en-para">In 1976, I was still on the "plateau" of my TI career, serving as chairman of the corporate Growth Committee, theoretically responsible for the company's growth and development strategy company-wide. At that time, everyone was making electronic watches at half the prevailing price. My speech took "the electronic watch" as its topic, describing how TI used market research to define the target watch's performance, design, and optimal selling price, and then mobilized internal R&D, engineering, and manufacturing not only to meet those performance and design targets but to achieve costs that would allow reasonable profit at a $20 retail price.</p>
<p class="en-para">The speech was well received. In 1976 Taiwan, the technology industry was quite backward. The story of how a world-class technology company systematically brings new products to market was something most of the audience had never heard before.</p>
<p class="en-para">Among the audience was Kuo-Ting Li (Li Guoding), a Minister without Portfolio. By then, eight years had passed since my first visit to Taiwan in 1968, when I had met him as Minister of Economic Affairs to discuss building the TI Taiwan factory. Minister Li was then 66, a lifelong government veteran who had served as Secretary-General of the Council for International Economic Cooperation, Minister of Economic Affairs, and Minister of Finance. But in 1976, he had just undergone major heart surgery. As he himself told me, Premier Chiang Ching-kuo had asked him to serve as Minister without Portfolio for technology to lighten his load. Although Mr. Li had studied physics in college, he had left technology behind long ago. Now as technology minister, he naturally wanted to build relationships with world-class technology companies. TI was precisely such a company — and what's more, it had a Chinese senior executive!</p>
<p class="en-para">After my speech, Minister Li came forward to compliment me and invited me to lunch. From then on, whenever I visited Taiwan or he visited the United States, he would arrange to see me. Every time he came to the U.S., he would visit TI to see what new developments there were. He also became acquainted with Eric Haggerty, who had recently retired as TI's Chairman.</p>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Growth Committee (成长委员会)**
**中文解释**：德仪最高管理层级的事业策略委员会，由董事长及总裁担任委员、主席负责全公司成长发展策略；张忠谋在一九七六年担任此委员会主席，但并无实权。
**商业造句**：*The Growth Committee was the apex of TI's strategic planning — in theory setting corporate-wide growth priorities, in practice often serving as a sounding board for the Chairman's vision.*

2. **Market research → cost targeting (市场研究→成本目标法)**
**中文解释**：一种逆向定价方法——先通过市场研究确定消费者可接受的零售价，再反推各环节可容忍的成本上限；这是德州仪器在消费电子产品中常用的「按价值定价」方法。
**商业造句**：*TI's approach was to start with the consumer price point and engineer backward — a discipline that forced every function to justify its cost contribution to the final product.*

3. **MITI-style technology Minister (科技政委)**
**中文解释**：台湾政府中负责科技政策规划的政务委员；李国鼎担任此职期间，正是台湾从劳力密集产业向技术密集产业转型的关键期。
**商业造句**：*Li Guoding's appointment as technology minister signaled Taiwan's determination to leap from assembly-based manufacturing into the knowledge economy.*
