## 给李政委的报告（一九八一年）

我就四个题目建言，谨建言如下：

**一、自动化**

方贤齐院长让我看他的初步工研院自动化技术研发计划，我的问题是：「研发了又如何呢？」「工业界又怎么享受工研院的研发成果？」

**二、VLSI计划**

VLSI案需要的经费和人才甚巨，可不必做。

**三、电脑**

我非常高兴看到小型「电脑相关」公司的蓬勃发展。建言：

1. 硬件制造应限于以微处理器为基础的路板、模组、或个人电脑。
2. 小型电脑公司的经营重点应为：软件开发、硬件适应、比较简单路板或电脑设计及组装，以及售后服务，或提供客户个别需要的服务。
3. 台湾本地市场甚小，大部分营收要从国外来。

**四、一般建言**

1. 创业基金（亦称风险基金）。
2. 考虑扩充台湾的证券市场。
3. 考虑如何以租税政策鼓励投资「技术密集」事业。

四十年后重温这报告，我相当惊异我那时的冲劲。当然我那时完全不了解台湾的政治和工业环境（不然不会建议工研院不做自动化及VLSI计划），我也高估了政府对民间企业的影响力（不然不会对政府做对电脑产业的建言），但纯从科技业策略面看，即使今天的我，也不惭愧我四十年前的建言。

事实上，在孙运璿任行政院院长时期，正是政府科技计划最抬头的时期。在我访问台湾之前，政府已决定要工研院做自动化和VLSI计划，我的「不要做」建议，当然无人理会。只有我的「一般建言」——关于创业基金、租税优惠、证券市场方向等等——倒是在以后几年政府陆续开始做，但是这些动作，也已有许多财经专家建议，我的建言只是附和他们一下而已。

几个月后，我就发现李政委「借调」我参观台湾工业，主要目的不是要我建言；「醉翁之意不在酒」，他只是要我进一步了解台湾，他的下一步就是邀请我到台湾工作。

<p class="en-para"><b>The Report to Minister Li (1981)</b></p>
<p class="en-para">I offered recommendations on four topics:</p>
<p class="en-para"><b>I. Automation</b></p>
<p class="en-para">President Fang Xianqi showed me ITRI's preliminary automation R&D plan. My questions: "What happens after the R&D?" "How would industry benefit from ITRI's research results?"</p>
<p class="en-para"><b>II. VLSI Program</b></p>
<p class="en-para">The VLSI program would require massive funding and talent — it need not be done.</p>
<p class="en-para"><b>III. Computers</b></p>
<p class="en-para">I was delighted to see the flourishing of small "computer-related" companies. My recommendations:</p>
<p class="en-para">1. Hardware manufacturing should be limited to microprocessor-based circuit boards, modules, or personal computers.</p>
<p class="en-para">2. Small computer companies should focus on: software development, hardware adaptation, relatively simple circuit board or computer design and assembly, after-sales service, and customized client solutions.</p>
<p class="en-para">3. Taiwan's domestic market is quite small; most revenue must come from abroad.</p>
<p class="en-para"><b>IV. General Recommendations</b></p>
<p class="en-para">1. Venture capital funds (also called risk funds).</p>
<p class="en-para">2. Consider expanding Taiwan's securities market.</p>
<p class="en-para">3. Consider tax policies to encourage investment in "technology-intensive" industries.</p>
<p class="en-para">Revisiting this report forty years later, I am quite amazed by my youthful vigor. Of course, I understood nothing of Taiwan's political and industrial environment at the time (otherwise I wouldn't have advised ITRI against the automation and VLSI programs). I also overestimated the government's influence over private enterprise (otherwise I wouldn't have made recommendations to the government about the computer industry). But purely from a technology strategy standpoint, even today's me feels no shame about the recommendations made forty years ago.</p>
<p class="en-para">In fact, during Sun Yun-suan's tenure as Premier, government technology programs were at their zenith. Before my visit, the government had already decided to have ITRI pursue automation and VLSI. My "don't do it" advice was, of course, ignored. Only my "general recommendations" — on venture capital, tax incentives, securities market direction, and so forth — were gradually implemented in subsequent years. But by then, many financial experts had already proposed similar ideas; my recommendations merely echoed theirs.</p>
<p class="en-para">A few months later, I realized that Minister Li's real purpose in "borrowing" me to visit Taiwan's industry was not for my advice. "The drinker's heart is not in the cup" — he simply wanted me to better understand Taiwan. His next step would be to invite me to work there.</p>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **VLSI (Very Large Scale Integration，超大型集成电路)**
**中文解释**：指将数十万乃至数百万个晶体管集成在一块芯片上的技术；工研院的VLSI计划后来成为台湾半导体产业的重要技术来源。
**商业造句**：*The VLSI program was Taiwan's bold bet on leapfrogging into the most advanced semiconductor manufacturing technologies of its era.*

2. **Venture capital / Risk fund (创业基金/风险基金)**
**中文解释**：以股权投资方式支持初创公司发展的资金；李国鼎积极推动的创投基金，后来成为台湾科技业蓬勃发展的关键金融基础设施。
**商业造句**：*Li Guoding's advocacy for venture capital funds was one of the critical financial innovations that enabled Taiwan's high-tech startup ecosystem.*

3. **Tax incentives for technology-intensive industry (租税优惠)**
**中文解释**：政府以减免税收方式鼓励企业投资技术密集产业；新竹科学工业园区的租税优惠是吸引高科技厂商入驻的关键政策工具。
**business造句**：*Tax incentives were the primary carrot that drew technology companies into the Hsinchu Science Park, transforming it into Taiwan's Silicon Valley.*
