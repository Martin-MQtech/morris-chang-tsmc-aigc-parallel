# 台积电篇一·第十四章｜我与台湾的渊源

## 来自台湾的同学

一九六一年前，我不认识任何来自台湾的人。一九六一年，德仪派遣我到斯坦福读博，在与他们的交谈中，我在斯坦福认识了从台湾来的同学，对台湾有初步的认识。

那时，半导体公司已纷纷到低工资的东南亚设立封装测试厂，他们选择的地点大部分在香港。德仪比较保守，还没有到东南亚设厂，但我已认识来自台湾的同学，也问他们对跨国公司利用低工资劳力的见解。他们异口同声地认为：对低工资地区，跨国公司去设厂绝对是好事，因为低工资地区有优秀的大学工程职校毕业的技工人才，也有勤奋的作业员——总之，跨国公司去香港而不去台湾，大概是因为并不知道台湾的好处。

那时我虽不认识台湾，但认识香港；香港给我的印象是一个商业城市，似乎很不适合发展工业，所以我也下意识地接受了同学对台湾的偏见。

<p class="en-para"><b>Classmates from Taiwan</b></p>
<p class="en-para">Before 1961, I did not know anyone from Taiwan. In 1961, TI sent me to Stanford for my PhD, and during my time there, I met several classmates from Taiwan. Through conversations with them, I gained a preliminary understanding of Taiwan.</p>
<p class="en-para">At that time, semiconductor companies were already rushing to set up assembly and test facilities in low-wage Southeast Asia, mostly in Hong Kong. TI was more conservative and had not yet established factories in Southeast Asia, but I had gotten to know classmates from Taiwan and asked their views on multinational companies exploiting low-wage labor. They unanimously believed that for low-wage regions, it was absolutely a good thing for multinationals to build factories there, because these regions had excellent technical talent graduating from universities and vocational schools, as well as diligent workers. In short, the reason multinationals went to Hong Kong rather than Taiwan was probably that they simply did not know Taiwan's advantages.</p>
<p class="en-para">At that time, although I did not know Taiwan, I did know Hong Kong. Hong Kong impressed me as a commercial city, seemingly quite unsuitable for industrial development, so I subconsciously accepted my classmates' bias against Taiwan.</p>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Assembly and test facility (封装测试厂)**
**中文解释**：半导体后段工序工厂，负责将晶圆切割成芯片并进行封装和测试；在半导体产业链中，封装测试是劳动力密集的环节，因此最早向低工资地区转移。
**商业造句**：*Assembly and test facilities were the first semiconductor operations to migrate to Southeast Asia, leveraging the region's cost advantages in labor-intensive back-end processing.*

2. **Vocational school talent (职校人才)**
**中文解释**：指职业学校培养的技术工人；在半导体制造业中，职校毕业生通常担任技术员角色，是产线运转的关键人力。
**商业造句**：*The availability of vocational school graduates with hands-on technical skills was a critical factor in site selection for early semiconductor assembly plants.*
