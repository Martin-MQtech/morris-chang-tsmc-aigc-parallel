# 德仪篇·第四章｜一帆风顺

我的集成电路部（Department）晋级为 Division，我成为公司副总裁，直接对彪希负责。我的上司吕斯被架空，也没有新职务给他。彪希对我说：「吕斯是一个好人君子，但是我要直接管理重要业务单位，他对我没有用。」吕斯本来是「助理副副总裁」，没有拿掉他的头衔，但他的原来下属——我——现在已是副总裁，头衔超越他。这又是等于要他走路的信号。吕斯当然也了解这信号，于是在一个月内也辞职了。

还有一个震撼：半导体集团内除了集成电路外，还有几个业务单位，现在把它们全交给一个新升上来的助理副副总裁管。这个新升上来的是谁？他就是我从前「矽部」下属——比利时籍「塑胶包装」处长！在我离开「矽部」后，比利时人仍留任原职，对新任的「矽部」总经理报告。现在他一跃为他原来上司的上司——对他原上司当然难堪。但难堪归难堪，原来上司到底仍保留了原职，公司并没有对他表示不信任。他本着「留得青山在，不怕没柴烧」的理念，仍在公司待下去了。这是一个明智的决定。为两、三年后，比利时人就因为表现不佳而免职，而这位一时难堪的「矽部」总经理，在此后几十年中在德仪继续发展，最后在副总裁职位上光荣退休。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">My Integrated Circuit Department was upgraded to a Division, and I became a corporate Vice President, reporting directly to Shep. My former boss Lewis was sidelined with no new role offered. Shep said to me: "Lewis is a good man and a gentleman, but I need to directly manage the key business units. He is of no use to me." Lewis retained his title of "Assistant Vice President," but his former direct report — me — was now a VP, a rank above him. This was yet another signal that he should leave. Lewis understood the signal perfectly and resigned within a month.</p>
<p class="en-para">There was another shock: the several business units in the Semiconductor Group other than Integrated Circuits were now all placed under a newly promoted Assistant VP. Who was this newly promoted person? He was my former subordinate from the Silicon Division — the Belgian-born director of the Plastic Packaging Department! After I left the Silicon Division, the Belgian had remained in his role, reporting to the new Silicon Division GM. Now he had leapfrogged to become his former boss's boss — certainly an awkward situation for his former superior. But awkward as it was, the former boss retained his position; the company had not expressed distrust in him. Clinging to the philosophy of "while there are green hills, there will be firewood," he stayed on. It was a wise decision. Two or three years later, the Belgian was removed for poor performance, and the Silicon GM who had endured the moment of awkwardness continued to develop at TI for decades, eventually retiring honorably as a Vice President.</p>
</div>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Sidelined**
**中文解释**：被架空，指保留头衔但剥夺实权；在组织政治中，「架空」是一种比「解雇」更隐蔽但也更残忍的权力手段。
**商业造句">The founder was sidelined as "Chairman Emeritus" — a title that sounded prestigious but came with no direct reports and no operating authority.*

2. **Leapfrogged**
**中文解释**：跃升超越，指跳过正常的晋升阶梯、直接超越原来的上司；在快速成长的组织中，「跃升」是常见的现象，但对被超越者而言往往难以接受。
**商业造句">She leapfrogged three levels in two years — a trajectory that delighted the board but mortified the managers she had just surpassed.*

3. **While there are green hills, there will be firewood**
**中文解释**：留得青山在，不怕没柴烧，指只要保住根本，未来就有希望；在组织政治中，这种「忍辱待机」的智慧往往比「愤而辞职」更有长远的回报。
**商业造句">He swallowed his pride and stayed — "while there are green hills, there will be firewood" — and within three years, the political winds had shifted in his favor.*

### 修辞赏析

<span class="rhetoric-note">
<span class="zh">「留得青山在，不怕没柴烧」——张忠谋在自传中引用这句中国谚语，来描述一位被下属超越的「矽部」总经理的选择。这个引用不仅精准地捕捉了当事人的心态，也暗示了张忠谋对这种「忍辱待机」策略的认可。在张忠谋的叙事中，这位「矽部」总经理后来在副总裁职位上光荣退休——这似乎验证了「留得青山在」的智慧。但读者也不禁会想：如果这位总经理没有选择留下，而是愤而辞职，他的人生会不会是另一个故事？张忠谋没有回答这个问题——他只是忠实地记录了两种选择及其后果。</span>
<span class="en">"While there are green hills, there will be firewood." — Chang quotes this Chinese proverb to describe the choice of a Silicon Division GM who had been leapfrogged by his former subordinate. The quotation not only captures the protagonist's mindset but also signals Chang's approval of this "endure and wait" strategy. In Chang's narrative, this GM later retired honorably as a VP — seemingly validating the "green hills" wisdom. But the reader wonders: if the GM had chosen to leave in anger, would his life have been a different story? Chang does not answer this question — he simply records both choices and their consequences with fidelity.</span>
</span>

### 第四章小结：一帆风顺的背后

从一九六四年到一九六七年，张忠谋完成了从「锗部」总经理到公司副总裁的惊人跳跃——三年间连升四级。但「一帆风顺」的标题背后，是每一次晋升都伴随着巨大的挑战：在萎缩的锗市场中增长、在矽市场中与快捷半导体竞争、在集成电路领域收拾史厥特的残局。张忠谋的「顺」，不是没有风浪，而是每一次风浪都被他变成了上升的动能。

<p class="en-para">From 1964 to 1967, Chang completed a stunning leap — from GM of the Germanium Division to corporate Vice President, four levels in three years. But behind the title "Smooth Sailing," every promotion came with enormous challenges: growing in a declining germanium market, competing with Fairchild in silicon, cleaning up the mess Strat left in ICs. Chang's "smooth" was not the absence of storms — it was the ability to turn every storm into upward momentum.</p>
