# 德仪篇·第四章｜一帆风顺

任「集成电路部」总经理——

一九六〇和七〇年代德仪最重要的「业务单位」，无疑是「集成电路」。德仪与快捷半导体共同发明了集成电路。十年后，集成电路已是所有半导体公司的重要业务，而且这项业务继续急速成长。在一九六六年，德仪的「集成电路部」总经理虽然还没有副总裁头衔，但这职位已被视为公司最重要的职位之一。

一九六六年，谁是德仪集成电路总经理？原来他就是我在本书第二章已提到——我初进德仪时下属一位工程师。此人名为史厥特（Strat），甚聪明，有强烈的企图心及进取心，又善于操纵他人，是一个不折不扣的「公司政客」。他与我几乎同时加入德仪，起先是我下属两位工程师之一；一年后我升了一级，我也升了他一级；后来我去史丹佛两年半，在那两年半内，史厥特足足升了三级！我自史丹佛回来，他已是「矽部」总经理，在我做「锗部」总经理时，他又调升为「集成电路部」总经理。

在一九五〇、六〇年代——也就是半导体的早期——德仪的确有过许多「猎富武士」（Soldiers of Fortune）。这些「武士」们不少拥有高学位、高能力，而且强烈的企图心。可惜他们的企图心并不是要与大家共同建立大企业，而只是为自己发一笔财。当他们在德仪赚了一票钱，或发现发不了财，就立刻去别处找机会了。我从史丹佛回德仪时，就发现以前许多高职位的人已走了。

<p class="en-para">Becoming GM of the Integrated Circuit Division —</p>
<p class="en-para">The most important "business unit" at TI in the 1960s and 1970s was unquestionably the "Integrated Circuit Division." TI and Fairchild Semiconductor had jointly invented the integrated circuit. A decade later, the IC had become a critical business for every semiconductor company, and it continued to grow at breakneck speed. In 1966, although the TI Integrated Circuit Division GM did not yet carry the VP title, the position was already regarded as one of the most important in the company.</p>
<p class="en-para">Who was TI's Integrated Circuit GM in 1966? It was the same engineer I mentioned in Chapter 2 — someone who had been my subordinate when I first joined TI. His name was Strat. Extremely clever, fiercely ambitious and aggressive, and skilled at manipulating others — a corporate politician through and through. He had joined TI at almost the same time I had. He started as one of two engineers under me. A year later when I was promoted one level, I promoted him one level too. Then I left for Stanford for two and a half years. In those two and a half years, Strat was promoted three full levels! When I returned from Stanford, he was already GM of the Silicon Division. While I was GM of the Germanium Division, he was transferred again — this time to GM of the Integrated Circuit Division.</p>
<p class="en-para">In the 1950s and 1960s — the early days of semiconductors — TI indeed had many "Soldiers of Fortune." Many of these "warriors" held advanced degrees, had high ability, and possessed fierce ambition. Unfortunately, their ambition was not to build a great enterprise together with others — it was simply to make a fortune for themselves. When they had made their killing at TI, or discovered they could not get rich there, they immediately went elsewhere for opportunities. When I returned to TI from Stanford, I found that many of the people who had held high positions were gone.</p>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Soldiers of Fortune (猎富武士)**
**中文解释**：猎富武士，指以个人财富积累为主要目标的职业经理人；在矽谷早期，这类人物极为常见——他们频繁跳槽、创业、上市、套现，构成了矽谷生态的「游牧民族」。
**商业造句">The Soldiers of Fortune who populated Fairchild and TI in the 1960s were not disloyal — they were simply following a different career script than the organizational man.*

2. **Corporate politician**
**中文解释**：公司政客，指善于在组织内部运用政治手段（而非绩效）获取晋升的人；在大型企业中，「公司政客」与「业务高手」的并存是一种常见但危险的组织现象。
**商业造句">The board promoted the corporate politician over the stronger operator — and spent the next three years cleaning up the customer relationships he had neglected during his ascent.*

3. **Breakneck speed**
**中文解释**：急速、极快速度；在半导体产业叙事中，这个词常用来描写技术迭代或市场扩张的节奏。
**商业造句">The integrated circuit market was growing at breakneck speed — doubling every eighteen months while the incumbents were still debating whether it was a "real" business.*

### 修辞赏析

<span class="rhetoric-note">
<span class="zh">「史厥特」这个名字在张忠谋的笔下是一个精心塑造的反面角色。张忠谋用了三个平行的形容词来描述他：「甚聪明、有强烈的企图心及进取心、又善于操纵他人」——每个词单独看都是褒义词，但组合在一起，却构成了一个令人不寒而栗的「高效自私人」画像。张忠谋的高明之处在于：他没有把史厥特写成坏人，而是把他写成了一类人——「猎富武士」的代表。这种不带恶意的精准刻画，比任何道德批判都更有力。</span>
<span class="en">"Strat" is a carefully crafted foil in Chang's narrative. Chang uses three parallel adjectives: "extremely clever, fiercely ambitious and aggressive, and skilled at manipulating others." Each word alone is positive, but together they compose a chilling portrait of an "efficient selfish person." Chang's brilliance is this: he does not write Strat as a villain. He writes him as a type — the representative of the "Soldiers of Fortune." This precise characterization without malice carries more force than any moral condemnation could.</span>

### 背景知识延伸

**「猎富武士」时代**：一九五〇至六〇年代的半导体产业，「猎富武士」是一种普遍现象。这些人聪明、有能力、有野心——但他们的野心不是建立一个伟大的企业，而是为自己赚一笔钱。一旦赚到（或发现赚不到），他们就走人。张忠谋在自传中说：「我从史丹佛回德仪时，就发现以前许多高职位的人已走了。」这种高流动性是矽谷早期生态的标志性特征——它既是创新的催化剂（人才不断流入新公司），也是组织稳定性的毒药（没有人愿意为长期目标做承诺）。张忠谋自己也将在十年后成为另一家公司的「创始人」——但他不是「猎富武士」，而是一个「建设者」。这个区别，或许就是他与其他人的根本分际。
