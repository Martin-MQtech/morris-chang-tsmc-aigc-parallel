## 微处理器的多头马车

七〇年代中期，半导体业出现了另一个划时代的产品——微处理器（Microprocessor）。英特尔在一九七一年发明了微处理器，但直到七〇年代中期，它的应用潜力才真正显现。

德仪也必须发展微处理器。但与英特尔不同，德仪的微处理器面临一个独特的难题：多头马车。

英特尔的微处理器完全对外销售。它的唯一使命就是满足外部客户。德仪的微处理器却要同时满足多个内部客户：国防系统要用、电脑部门要用、消费者产品部门要用。每个内部客户都有不同的需求，不同的性能规格，不同的交货时程。我们像一个马同时被几辆马车拖着跑——每一辆都往不同的方向出力。

更糟的是，内部客户优先的思维让德仪的微处理器团队在满足外部客户时总是慢半拍。外部客户需要快速交货、标准规格，但我们的团队首先得应付内部那些不断修改规格的需求。

英特尔微处理器和软体——微软的作业系统，也成为此后个人电脑的「技术标准」；英特尔（和微软）的命运也随波而起。相形之下，德仪渐渐失色。

德仪微处理器不是有内部客户吗？他们到哪里去了？的确，国防系统用了少量；电脑部门决定不用半导体部门的微处理器；倒是消费者产品部门决定用半导体部门的微处理器来开发「家庭电脑」。

「家庭电脑」在一九七九年问世，起初定价太高，销路不佳，搞新设计；一九八一年再问世，这次定价奇低，销路很好，但却是卖一个亏一个；一九八三年竞争加剧，价格更低，亏本更严重，撑到一九八四年，家庭电脑的亏损已经到了要拖垮全公司地步。终于，夏伯特和彪希决定完全退出家庭电脑市场。

没有外部客户、只有内部家庭电脑这一个大客户的德仪微处理器，也与德仪家庭电脑同归于尽。

<p class="en-para"><b>The Microprocessor's Multi-Head Horse</b></p>
<p class="en-para">By the mid-1970s, the semiconductor industry witnessed another epochal product — the microprocessor. Intel invented the microprocessor in 1971, but it wasn't until the mid-1970s that its application potential truly emerged.</p>
<p class="en-para">TI, too, had to develop microprocessors. But unlike Intel, TI's microprocessor faced a unique dilemma: the multi-head horse.</p>
<p class="en-para">Intel's microprocessors were sold entirely externally. Its sole mission was to serve outside customers. TI's microprocessors, however, had to simultaneously serve multiple internal customers: defense systems needed them, the computer department needed them, the consumer products department needed them. Each internal customer had different requirements, different performance specifications, different delivery schedules. We were like a single horse being pulled by several carriages — each pulling in a different direction.</p>
<p class="en-para">Worse, the internal-customer-first mindset caused TI's microprocessor team to always lag when serving external customers. External customers needed rapid delivery and standard specifications, but our team first had to deal with internal demands that kept changing specifications.</p>
<p class="en-para">Intel's microprocessors and Microsoft's operating system became the "technical standard" for personal computers. Intel's (and Microsoft's) fortunes soared. By contrast, TI gradually faded.</p>
<p class="en-para">Didn't TI's microprocessors have internal customers? Where did they go? Indeed, defense systems used a small quantity; the computer department decided not to use the Semiconductor Division's microprocessors. It was the Consumer Products Department that decided to use them to develop "home computers."</p>
<p class="en-para">The "home computer" launched in 1979. The initial price was too high; sales were poor. A new design followed. It re-launched in 1981, this time at an amazingly low price. Sales were strong — but every unit sold lost money. By 1983, competition intensified, prices dropped further, losses mounted. In 1984, home computer losses reached the point of threatening to drag down the entire company. Finally, Shepherd and Shep decided to exit the home computer market entirely.</p>
<p class="en-para">TI's microprocessors, which had no external customers and only the internal home computer as a major customer, perished alongside TI's home computers.</p>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Multi-head horse (多头马车)**
**中文解释**：指一个团队或资源被多个利益主体同时拉向不同方向的管理困境；在组织理论中，「多头马车」是内部客户优先策略的必然结果——当单一资源要服务多个目标时，所有目标最终都得不到最优服务。
**商业造句">The multi-head horse problem destroyed TI's microprocessor competitiveness — every internal customer pulled in a different direction, and the result was that none of them won.*

2. **Wintel standard (英特尔-微软技术标准的崛起)**
**中文解释**：指英特尔微处理器与微软作业系统共同构成的个人电脑产业标准；「Wintel」标准是一个典型的平台垄断案例——它让後进者只能在相容与替代之间做困难的选择。
**商业造句">The Wintel duopoly created a technical standard so powerful that any semiconductor company without a sprawling internal customer base — unlike TI — could compete on a relatively level playing field.*
