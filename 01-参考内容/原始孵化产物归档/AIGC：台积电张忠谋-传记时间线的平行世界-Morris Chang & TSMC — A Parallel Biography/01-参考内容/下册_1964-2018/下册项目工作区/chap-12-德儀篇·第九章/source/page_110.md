这是五十几年前的事。现在诺伊斯早已过世，但摩尔健在。二〇一八年《福布斯》杂志的财富调查：摩尔的财富八十五亿美元，起源就是那12.25%英特尔股票。

诺伊斯、摩尔创办英特尔后，雇进的第一号员工就是在快捷追随摩尔的安迪·葛洛夫。以后的三十几年，诺伊斯、摩尔和葛洛夫相继为英特尔的总裁和董事长。这三人组合是一个黄金组合，三人均有杰出的技术背景，诺伊斯又是一个富有魅力的领导者，也是精明的商人；摩尔有敏锐观察力（摩尔定律就是他观察力的明证），也是脚踏实地的领导者。葛洛夫与摩尔共事最久，与摩尔有互补功能。摩尔比较温和，而葛洛夫是严格的纪律执行者。

我接掌德仪半导体集团时（一九七二年），英特尔已是强劲的德仪竞争对手。以后六年（一九七二至一九七八年），我任半导体集团总经理的主要竞争对手，就是英特尔。

<p class="en-para">This was over fifty years ago. Noyce has long since passed away, but Moore was still alive at the time of writing. Forbes' 2018 wealth survey estimated Moore's fortune at $8.5 billion — originating from that 12.25% Intel stake.</p>
<p class="en-para">After Noyce and Moore founded Intel, the first employee they hired was Andy Grove, who had followed Moore at Fairchild. For the next thirty-plus years, Noyce, Moore, and Grove successively served as Intel's President and Chairman. This trio was a golden combination — all three had outstanding technical backgrounds. Noyce was also a charismatic leader and shrewd businessman. Moore had keen observational powers (Moore's Law being the clearest evidence) and was a down-to-earth leader. Grove had worked with Moore the longest and served as his complement: Moore was relatively gentle, while Grove was a strict enforcer of discipline.</p>
<p class="en-para">When I took over TI's Semiconductor Group in 1972, Intel was already a formidable competitor. For the next six years (1972–1978), Intel would be my primary rival as Semiconductor Group GM.</p>

### 德仪的人才问题

面对以技术取胜的强劲竞争对手，我最大的挑战就是要找技术人才。现在先回忆一下当时（一九七二年）德仪的人才情况。当我初加入德仪时（一九五八年），德仪员工几乎都是德州人，我和基比是极少数的「外省人」（我来自麻省，基比来自俄克拉荷马州），我们也因此建立了一层「外省情谊」；在接受外省、甚至外国人这点上，德仪在一九五八至一九七二这十几年内，有很大的进步，德仪已是世界级的公司，吸引全世界的人才。

一九七二年，德仪半导体绝大部分员工都在达拉斯、休士顿两城居住。这两个城市都相当适宜技术人才居住，也不缺基层技术人才的来源——德州大学奥斯汀分校是相当优秀的学校，每年供应足够学士和硕士毕业生。德仪缺的是高阶研发人才。这个缺口有它的历史渊源：如第六章所述，一直到一九六八年，MOS还不被德仪重视，德仪重起MOS炉灶，又被那一年全部MOS研发人员出走打乱，此后比较容易被计算器IC吸引，而忽略了MOS，直到一九七一年，MOS集成电路还只做到记忆体上。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Facing a formidable competitor that won through technology, my biggest challenge was recruiting technical talent. Let me recall the talent situation at the time (1972). When I first joined TI in 1958, nearly all TI employees were Texans. Kilby and I were among the few "out-of-staters" (I was from Massachusetts, Kilby from Oklahoma), and we forged an "out-of-state bond" because of it. In terms of accepting out-of-staters and even foreigners, TI had made enormous progress over the next fourteen years. TI had become a world-class company attracting talent from around the globe.</p>
<p class="en-para">In 1972, the vast majority of TI Semiconductor employees lived in Dallas and Houston. Both cities were quite suitable for technical talent and had no shortage of entry-level technical talent — the University of Texas at Austin was an excellent school supplying ample bachelor's and master's graduates each year. What TI lacked was high-level R&D talent. This gap had its historical roots: as described in Chapter 6, MOS was not valued at TI until 1968. TI had to rebuild its MOS effort from scratch, only to have the entire MOS R&D team quit that year. After that, the relatively easier calculator IC work drew attention while MOS was neglected. By 1971, MOS integrated circuits were still only being applied to memory.</p>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Golden combination (黄金组合)**
**中文解释**：指三位创始人（诺伊斯、摩尔、葛洛夫）在技术远见、商业敏锐和组织纪律上的完美互补；在创业研究中，「三人黄金组合」是英特尔早期成功的关键——它证明了一个好的创始团队需要的不只是技术，还需要领导力和执行纪律的平衡。
**商业造句">Intel's golden combination — Noyce the visionary, Moore the scientist, Grove the disciplinarian — is one of the most studied founding trios in business history because each member compensated for the others' blind spots.*

2. **High-level R&D talent gap (高阶研发人才缺口)**
**中文解释**：指企业在高端技术人才供给上的结构性短缺；在半导体产业中，「高阶研发人才」的争夺往往决定技术路线的成败——谁拥有最好的MOS工程师，谁就拥有下一代产品的入场券。
**商业造句">TI's high-level R&D talent gap in MOS technology was not merely a recruiting problem — it was a strategic vulnerability that Intel exploited to leapfrog the industry leader.*
