## 拉吧厂

加深德仪在寻觅人才上的地理劣劣势及没有股票承购权劣势的，是彪希在七〇年代中期的决定：命令半导体集团在拉吧城（Lubbock）建尖端技术晶圆厂。

拉吧是位在德州西部、距达拉斯约五百公里的小城市（一九七五年人口约十五万），与达拉斯有直接航线，飞行时间约一小时，所以交通尚称便利，但绝对不在达拉斯「一日生活圈」内。对住惯大城市（达拉斯、休士顿、硅谷）的技术人才来说，拉吧绝对是他们不想去住的穷乡僻野。

那么，为什么彪希要在那里建晶圆厂呢？

原来彪希是在离拉吧不远的乡下棉农家庭出生，对少年彪希，拉吧是一个繁华城市，后来他上拉吧的「德州技术学院」，在那边获得学士、硕士学位，当然与拉吧情感更深。

少年对一个地方的倾慕，往往成为一生回忆的资产。如我在小学时倾慕香港为「世外桃源」，在高中时倾慕上海为「东方巴黎」，尽管我后来到过许多比香港更美丽、安详，比上海更繁华的地方，但是每当我想起少年的香港和上海时，我仍有美好的缅怀。

不过，我没有要德仪去上海或香港设厂。

彪希却不同了。当他接执行副总裁时（一九七二年，与我接半导体总经理职同时），他就开始在拉吧建厂，命令新成立的消费者集团搬去，以拉吧为他们的总部。几年后，彪希又命令半导体集团在拉吧建高科技MOS晶圆厂。

<p class="en-para"><b>The Lubbock Fab</b></p>
<p class="en-para">Compounding TI's geographic disadvantage in recruiting talent and its disadvantage of lacking stock options, Shep made a mid-1970s decision: he ordered the Semiconductor Group to build an advanced-technology wafer fab in Lubbock.</p>
<p class="en-para">Lubbock is a small city in western Texas, about 500 kilometers from Dallas (population roughly 150,000 in 1975). It had direct flights to Dallas, about an hour's flight, so transportation was reasonably convenient — but it was certainly not within Dallas's "daily living circle." For technical talent accustomed to living in major cities (Dallas, Houston, Silicon Valley), Lubbock was absolutely a backwater they had no desire to live in.</p>
<p class="en-para">So why did Shep want to build a fab there?</p>
<p class="en-para">It turned out Shep had been born to a cotton-farming family in the countryside not far from Lubbock. To the young Shep, Lubbock had been a metropolis. He later attended "Texas Tech" in Lubbock, earning both his bachelor's and master's degrees there. Naturally, he had deep emotional ties to Lubbock.</p>
<p class="en-para">Adolescent admiration for a place often becomes a lifelong cherished memory. Just as I had admired Hong Kong as a "paradise" in elementary school and Shanghai as the "Paris of the East" in high school — though I later visited places far more beautiful and serene than Hong Kong, far more prosperous than Shanghai — whenever I think of the Hong Kong and Shanghai of my youth, I still feel a fond nostalgia.</p>
<p class="en-para">However, I never asked TI to build a plant in Shanghai or Hong Kong.</p>
<p class="en-para">Shep was different. When he became Executive Vice President (1972, the same year I took the Semiconductor GM role), he began building in Lubbock and ordered the newly formed Consumer Products Group to relocate there, making Lubbock their headquarters. Several years later, Shep ordered the Semiconductor Group to build a high-tech MOS fab in Lubbock.</p>

### 修辞赏析

<span class="rhetoric-note">
<span class="zh">「少年对一个地方的倾慕，往往成为一生回忆的资产」——张忠谋在此处罕见地做了一个私人比喻：少年香港、少年上海、少年拉吧，三个人的三个少年倾慕，三种迥异的结局。他用自己对标彪希——两人都是「少年倾慕」型的怀旧者——但做了一个关键区分：「不过，我没有要德仪去上海或香港设厂。」这一句话是整段叙事的重心，它用一种极其节制的幽默，揭穿了彪希以个人经历替代商业判断的荒谬。</span>
<span class="en">"Adolescent admiration for a place often becomes a lifelong cherished memory." Chang rarelly deploys a personal analogy here: youthful admiration for Hong Kong, for Shanghai, for Lubbock — three people's three youthful infatuations, three vastly different outcomes. He draws a parallel with himself and Shep — both are "adolescent admiration" types — but makes a critical distinction: "However, I never asked TI to build a plant in Shanghai or Hong Kong." This single sentence is the narrative anchor of the entire passage. With exquisite understated humor, it exposes the absurdity of Shep substituting personal nostalgia for business judgment.</span>
</span>
