在我任半导体集团总经理的六年（一九七二至七八年），我与夏伯特、彪希在「生产设备由谁开发」的问题上不断冲突。

夏伯特每次见到我，都会问：「你自己不开发生产设备，怎么能胜过竞争者？」

我的回答总是：「生产设备。」

但是在一九七〇年代，夏伯特已不懂设计和技术，所以在「生产设备」这一项上，我全盘皆输。最大的输是整条「光阻」线——这是MOS IC制程中非常重要的一部分，价格相当昂贵。日本供应商可以供应此生产线，我力争，我认为应该让享有广大市场的设备供应商负责。但我输了！不但没有自由在外面买设备，反而被夏伯特和彪希赏上「顽固」、「不够灵活」的污名。

这次输局的唯一慰藉来自我卸任半导体总经理后的一九八〇年。德仪日本美浦厂开工，忽然消息传到总部：美浦厂的良率远高于德克萨斯厂。夏伯特下令调查两厂的差异。调查工程师、领班、设备工程师的结论是：日本厂的光阻线设备是在市场上买现成的，德克萨斯厂用的却是德仪自己开发的。这并不证明市场现成的比自己开的好，但至少证明市场现成的也可以达到高良率。经过这事件，德仪自制生产设备的计划寿终正寝。七、八年前我的「力争」不但没用，而且伤害了自己。

<p class="en-para">During my six years as Semiconductor Group GM (1972–1978), I clashed repeatedly with Shepherd and Shep over the question of "who should develop production equipment."</p>

I fought hard, arguing that equipment suppliers serving the broad market should handle it. But I lost! Not only was I denied the freedom to buy equipment externally — I was even labeled by Shepherd and Shep as "stubborn" and "inflexible."</p>
<p class="en-para">The only consolation from this defeat came after I stepped down as Semiconductor GM, in 1980. When TI's Miho, Japan fab began operations, news suddenly reached headquarters: Miho's yields were far higher than the Texas fab's. Shepherd ordered an investigation into the differences between the two fabs. The investigating engineers, supervisors, and equipment engineers concluded: the Japanese fab's photoresist line equipment was purchased off the market, while the Texas fab used TI's self-developed equipment. This didn't prove that off-market equipment was better than self-developed — but it at least proved that off-market equipment could achieve high yields. After this episode, TI's self-developed production equipment program was laid to rest. My "fought hard" from seven or eight years earlier had not only been useless — it had actually hurt me.</p>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Photoresist line (光阻线)**
**中文解释**：指半导体制造中用于光刻（photolithography）的光阻涂布、显影、蚀刻等关键制程设备；在MOS IC制造中，光阻线是决定良率和制程精度的核心——它的品质直接影响芯片的电气性能。
**商业造句">The photoresist line was the most technically demanding and expensive part of the MOS IC process — and the battleground where TI's self-development dogma collided with market reality.*

2. **Yield gap as verdict (良率差距作为最终判决)**
**中文解释**：指以实际生产良率数据来验证设备策略的成败；在半导体制造中，良率是最硬的指标——它不认组织政治，只认工程事实。美浦厂与德克萨斯厂的良率对比，成为压垮「自研派」的最后一根稻草。
**商业造句">The yield gap between Miho and Texas was the verdict that no amount of internal politics could overturn — when the numbers spoke, the self-development myth died.*
