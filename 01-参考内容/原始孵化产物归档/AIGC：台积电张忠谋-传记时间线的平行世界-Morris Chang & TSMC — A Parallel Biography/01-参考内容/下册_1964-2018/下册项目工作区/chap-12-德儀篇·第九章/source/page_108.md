# 德仪篇·第九章｜半导体集团总经理的奋斗及挣扎

一九六八年，在我任德仪集成电路总经理时，创业投资人阿瑟·洛克（Arthur Rock）邀请诺伊斯（Robert Noyce）和摩尔（Gordon Moore）创办英特尔。这是当年科技界的大新闻。

我自五〇年代后期就认识诺伊斯和摩尔，那时我每年都参加十月在华盛顿召开的「国际电子元件会议」（International Electron Devices Meeting, IEDM），诺伊斯与摩尔都在快捷半导体任职，诺伊斯是总经理，摩尔是他的研发副总，他们两人每年轮流参加此会，我与他们每次在会议中见面都有愉快的交谈，有时还与别的会友一起出去喝酒、吃饭、在深秋夜晚唱歌回旅馆。那时我们都年轻，二十几岁，自以为已投入了一个前途无限的行业，自己也变成前途无限了；年轻人在他们的憧憬和希望里，享受无穷的乐趣。

<p class="en-para">In 1968, when I was General Manager of TI's Integrated Circuits Division, venture capitalist Arthur Rock invited Robert Noyce and Gordon Moore to found Intel. This was major news in the technology world that year.</p>
<p class="en-para">I had known Noyce and Moore since the late 1950s. Back then, I attended the International Electron Devices Meeting (IEDM) held every October in Washington, D.C. Noyce and Moore were both at Fairchild Semiconductor — Noyce as General Manager, Moore as his VP of R&D. They alternated attending the meeting each year, and whenever we met, we had delightful conversations. Sometimes we would go out for drinks and dinner with other conference friends and sing our way back to the hotel on late autumn nights. We were all young then, in our late twenties, convinced we had entered a limitless industry and that we ourselves had limitless futures. Young people revel in their dreams and hopes, enjoying boundless joy.</p>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Integrated Circuits Division (集成电路部门)**
**中文解释**：集成电路（IC）部门是德仪的核心业务单位，负责设计、制造和销售集成电路芯片；在半导体的商业版图中，「部门」是战略执行的基本单元——它的总经理既是技术决策者，也是利润责任人。
**商业造句">As GM of the Integrated Circuits Division, Chang was responsible not only for technical roadmap decisions but also for the P&L of one of TI's most strategic business units.*

2. **Venture capitalist (创业投资人)**
**中文解释**：创业投资人是向新创公司提供资金以换取股权的投资者；阿瑟·洛克是矽谷早期最重要的创投家之一——他先后投资了英特尔、苹果、科学数据系统（SDS）等，被称为「矽谷创投之父」。
**商业造句">Arthur Rock, the venture capitalist who assembled the Intel founding team, is often called the father of Silicon Valley venture capital — his investments in Intel, Apple, and Teledyne reshaped the technology landscape.*
