## The '90s Performance

TSMC's performance from 1991 to 2000 is shown in the table below.

| Compound Annual | 1991 | 1992 | 1993 | 1994 | 1995 | 1996 | 1997 | 1998 | 1999 | 2000 | |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---| | Revenue (NT$ B) | 4.48 | 6.51 | 12.33 | 19.34 | 28.77 | 39.40 | 43.92 | 50.42 | 73.07 | 166.20 | | Net Operating Profit | 0.71 | 1.24 | 4.42 | 8.62 | 13.90 | 18.24 | 14.93 | 13.81 | 24.83 | 61.30 |

Revenue compound annual growth rate (CAGR) for this decade was 49.4%; net operating profit CAGR was 64.1%. 1996-1998 were three years of global semiconductor downturn: the global semiconductor industry shrank 8% in 1996, grew only 4% in 1997, and shrank 8% again in 1998. But even during these three recession years, TSMC's performance was still decent.

Once revenue reached $1 billion, the venture could be considered successful. By this standard, TSMC's 1995 revenue of $1.1 billion (at an exchange rate of 1 USD to 26 NT) could also be considered a successful venture.

设计（无晶圆厂）公司风起云涌

台积电成立后头两年（一九八七至八八年）的客户几乎全是台湾的小型设计公司。一九八九年起，先有国外大型IDM（自有晶圆厂公司），如英特尔、摩托罗拉、德仪、飞利浦等，将他们工厂内比较不具经济价值的产品，委托给我们生产，借以提升这些产品的营业利益。但自一九九一年起，美国设计公司风起云涌地成立。

根据无晶圆厂半导体公司联盟（FSA）在二零零七年给我的资料，在一九九一年中，全球无晶圆厂公司自两百零七家增加至七百五十家；它们的总营收也自一九九三年的二十四亿美元，增加至二零零零年的一百七十亿美元。根据台积电的经验，无晶圆厂一百亿美元大约需要三十四亿美元晶圆代工。而台积电二零零零年营收为一百三十二亿美元，其中约四成来自IDM，即"自有晶圆厂公司"。那幺，另外六十成（三十二亿美元）来自"无晶圆厂公司"。这幺说来，台积电难道把所有"无晶圆厂公司"的生意都接下来了？我想不见得，FSA可能低估了二零零零年"无晶圆厂公司"的营收。但是，有一点我相信：台积电很早就是"无晶圆厂半导体公司"的首选代工者。

ReadShift 双语翻译

In the first two years after TSMC's founding (1987-88), customers were almost entirely small Taiwanese design houses. From 1989 onward, established foreign IDMs (Integrated Device Manufacturers with their own fabs)—Intel, Motorola, TI, Philips, etc.—began outsourcing less economically viable products from their fabs to us to improve the profitability of those products. But from 1991 onward, American design companies sprang up like a tidal wave.

According to the Fabless Semiconductor Alliance (FSA), which provided me data in 2007, in 1991 the number of fabless companies worldwide grew from 207 to 750; their total revenue also grew from $2.4 billion in 1993 to $17 billion in 2000. Based on TSMC's experience, $10 billion of fabless revenue requires about $3.4 billion of wafer foundry services. TSMC's 2000 revenue was $13.2 billion, of which about 40% came from IDMs. The remaining 60% ($8 billion) came from fabless companies. Did TSMC really take all the business of every fabless company? I think not—FSA may have underestimated fabless company revenue in 2000. But one thing I believe: TSMC was very early on the foundry of choice for fabless semiconductor companies.

IDM转向台积电

我也以为：一旦IDM（自有晶圆厂）公司试用台积电的晶圆代工，他们也会逐渐停止他们自己的晶圆制造，而改用台积电。为什幺"自有晶圆厂公司"转向台积电是一个逐渐、而不是很快的过程？第一，"自有晶圆厂公司"有许多制造部员工，他们不会一下子把制造部员工都遣散；第二，"自有晶圆厂公司"也有不少尚未完全折旧，以及即使已完全折旧，仍有剩余价值的生产设备，这些设备也不会被当作废铜烂铁卖掉。

ReadShift 双语翻译

I also believed: once IDMs (integrated device manufacturers) tried TSMC's foundry services, they would gradually cease their own wafer manufacturing and switch to TSMC. Why was the shift of IDMs toward TSMC a gradual rather than rapid process? First, IDMs had many manufacturing department employees; they wouldn't lay them off all at once. Second, IDMs also had considerable production equipment that was not fully depreciated, or even if fully depreciated still had residual value. This equipment wouldn't be sold as scrap iron either.

"服务导向"策略的成功

为什幺台积电成为"晶圆代工"的首选？因为我们"专业晶圆代工"商业模式和我们"服务导向"策略完全成功了！在台积电成立之前，"晶圆代工"是许多美国、日本、台湾IDM公司的副业，"副业"的客户不受重视，甚至还遭受委托的"代工"者竞争。台积电的商业模式是"专业晶圆代工"——完全"服务导向"、"为客户赴汤蹈火"。台积电的技术长处是高良率——台积电的一切所为，完全迎合客户的需求。

ReadShift 双语翻译

Why did TSMC become the foundry of choice? Because our "dedicated foundry" business model and our "service-oriented" strategy completely succeeded! Before TSMC's founding, "wafer foundry" was a sideline for many American, Japanese, and Taiwanese IDMs. As a "sideline," customers were not valued and even suffered competition from the very company they outsourced to. TSMC's business model was "dedicated foundry"—thoroughly "service-oriented," "going through fire and water for customers." TSMC's technological strength was high yield—everything TSMC did was completely oriented toward meeting customer needs.

行销美国成功

除了创新的商业模式外，初期台积电另一个重要策略是行销美国。用美国总经理、美国行销人员，加上我自己对美国客户的熟识，台积电来自美国客户的营收自一九八七年（成立年）的零美元，到一九九一年的八千七百万美元（占全部营收53%），到一九九八年的八亿六千万美元（占全部营收57%）。自二零零零年后，台积电来自美国的营收大概都在全部营收六成以上。这与美国蓬勃发展的设计公司有关。

ReadShift 双语翻译

Beyond the innovative business model, another key early TSMC strategy was marketing in the U.S. Using an American general manager, American marketing personnel, plus my own familiarity with American customers, TSMC's revenue from American customers grew from zero in 1987 (the founding year) to $87 million in 1991 (53% of total revenue), to $860 million in 1998 (57% of total revenue). From 2000 onward, TSMC's U.S.-origin revenue has been roughly above 60% of total revenue. This correlates with the flourishing design company ecosystem in the U.S.

日本市场的衰退

相反的是日本市场。日本半导体业在八零年代非常强劲，但后来渐渐衰退，我认为这与日本简直没有IC设计公司有关。记得二零零七年春，我受邀到日本福冈的一个电子业会议致词，在半导体业者座谈会内，业者对我的致词完全没有理会，反而频频要日本政府资助他们建晶圆厂。我对此点提出此已在时不晚矣，但日本半导体业者尚抱守老旧观念。

在我孤独飞返台湾途中，我想：从今以后日本半导体业大概也就不过如此了。

ReadShift 双语翻译

The Japanese market told the opposite story. Japan's semiconductor industry was very strong during the 1980s, but later gradually declined. I believe this was related to the fact that Japan had virtually no IC design companies. I recall that in the spring of 2007, I was invited to give a speech at an electronics industry conference in Fukuoka, Japan. At the semiconductor roundtable, the industry participants completely ignored my speech and instead repeatedly asked the Japanese government to subsidize them in building wafer fabs. I pointed out that it was already too late, but the Japanese semiconductor industry still clung to outdated notions.

On my lonely flight back to Taiwan, I thought: from now on, the Japanese semiconductor industry would probably be just that—ordinary.
