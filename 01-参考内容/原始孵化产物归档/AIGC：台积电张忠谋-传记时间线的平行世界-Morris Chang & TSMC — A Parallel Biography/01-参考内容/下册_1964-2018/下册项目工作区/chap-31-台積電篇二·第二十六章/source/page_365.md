台积电的客户数，自八七年成立时的十五户，到九七年我自兼总经理时，已增加到一百九十九户。二○一八年我退休时又增加到四百八十户。但在三十年内，占公司营收百分之七十至七十五的客户，通常只有十五户。我给自己的任务，就是好好照顾这十五家客户。更重要的是，随时展开与有潜力的新客户合作——因为任何时间的「十五大」总有几户的成长率不如我们，我们需要与高成长者一起成长。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">TSMC's client count grew from fifteen at its founding in 1987 to 199 when I assumed the general manager role in 1997, and further to 480 by the time I retired in 2018. Yet throughout those thirty years, the clients accounting for 70 to 75 percent of revenue typically numbered only fifteen. My self-imposed task was to take good care of those fifteen clients. More importantly, I was always seeking partnerships with promising new clients—for at any given time, several of the "Top Fifteen" would grow more slowly than we did, and we needed to grow alongside the high-growth companies.</p>
</div>

在九○年代，「十五大」的变动相当快。举例来说，九七年的「十五大」，只有五户还在二○○○年的「十五大」内。失去的十户，有几家倒闭，有几家自己的业务衰退，被台积电更成功的客户替代。

<p class="en-para">In the 1990s, the "Top Fifteen" turned over quite rapidly. For example, of the Top Fifteen in 1997, only five remained in the Top Fifteen by 2000. Of the ten that fell away, some went out of business, others saw their own businesses decline, and were replaced by TSMC's more successful clients.</p>

二○○○年后，「十五大」的变动比较少。二○○○年的「十五大」，有九家还在二○一八年「十五大」内。

The CEOs of these clients also frequently visited Taiwan and often sought me out. Although these exchanges were predominantly serious business discussions, the vast majority were conducted on a foundation of established friendship.</p>
