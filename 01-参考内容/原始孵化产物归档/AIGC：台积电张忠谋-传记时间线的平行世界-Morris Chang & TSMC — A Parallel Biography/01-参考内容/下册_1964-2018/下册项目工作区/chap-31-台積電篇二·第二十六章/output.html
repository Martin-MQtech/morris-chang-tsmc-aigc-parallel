张忠谋

台积电篇二·第二十六章｜九○年代建立的几个重要客户

九十年代的台积电，已从初创期的蹒跚学步成长为全球晶圆代工产业的领头羊。这十年间，我们建立并巩固了一批至今仍在合作的核心客户关系。这些客户中，既有老牌巨头英特尔，也有出生于九十年代的新锐设计公司和专攻通讯芯片的博通。他们的交织与更替，折射出半导体产业从IDM（整合元件制造）模式向晶圆代工模式转移的历史洪流。

ReadShift 双语翻译

By the 1990s, TSMC had grown from a fledgling operation into the global leader in dedicated foundry services. During that decade, we forged and deepened relationships with a core set of clients—ranging from the venerable Intel to upstart fabless design houses and the communications-focused Broadcom—that remain with us to this day. Their interplay and turnover mirrored the broader structural shift in the semiconductor industry away from the IDM model toward the foundry paradigm.

然而，在回顾这些商业成就之前，必须先讲述一个关乎公司治理与个人体面离场的故事——第三任总经理布鲁克的「净离」。

ReadShift 双语翻译

But before recounting these commercial triumphs, a story of corporate governance and dignified departure must be told: Brook's "clean break" from TSMC.

布鲁克「净离」台积电，我自兼总经理

布鲁克（Brook）是台积电第三任总经理，曾在德仪做了我十几年的得力下属。一九九一年三月加入台积电，起初他并不怎幺看好这家公司，对我说：「做一年就回美国。」一年后觉得台积电比他想象的好，便说：「再做一年吧。」再一年后就开始有较长的打算，但仍说：「不会超过两、三年。」

ReadShift 双语翻译

Brook, TSMC's third general manager, had been my trusted lieutenant at Texas Instruments for over a decade. He joined TSMC in March 1991, initially skeptical of the venture's prospects. "I'll do one year, then go back to the U.S.," he declared. Impressed by what he saw after the first year, he extended his stay—only to extend it again. By the third year, he began contemplating a longer horizon, but still insisted, "Two or three years at most."

我雇用布鲁克，主因是借重他在欧美市场营销的技能。在这方面，他确实没有令我失望。至于台湾的经营层面，我倒不很需要布鲁克。第一，我们的台湾团队在九○年代中期已经相当坚强。曾繁城自工研院带过来的核心团队一直在成长，我们也雇了许多优秀人才，包括魏力行、刘德音、林坤禧等；第二，布鲁克本人并未赢得本土团队的尊敬和信任。倒是我自己不遗余力地让制造部门配合营销——数度否决布鲁克要求雇美国人担任营运副总，以及不让曾繁城辞职而让他去世界先进任总经理，就是明证。

I had hired Brook primarily for his marketing expertise in the American and European markets. On this front, he did not disappoint. As for managing operations in Taiwan, I genuinely needed less from him. Our local team had grown formidable by the mid-1990s. The core group led by Zeng Fan-cheng from ITRI had flourished, and we had recruited outstanding talents like Wei Li-hsing, Liu De-yin, and Robert Lin. More crucially, Brook himself had not earned the deep respect and trust of the local team—a reality reflected in my repeated veto of his proposals to hire Americans as COO, and in my orchestrating an alternative role for Zeng Fan-cheng at Vanguard rather than letting him resign.

到了九六年底，布鲁克已经做了六年，这次他真的想回美国了。但他仍想留在台积电，做什幺呢？起初他认为他可以住在美国，仍做总经理。我认为这是一个不可思议的建议，立刻否决了。经过两三次会议后，我答应让他管美国营销以及WaferTech，直接对我报告——那时WaferTech刚成立不久，大家还对它寄予厚望。布鲁克似乎也接受这个任务，那我们也就有了一个留住布鲁克的方案了。

By the end of 1996, Brook had been with us for six years, and this time he genuinely wanted to return to the United States. Yet he still wished to stay at TSMC—in what capacity? Initially, he proposed living in the U.S. while remaining general manager. I considered this an untenable suggestion and vetoed it immediately. After two or three meetings, I agreed to let him oversee U.S. marketing and WaferTech, reporting directly to me. WaferTech had been established only recently, and everyone still harbored high hopes for it. Brook seemed to accept this assignment, and we appeared to have a plan to retain him.

但是不然。布鲁克把他真正要的东西留到最后：「我要保留我的董事席。」他说。

But that was not how it ended. Brook reserved his true demand for last: "I want to keep my board seat," he said.

这，我不能给他。台积电自始就是一个专业经理人经营的公司，我自己的股权非常微小。在九七年，两位大股东（行政院开发基金及飞利浦）占董事会十席董事的七席，他们同意给一席给我，一席给总经理，还有一席留给其他原始股东的代表。现在布鲁克不做总经理了，我的计划是自兼一阵子后让曾繁城接任总经理。如果让布鲁克保留他的董事席，岂不造成一个奇怪的局面：新的总经理不是董事，而只管美国营销及美国厂的经理反倒是董事？

ReadShift 双语翻译

That, I could not give him. TSMC had always been a professionally managed company, and my own equity stake was minuscule. In 1997, the two largest shareholders—the Administrative Yuan Development Fund and Philips—held seven of ten board seats. They had agreed to allocate one seat to me, one to the general manager, and one to a representative of other founding shareholders. With Brook no longer serving as general manager, my plan was to serve in that capacity temporarily before handing it to Zeng Fan-cheng. If I allowed Brook to retain his board seat, it would create an absurd situation: the new general manager would not be a director, while a manager responsible only for U.S. marketing and the American factory would be?

我坚决拒绝布鲁克的董事要求。布鲁克静默了一下，冷冷地说：「那我还是要『净离』（clean break）吧。」当时我不太了解「净离」的意思。「离」就是「离」，还有什幺「净离」？几个月后，布鲁克加入竞争者，我才了解「净离」深一层的用意：再没有什幺牵挂，缘已尽了！

ReadShift 双语翻译

I firmly rejected Brook's request for a board seat. Brook fell silent for a moment, then said coldly, "Then I will make a 'clean break.'" At the time, I did not fully understand the deeper meaning of "clean break." "Break" simply meant leaving—what else could it be? Months later, when Brook joined a competitor, I finally grasped its full implication: no ties remained; the relationship was entirely severed.

九七年一月三十日，布鲁克告诉我他「净离」的决定，但他要到四月底才走。十天后（二月十日），我就去硅谷访问了九个大客户。对我而言，访问客户是「重拾旧技」——我在德仪事业部总经理任内就喜欢访问客户。这九个客户都很稳定，毕竟我们多年来「争取客户信任」已有相当根基。

On January 30, 1997, Brook informed me of his decision to make a clean break, though he would not leave until the end of April. Ten days later, on February 10, I traveled to Silicon Valley to visit nine key customers. For me, visiting customers was a matter of "restoring old skills"—I had enjoyed client visits during my tenure as general manager of a TI business unit. All nine customers were stable, given that we had built considerable trust over many years.

二月十九日，我自硅谷打电话告知飞利浦布鲁克即将离去、我拟自兼总经理的消息。飞利浦稍表不安。我也知道他们为何不安——他们看到的台积电十年历史中，有六年是布鲁克当总经理，而我从来没有自己做过总经理。这就是为什幺飞利浦在半导体业一直平庸，他们只看表面，没有真正了解为什幺台积电这幺成功。虽然他们表示不安，但我坚持要自兼总经理，他们也就接受了。

On February 19, I called Philips from Silicon Valley to inform them that Brook was about to leave and that I intended to serve as general manager myself. Philips expressed some unease. I understood why—they had seen six of TSMC's ten years with Brook as general manager, while I had never personally held that role. This was precisely why Philips had remained mediocre in the semiconductor industry: they only observed the surface and did not truly understand what made TSMC successful. Although they expressed concern, I insisted on serving as general manager, and they ultimately accepted.

四月中旬我们为布鲁克举办了欢送会，但气氛一点都不「欢」。欢送会上很少笑容。当布鲁克致词时，他凌厉地说：「我会常常到台湾来，我在台湾还有很多投资。」我觉得很奇怪，我不知道他在台湾有很多投资；我也很恼感，六年前我们给他的欢迎会里，他的美国口音跟着大家喊「一百亿」还在我耳中荡漾。今天却是一个截然不同的布鲁克。是台积电改变了他？还是台湾改变了他？还是他本来就是一个多变的人？

In mid-April, we held a farewell gathering for Brook, but the atmosphere was far from cheerful. Smiles were rare. When Brook took the podium, he spoke sharply: "I will come to Taiwan often; I have many investments here." I found this puzzling—I was unaware he held significant investments in Taiwan. I also felt stung, for in the welcome party we had thrown for him six years earlier, his American-accented voice had echoed alongside ours chanting "one hundred billion." Today, he was an entirely different person. Had TSMC changed him? Had Taiwan changed him? Or had he always been a man of shifting loyalties?

每年变动的「十五大」

从本书第三章〈总经理的学习〉，读者也许可以领会：在我三十三岁跃升为德仪业务单位总经理时，我已拥有相当深厚的技术和生产背景。所以我任总经理后的学习重点，起先是营销和市场，两三年后是策略。至今，我仍认为策略、技术、生产、营销和市场，是一个半导体企业总经理必须亲身经历过或至少深度了解的重要领域。事实上，在我五十四年总经理/董事长职业生涯（自一九六四年我任德仪晶体管部总经理算起，至二○一八年自台积电退休）中，在以上每一个「重点领域」内都曾度过不少愉快时光。

ReadShift 双语翻译

Readers may recall from Chapter 3, "The General Manager's Learning," that when I was promoted to general manager of a TI business unit at age thirty-three, I already possessed substantial technical and manufacturing background. My learning focus after becoming general manager was initially marketing and sales, then strategy after two or three years. To this day, I maintain that strategy, technology, production, marketing, and sales are the critical domains a semiconductor general manager must have personally experienced or at least deeply understood. In fact, throughout my fifty-four-year career as general manager and chairman—from my appointment as general manager of TI's transistor division in 1964 to my retirement from TSMC in 2018—I have found genuine pleasure in each of these areas.

所以在九七年布鲁克「净离」台积电、我自兼总经理时，我几乎有「如鱼得水」的兴奋感。公司的策略除「技术领先」外早已定调，而且执行相当顺利。技术和生产的进展也可圈可点。而且「无晶圆厂设计公司」正如雨后春笋般成立，争取它们成为我们的客户是九○年代的要务——我在这方面应有可为。

So when Brook made his clean break in 1997 and I assumed the role of general manager, I felt an almost effortless sense of being exactly where I belonged. The company's strategy had long been set—technology leadership—and execution was proceeding smoothly. Progress in technology and production was commendable. Moreover, fabless design houses were emerging like mushrooms after rain, and winning them as clients was the imperative of the 1990s—an area where I believed I could make a meaningful contribution.

台积电的客户数，自八七年成立时的十五户，到九七年我自兼总经理时，已增加到一百九十九户。二○一八年我退休时又增加到四百八十户。但在三十年内，占公司营收百分之七十至七十五的客户，通常只有十五户。我给自己的任务，就是好好照顾这十五家客户。更重要的是，随时展开与有潜力的新客户合作——因为任何时间的「十五大」总有几户的成长率不如我们，我们需要与高成长者一起成长。

TSMC's client count grew from fifteen at its founding in 1987 to 199 when I assumed the general manager role in 1997, and further to 480 by the time I retired in 2018. Yet throughout those thirty years, the clients accounting for 70 to 75 percent of revenue typically numbered only fifteen. My self-imposed task was to take good care of those fifteen clients. More importantly, I was always seeking partnerships with promising new clients—for at any given time, several of the "Top Fifteen" would grow more slowly than we did, and we needed to grow alongside the high-growth companies.

在九○年代，「十五大」的变动相当快。举例来说，九七年的「十五大」，只有五户还在二○○○年的「十五大」内。失去的十户，有几家倒闭，有几家自己的业务衰退，被台积电更成功的客户替代。

In the 1990s, the "Top Fifteen" turned over quite rapidly. For example, of the Top Fifteen in 1997, only five remained in the Top Fifteen by 2000. Of the ten that fell away, some went out of business, others saw their own businesses decline, and were replaced by TSMC's more successful clients.

二○○○年后，「十五大」的变动比较少。二○○○年的「十五大」，有九家还在二○一八年「十五大」内。

After 2000, turnover among the Top Fifteen slowed considerably. Of the Top Fifteen in 2000, nine remained in the Top Fifteen by 2018.

在我任董事长兼CEO时期，我访问「十五大」至少每年一次，有特别问题时甚至一年内访问三四次。「十五大」的CEO也常来台湾，来时也常找我。虽然这些往来多是严肃谈生意的场面，但绝大部分仍是在建立的友谊基础上进行。

During my tenure as chairman and CEO, I visited the Top Fifteen at least once a year, and up to three or four times annually when special issues arose. The CEOs of these clients also frequently visited Taiwan and often sought me out. Although these exchanges were predominantly serious business discussions, the vast majority were conducted on a foundation of established friendship.

在九○年代，我们增加了四户「十五大」，而且这四户一直到二○二二年仍在「十五大」内。现在，我们依开始合作的时间次序，简单地叙述这四个重要客户。

In the 1990s, we added four new clients to the Top Fifteen, and all four remained in the Top Fifteen through 2022. Now, in the order in which we began working with them, I will briefly recount these four important clients.

英特尔（Intel）

ReadShift 双语翻译

英特尔无疑是我们关系最悠久的大客户，与个人的历史最长。五○年代后期及六○年代初期，我与后来创办英特尔的诺伊斯（Noyce）和摩尔（Moore）同在华盛顿参加每年十月举行的IEDM（国际电子元件会议）。三十岁左右的我们，会后与别的会友去附近餐厅用餐，餐后在华盛顿深秋夜晚中唱歌回旅馆（见第九章）。

ReadShift 双语翻译

## Intel

Intel is undoubtedly our longest-standing major client, and our personal history with them runs deepest. In the late 1950s and early 1960s, I attended the IEDM (International Electron Devices Meeting) held each October in Washington alongside Noyce and Moore, who would later found Intel. Around thirty years old, we would dine together at a nearby restaurant after the sessions, then sing our way back to the hotel through the late-autumn nights of Washington (see Chapter 9).

初期我为台积电募资时，也找过英特尔，但遭他们拒绝（见第十七章）。但台积电成立后一年内，英特尔总裁就来访问台积电（见第十九章），此后就有密切的接触，英特尔的订单也在九○年开始流入台积电。

ReadShift 双语翻译

When I initially sought funding for TSMC, I also approached Intel, but they declined (see Chapter 17). Within a year of TSMC's establishment, however, Intel's president visited us (see Chapter 19), and close contact followed. Intel's orders began flowing to TSMC in 1990.

九○年代的英特尔，以他们自己的晶圆厂和生产技术为傲。那幺他们为什幺要找台积电代工呢？原因是他们产品的「节点」（technology node）每一年半或两年就有一代进步，他们宁愿把所有生产资源（工厂、设备、人才）都放在先进产品上，而让台积电生产较老的产品。

ReadShift 双语翻译

In the 1990s, Intel took pride in its own fabrication plants and production technology. So why did they turn to TSMC for foundry services? The reason was that their product technology nodes advanced every one and a half to two years. They preferred to concentrate all production resources—factories, equipment, and talent—on advanced products, while letting TSMC manufacture older-generation products.

在英特尔眼中，台积电的好处是什幺？他们最惊奇的是：台积电居然能用同一个技术，同时为许多客户做不同的产品。对英特尔而言，这是一个不可思议的能力。其实，这只是台积电「商业模式」的结果。英特尔的商业模式是自己设计并制造高科技产品，所以在执行这一商业模式时，每一环节必求最高效率。英特尔的高纪律规定——每工厂必须与其它厂一样（copy exactly）——即是一例。反观台积电的商业模式是「专业为人人做晶圆代工」，在执行此商业模式时，必先顾及「为人人」。

ReadShift 双语翻译

What, in Intel's view, was TSMC's advantage? What astonished them most was that TSMC could use the same technology to produce different products for multiple customers simultaneously. To Intel, this was an almost unimaginable capability. In reality, this was simply the result of TSMC's business model. Intel's model was to design and manufacture high-tech products in-house, so every step had to achieve maximum efficiency. Intel's discipline rule—that every fab must copy exactly—is one example. By contrast, TSMC's business model was "professional foundry services for all," which required prioritizing service to everyone.

除了「为人人」的能力外，台积电的特殊商业模式也给予台积电别的特点。我们自视为服务业，所以只要台积电也能获利，总以客户为尊。台积电也不歧视老技术——只要客户需要这个技术，而台积电有这个技术，就会替他做。台积电的二厂是六吋厂，做一微米技术，已有三十几年历史，但至今仍在运作。

ReadShift 双语翻译

Beyond the ability to serve everyone, TSMC's unique business model endowed it with other characteristics. We viewed ourselves as a service industry, so as long as TSMC could generate profit, the customer always came first. TSMC also did not discriminate against legacy technology—if a client needed a particular process and TSMC possessed it, we would produce it. TSMC's second factory, a six-inch facility using one-micron technology, had been operating for over thirty years and remained in service to this day.

多年以后（二○一○年后），英特尔也想做晶圆代工。但是他们的IDM积习难改，在代工业中一直没有成功。二○二一年初，英特尔换了新CEO，矢志要兼做晶圆代工。但同时对台积电的订单不断增加。对于这个既是客户又与我们竞争的公司——我祝它好运！只是，不要把好运出在台积电头上。

ReadShift 双语翻译

Many years later, after 2010, Intel also sought to enter foundry services. But their IDM habits proved difficult to break, and they never succeeded in the foundry business. In early 2021, Intel appointed a new CEO determined to pursue foundry operations while continuing to grow. Yet its orders with TSMC kept increasing. For this company that was both a customer and a competitor—I wish them well. Just not at TSMC's expense.

## Broadcom

Two men named Henry—Henry Samueli and Henry Nicholas—founded Broadcom in 1991. Both came from the University of California, Los Angeles. Samueli was a professor in the electrical engineering department; Nicholas was his student. They founded Broadcom around high-frequency telecommunications IC design.

九○年代出现不少以创办者IC设计专才为基础的IC设计公司，但成功的并不多。要成功，设计公司的产品要有相当广的市场，又要比它的竞争者好，而且要找到一个能够和它充分合作的晶圆代工厂。
