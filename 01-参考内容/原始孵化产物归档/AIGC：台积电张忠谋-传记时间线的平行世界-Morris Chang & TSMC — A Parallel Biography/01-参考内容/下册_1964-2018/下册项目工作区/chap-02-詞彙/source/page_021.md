# 词汇

## 年份说明

书中所有年份均为公元纪年，例：「七五年」即指一九七五年；「六〇年代」即指一九六〇年代。

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **公元纪年**
**中文解释**：即以公历（格里高利历）纪年；在中英文商业文献中为通用标准，避免与农历纪年混淆。
**商业造句**：*All financial projections in the shareholder letter are in Gregorian calendar years.*

## 公司

### 德州仪器公司（Texas Instruments Inc.）

简称「德州仪器」，是美国一家大型科技企业，总部设在德克萨斯州达拉斯市。在五〇至八〇年代，是全球最大的半导体公司。作者于一九五八至一九八三年在此服务二十五年。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Texas Instruments Inc. (TI) — a major American technology corporation headquartered in Dallas, Texas. During the 1950s–1980s, TI was the world's largest semiconductor company. Morris Chang worked here for 25 years from 1958 to 1983.</p>
</div>

### 通用器材公司（General Instrument Corporation）

简称「通用」，是美国一家多元化科技公司，总部设在纽约市，分部遍及全球各地及台湾。作者于一九八四至一九八五年在此任总经理暨运营长。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">General Instrument Corporation (GI) — an American diversified technology company headquartered in New York, with divisions worldwide and in Taiwan. Chang served as President and COO from 1984 to 1985.</p>
</div>

## 德州仪器相关人物

### 吕斯（Sheldon Roberts, 1926—2019）

一九六二至一九六七年，任德州仪器助理副总裁（Assistant VP），也是作者的上司；在本书中，作者称他为「最好的上司」。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Sheldon Roberts (1926–2019) served as TI Assistant VP from 1962 to 1967; he was Morris Chang's direct supervisor. In this book, Chang calls him "the best boss I ever had."</p>
</div>

### 道森（Cecil Dotson, 1923—2010）

一九六二至一九六七年，任德州仪器集团副总裁（Group VP），也是半导体集团总经理，是当时作者的上司（吕斯）的上司。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Cecil Dotson (1923–2010) served as TI Group VP and head of the Semiconductor Group from 1962 to 1967, making him the superior of Chang's boss (Roberts).</p>
</div>

### 彪希（J. Fred Bucy, 1928—2021）

一九六七年至一九八三年作者辞职，一直是作者在德仪的顶头上司，历任集团副总裁暨半导体集团总经理、执行副总裁、运营长、总经理、总裁，直到一九八五年被革职。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">J. Fred Bucy (1928–2021) was Chang's direct superior at TI from 1967 until Chang's 1983 resignation, serving successively as Group VP & GM of the Semiconductor Group, Executive VP, COO, President, and CEO, before being dismissed in 1985.</p>
</div>

### 夏伯特（Mark Shepherd, 1923—2009）

一九六一年起任德仪执行副总裁暨运营长，其后历任总经理、总裁、董事长，直到一九八八年退休。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Mark Shepherd (1923–2009) served as TI Executive VP and COO from 1961, later rising to President, CEO, and Chairman, before retiring in 1888.</p>
</div>

### 海格底（Patrick Haggerty, 1914—1980）

在一九六〇至七〇年代，是德仪全盛时代的董事长，非常赏识作者。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Patrick Haggerty (1914–1980) was Chairman of TI during its heyday in the 1960s–1970s and a great patron of Chang.</p>
</div>

### 基尔比（Jack Kilby, 1923—2005）

一九五八年与作者几乎同时加入德仪，几个月后便发明了集成电路，并因此于二〇〇年获得诺贝尔物理学奖。在六〇年代的德仪，他堪称半导体技术研究教主，有无可争议的权威。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Jack Kilby (1923–2005) joined TI almost simultaneously with Chang in 1958 and invented the integrated circuit just months later, an achievement for which he was awarded the 2000 Nobel Prize in Physics. He was the undisputed authority on semiconductor technology research at 1960s TI.</p>
</div>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Patron**
**中文解释**：赏识者、提携人，指在事业早期识别并扶持你的人。
**商业造句**：*Behind many celebrated founders stands a patron — the early believer who opened the first doors.*

2. **Undisputed authority**
**中文解释**：无可争议的权威，指在某一领域内所有人都信服的最顶级专家。
**商业造句**：*Her two decades of litigation experience made her the undisputed authority on IP strategy at the firm.*

## 台积电相关人物

### 李国鼎（1910—2001）

一九五八至一九六九年历任美援会秘书长、经合会秘书长与副主委、经济部长。一九六九至一九七六年任财政部长。之后，任行政院政务委员，成立资讯工业策进会，也支持台积电的成立，协助台湾科技发展直到一九八八年退休。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Li Guoding (1910–2001) served in succession as Secretary-General of the Council for International Economic Cooperation, Minister of Economic Affairs (1958–1969), and Minister of Finance (1969–1976). As a Minister without Portfolio, he founded the Institute for Information Industry and supported the creation of TSMC, contributing to Taiwan's technology development until his retirement in 1988.</p>
</div>

### 孙运璿（1913—2006）

一九六二至一九六七年先后任台湾电力公司、尼日利亚电力公司总经理。一九六七年任交通部长，一九六九年转任经济部长，除促成工业技术研究院的诞生，也支持自美引进集成电路技术。一九七八年起任行政院院长，带领台湾度过中美断交危机；一九八四年因脑溢血辞卸院长职务。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Sun Yun-suan (1913–2006) served as General Manager of Taiwan Power Company and later Nigeria's national power utility. As Minister of Communications (1967) and then Minister of Economic Affairs (1969), he championed the founding of ITRI and the acquisition of IC technology from the United States. As Premier from 1978, he guided Taiwan through the trauma of US diplomatic recognition of the PRC, before a stroke forced his resignation in 1984.</p>
</div>

### 方贤齐（1912—2015）

一九六九至一九七八年任电信总局局长，支持自美引进集成电路技术。一九七八年转任工研院第二任院长直到一九八五年退休，期间还参与筹设资讯工业策进会，并于一九七九至一九八一年出任第一任执行长。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Fang Xianqi (1912–2015) served as Director-General of the Directorate General of Telecommunications from 1969 to 1978, championing the import of IC technology from the United States. In 1978 he became the second Chairman of ITRI, serving until 1985; during this period he helped found the Institute for Information Industry and served as its first CEO (1979–1981).</p>
</div>
