# 半导體術語

## 半导体名词

### 双极（Bipolar）

亦即双极性，是一九五二至一九七〇年间重要的半导体技术。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Bipolar — a key semiconductor technology between 1952 and 1970.</p>
</div>

### 金属—氧化物—半导体（MOS: Metal-Oxide-Semiconductor）

由金属、氧化层、半导体堆叠而成的半导体技术，自一九七〇年代后逐渐成为主流。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">MOS (Metal-Oxide-Semiconductor) — a technology formed by stacking metal, oxide, and semiconductor layers; became dominant after the 1970s.</p>
</div>

### 记忆体（Memory）

重要电子元件，其记忆容量通常以「位元」（bit）数字表达。16K 代表「十六千位元」，4M 代表「四百万位元」。SRAM（静态随机存取记忆体）通电即可恒常保持资料；EPROM（可擦除可程式唯读记忆体）可经由电子方式多次覆写。DRAM（动态随机存取记忆体）是最主要的记忆体，常被当作电脑中的主记忆器。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Memory — a key electronic component whose storage capacity is expressed in bits. 16K means 16 kilobits; 4M means 4 megabits. SRAM (Static RAM) retains data as long as power is applied; EPROM (Erasable Programmable ROM) can be electronically reprogrammed many times. DRAM (Dynamic RAM) is the primary form of memory used as a computer's main storage.</p>
</div>

### 摩尔定律（Moore's Law）

为英特尔创办人之一的摩尔（Gordon Moore）于一九六五年提出。他预期积体电路上可容纳的电晶体数目，约每隔一年半至两年便会增加一倍。其后，全球半导体业大致按照此定律发展了半个多世纪。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Moore's Law — proposed by Intel co-founder Gordon Moore in 1965, predicting that the number of transistors on an integrated circuit would double every eighteen months to two years. The global semiconductor industry has broadly followed this law for over half a century.</p>
</div>

### 微处理器（Microprocessor）

重要电子元件，将一部电脑的主要功能整合到一颗积体电路上。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Microprocessor — a key electronic component that integrates the principal functions of a computer onto a single integrated circuit.</p>
</div>

### 积体电路（Integrated Circuit, IC）

重要电子元件，是在一个微小的晶粒上聚集许多电晶体、电阻、电容等元件的微型化电路。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Integrated Circuit (IC) — a key electronic component that miniaturizes a complete circuit by assembling many transistors, resistors, and capacitors on a single tiny chip.</p>
</div>

### 光刻（Photolithography）

半导体制程中一个重要步骤。先在矽晶圆上涂布一层光阻液，再经过曝光、显影、蚀刻等步骤，将光罩上的图形转移到矽晶圆上。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Photolithography — a critical semiconductor manufacturing step. A photoresist liquid is applied to a silicon wafer, followed by exposure, development, and etching to transfer the mask's pattern onto the wafer.</p>
</div>

### 电晶体（Transistor）

重要电子元件，于一九四八年为美国贝尔实验室三位物理学家萧克利（William Shockley）、布律敦（Walter Brattain）和巴丁（John Bardeen）发明，具有放大和开关电讯号的功能。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Transistor — a key electronic component invented in 1948 at Bell Labs by William Shockley, Walter Brattain, and John Bardeen, capable of amplifying and switching electrical signals.</p>
</div>

### 奈米（nm）与微米（μm）

长度单位。一奈米代表十亿分之一公尺，相当于一微米的一千分之一。一微米代表百万分之一公尺；人的头发粗细约为一百微米。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Nanometer (nm) and micrometer (μm) — units of length. One nanometer is one billionth of a meter, or one-thousandth of a micrometer. One micrometer is one millionth of a meter; a human hair is roughly one hundred micrometers thick.</p>
</div>

### 锗（Germanium, Ge）

原子序为 32 的化学元素，是五〇至六〇年代的重要半导体材料。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Germanium (Ge) — chemical element with atomic number 32, an important semiconductor material in the 1950s–1960s.</p>
</div>

### 矽（Silicon, Si）

原子序为 14 的化学元素，是六○年代中迄今的主流半导体材料。今日全球绝大多数晶片均由矽晶圆制成。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Silicon (Si) — chemical element with atomic number 14, the dominant semiconductor material from the mid-1960s to the present day. The vast majority of chips worldwide today are fabricated on silicon wafers.</p>
</div>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Moore's Law**
**中文解释**：摩尔定律，由英特尔创办人戈登·摩尔提出，每隔约 18—24 个月晶片上电晶体数目翻倍；这一定律驱动了半导体业超过半个世纪的资本支出节奏。
**商业造句**：*Moore's Law is not a law of physics but a self-fulfilling prophecy sustained by collective capital investment across the industry.*

2. **Photolithography**
**中文解释**：光刻，半导体成像制程的核心步骤，台积电在 EUV 光刻领域的领先是其护城河之一。
**商业造句**：*ASML's monopoly on EUV lithography equipment has become the single most consequential bottleneck in the global chip supply chain.*

3. **Wafer**
**中文解释**：晶圆，指用于制造积体电路的圆形矽片；尺寸通常以直径表示（如八吋、十二吋晶圆）。
**商业造句**：*Shifting from 200 mm to 300 mm wafers reduced per-chip costs by roughly 30%, but required billions in new capital equipment.*

4. **Dominant**
**中文解释**：主流的、主导的，指在市场中占据压倒性份额或技术领先地位。
**商业造句**：*TSMC's dominant position in advanced nodes above 7 nm gives it pricing power that competitors cannot easily challenge.*

5. **Transistor**
**中文解释**：电晶体，使所有现代电子装置成为可能的基本元件；一片现代智慧型手机晶片中约有上百亿个电晶体。
**商业造句**：*The transistor is to the 21st century what the steam engine was to the 18th: the enabling invention that transformed every industry it touched.*

### 背景知识延伸

**从锗到矽的材料革命**：五〇年代第一批商用电晶体由锗（Ge）制成。一九五四年德州仪器推出第一批矽电晶体后，矽因其天然氧化物（SiO₂）优异的绝缘性能和更宽的能带间隙（1.12 eV vs. 0.67 eV），迅速取代锗成为主流半导体材料。此后半个多世纪，几乎所有半导体装置均基于矽——仅在高频（GaAs、GaN）和功率（SiC、GaN）等特殊领域，才由化合物半导体取而代之。

**光刻技术的演进**：光刻是将设计的电路图案转移到晶圆上的过程。一九六〇年代使用可见光（波长 400—700 nm），随着电晶体尺寸的缩小，产业陆续转向 ultraviolet（UV）、深紫外（DUV）、乃至今日极紫外（EUV，波长 13.5 nm）光刻。台积电在 2019 年率先将 EUV 应用于七奈量产，是其拉开与竞争对手差距的关键战役。

**DRAM 作为半导体业指标**：动态随机存取记忆体（DRAM）因价格高度周期性、制程要求严苛，被业界视为半导体产业的「晴雨体」。台积电虽不以记忆体为主营业务，但 DRAM 代工仍是其重要业务之一。
