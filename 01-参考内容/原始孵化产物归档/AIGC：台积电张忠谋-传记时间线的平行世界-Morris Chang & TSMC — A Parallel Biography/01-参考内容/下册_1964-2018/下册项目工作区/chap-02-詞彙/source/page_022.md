### 潘文渊（1912—1995）

一九七四年提议「加速台湾电子产业成长，应自美引进集成电路技术」，随后在美国召集海外学人组成「美洲技术顾问团」（TAC），促成工研院自美引进 CMOS 半导体制程技术。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Pan Wenyuan (1912–1995) proposed in 1974 that Taiwan should accelerate its electronics industry growth by importing IC technology from the United States. He then convened overseas Chinese engineers in the U.S. to form the Technical Advisory Committee (TAC), which brokered the transfer of CMOS process technology to ITRI.</p>
</div>

### 徐贤修（1912—2001）

一九七〇至一九七五年任国立清华大学校长，一九七三至一九八一年兼任国科会主委，并筹设「单一服务窗口」的新竹科学工业园区。一九七八年起任工研院董事长直到一九八八年退休。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Xu Xianxiu (1912–2001) served as President of National Tsing Hua University (1970–1975) and concurrently as Chairman of the National Science Council (1973–1981), during which he established the Hsinchu Science-Based Industrial Park with a "single-service-window" concept. He became ITRI Chairman in 1978 and served until 1988.</p>
</div>

<div class="rebook-translation">

As Premier, he backed TSMC's establishment on the condition that a multinational semiconductor company be brought in as the second-largest shareholder.</p>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Brokered the transfer**
**中文解释**：促成技术转让，指在买卖方或合作方之间居中协调、促成知识产权或技术的跨境转移。
**商业造句**：*The licensing team brokered the transfer of the gene-editing patent from the university to the biotech startup.*

2. **Instrumental**
**中文解释**：至关重要的、有帮助的，指某人对一件事的成功发挥了核心作用。
**商业造句**：*She was instrumental in securing the Series A funding, personally introducing the founder to three lead investors.*

3. **Founding team**
**中文解释**：创始团队，指一家公司或机构最早期的核心成员群体。
**商业造句**：*The founding team's complementary skills — engineering, sales, and regulatory — set the template for the company's DNA.*
