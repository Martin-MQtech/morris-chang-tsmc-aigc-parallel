## 在通用最后的几个月

一九八四年第三季起，市场渐不景气，通用业绩逆转。歇基的注意力马上从「买卖公司」转移到「维持业绩」。当然我也想维持业绩，但当我面对敷衍或敌视我的下属，自己又不熟悉大部分业务，无力回天时，歇基和我的关系很快地转坏了。

歇基富有战术思想，战术精髓在于反应快。在业绩逆转之时，他立刻雇回了一位与他战术思维差不多的前副总（名乔治），把乔治塞在我下面，而且要我手里的事业单位分几个给乔治。我从命。

乔治的确「新官上任三把火」，一上任就飞到他负责的事业单位所在地（加州、亚利桑那州、马里兰州），我很少在见到他，只有打电话。他常有好消息报告，但他的好消息都是治标性的，例如：「我把光电部门一些存货以廉价卖掉了」、「我得到某客户的同意把他们下个月要的货这个月就运出了」。当我问：「那下个月怎么办？」他居然说：「下个月再想办法。」其实下个月的「办法」，也不过与这个月一样——寅吃卯粮。

至于我谆谆教诲的「基本面」，乔治似乎毫不在意。

另一人事变更：负责半导体的资深副总辞职了。此人起初就反对我，后来也没有和我合作，所以我并不惋惜他的辞职，而且立刻任命半导体部门第二号人接位。但歇基似乎很不舍得此人，而且认为此人的离开是我不能留人，在歇基心目中，这是我不适任总经理的又一证据。

一九八五年二月八日，距我上任不到一年，我接到歇基从他亚利桑那州农场以「特邮」寄来的「绝对机密」备忘录（长达三页）：

「你与我对如何解决公司里六个单位的惊人亏损，并无共识。结论：Chang的精力太分散了。我要你集中精力在半导体和光电上；我要乔治集中在电脑产品上；我自己则主要集中在宽频和赌注产品上。」

<p class="en-para"><b>The Final Months at GI</b></p>
<p class="en-para">From the third quarter of 1984, the market turned, and GI's performance reversed. Griek's attention immediately shifted from "buying and selling companies" to "maintaining performance." Of course I wanted to maintain performance too, but facing subordinates who were either perfunctory or hostile, and myself still unfamiliar with most of the businesses, I was powerless to reverse the tide. Griek and my relationship quickly deteriorated.</p>
<p class="en-para">Griek had a tactical mind, and tactical thinking prized fast reaction. Amid the performance reversal, he immediately rehired a former VP whose tactical mindset mirrored his own — named George — and inserted George beneath me, demanding that I transfer several business units from my purview to George. I complied.</p>
<p class="en-para">George indeed "hit the ground running" — upon starting, he flew to where his businesses were located (California, Arizona, Maryland) and I rarely saw him in New York, only hearing him by phone. He frequently reported good news, but his good news was all symptomatic treatment, such as: "I sold off some optoelectronics inventory at a fire-sale price" or "I got a customer to agree to take next month's shipment this month." When I asked, "Then what happens next month?" he would actually say, "We'll figure out next month when we get there." In reality, next month's "solution" was no different from this month's — borrowing from next year's harvest to feed this year.</p>
<p class="en-para">As for the "fundamentals" I earnestly taught, George seemed utterly uninterested.</p>
<p class="en-para">Another personnel change: the Senior VP in charge of semiconductors resigned. This person had opposed me from the start and never cooperated with me, so I did not regret his departure and immediately appointed the semiconductor division's #2 person as his replacement. But Griek seemed quite reluctant to lose this person and viewed his departure as evidence that I couldn't retain people — in Griek's mind, further proof that I was unsuitable as GM.</p>
<p class="en-para">On February 8, 1985 — less than a year into my tenure — I received an "absolute confidential" memorandum (three pages long) from Griek, sent via "special mail" from his Arizona ranch:</p>
<p class="en-para">"You and I have no consensus on how to address the staggering losses in six of our units. Conclusion: Chang's attention is too scattered. I want you to focus on semiconductors and optoelectronics; I want George to focus on computer products; I myself will focus primarily on broadband and wagering products."</p>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Tactical vs. strategic in downturn (不景气时的战术与战略)**
**中文解释**：不景气时歇基转向「战术」——卖存货、催客户提货；但这些都是「治标」而非「治本」；在不景气时，「治标」是战术，「治本」是战略——张忠谋认为真正该做的是改善基本面（研发、产品力），歇基则认为先撑过眼前再说。
**商业造句">In a downturn, Griek's tactical instincts — sell inventory, accelerate shipments — clashed with Chang's strategic instincts: invest in fundamentals (R&D, product capability) to emerge stronger.*

2. **Special-mail memorandum as conflict escalation (特邮备忘录作为冲突升级)**
**中文解释**：歇基以「特邮」寄出「绝对机密」备忘录——这是正式的组织警告，也是对张忠谋职权的公开削限；在企业管理中，书面备忘录通常意味着「口头谈判已失败，转入正式冲突程序」。
**商业造句">Griek's "special mail" memorandum — "absolute confidential," three pages — was a formal organizational warning: oral negotiation had failed, and the conflict was now on the record.*
