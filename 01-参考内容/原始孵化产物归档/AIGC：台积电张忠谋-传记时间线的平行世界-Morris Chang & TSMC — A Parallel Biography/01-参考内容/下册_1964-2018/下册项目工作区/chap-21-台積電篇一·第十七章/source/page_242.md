## 七个月谈判的开启

十二月二日，我去加州森尼韦尔（Sunnyvale），首次晤克列斯曼，自此展开长达七个月的谈判。在此谈判中，我代表台積電，我背后的决策者是"五人小组"，我主要助手是胡定华、史钦泰以及政府常用的联鼎法律事务所的陈国慈律师。飞利浦方面则有许多谈判者——技术、业务方面由克列斯曼负责，台湾飞利浦技术长罗益强君助理；财务、法务另有荷兰飞利浦专人负责。

<p class="en-para">On December 2, I went to Sunnyvale, California, and met Krigsman for the first time, launching a negotiation that would last seven months. In this negotiation, I represented TSMC. ## 谈判重点一——公司命名

在第一次对院长简报"共同晶圆厂"时，我已暂时命名新公司为 Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company（TSMC）。我觉得这名字表达了公司的要点：（一）在台湾成立（Taiwan）＋（二）做半导体（Semiconductor）＋（三）做制造（Manufacturing）。后来的投资协议都以英文为主文；一直到公司登记，才有中文名字问题。我的第一个反应就是直译英文："台湾半导体制造公司"，但发现此名已为人登记，所以改成"台湾积体电路制造公司"，简称"台積電"。英文名字及英文简写都不必改，现在TSMC已闻名世界了。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">When I first briefed the Premier on the "Common Wafer Fab," I had tentatively named the new company Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (TSMC). I felt this name captured the company's essentials: (1) established in Taiwan (Taiwan) + (2) in the semiconductor business (Semiconductor) + (3) in manufacturing (Manufacturing). Subsequent investment agreements used English as the main text; the Chinese name issue didn't arise until company registration. My first instinct was a direct translation: "Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company," but discovered this name was already registered by someone else, so we changed it to "Taiwan Integrated Circuit Manufacturing Company," abbreviated as "TSMC." The English name and acronym didn't need to change—and now TSMC is world-famous.</p>
</div>

## 谈判重点二——营业范围

开始与飞利浦谈判，我就强调"专业晶圆代工"商业模式。飞利浦不但同意，而且说如果台湾要办设计公司或IDM型的公司，他们不会考虑投资，因为设计公司或IDM型公司会是他们的竞争者。既有这样共识，我们在"投资协议纲要"（Heads of Agreement）中，更清楚地界定"专业晶圆代工"的营业范围：

台積電不设计或推销自己的产品，但依照客户的设计，为客户制造IC晶圆，并提供或安排封装测试服务。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">From the start of negotiations with Philips, I emphasized the "dedicated foundry" business model. Philips not only agreed, but said that if Taiwan wanted to establish a design house or IDM-type company, they would not consider investing, because a design house or IDM-type company would be their competitor. With this consensus, we more clearly defined the business scope of "dedicated foundry" in the "Heads of Agreement":</p>
<p class="en-para">TSMC does not design or sell its own products, but manufactures IC wafers according to customers' designs, and provides or arranges packaging and testing services.</p>
</div>
