# 台积电篇二·第二十四章｜争取客户信任

## 九零年代产能供不应求

现在回想起来，台积电的三块基石：制造优越、客户信任、技术领先；前两者在九零年代都已奠定。第三块：技术领先，也在九零年代后期开始成形。在这章中，我想谈谈台积电如何在九零年代奠定"客户信任"的基础。

台积电一厂是规模甚小的厂，但在开张两年后才有足够订单把它填满。二厂在一九八九年落成，九零年开始量产，虽然并不是很快就产能满载，但是客户自工厂落成后就给我们很乐观的需求预测，使得我们也很乐观。果然，九二年他们的实际需求甚至超过他们原先以为乐观的预测。同时，新的无晶圆厂IC设计公司纷纷成立，成为我们客户。这真是我们兴奋的时光！我们立刻决定建三厂，三厂在九五年落成。

事实上，除了九六、九七、九八这三年世界半导体业不景气，台积电营收也只能以36%、18%、15%成长率稍缓成长外，整个九零年代台积电都在产能不足、供不应求状态中。在这情形下，客户最殷切的要求就是要我们大胆建新厂，积极扩产。这点，我们做到了。三厂在九五年落成时，我们立刻为四厂、五厂动土。

<p class="en-para">## Capacity Demand Exceeded Supply Throughout the '90s</p>
<p class="en-para">Looking back now, TSMC's three cornerstones—manufacturing excellence, customer trust, and technology leadership—the first two were already laid in the 1990s. The third, technology leadership, also began taking shape in the late 1990s. In this chapter, I want to discuss how TSMC laid the foundation of "customer trust" in the 1990s.</p>
<p class="en-para">TSMC Fab 1 was a rather small facility, but it took two years after opening before there were enough orders to fill it. Fab 2 was completed in 1989 and began volume production in 1990. Although it didn't reach full capacity quickly, customers gave us optimistic demand projections after the fab was completed, which made us optimistic too. Sure enough, in 1992 their actual demand even exceeded what they had originally considered optimistic forecasts. Meanwhile, new fabless IC design companies were springing up and becoming our customers. These were exciting times! We immediately decided to build Fab 3, which was completed in 1995.</p>
<p class="en-para">In fact, except for 1996, 1997, and 1998—three years of global semiconductor downturn when TSMC revenue grew at more modest rates of 36%, 18%, and 15%—throughout the 1990s TSMC was in a state of insufficient capacity and demand exceeding supply. Under these circumstances, customers' most urgent request was for us to boldly build new fabs and aggressively expand capacity. This we did. When Fab 3 was completed in 1995, we immediately broke ground for Fabs 4 and 5.</p>

## 台南科学园区

一九九六年国科会宣布建立台南科学园区。这是政府对科技业的重要德政。台积电是最早加入的公司之一。我在九七年接受《中国时报》专访时表示：

"十年前我在台湾成立台积电时，当时我看到一个趋势，许多小型的集成电路设计公司即将成立，但晶圆制造工厂是一种资金非常大的投资。这些设计公司多半没有太多资本用来兴建自己的制造工厂，我相信这就是专业晶圆制造工厂生存的空间。

现在，我又看到第二波正在形成，也是南科制造中心的基本出发点。这个新的趋势是，目前即使大的集成电路公司也不见得有能力或意愿兴建自己的晶圆制造工厂。但对台积电这种专业晶圆制造工厂来说，我们的客户不只一家，当某一种半导体产品不景气时，我们可以为其他半导体客户服务，这是'专业集成电路制造服务工厂的优势。"

一九九七年四月，我宣布台积电将在未来十年在南科投资四千亿元，并将提供五千个以上就业机会。当时台塑集团王永庆董事长讥我的投资计画为"膨风"。王先生错了，此后十年台积电在南科投资六千余亿元，提供就业机会七千个。

<p class="en-para">In 1996, the National Science Council announced the establishment of Tainan Science Park. This was an important government benefaction to the technology industry. TSMC was one of the earliest companies to join. In a 1997 exclusive interview with the China Times, I stated:</p>
<p class="en-para">"Ten years ago when I established TSMC in Taiwan, I saw a trend at that time: many small IC design companies were about to be established, but wafer manufacturing fabs require very large capital investments. Most of these design companies didn't have much capital to build their own manufacturing fabs. I believed this was the survival space for professional wafer manufacturing fabs.</p>
<p class="en-para">Now, I see a second wave forming, which is also the fundamental starting point of the Tainan Science Park manufacturing center. This new trend is: currently, even large IC companies may not have the capability or willingness to build their own wafer fabs. But for a professional wafer manufacturing fab like TSMC, we have more than one customer. When a certain semiconductor product is in a downturn, we can serve other semiconductor customers. This is the advantage of a 'professional IC manufacturing services fab.'"</p>
<p class="en-para">In April 1997, I announced that TSMC would invest NT$400 billion in Tainan Science Park over the next ten years, creating over 5,000 jobs. At that time, Formosa Plastics Chairman Wang Yung-ching mocked my investment plan as "puffery." Mr. Wang was wrong—over the next ten years, TSMC invested over NT$600 billion in Tainan Science Park, creating 7,000 jobs.</p>
