年龄迈进的恋，大约可以以年龄定义。少年（十八至三十岁）恋充满幻想、理想、互扶持；中年（三十至六十岁）恋重于工作或事业；晚年（六十岁以后）恋却是相当纯真的相互照应。「执子之手，与子偕老」的伴侣，百年不渝，又能享受这种恋的，随又有多少人？我享受了少年恋爱，也享受了纯真的晚年恋，已经算是福气。

<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Love at advancing ages can roughly be defined by age. Youthful love (eighteen to thirty) is filled with fantasy, ideals, and mutual support; middle-aged love (thirty to sixty) centers on work and career; and love in later life (after sixty) is marked by a rather pure mutual care. How many people can enjoy such a bond—holding your partner's hand and growing old together—unswerving for a century? I have enjoyed youthful love, and I have also enjoyed the pure love of later years. That is, I consider myself fortunate.</p>
