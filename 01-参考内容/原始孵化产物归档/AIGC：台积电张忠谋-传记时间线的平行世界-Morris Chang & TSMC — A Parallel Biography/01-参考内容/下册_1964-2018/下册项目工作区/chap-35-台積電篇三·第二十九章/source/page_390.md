## 「直线」投资理念的由来

建产能需要很长的时间。在七○、八○年代，如果要从建厂房开始，大约需要三年；即使有了厂房，从建洁净室开始，大约也要两年。时至今日，制程更为复杂，设备更为精密，投资更为巨大，新人员的训练需要更长时间，所以建产能的时间比七○、八○年代还须增加至少一年。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Building capacity requires a considerable amount of time. In the 1970s and 1980s, if one started from constructing a fabrication plant, it took approximately three years; even with a building in place, starting from cleanroom construction required roughly two years. To the present day, processes are more complex, equipment more precise, investments far larger, and training for new personnel takes longer—so the time required to build capacity has increased by at least one year beyond the 1970s and 1980s.</p>
</div>

既然建产能需时三、四年，业者投资心态就成为供需平衡与否的重要因素。通常当需求强劲时，业者猛建产能，几年后产能过剩；一旦需求趋缓，业者一面杀价抢订单，一面缩手不建产能；再过几年就会产能不足。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Since building capacity takes three to four years, the investment mindset of industry participants becomes a crucial factor in supply-demand balance. Typically, when demand is strong, companies build capacity aggressively; a few years later, overcapacity results. Once demand slows, companies engage in price-cutting to抢 orders while simultaneously pulling back on capacity investment; a few years later, capacity shortage follows.</p>
</div>

我在七○年代在德仪时就倡议「直线建产能」。在一九七四年的德仪全公司策略会议中，我建议每年以「滚动十季计划」为根据，增加一定数量的产能——也就是：景气好时不猛建，景气坏时不缩手。我的建议在策略会议中颇受赞赏，但到真正执行时，我的上面两层上司（公司的COO及CEO），以及他们上面的董事会，还是把我的计划折扣到我都不认识的地步。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">I advocated "straight-line capacity building" as early as the 1970s at TI. At TI's 1974 company-wide strategy meeting, I proposed increasing a fixed amount of capacity each year based on a "rolling ten-quarter plan"—that is, not building aggressively during booms, and not pulling back during downturns. My proposal was well-received at the strategy meeting, but when it came to actual execution, my two layers of superiors (the company's COO and CEO), as well as the board above them, still discounted my plan to the point where it was unrecognizable.</p>
</div>

在台积电，我终于可以实行我的「直线」投资理念了。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">At TSMC, I was finally able to put my "straight-line" investment philosophy into practice.</p>
</div>
