## 飞利浦的投资与五人小组

十月初，也就是李政委担心俞院长听了陈主委话后「不自在」之时，荷兰飞利浦公司主动对李政委表示：飞利浦有兴趣投资他们在报章上读到的新IC公司计划。飞利浦是一个多元跨国公司，其中一元是IC，他们也有自主IC技术。所以，如果他们的投资能实现，也就满足了俞院长的筹资要求。

获李政委消息后，我紧锣密鼓进行飞利浦案。我花几天时间写就了对飞利浦的投资建议，交给飞利浦在台湾的技术经理转达飞利浦总部。十一月三日至六日，我亲赴荷兰访问飞利浦在荷兰的两大基地：奈梅亨（Nijmegen）及恩荷芬（Eindhoven）。十一月中旬，飞利浦重申他们的合资兴趣，当然许多条件和细节尚待谈判，而这类谈判往往需要好几个月，但我相信他们有诚意。

十月八日我去见俞院长，俞把李政委的签呈（建议照我意见进行台积电）交给我，要我「再研究」；同时他指示了两点：我们建议的自有资本对贷款比例（五十/五十）不够保守，应该改为七十（自有资本）/三十（贷款）；第二点：经过这样一改，民间投资的需要变成七千五百万美元。俞院长说：在台湾绝对募不到这么多民间投资，必须先找一家「跨国」、有技术的IC公司，要他们做主要投资者；然后台湾民间才有信心凑足余额。

俞院长的第一指示是金玉良言，我完全接受。第二指示虽然令我失望，但他一定知道台湾民间的投资心态及投资胃纳，所以我也不得不接受。当然，邀请跨国、有技术的IC公司做主要投资者等于「引狼入室」，一定会带来「谁有控制权、经营权」问题。但是，当时第一要务是把公司办成，控制权、经营只好以后再见烦恼了。

见了俞院长后我就去李政委办公室，对他报告俞院长对我说的话，同时也告诉他我这几天听到关于陈主委的阻力：陈主委想找国外顾问公司评审我的「专业晶圆代工」公司建议，他又召集他的替代方案的座谈会，就在次日开会。

李政委对俞院长指示的两点倒没说什么，但对陈主委的阻挠行动却勃然大怒。他立刻命他秘书接电话给国科会副部长王纪五，在电话上对王怒吼：「告诉你的主管，他这样干涉我要做的事情，我要辞职了！」对陈要找国外顾问公司评审我的建议，李也气愤地对王说：「关于半导体问题，国外顾问公司是要请教张忠谋的，哪里有要他们评估张忠谋的道理。」

李以「四朝重臣」之尊，暂时压倒了这次陈主委的阻力。当天我就接到「座谈会延期」的通知。几天后，我也间接被告知陈主委已不再阻挠。

十一月十七日（星期日），我访李政委于他家。看起来，俞院长对我的两指示都可遵守，陈主委也不再阻挠，我们应该请俞院长准许我们正式开始筹备工作。李政委回答：还是先见俞院长，看他有什么指示。

十一月二十二日，与李政委同见俞院长，我对俞院长报告新公司进行之目前状况，约半小时。俞院长简短回答：「写公文给我。」

此后几天，我写我生平第一篇中文公文，也是我三十几年来第一篇中文作文。上次正式写中文是三十几年前写《伊莉莎伯》短篇小说。那次写中文小说的诗情画意，这次写公没有，有的只是硬邦邦台积电的雏型。

<p class="en-para"><b>Philips' Investment and the Five-Person Committee</b></p>
<p class="en-para">In early October — around the time Minister Li was worried about Premier Yu being "uncomfortable" after hearing Minister Chen's objections — Philips of the Netherlands approached Minister Li proactively: Philips was interested in investing in the new IC company plan they had read about in the newspapers. Philips was a diversified multinational with a semiconductor division and its own IC technology. So if their investment materialized, it would meet Premier Yu's funding requirement.</p>
<p class="en-para">Upon hearing from Minister Li, I moved quickly on the Philips deal. I spent several days writing an investment proposal for Philips, delivered through Philips' technical manager in Taiwan to their headquarters. From November 3 to 6, I traveled to the Netherlands to visit Philips' two major sites: Nijmegen and Eindhoven. In mid-November, Philips reaffirmed their interest in a joint venture. Of course, many conditions and details remained to be negotiated, and such negotiations often take months, but I believed they were sincere.</p>
<p class="en-para">On October 8, I met with Premier Yu. Yu handed me Minister Li's memorandum (recommending they proceed with TSMC as I had proposed) and asked me to "study it further." At the same time, he issued two directives: first, our proposed equity-to-loan ratio (50/50) was not conservative enough — it should be changed to 70 (equity) / 30 (loans). Second, with this change, the private investment needed became $75 million USD. Premier Yu said: This amount could absolutely not be raised from Taiwan's private sector; they had to find a "multinational," technology-bearing IC company to be the lead investor, and only then would Taiwan's private sector have confidence to raise the remainder.</p>
<p class="en-para">Premier Yu's first directive was golden advice; I accepted it wholeheartedly. His second directive, though disappointing, reflected his knowledge of Taiwan's private investment appetite and mentality, so I had tooaccept it. Of course, inviting a multinational IC company to be the lead investor was tantamount to "inviting the wolf into the house" and would certainly raise "who controls and manages" issues. But at that moment, the first priority was to get the company founded; control and management could be worried about later.</p>
<p class="en-para">After seeing Premier Yu, I went to Minister Li's office to report what Premier Yu had told me. I also informed him about Minister Chen's resistance: Chen wanted to engage a foreign consulting firm to evaluate my "dedicated foundry" proposal, and had convened a symposium on his alternative plan, scheduled for the next day.</p>
<p class="en-para">Minister Li said little about Premier Yu's two directives, but he flew into a rage at Minister Chen's obstruction. He immediately ordered his secretary to call NSC Vice Minister Wang Jiwu, roaring at Wang on the phone: "Tell his superior that if he interferes with what I'm doing like this, I'll resign!" Regarding Chen's plan to have foreign consultants evaluate me, Li angrily told Wang: "On semiconductor matters, foreign consultants should be consulting Morris Chang — how could they possibly evaluate him?"</p>
<p class="en-para">Li, leveraging his stature as a "four-dynasty elder statesman," temporarily crushed Minister Chen's resistance. That very day I received notice that the symposium was "postponed." A few days later, I was indirectly informed that Minister Chen was no longer obstructing.</p>
<p class="en-para">On November 17 (Sunday), I visited Minister Li at his home. It appeared that Premier Yu's two directives could be followed, Minister Chen was no longer obstructing, and we should ask Premier Yu to authorize us to formally begin preparations. Minister Li responded: Let's still see Premier Yu first and see what he says.</p>
<p class="en-para">On November 22, I accompanied Minister Li to see Premier Yu. I briefed Premier Yu on the current status of the new company for about half an hour. Premier Yu gave a brief answer: "Write me an official document."</p>
<p class="en-para">In the following days, I wrote the first official document of my life — and my first piece of Chinese writing in over thirty years. The last time I had formally written in Chinese was more than thirty years earlier, writing the short story "Elizabeth." That time had been full of poetic sentiment; this time there was nothing romantic — only the hard, bare prototype of TSMC.</p>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **"Inviting the wolf into the house" (引狼入室)**
**中文解释**：Invite a wolf into the house — 比喻引来强大对手进入自己的地盘；张忠谋深知飞利浦作为主要投资者将带来控制权问题，但当时别无选择。
**商业造句**：*Chang's decision to bring in Philips as the lead investor was a calculated risk — "inviting the wolf into the house" was the price of getting the company funded.*

2. **Four-dynasty elder statesman (四朝老臣)**
**中文解释**：指李国鼎历经蒋中正、严家淦、蒋经国、李登辉四朝均居要职的政治地位；李政委借此身份暂时压倒了陈履安的阻挠。
**商业造句**：*Li Guoding's clout as a "four-dynasty elder" gave him the political muscle to override bureaucratic resistance — but such personal authority would fade after his retirement.*

3. **Official document (公文)**
**中文解释**：政府机关间正式的书面文件；张忠谋生平第一次写中文公文——距他上次正式写中文（短篇小说）已隔三十余年。
**商业造句**：*Chang's first Chinese official document was more than a bureaucratic formality — it was the founding charter of what would become one of the world's most valuable companies.*
