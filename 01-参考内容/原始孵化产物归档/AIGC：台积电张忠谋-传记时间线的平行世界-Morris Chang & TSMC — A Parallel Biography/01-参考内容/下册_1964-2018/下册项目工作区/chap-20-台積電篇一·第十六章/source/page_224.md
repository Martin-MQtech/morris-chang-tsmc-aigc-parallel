## 「专业晶圆代工」商业模式

三天后（九月七日，星期六）李政委又开会，这次有谢森中（交通银行董事长）、GRIER（国库署长兼开发基金执行长）及江万龄（中华开发银行总经理）。假使政府同意办新的公司，开发基金是最可能的投资者；交银也是可能的投资者；江万龄在场，我想是因为中华开发曾投资国善，所以江很在意政府如何对国善交代。

在会议中，李政委要我重述一次我三天前对他提的「共同晶圆厂」观念，然后征求谢、张及江的意见。谢和张赞同「共同晶圆厂」，江表示国善仍希望有他们自己的晶圆厂。李政委是一位决定明快、而且没有太多耐心与人辩论的人。他很快嘱咐我准备对俞国华院长简报。

当天下午，李打电话给我：「已约定九月十日（星期二）下午四点半」，他兴奋地对我说，「想不到他（俞院长）那么热心，那么快就答应听你报告。」

一九八五年八月二十一日（方贤齐对我讲「要办事项」）至九月四日（我对李国鼎提「共同晶圆厂」）这十四天，可说是我一生中创新价值最高的两周。价值连城的「专业晶圆代工」（Dedicated Foundry）商业模式在此两周诞生。

在这两周，我把我过去二十几年经营半导体业的经验，以及对工研院IC计划的观察和了解，整理、综合起来，达到了一个在当时台湾具备的条件下，最有成功机会的半导体公司商业模式。

当时，世界上几乎所有稍具规模的IC公司都是「垂直整合型」（Integrated Device Manufacturer, IDM），营业范围包含IC设计、晶圆制程研发、晶圆生产、IC营销。这些IDM如有多余产能，也为没有晶圆厂的设计公司（例如在通用器材公司碰到的Chips&Technologies及Atmel）代工。

台湾要成立一家新的IC公司，要从工研院IC案出发，我检验工研院IC案的能力：

- IC设计——能力低。
- 晶圆制程研发——当时（一九八五年）世界最先进的「节点」技术是一微米；工研院还在二微米，落后二代半（一.五微米是一代，一.二五微米是半代），大约三年左右。
- 晶圆生产——工研院「示范线」的良率优异。这是工研院很重要、也是唯一强点。
- 没有在世界市场营销能力。

经过这样的分析，新公司应该具有的商业模式已呼之欲出——只帮客户做晶圆生产，而且目前只能做「成熟节点」技术的生产。当然，新公司必须继续晶圆制程研发，否则制程技术会愈来愈落后；而且希望有朝一日，也能做尖端技术的生产服务。

<p class="en-para"><b>The "Dedicated Foundry" Business Model</b></p>
<p class="en-para">Three days later (September 7, Saturday), Minister Li convened another meeting, this time with Xie Senzhong (Chairman of the Bank of Communications), the Director-General of the National Treasury Agency (also Executive Director of the Development Fund), and Jiang Wanling (General Manager of China Development Bank). If the government agreed to establish a new company, the Development Fund was the most likely investor; the Bank of Communications was also a possible investor. I believe Jiang was present because China Development had invested in Mosel, so Jiang cared how the government would handle Mosel.</p>
<p class="en-para">At the meeting, Minister Li asked me to restate the "Common Wafer Fab" concept I had presented three days earlier, and then solicited the opinions of Xie, the Director-General, and Jiang. Xie and the Director-General endorsed the Common Wafer Fab. Jiang indicated that Mosel still wanted their own dedicated fab. Minister Li was a man of swift decisions and not much patience for debate. He quickly instructed me to prepare a briefing for Premier Yu.</p>
<p class="en-para">That afternoon, Li called me: "Scheduled for September 10 (Tuesday) at 4:30 PM," he said excitedly. "I didn't expect him (Premier Yu) to be so enthusiastic and agree so quickly to hear your report."</p>
<p class="en-para">Those fourteen days — from August 21 (when Fang Xianqi gave me the "action items") to September 4 (when I proposed the "Common Wafer Fab" to Li Guoding) — were perhaps the two most innovative weeks of my life. The priceless "Dedicated Foundry" business model was born in those two weeks.</p>
<p class="en-para">During those two weeks, I organized and synthesized everything I had learned from over twenty years in the semiconductor industry, together with my observations of ITRI's IC program, to arrive at the business model that offered the highest probability of success given Taiwan's conditions at the time.</p>
<p class="en-para">Back then, virtually every semiconductor company of any scale in the world was an Integrated Device Manufacturer (IDM), spanning IC design, process R&D, wafer fabrication, and IC marketing. When these IDMs had surplus capacity, they did foundry work for fabless design companies (such as Chips&Technologies and Atmel, which I had encountered at General Instruments).</p>
<p class="en-para">For Taiwan to create a new IC company based on ITRI's program, I assessed ITRI's capabilities:</p>
<p class="en-para">• IC design — low capability.</p>
<p class="en-para">• Process R&D — the world's most advanced "node" in 1985 was 1 micron; ITRI was at 2 microns, two-and-a-half generations behind (1.5 microns is one generation; 1.25 microns is half a generation), roughly three years behind.</p>
<p class="en-para">• Wafer production — ITRI's "demonstration line" had excellent yield. This was ITRI's most important — and only — strength.</p>
<p class="en-para">• No capability to market in world markets.</p>
<p class="en-para">From this analysis, the new company's optimal business model became clear:只做晶圆生产，而且目前只能做「成熟节点」技术的生产。</p>
<p class="en-para">Of course, the new company would need to pursue process R&D, otherwise its technology would fall further behind. And hopefully, one day it would be able to offer leading-edge technology as well.</p>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **IDM (Integrated Device Manufacturer，垂直整合型制造商)**
**中文解释**：涵盖设计、制造、销售全流程的半导体公司（如德仪、英特尔）；一九八五年的全球半导体产业几乎全由IDM主导。
**商业造句**：*The IDM model dominated the semiconductor industry for decades — Chang's genius was to see that a pure-play foundry could carve out a viable niche by serving only the manufacturing needs of others.*

2. **Dedicated Foundry (专业晶圆代工厂)**
**中文解释**：「只制造、不设计」的专业晶圆代工模式——这是张忠谋对半导体产业最重要的商业模式创新。
**商业造句**：*The Dedicated Foundry model created an entirely new industry segment — enabling fabless design companies to exist without owning a single wafer fab.*

3. **Node / Process node (节点/制程节点)**
**中文解释**：指晶圆制造中最小特征尺寸的世代（如1微米、0.5微米）；「节点」的推进是半导体技术进步的核心指标。
**商业造句**：*TSMC's strategy was to start at mature nodes and fund an aggressive R&D roadmap to catch up — node by node — with the global leaders.*
