<p class="en-para">October 23 was the thirtieth-anniversary celebration. In the morning I met separately with several key customers to discuss our cooperation. Because my retirement was already no longer news, we could discuss current issues as usual. I was glad we had announced the succession plan early.</p>
<p class="en-para">The afternoon symposium featured presidents from NVIDIA, Qualcomm, Broadcom, ASML, Analog Devices, ARM, and Apple's COO—the discussions were quite splendid.</p>
<p class="en-para">What delighted me most was the evening concert. During the intermission, just before Beethoven's Ninth Symphony was about to begin, I broke with tradition and walked onto the stage to deliver a retirement address in English. After I finished, the presidents of several key customers rose to applaud first; then the entire audience rose to their feet, thunderous in their applause. For a career in which I regarded customers as partners above all, their applause was a magnificent reward.</p>
<p class="en-para">After I announced my retirement and the succession plan for Liu and Wei in October 2017, I continued to work normally both internally and externally. In April and May 2018, the company held several large farewell gatherings: one each in Hsinchu, Tainan, and Taichung, each attended by hundreds to thousands of colleagues. Marketing staff, most of whom were based abroad, also took the opportunity of their April quarterly meeting in Hsinchu to host a farewell evening for me.</p>
<p class="en-para">The shareholders' meeting on June 5, 2018, finally arrived. I retired as planned upon the election of the new board. The newly elected directors, as previously arranged, selected Tim Liu as chairman and Morris Wei as president, launching TSMC's dual-leadership system.</p>
