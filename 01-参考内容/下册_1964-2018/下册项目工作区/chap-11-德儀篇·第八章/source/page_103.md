# 德仪篇·第八章｜德仪迷途

野心与诱惑的致命交集——

一九七二年，几乎与我接任半导体集团总经理同时，夏伯特「大有为」的野心与在德仪潜伏多年的「消费者产品」诱惑做了一个致命的交集。

夏伯特宣布德仪进入「消费者产品」事业，先以普通计算机开始，接下来会有「科学计算机」、电子表、家庭电脑等。他同时宣布：德仪要利用它在半导体的优势，全力支援消费者产品的发展。

一九五〇年代以成功经营半导体事业崛起的夏伯特，在一九七二年已与半导体业脱节。他并不充分了解一九六五年提出的「摩尔定律」，也不了解半导体还有多年快速成长。事实上，从一九七二到二〇〇〇年，世界半导体市场每年平均成长率一五%。德仪只要专心做半导体、保持它的半导体市占率（十几%），它的半导体集团就可以在二〇〇〇年达到两百二十亿美元营收——已远远超过全公司二〇〇〇年营收目标：一百亿美元。

夏伯特想「大有为」没有错，错的是他的方向。他只要专心继续耕耘半导体，德仪就可以超出他的长期目标。但他开启了德仪不熟悉的新战场。夏伯特不但分散了德仪的焦点、没有给半导体足够资源——他更分散了德仪半导体集团的焦点。本来德仪半导体集团的焦点是外部市场，现在夏伯特却钦定「服务内部客户」为半导体集团首要任务；而且，他把半导体集团当作消费者产品集团的人才库，调走了不少经理及技术人才。

<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">The fatal intersection of ambition and temptation —</p>
<p class="en-para">In 1972, almost simultaneously with my taking over as GM of the Semiconductor Group, Shepherd's ambition to "achieve great things" and the long-simmering temptation of "consumer products" converged in a fatal intersection.</p>
<p class="en-para">Shepherd announced that TI would enter the "consumer products" business, beginning with basic calculators and followed by "scientific calculators," electronic watches, home computers, and more. He also announced that TI would leverage its semiconductor strength to fully support the development of consumer products.</p>
<p class="en-para">Shepherd, who had risen to prominence by successfully running the semiconductor business in the 1950s, had by 1972 grown disconnected from the semiconductor industry. He did not fully understand Moore's Law, proposed in 1965, nor did he appreciate how many years of rapid growth still lay ahead for semiconductors. In fact, from 1972 to 2000, the world semiconductor market grew at an average annual rate of 15%. Had TI simply focused on semiconductors and maintained its market share (in the mid-teens), its Semiconductor Group alone could have reached $22 billion in revenue by 2000 — far exceeding the company's total 2000 revenue target of $10 billion.</p>
<p class="en-para">Shepherd was not wrong to want to "achieve great things" — he was wrong in his direction. Had he simply continued to focus on semiconductors, TI could have exceeded his long-term targets. But instead he opened a new battlefield unfamiliar to TI. Shepherd not only分散了 TI's focus and denied semiconductors sufficient resources — he further scattered the Semiconductor Group's own focus. The Semiconductor Group's original focus had been external markets; now Shepherd decreed "serving internal customers" as its primary mission. Moreover, he treated the Semiconductor Group as a talent pool for the Consumer Products Group, transferring away numerous managers and technical staff.</p>
</div>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Fatal intersection**
**中文解释**：致命交集，指两个独立趋势（野心与诱惑）在错误时机的碰撞；在战略管理中，「致命交集」往往导致资源分散和核心能力侵蚀。
**商业造句">The fatal intersection of Shepherd's ambition and the consumer products temptation was not just a strategic error — it was the beginning of TI's three-decade decline from semiconductor supremacy.*

2. **Moore's Law (摩尔定律)**
**中文解释**：摩尔定律，由英特尔联合创始人戈登·摩尔于一九六五年提出，指集成电路上晶体管数目约每十八至二十四个月增加一倍；这一定律是半导体产业最具预测力的经验法则——忽视它的管理者将付出高昂代价。
**商业造句">Shepherd's ignorance of Moore's Law was not merely a knowledge gap — it was a strategic blindness that led him to underestimate the very industry that had made TI great.*

3. **Serving internal customers**
**中文解释**：服务内部客户，指将原本面向外部市场的生产能力转为服务内部需求；在组织行为学中，「内部客户优先」往往会削弱企业对外部市场的响应能力。
**商业造句">Decreeing "serving internal customers" as the Semiconductor Group's primary mission was a subtle way of turning a profit center into a cost center — and a direct path to competitive decline.*

### 修辞赏析

<span class="rhetoric-note">
<span class="zh">「两百二十亿美元营收 vs. 一百亿美元全公司目标」——张忠谋时隔近三十年後仍然精准地算出了德仪在「专注半导体」和「分心消费者产品」之间的机会成本。这个数字对比（仅半导体一项潜力即超过全公司目标一倍）是整章最有力的论证——它证明夏伯特的选择不是「做不做消费者产品」的问题，而是「要不要放弃一个确定性巨大的未来」的问题。这种「事後精算」的读法，不是「後见之明」，而是张忠谋对产业发展规律的深刻洞察。</span>
<span class="en">"$22 billion in semiconductor revenue vs. $10 billion total company target." — After nearly thirty years, Chang still precisely calculated the opportunity cost between "focusing on semiconductors" and "distracting with consumer products." This numerical contrast (semiconductor potential alone exceeding the total corporate target by more than double) is the chapter's most devastating argument: it proves that Shepherd's choice was not about "whether to enter consumer products" but about "whether to forfeit a staggeringly certain future." This "retrospective precision" is not hindsight — it is Chang's profound insight into the industry's developmental laws.</span>
</span>

### 第八章小结：德仪迷途的代价

一九七二年的消费者产品决定，是德仪从半导体霸主走向漫长衰退的起点。张忠谋在自传中精确诊断了这场战略失误的三个根源：

第一，夏伯特的「大有为」野心——他想成为海格底第二，却选择了错误的方向。

第二，半导体技术脱节——夏伯特自五〇年代後不再懂技术，也不知摩尔定律，低估了半导体业的成长潜力。

第三，人才掠夺——把半导体集团的焦点从「服务外部市场」改为「服务内部客户」，并把半导体集团的优秀经理和技术人员调往消费者产品事业。

这三重打击叠加在一起，使德仪在最需要专注半导体的时候分散了焦点。张忠谋在自传中写道：「一九七二年的『大升级』只带来暂时的兴奋和喜悦——即将来临的是六年的奋斗，尤其是对公司内部环境的奋斗。」
