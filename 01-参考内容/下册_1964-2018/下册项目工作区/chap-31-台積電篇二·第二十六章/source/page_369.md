与辉达的合作，与许多其他客户很不一样。第一，辉达的确有杰出的绘图芯片（graphics chips）设计团队。第二，在挑选代工伙伴时，Jensen愿意孤注一掷，挑选台积电独家为他代工。第三，我也抱着惺惺相惜的态度全力支持——甚至在九八年时，辉达人手缺乏（当时全公司只有八十几名员工），业务又在急遵成长，台积电还特地派了两位「生产计划师」进驻辉达一个月，为他们规划进货、存货、售出等流程，并协助训练新进员工。这是一个很不寻常的客户服务。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">The partnership with Nvidia differed markedly from many others. First, Nvidia indeed possessed an exceptional graphics chip design team. Second, when selecting a foundry partner, Jensen was willing to place an extraordinary bet by choosing TSMC as his exclusive manufacturer. ## 高通（Qualcomm）
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
</div>

高通成立于一九八五年，早台积电两年。首任CEO欧文·雅各布斯（Irwin Jacobs）亦为共同创办人，曾经当过麻省理工学院及加州大学圣地牙哥分校计算机科学系教授。自成立后，高通就以通讯芯片的建构及设计为主业。它的两个业务是：（一）授权发明，收取权利金；（二）自己设计芯片，找晶圆制造商代工，然后销售芯片。高通在通讯芯片技术及设计上享有极佳声誉。它位于加州圣地牙哥总部入门大厅的墙上，满满挂着专利牌子——一个专利一张牌子，大约总有成千块牌子。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">## Qualcomm</p>
<p class="en-para">Qualcomm was founded in 1985, two years before TSMC. Its first CEO, Irwin Jacobs, was also a co-founder and had served as a professor of computer science at MIT and UC San Diego. From its inception, Qualcomm focused primarily on telecommunications chip development and design. Its two businesses were: (1) licensing inventions and collecting royalties, and (2) designing chips in-house, contracting foundry manufacturing, and selling the finished products. Qualcomm enjoyed an excellent reputation in communication chip technology and design. On the walls of its headquarters' entrance hall in San Diego, California, patents were displayed—one plaque per patent, numbering in the thousands.</p>
</div>
