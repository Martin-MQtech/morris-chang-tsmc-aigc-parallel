# 台积电篇二·第二十六章｜九○年代建立的几个重要客户

九十年代的台积电，已从初创期的蹒跚学步成长为全球晶圆代工产业的领头羊。这十年间，我们建立并巩固了一批至今仍在合作的核心客户关系。这些客户中，既有老牌巨头英特尔，也有出生于九十年代的新锐设计公司和专攻通讯芯片的博通。他们的交织与更替，折射出半导体产业从IDM（整合元件制造）模式向晶圆代工模式转移的历史洪流。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">By the 1990s, TSMC had grown from a fledgling operation into the global leader in dedicated foundry services. During that decade, we forged and deepened relationships with a core set of clients—ranging from the venerable Intel to upstart fabless design houses and the communications-focused Broadcom—that remain with us to this day. Their interplay and turnover mirrored the broader structural shift in the semiconductor industry away from the IDM model toward the foundry paradigm.</p>
</div>

然而，在回顾这些商业成就之前，必须先讲述一个关乎公司治理与个人体面离场的故事——第三任总经理布鲁克的「净离」。

<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">But before recounting these commercial triumphs, a story of corporate governance and dignified departure must be told: Brook's "clean break" from TSMC.</p>

## 布鲁克「净离」台积电，我自兼总经理

布鲁克（Brook）是台积电第三任总经理，曾在德仪做了我十几年的得力下属。一九九一年三月加入台积电，起初他并不怎么看好这家公司，对我说：「做一年就回美国。」一年后觉得台积电比他想象的好，便说：「再做一年吧。」再一年后就开始有较长的打算，但仍说：「不会超过两、三年。」
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Brook, TSMC's third general manager, had been my trusted lieutenant at Texas Instruments for over a decade. He joined TSMC in March 1991, initially skeptical of the venture's prospects. "I'll do one year, then go back to the U.S.," he declared. Impressed by what he saw after the first year, he extended his stay—only to extend it again. By the third year, he began contemplating a longer horizon, but still insisted, "Two or three years at most."</p>
</div>

我雇用布鲁克，主因是借重他在欧美市场营销的技能。在这方面，他确实没有令我失望。至于台湾的经营层面，我倒不很需要布鲁克。第一，我们的台湾团队在九○年代中期已经相当坚强。曾繁城自工研院带过来的核心团队一直在成长，我们也雇了许多优秀人才，包括魏力行、刘德音、林坤禧等；第二，布鲁克本人并未赢得本土团队的尊敬和信任。倒是我自己不遗余力地让制造部门配合营销——数度否决布鲁克要求雇美国人担任营运副总，以及不让曾繁城辞职而让他去世界先进任总经理，就是明证。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">I had hired Brook primarily for his marketing expertise in the American and European markets. On this front, he did not disappoint. As for managing operations in Taiwan, I genuinely needed less from him. Our local team had grown formidable by the mid-1990s. The core group led by Zeng Fan-cheng from ITRI had flourished, and we had recruited outstanding talents like Wei Li-hsing, Liu De-yin, and Robert Lin. More crucially, Brook himself had not earned the deep respect and trust of the local team—a reality reflected in my repeated veto of his proposals to hire Americans as COO, and in my orchestrating an alternative role for Zeng Fan-cheng at Vanguard rather than letting him resign.</p>
</div>
