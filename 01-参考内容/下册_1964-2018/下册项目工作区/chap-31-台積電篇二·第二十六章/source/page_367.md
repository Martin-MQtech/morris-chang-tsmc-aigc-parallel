除了「为人人」的能力外，台积电的特殊商业模式也给予台积电别的特点。我们自视为服务业，所以只要台积电也能获利，总以客户为尊。台积电也不歧视老技术——只要客户需要这个技术，而台积电有这个技术，就会替他做。台积电的二厂是六吋厂，做一微米技术，已有三十几年历史，但至今仍在运作。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Beyond the ability to serve everyone, TSMC's unique business model endowed it with other characteristics. We viewed ourselves as a service industry, so as long as TSMC could generate profit, the customer always came first. TSMC also did not discriminate against legacy technology—if a client needed a particular process and TSMC possessed it, we would produce it. TSMC's second factory, a six-inch facility using one-micron technology, had been operating for over thirty years and remained in service to this day.</p>
</div>

多年以后（二○一○年后），英特尔也想做晶圆代工。但是他们的IDM积习难改，在代工业中一直没有成功。二○二一年初，英特尔换了新CEO，矢志要兼做晶圆代工。但同时对台积电的订单不断增加。对于这个既是客户又与我们竞争的公司——我祝它好运！只是，不要把好运出在台积电头上。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Many years later, after 2010, Intel also sought to enter foundry services. But their IDM habits proved difficult to break, and they never succeeded in the foundry business. In early 2021, Intel appointed a new CEO determined to pursue foundry operations while continuing to grow. Yet its orders with TSMC kept increasing. For this company that was both a customer and a competitor—I wish them well. Just not at TSMC's expense.</p>

## 博通（Broadcom）
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
</div>

两个亨利：亨利·萨穆埃利（Henry Samueli）及亨利·尼古拉斯（Henry Nicholas），在一九九一年创办博通。两人皆出自加州大学洛杉矶分校（UCLA）。萨穆埃利是该校电机系教授，尼古拉斯是他的学生。两人就高频电讯IC设计创办博通。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">## Broadcom</p>
<p class="en-para">Two men named Henry—Henry Samueli and Henry Nicholas—founded Broadcom in 1991. Both came from the University of California, Los Angeles. Samueli was a professor in the electrical engineering department; Nicholas was his student. They founded Broadcom around high-frequency telecommunications IC design.</p>
</div>

九○年代出现不少以创办者IC设计专才为基础的IC设计公司，但成功的并不多。要成功，设计公司的产品要有相当广的市场，又要比它的竞争者好，而且要找到一个能够和它充分合作的晶圆代工厂。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">The 1990s saw the emergence of numerous IC design companies founded on the design expertise of their creators, but few succeeded. To succeed, a design company's products needed a sufficiently broad market, had to outperform competitors, and required a foundry partner capable of full collaboration.</p>
</div>
