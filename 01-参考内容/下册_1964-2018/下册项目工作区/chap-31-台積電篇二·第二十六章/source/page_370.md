这样一个高技术声誉的公司，当然是台积电争取的对象。但高通在选择晶圆代工厂上也非常谨慎——它不愿找一个可能的竞争者，但也需要一个制程技术和它的设计技术匹配的晶圆制造商。在八○年代以及大部分九○年代，他们找到了IBM。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">A company with such a strong technical reputation was naturally a target for TSMC's pursuit. But Qualcomm was equally cautious in selecting a foundry partner—it did not wish to partner with a potential competitor, yet it needed a fabricator whose process technology matched its design capabilities. Throughout the 1980s and most of the 1990s, they turned to IBM.</p>
</div>

对九○年代的台积电而言，IBM是一个可畏的竞争者。虽然IBM的晶圆制造技术在九○年代已落后英特尔等领先者，但与台积电仍在伯仲之间。撇开技术，IBM还有它在信息界如「神」般的形象，但也有随这形象而来的高傲。台积电却有着「客户是伙伴」的谦逊IBM的担心也是下章的故事之一。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">For TSMC in the 1990s, IBM was a formidable competitor. Although IBM's wafer fabrication technology had lagged behind leaders like Intel by the 1990s, it remained roughly on par with TSMC. Beyond technology, IBM carried an almost "godlike" image in the information industry, along with the arrogance that accompanied it. TSMC, by contrast, carried the humility of "customers are partners." IBM's concerns would become the subject of the next chapter.</p>
</div>

后来，台积电在这场竞争中胜出应该不是意外。九八年高通总经理告诉我高通要开始和我们合作时，我非常兴奋——我已争取高通近二年，现在终于有成果了！我相信高通会是一个大客户。何况，获得高通为客户是对我们技术的一个认证。果然，自二○○一年起，高通就是我们「十五大」之一，有许多年它是我们最大的客户。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">It was hardly surprising that TSMC eventually prevailed in this competition. When Qualcomm's general manager informed us in 1998 that Qualcomm would begin partnering with us, I was extremely excited—I had been pursuing Qualcomm for nearly two years, and results had finally come. I believed Qualcomm would become a major client. Moreover, gaining Qualcomm as a customer was a certification of our technology. Indeed, from 2001 onward, Qualcomm became one of our "Top Fifteen" clients, and in many years it was our largest customer.</p>
</div>

我们的凯歌也是IBM微电子部门（即半导体部门）的暮鼓。他们知道他们的制造技术正在快速落后，连台积电这样的后起之秀都追上来了。

<p class="en-para">Our triumph also sounded the death knell for IBM's Microelectronics Division—that is, its semiconductor business. They knew their manufacturing technology was rapidly falling behind, and that a rising star like TSMC had caught up.</p>
