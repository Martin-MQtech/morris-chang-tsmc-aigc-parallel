## 英特尔（Intel）
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
</div>

英特尔无疑是我们关系最悠久的大客户，与个人的历史最长。五○年代后期及六○年代初期，我与后来创办英特尔的诺伊斯（Noyce）和摩尔（Moore）同在华盛顿参加每年十月举行的IEDM（国际电子元件会议）。三十岁左右的我们，会后与别的会友去附近餐厅用餐，餐后在华盛顿深秋夜晚中唱歌回旅馆（见第九章）。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">## Intel</p>
<p class="en-para">Intel is undoubtedly our longest-standing major client, and our personal history with them runs deepest. In the late 1950s and early 1960s, I attended the IEDM (International Electron Devices Meeting) held each October in Washington alongside Noyce and Moore, who would later found Intel. Around thirty years old, we would dine together at a nearby restaurant after the sessions, then sing our way back to the hotel through the late-autumn nights of Washington (see Chapter 9).</p>
</div>

初期我为台积电募资时，也找过英特尔，但遭他们拒绝（见第十七章）。但台积电成立后一年内，英特尔总裁就来访问台积电（见第十九章），此后就有密切的接触，英特尔的订单也在九○年开始流入台积电。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">When I initially sought funding for TSMC, I also approached Intel, but they declined (see Chapter 17). Within a year of TSMC's establishment, however, Intel's president visited us (see Chapter 19), and close contact followed. Intel's orders began flowing to TSMC in 1990.</p>
</div>

九○年代的英特尔，以他们自己的晶圆厂和生产技术为傲。那么他们为什么要找台积电代工呢？原因是他们产品的「节点」（technology node）每一年半或两年就有一代进步，他们宁愿把所有生产资源（工厂、设备、人才）都放在先进产品上，而让台积电生产较老的产品。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">In the 1990s, Intel took pride in its own fabrication plants and production technology. So why did they turn to TSMC for foundry services? The reason was that their product technology nodes advanced every one and a half to two years. They preferred to concentrate all production resources—factories, equipment, and talent—on advanced products, while letting TSMC manufacture older-generation products.</p>
</div>

在英特尔眼中，台积电的好处是什么？他们最惊奇的是：台积电居然能用同一个技术，同时为许多客户做不同的产品。对英特尔而言，这是一个不可思议的能力。其实，这只是台积电「商业模式」的结果。英特尔的商业模式是自己设计并制造高科技产品，所以在执行这一商业模式时，每一环节必求最高效率。英特尔的高纪律规定——每工厂必须与其它厂一样（copy exactly）——即是一例。反观台积电的商业模式是「专业为人人做晶圆代工」，在执行此商业模式时，必先顾及「为人人」。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">What, in Intel's view, was TSMC's advantage? What astonished them most was that TSMC could use the same technology to produce different products for multiple customers simultaneously. To Intel, this was an almost unimaginable capability. In reality, this was simply the result of TSMC's business model. Intel's model was to design and manufacture high-tech products in-house, so every step had to achieve maximum efficiency. Intel's discipline rule—that every fab must copy exactly—is one example. By contrast, TSMC's business model was "professional foundry services for all," which required prioritizing service to everyone.</p>
</div>
