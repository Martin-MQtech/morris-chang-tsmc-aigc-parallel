# 德仪篇·第三章｜总经理的学习

学习定价——

在学习行销时，我同时累积定价的经验。德仪半导体的业务，部分是「标准式商品」，部分是「客制品」。「标准式商品」卖给许多客户，也有不少竞争者，所以价格往往是市场订。供应商往往只能决定在某价格要不要这订单。但是，供应商也不是完全无戏可唱——在二十世纪，半导体的生产良率以及品质和可靠度都不很稳定，而且在那「半导体春秋战国」时代，「诚信」成为难得的美德。假如一个经营者建立了一个「可信任」的名誉，他在议价他的「商品」时，就享有某种优势。当然这优势到底多强，完全看他的竞争者的强弱。

<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Learning pricing —</p>
<p class="en-para">While learning marketing, I simultaneously accumulated pricing experience. TI's semiconductor business was part "standard commodity" and part "custom product." Standard commodities were sold to many customers with numerous competitors, so prices were often set by the market. Suppliers could usually only decide whether to take an order at a given price. But suppliers were not entirely without leverage — in the twentieth century, semiconductor production yields, quality, and reliability were all quite unstable, and in that "Warring States era of semiconductors," integrity was a rare virtue. If a businessman established a "trustworthy" reputation, he enjoyed a certain advantage when negotiating commodity prices. Of course, how strong that advantage was depended entirely on how strong his competitors were.</p>
</div>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Standard commodity / Custom product**
**中文解释**：标准商品 / 客制品，指面向多个客户的通用产品 vs. 为特定客户定制的产品；在半导体产业中，标准商品的定价由市场决定，而客制品的定价则取决于供需双方的博弈。
**商业造句">Memory chips are standard commodities — price is set by the market, and your only choice is whether to play. ASICs are custom products — pricing is a negotiation, and reputation is your strongest card.*

2. **Warring States era**
**中文解释**：春秋战国时代，比喻竞争激烈、诸侯割据的市场格局；在商业史叙事中，这个比喻常用来描写一个行业标准尚未确立、竞争者众多的早期阶段。
**商业造句">The cloud infrastructure market in 2010 was a Warring States era — dozens of warlords, no clear hegemon, and the only certainty was that most would not survive.*

3. **Reputation premium**
**中文解释**：信誉溢价，指因「可信任」名声而获得的定价优势；在良率和品质不稳定的产业中，这种溢价可能高达十至二十个百分点。
**商业造句">TI's reliability track record gave it a 15% reputation premium on automotive-grade semiconductors when Japanese entrants were still struggling with field failure rates.*

### 修辞赏析

<span class="rhetoric-note">
<span class="zh">「在二十世纪，半导体的生产良率以及品质和可靠度都不很稳定」——张忠谋用「二十世纪」这个时间坐标，精妙地拉开了历史距离：他是在二十一世纪的书桌前回望六十年前的产业状况。这种「时空倒错」的叙事视角，让他的自传同时具备了企业史的价值。当他写道「那个半导体春秋战国时代」，读者仿佛看到了一个老人站在山巅，俯瞰自己年轻时混战的战场。</span>
<span class="en">"In the twentieth century, semiconductor production yields, quality, and reliability were all quite unstable" — Chang uses the temporal coordinate "in the twentieth century" to elegantly open historical distance: he is looking back at the industry of sixty years ago from his twenty-first-century desk. This "temporally displaced" narrative perspective gives his autobiography the value of corporate history as well. When he writes "that Warring States era of semiconductors," the reader can almost see an old man standing on a mountaintop, gazing down at the battlefield of his youth.</span>
</span>

### 背景知识延伸

**「客制品」的定价艺术**：张忠谋在自传中透露了一个定价秘诀：对于「客制品」（为特定客户定制的产品），总经理必须亲自出马议价。因为只有总经理才能同时了解「客户需求的真真假假、竞争者优势劣势、以及自己的成本会计」这三个维度的信息。张忠谋在德仪建立了「不轻易承诺，但一旦承诺必定不计成本实践」的信誉。这个信誉让他的定价可以比市价略高——在良率不稳定、交期常跳票的半导体产业中，「说到做到」本身就是最值钱的差异化。这个定价哲学后来被张忠谋完整地带入了台积电。
