# 德仪篇·第三章｜总经理的学习

韩德生和贝因都有显赫的成就。今日，韩德生已被公认为「商业策略」鼻祖之一。记得今日策略大师迈克尔·波特（Michael Porter）二十年前首次受《天下杂志》邀访台湾时，做了一次演讲，演讲后与我和另两位台湾企业家对谈——在对谈时显有看不起台湾这个落后地区的神态。对谈后我与波特个别聊天，提起我在七〇年代与韩德生合作事。波特的眼睛突然亮了：「啊，你和韩德生合作过？」接着又问了我许多与韩德生合作往事。事实上，我与波特的这段谈话，也开启了后来波特为台积电撰写的第一件「个案研究」（case study），以及我聘波特为台积电监察人。

贝因呢？他创办了很成功的贝因顾问公司（Bain & Company），后来又创办了很成功的贝因投资公司（Bain Capital）。我和他还有一段后话。二〇一〇年，我想找一家顾问公司对台积电高级经理人讲价格理论，找了三家顾问公司来面试——贝因顾问公司是三家之一。可惜应徵的代表，已不能与四十年前的韩德生或贝因相比，所以我们没有用贝因公司。我又想到了贝因本人，就设法与他联络并约他在我去波士顿时同进午餐。两人都赴约了。四十年前我们曾密切合作一年多，四十年后重逢，假如在路上相遇，一定不认识了。故人谈往事，我们有一个愉快的午餐。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Both Henderson and Bain went on to achieve great things. Today, Henderson is recognized as one of the founding fathers of "business strategy." I remember that when the strategy guru Michael Porter first visited Taiwan at the invitation of CommonWealth Magazine some twenty years ago, he gave a talk and later engaged in a dialogue with me and two other Taiwanese entrepreneurs. During the dialogue, he displayed a demeanor that clearly looked down on Taiwan as a "backward region." After the session, I chatted with Porter individually and mentioned my collaboration with Henderson in the 1970s. Porter's eyes suddenly lit up: "Ah, you worked with Henderson?" He then asked me many details about the collaboration. In fact, that conversation with Porter later led to Porter writing the first "case study" on TSMC, and to my appointing Porter as a supervisor of TSMC.</p>
<p class="en-para">As for Bain? He founded the highly successful Bain & Company, and later the highly successful Bain Capital. There is an epilogue to our story. In 2010, I wanted to find a consulting firm to teach pricing theory to TSMC's senior managers and invited three firms to interview. Bain & Company was one of the three — but the representative they sent could not compare to the Henderson or Bain of forty years earlier, so we did not hire them. I then thought of Bain himself, managed to contact him, and arranged to have lunch together when I visited Boston. We both showed up. We had collaborated closely for over a year forty years earlier; meeting again after forty years, we would certainly not have recognized each other on the street. Old friends talking about old times — we had a wonderful lunch.</p>
</div>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Founding father of business strategy**
**中文解释**：商业策略鼻祖，指对策略管理学科有奠基性贡献的人；韩德生（BCG创始人）因提出学习曲线、经验曲线和成长—市占率矩阵而被尊为策略之父之一。
**商业造句">Henderson is revered as a founding father of business strategy — not for writing textbooks, but for creating frameworks that CEOs actually used to make decisions.*

2. **Case study**
**中文解释**：个案研究，指对特定企业或商业情境的深度分析；在商学院教学中，case study 是连接理论与实践的核心教学方法。
**商业造句">The Harvard case study on TSMC became required reading in MBA programs worldwide — not because it celebrated success, but because it revealed the logic behind each strategic choice.*

3. **Epilogue**
**中文解释**：后话、尾声，指在主线叙事结束后补充的后续故事；在传记写作中，「epilogue」通常暗示一段人际关系的延续或回响。
**商业造句">The epilogue to their forty-year friendship was not a deal or a board seat — it was a quiet lunch in Boston where two old men talked about the past.*

### 修辞赏析

<span class="rhetoric-note">
<span class="zh">「波特的眼睛突然亮了：『啊，你和韩德生合作过？』」——这个细节是张忠谋自传中令人会心一笑的瞬间。一个「看不起台湾这个落后地区的神态」的策略大师，在听到韩德生的名字瞬间，突然收起了傲慢。这个小故事不仅是张忠谋的私人恩怨记录，也是商业学术史的一个生动注脚：在策略学圈子里，韩德生的名字是一个「通行证」——谁和他合作过，就是「圈内人」。</span>
<span class="en">"Porter's eyes suddenly lit up: 'Ah, you worked with Henderson?'" — This detail is one of the most quietly satisfying moments in Chang's autobiography. A strategy guru who had displayed a demeanor of looking down on Taiwan as a "backward region" suddenly dropped his arrogance the instant he heard Henderson's name. This anecdote is not merely a personal grudge noted but a vivid footnote to business academia's history: in strategy circles, Henderson's name was a "passport" — whoever had worked with him was "in the club."</span>
</span>

### 第三章小结：总经理学习的核心命题

张忠谋在德仪的二十八年中，从三十三岁的「锗部」总经理一路成长。他学习行销——用自己的「绿邮票」激励技巧扭转了冷门产品的销售劣势；学习定价——以「诚信」建立了高于市场的溢价能力；学习会计和财务——三张报表的基础知识为未来的企业决策打下了根基；学习技术——保持每两三日与技术人员会谈；学习领导——定义领导力为「凝聚团队」和「带领团队赢」两个维度；学习策略——通过BCG的学习曲线理论，把直觉变成了系统框架。

这一章的核心命题是：一个工程师如何变成总经理？答案是：不断学习、不断质疑、不断把「不懂」变成「懂」。

<p class="en-para">Over twenty-eight years at TI, Chang grew from the thirty-three-year-old GM of the Germanium Division. <p class="en-para">The central question of this chapter is: How does an engineer become a general manager? The answer: Never stop learning, never stop questioning, never stop turning "I don't understand" into "I do."</p>
