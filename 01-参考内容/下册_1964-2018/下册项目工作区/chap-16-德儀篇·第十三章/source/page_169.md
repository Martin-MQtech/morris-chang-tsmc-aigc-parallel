# 德仪篇·第十三章｜从德仪到通用

## 找工作

我在德仪辞职的消息，在一、两天内就在《华尔街日报》、达拉斯两份日报及《电子新闻》发表。此后一、两星期，家中电话铃声响不停。我那时家里电话号码公开，所以陌生人要打电话给我只要查达拉斯电话簿或向电话公司询问就可以。而且我装了答话机，如我不在家，答话机可录下对方的讯息。

头两星期，大约有七、八个「接触」，就是对方试探我对他所提供的「机会」有无兴趣。这类试探大部分来自猎头者。经过一两次通话后，有好几个接触都被我谢绝了。

接下来两个月（一九八三年十一月下旬、十二年月及次年一月上旬），我与四个公司举行十几次面谈（光是通用器材公司就与我面谈了五次）。一九八四年一月三日，我收到凡爱创投的聘请；一月十日，我收到通用的聘请；一月十六日，我接受了通用的聘请。

另外两家公司与我面谈但后来没聘我。其中一家是因为董事会不能决定大策略（继续经营？卖掉？了结掉？）。另一家是柯达（Eastman Kodak，著名照相软片公司）。我与他们面谈经过，值得在此一提。

在二十世纪大部分，柯达是一家非常出色的公司。随著一般人民生活渐趋富裕，摄影愈普遍，柯达的软片生意也蒸蒸日上。我开始有些积蓄投资股票时，曾订阅一份很畅销的股票分析师的定期报告，柯达几乎每月都是「必须持有」的蓝筹股。

<p class="en-para"><b>Job Hunting</b></p>
<p class="en-para">News of my resignation from TI appeared in The Wall Street Journal, both Dallas daily newspapers, and Electronic News within a day or two. For the next week or two, my home phone rang constantly. My home phone number was publicly listed — anyone could find it through the Dallas phone directory or by calling the phone company. I had also installed an answering machine to record messages when I was out.</p>
<p class="en-para">In the first two weeks, there were about seven or eight "contacts" — parties testing my interest in "opportunities" they could offer. Most of these probes came from headhunters. After one or two phone calls, I declined several.</p>
<p class="en-para">Over the next two months (late November and December 1983, and early January 1984), I held over a dozen interviews with four companies (General Instruments alone interviewed me five times). On January 3, 1984, I received an offer from a venture capital firm; on January 10, an offer from General Instruments; on January 16, I accepted the General Instruments offer.</p>
<p class="en-para">Two other companies interviewed me but did not extend offers. One couldn't decide on its board-level strategy (continue operating? Sell? Wind down?). The other was Eastman Kodak, the renowned photographic film company. My interview experience with them is worth recounting.</p>
<p class="en-para">For most of the 20th century, Kodak was a superb company. As ordinary people's lives grew more prosperous and photography became more universal, Kodak's film business thrived. When I had begun investing in stocks, I subscribed to a popular analyst's periodic reports. Kodak was almost every month a "must-hold" blue chip.</p>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Headhunter probes (猎头者的试探)**
**中文解释**：猎头者在得知高阶主管离职后，以「机会」为饵进行的初步接触；在猎头行业中，「接触」（contact）是正式招聘的前奏——它的目的是评估候选人对机会的兴趣程度，而非立即提供具体职位。
**商业造句">The headhunter's initial "contact" is a calibrated probe — not a job offer, but an interest gauge designed to assess whether the departed executive is open to new opportunities.*

2. **Blue chip "must-hold" (蓝筹股「必须持有」)**
**中文解释**：指分析师对柯达的评级——「必须持有」是最强的买入建议；在投资分析中，「蓝筹股」（blue chip）指那些历史悠久、财务稳健、长期表现优异的大型上市公司。
**商业造句">Kodak was a "must-hold" blue chip in analyst reports throughout the 1970s and 1980s — a seemingly eternal growth story that would be devastated by the digital photography revolution.*
