<p class="en-para">## Supplementary Notes</p>
<p class="en-para">This chronology covers Morris Chang's important life milestones from birth through 2024. Each age-corresponding entry was originally recorded in traditional Chinese. In compiling this text, I preserved the format and content of the original records as closely as possible, making only corrections to typesetting and a few typographical errors.</p>
<p class="en-para">The following points warrant special note:</p>
<p class="en-para">1. The original text calculates age using the traditional East Asian reckoning system, in which a person is one year old at birth.</p>
<p class="en-para">2. All Republic of China calendar dates in the original have been uniformly converted to the Common Era.</p>
<p class="en-para">3. Some years have gaps in recorded events (such as the relatively sparse records from 1936 to 1940). These gaps reflect the state of the original source material.</p>
<p class="en-para">4. The post-retirement APEC records in the original were sparse; supplementary entries here are based on publicly available information.</p>
