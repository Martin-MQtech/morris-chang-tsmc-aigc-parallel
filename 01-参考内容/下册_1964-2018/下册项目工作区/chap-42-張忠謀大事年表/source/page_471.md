**Morris Chang: Chronology**

**Early Years (1930-1955)**

| Year | Age | Event |
|---|---|---|
| 1930 | Born | Born in Ningbo, Zhejiang Province |
| 1949 | 18 | Entered Harvard University as a freshman |
| 1952 | 21 | B.S. in Mechanical Engineering, MIT |
| 1953 | 22 | M.S. in Mechanical Engineering, MIT |
| 1955 | 24 | Entered Sylvania's semiconductor division, beginning his career in semiconductors |

**Texas Instruments Era (1958-1983)**

| Year | Age | Event |
|---|---|---|
| 1958 | 27 | Joined Texas Instruments |
| 1964 | 33 | Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering, Stanford University. Returned to TI as General Manager of the Transistor Division |
| 1967 | 36 | Vice President of TI |

**TSMC Era (1987-2018)**

| Year | Age | Event |
|---|---|---|
| 1987 | 56 | Founded TSMC |
| 1994 | 63 | TSMC IPO on the Taiwan Stock Exchange |
| 1997 | 66 | TSMC ADR listed on the NYSE. Published Volume 1 of autobiography |
| 2001 | 70 | Married Susan (Sophie) Chang |
| 2005 | 74 | First succession: Tsai Li-hsing as CEO |
| 2009 | 78 | Returned as CEO following the global financial crisis |
| 2017 | 86 | Announced retirement and succession plan |
| 2018 | 87 | Retired June 5. Liu De-yin became chairman and Wei Che-chia became president |
