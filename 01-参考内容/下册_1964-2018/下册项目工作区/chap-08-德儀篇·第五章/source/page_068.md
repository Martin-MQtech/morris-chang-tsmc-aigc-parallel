# 德仪篇·第五章｜四个上司

吕斯给我第一个「业务单位总经理」职位，也是我一生最欣赏的上司。这个上司与我的接触，几乎完全是非正式、一对一、建设性的。他很少召开幕僚会，但常常到我的办公室找我，我也常常到他办公室找他。那时我们的办公室墙壁都是玻璃，而且相隔不远，所以只要走出自己办公室几步路，就可以看到对方是否独自在办公室。我们找对方，从来不是要瞎聊，总有大或小问题要找对方谈谈。这样近便的接触，使得我们之间从来没有误会；即使最后决定是他的而不是我最喜欢的，我也会了解他的理由，而且全力执行他的决定。

有这样好的上司，可惜只有三年（一九六四至六七年）。彪希一到半导体部，吕斯就被架空。一直到我离开德仪（一九八三年）我仍偶然与吕斯联络。吕斯离开德仪后的事业生涯并不顺畅，他接到了一个小公司 CEO 的职位，吕斯也没有把这家小公司做大。他五十几岁就退休了，回到达拉斯住，我与他吃了几顿饭，感受到他绵绵不得志的心情。他的「德仪后」职业生涯，与他在德仪时的节节上升（直到被架空）截然不同。

「机会」与「运气」，与「成功」紧不可分！

<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Lewis gave me my first "business unit general manager" position, and he was the most admired boss of my entire career. This boss's interactions with me were almost entirely informal, one-on-one, and constructive. He rarely called staff meetings but often came to my office to see me, and I often went to his. At that time, our office walls were all glass and not far apart, so a few steps out of your own office would tell you whether the other person was alone. We never sought each other out just to chat — there was always a problem, large or small, to discuss. This proximity meant there were never any misunderstandings between us. Even when the final decision was his and not my preference, I understood his reasoning and would execute it with full commitment.</p>
<p class="en-para">Such a good boss — and I had him for only three years (1964 to 1967). The moment Shep arrived at the semiconductor division, Lewis was sidelined. I still kept in occasional touch with Lewis until I left TI in 1983. Lewis's post-TI career was not smooth. He took a CEO position at a small company but did not grow it. He retired in his fifties, moved back to Dallas, and I had several meals with him, sensing his lingering frustration. His "post-TI" career was a stark contrast to his steady rise at TI (until he was sidelined).</p>
<p class="en-para">"Opportunity" and "luck" are inseparable from "success"!</p>
</div>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Informal, one-on-one, constructive**
**中文解释**：非正式、一对一、建设性，指最高效的管理沟通方式；在组织行为学中，这种沟通模式被证明比正式会议更能建立信任和解决实质问题。
**商业造句">The best performance feedback I ever received was informal, one-on-one, and constructive — a fifteen-minute conversation in a coffee line that changed how I thought about my role.*

2. **Lingering frustration**
**中文解释**：绵绵不得志，指长期无法实现抱负的心理状态；在职业生涯研究中，「不得志」往往比「失败」更具心理破坏性——因为它意味着希望还在，但机会已逝。
**商业造句">His lingering frustration was not about money — he had made enough. It was about the gap between what he believed he could do and what the market would let him.*

3. **Inseparable from success**
**中文解释」：与成功紧不可分，指「机会」和「运气」是成功的必要条件而非充分条件；在张忠谋的叙事中，吕斯的「不得志」不是因为能力不足，而是因为「运气」不再站在他那边。
**商业造句">Talent and hard work are necessary but not sufficient — opportunity and luck are inseparable from success, a truth that the successful rarely acknowledge and the unsuccessful cannot accept.*

### 修辞赏析

<span class="rhetoric-note">
<span class="zh">「有这样好的上司，可惜只有三年。」——张忠谋用一个「可惜」，道出了所有职场人共同的遗憾：最好的上司往往留不住。这不是因为上司不好，而是因为组织政治的残酷逻辑——新老板到来时，旧老板的亲信往往首当其冲。吕斯被彪希架空，不是因为吕斯做得不好，而是因为彪希需要「直接管理重要业务单位」。张忠谋对吕斯的怀念，不仅是对一个人的感情，更是对一种管理风格的怀念——那种「非正式、一对一、建设性」的沟通方式，在他此后十六年的彪希时代再也没有出现过。</span>
<span class="en">"Such a good boss — and I had him for only three years." — Chang uses a "unfortunately" to voice the universal workplace regret: the best bosses are often kept not long. This is not because the boss is not good, but because of the cruel logic of organizational politics — when a new boss arrives, the old boss's allies are often the first to go. Lewis was sidelined by Shep not because Lewis performed poorly, but because Shep "needed to directly manage the key business units." Chang's nostalgia for Lewis is not merely personal affection but a longing for a management style — that "informal, one-on-one, constructive" mode of communication that would never reappear during his sixteen years under Shep.</span>
</span>

### 背景知识延伸

**玻璃墙办公室**：张忠谋在自传中描述的「玻璃墙办公室」，是六〇年代美国企业常见的开放式办公设计。这种设计不仅是为了节约成本，更是一种管理哲学的体现——透明、平等、随时可沟通。张忠谋和吕斯之间的「非正式、一对一、建设性」沟通，很大程度上得益于这种物理空间的便利。当彪希到来后，虽然办公室的物理结构没有变，但沟通的方式却从「非正式」变成了「正式」——这不仅是个人风格的差异，也是组织文化变迁的信号。
