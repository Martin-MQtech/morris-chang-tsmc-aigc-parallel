# 德仪篇·第五章｜四个上司

海格底退休时选择夏伯特为他的继承董事长。事后看来，这是海格底一生稀有的败笔。但当时（一九七六年）看来，却是当然的事。夏伯特在二十五岁加入德仪（一九四八年）后就追随海格底。当海格底把全公司赌在半导体业上时（一九五二年），夏伯特主管半导体部。夏伯特与海格底一同把德仪推上半导体霸主宝座。此后，夏伯特的事业一帆风顺，升到执行副总裁兼营运长，再 CEO；到一九七六年，继承海格底的董事长职位已是水到渠成。

夏伯特正直、直爽、相当聪明，但也有大缺点：他刚愎自用，有了成见后就不再听别人的意见。在一九七二年他决定进入「消费者产品」（计算机、电子表、教育玩具、家庭电脑等）后，虽然几年内就开始亏本，几位董事及他的下属（包括我）开始劝他退出，但他仍坚持下去，一直撑到几乎拖垮整个公司。

还有，他与半导体技术脱节。当公司渐渐庞大，领导高层的内务、外务逐渐增加，但大半与技术无关。为了「日理万机」，自己倒与快速进步的技术脱节了。以夏伯特为例：五〇年代他是半导体行家；六〇年代提升为主管全公司业务的执行副总，开始与半导体逐渐脱节；到了七〇年代，他已成半导体外行——更可悲的是，他自己还不知道已脱节，仍以为七〇年代的半导体业与二十年前一样。

<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">When Haggerty retired, he selected Shepherd as his successor as Chairman. In hindsight, this was one of the rare misjudgments of Haggerty's career. But at the time (1976), it seemed the natural choice. Shepherd had joined TI at twenty-five (1948) and had followed Haggerty ever since. When Haggerty bet the entire company on semiconductors (1952), Shepherd ran the semiconductor division. Shepherd and Haggerty together had propelled TI to semiconductor dominance. After that, Shepherd's career had risen steadily — Executive VP and COO, then CEO. By 1976, succeeding Haggerty as Chairman was the natural culmination.</p>
<p class="en-para">Shepherd was upright, straightforward, and quite intelligent. But he had a major flaw: he was rigidly self-opinionated. Once he had formed a view, he would no longer listen to others' input. In 1972, he decided to enter "consumer products" (calculators, electronic watches, educational toys, home computers, and so on). Although losses materialized within a few years, and several board members along with his subordinates (myself included) urged him to exit, he persisted — carrying on until it nearly bankrupted the entire company.</p>
<p class="en-para">Moreover, he grew disconnected from semiconductor technology. As the company expanded, the internals and external obligations of senior leadership multiplied — most of them unrelated to technology. In order to "manage a thousand things a day," he lost touch with fast-advancing technology. Take Shepherd as an example: in the 1950s, he was a semiconductor expert. In the 1960s, promoted to Executive VP overseeing all company operations, he began to gradually lose touch with semiconductors. By the 1970s, he had become a semiconductor layman — and more tragically, he himself did not know he had lost touch, still believing that the semiconductor industry of the 1970s was the same as it had been twenty years earlier.</p>
</div>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Rigidly self-opinionated**
**中文解释**：刚愎自用，指一旦形成观点就拒绝接受反馈；在组织行为学中，这种特质在创始人或长期任职的 CEO 中尤为常见，且与「企业衰退」高度相关。
**商业造句">The founder's greatest strength — an unshakeable conviction in his own vision — became his greatest weakness when the market shifted and he could not.</p>

2. **Consumer products misadventure**
**中文解释**：消费者产品歧途，指B2B企业盲目进入B2C市场的战略错误；在德仪的案例中，这不仅消耗了资源，更使公司从核心业务中失焦。
**商业造句">TI's consumer products misadventure became a textbook case of a B2B giant mistaking its manufacturing prowess for marketing skill — a mistake that cost shareholders a decade of returns.*

3. **Disconnected from technology**
**中文解释**：与技术脱节，指管理者因行政事务而失去对技术前沿的感知；在科技驱动型产业中，「技术脱节」是管理者最危险的能力退化。
**商业造句">The CTO had not written code in five years and had not reviewed a technical architecture in three — a classic case of disconnection from technology that rendered his strategic advice hollow.*

### 修辞赏析

<span class="rhetoric-note">
<span class="zh">「更可悲的是，他自己还不知道已脱节，仍以为七〇年代的半导体业与二十年前一样。」——张忠谋用这句冷静的判断，提出了一个管理学中最为致命的现象：「无意识的无能」（unconscious incompetence）。夏伯特不是故意忽视技术——他是真心以为自己还懂技术。这种「不知道自己不知道」的状态，比「知道自己不知道」危险得多。因为它消除了学习的原动力。张忠谋对夏伯特的这段分析，不仅是对一个人、更是对所有「在位太久、脱节太久」的管理者的深刻警示。</span>
<span class="en">"And more tragically, he himself did not know he had lost touch." — With this calm judgment, Chang identifies one of the deadliest phenomena in management: "unconscious incompetence." Shepherd was not deliberately ignoring technology — he genuinely believed he still understood it. This state of "not knowing that you don't know" is far more dangerous than "knowing that you don't know," because it removes the motivation to learn. Chang's analysis of Shepherd is not merely about one man — it is a profound warning to all managers who have "stayed too long and disconnected too deeply."</span>
</span>

### 背景知识延伸

**一九七〇年代德仪半导体的两个技术挑战**：张忠谋指出，七〇年代德仪半导体面临两个最大技术挑战：如何追上（日本及美国）竞争者的 MOS 记忆体，以及如何追上英特尔的微处理器。这两个挑战都是技术问题。当张忠谋与这两个技术问题时，夏伯特始终不了解他的需要——他不但不给资源，反而为公司设立了新焦点（消费者产品），把公司的资源都给了新焦点。张忠谋总结道：「德仪自此失焦迷途。」这五个字浓缩了德仪从半导体霸主走向衰退的核心原因。

第五章小结：四个上司、两种遗产

张忠谋在德仪的二十年间，经历了四种截然不同的管理风格。吕斯的「非正式、一对一、建设性」让他学会了信任和沟通；彪希的「正式、集体会议、书面报告」让他学会了如何在层级中保持效率；海格底的「远见和勇气」让他看到了最高管理者应该如何押注未来；夏伯特的「刚愎自用」则让他警惕：一个管理者如何在权力中失去学习的能力。

对张忠谋而言，这四个上司带来的最大遗产不是知识，而是一种「镜鉴」——他从每个人身上都看到了自己想成为和不想成为的样子。

<p class="en-para">Over twenty years at TI, Chang experienced four distinct management styles. Lewis's "informal, one-on-one, constructive" taught him trust and communication. Shep's "formal, group-based, written" taught him how to maintain efficiency in a hierarchy. Haggerty's "vision and courage" showed him how top leaders should bet on the future. Shepherd's "rigid self-opinion" warned him how managers can lose the ability to learn amid power.</p>
<p class="en-para">The greatest legacy of these four bosses was not knowledge, but a mirror — from each person, Chang saw both what he wanted to become and what he did not.</p>
