# 德仪篇·第五章｜四个上司

不回「话」的结果是——后来连「卡」都没有了。彪希也渐渐在我记忆中淡出。但是几年前（二〇一三年），我忽然从一个德仪老同事处听到：彪希出自传了！我买了一本看。他主要想澄清他在德仪最后几年——也就是我认为他与夏伯特非常「一致」的时期——他与夏伯特的关系。照彪希现在的说法，那时他和夏伯特并不完全「一致」。看起来，事过三十几年后，他已不想为当时德仪衰退负责。但是，为什么等那么久才出书？尤其，为什么等到夏伯特已离世、「死无对证」才出书？

关于我，彪希的自传也提到了。他说我是「全世界最佳半导体经营者」。这句话假如我在任半导体总经理的后期听到，我会受鼓励。四十年后在台湾听到——太晚了。

<p class="en-para">The consequence of not returning the "words" was that eventually even the "cards" stopped coming. Shep gradually faded from my memory. But a few years ago (2013), I suddenly heard from an old TI colleague: Shep had written an autobiography! I bought a copy. His main objective seemed to be clarifying his relationship with Shepherd during his final years at TI — the period when I believed he and Shepherd were very "aligned." According to Shep's account today, he and Shepherd were not entirely "aligned" back then. It seems that more than thirty years later, he no longer wanted to take responsibility for TI's decline during that period. But why wait so long to publish? And especially, why wait until Shepherd had passed away — "dead men tell no tales" — to publish?</p>
<p class="en-para">Regarding me, Shep's autobiography also mentioned me. He called me "the best semiconductor operator in the world." If I had heard this in the later years of my tenure as Semiconductor GM, I would have been encouraged. Hearing it forty years later in Taiwan — it was too late.</p>
</div>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **The best semiconductor operator in the world**
**中文解释**：全世界最佳半导体经营者，指对一个半导体企业管理者的最高评价；在商业叙事中，「经营者」（operator）不同于「发明家」（inventor）或「企业家」（entrepreneur），它强调的是「把事情做好」的日常管理能力。
**商业造句">Being called "the best semiconductor operator in the world" is not a compliment about technology — it is a compliment about execution, about consistently delivering results quarter after quarter.*

2. **Too late**
**中文解释**：太晚了，指认可来得太迟，已经无法产生实际效用；在职业生涯中，「迟到的认可」比「不认可」更令人感伤——因为它证明了你的价值，却无法改变你的命运。
**商业造句">The apology, when it finally came, was sincere — but three years too late to undo the damage to her reputation and her career.*

3. **Dead men tell no tales**
**中文解释**：死无对证，指当事人已无法为自己辩护；在历史和商业回忆录中，等到关键当事人已离世才发表争议性叙事，往往被认为是不公正的策略。
**商业造句">She waited until her former CEO had passed before publishing her version of events — dead men tell no tales, and she knew it.*

### 修辞赏析

<span class="rhetoric-note">
<span class="zh">「四十年后在台湾听到——太晚了。」——这句话是张忠谋自传中最为伤感的旁白之一。一个前上司的赞美，在四十年后才到来——那时被赞美者已经从「三十几岁风华」走到了「五十几岁憔悴」，又走过了创办台积电的艰辛岁月。张忠谋用破折号引出「太晚了」三个字，语气平静得像在说别人的故事。但正是这种平静，让读者感受到了一种深沉的悲凉：不是愤怒、不是遗憾，只是一种对时间无情流逝的默默接受。</span>
<span class="en">"Hearing it forty years later in Taiwan — it was too late." — This is one of the most quietly devastating asides in Chang's autobiography. A former boss's praise, arriving forty years late — by which time the praised had journeyed from "the vigor of thirty-something" through "the weariness of fifty-something" and on to the trials of founding TSMC. Chang uses a dash to introduce the three characters "太晚了" (it was too late), his tone calm as if speaking of someone else's story. But it is precisely this calm that makes the reader feel a profound desolation: not anger, not regret — just a silent acceptance of time's merciless passage.</span>
</span>

### 背景知识延伸

**死在后面的人书写历史**：彪希的自传在二〇一三年出版，而夏伯特已于二〇〇〇年去世。张忠谋对这种「死无对证」的叙事策略显然持保留态度——他没有明批评，只是用两个精准的提问让读者自己去判断。这种处理方式在传记文学中极为常见：与其与逝者争辩，不如把事实交给读者。张忠谋的克制在这里再次展现了他作为一个管理者的核心特质——他更关心的是下一步该怎么做，而不是上一件事谁对谁错。
