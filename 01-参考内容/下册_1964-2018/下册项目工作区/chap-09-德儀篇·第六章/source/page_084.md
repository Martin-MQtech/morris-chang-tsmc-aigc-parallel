# 德仪篇·第六章｜实现「美国梦」

一九七〇年代集成电路市场的实际发展是：MOS 逐渐取代双极。虽然双极集成电路市场逐渐缩小，但德仪的高市占率及高获利率着实为德仪争取了时间，让德仪有机会在 MOS 竞争——这是本书第九章的故事。

一九七〇年裁员与「年资原则」——

我经营的德仪集成电路部门，倒是这次景气萧条的佼佼者。我们受惠于高技术（MSI）、低成本（休士顿新晶圆厂）以及定制产品，所以在业界一片哀号声中，还能每季赚不少钱。这一切当然归功于我们战略的成功，以及高士气同仁团队的执行。

虽然集成电路部门不需要裁员，但我们的同僚——锗、矽以及别的晶体管及其他零件部门——却相当淒惨。前几年扩充得太快了！于是夏伯特和彪希决定：所有半导体部门一致裁员，幅度大约十分之一。一致裁员的结果会是晶体管等部门还是人太多，而集成电路部门却是人太少。夏伯特和彪希的简单补救办法就是：调晶体管部门多余的人给集成电路部门。这当然只是一个算术式、纸上谈兵的补救办法。真正的问题是：集成电路裁了有集成电路经验的员工，却以没有集成电路经验的员工来弥补——简直是一个闹剧！但为了「一致行动」团队精神，我们还是这样做了。

裁员的幅度可以让会计财务计算，但裁谁？这问题在彪希的经理人会议里众议纷纷。很多经理人主张应以考绩为准——最低考绩的员工被裁。在这点上，我采取了坚定立场。德仪没有工会，也绝对不要工会，所以裁员标准一定要公平且具有公信力才行。以考绩为准不会公平——因为每个员工考绩是他上司打的。每个上司打考绩标准都不一样，即使同一个上司同一个员工在不同时间考绩也不一样。依考绩裁员实在是自找麻烦。

唯一不容质疑的公平裁员标准，是年资。最后进来的员工最先被裁，所有职阶都如此。当然，这又产生另一趣剧：几个月前才被告知公司很需要他的新员工忽然就要走路了——公司忽然不需要他了。基本上，这只能怪高阶经理人（业务单位总经理及以上），他们雇人太快！

<p class="en-para">The 1970 layoffs and the "seniority principle" —</p>
<p class="en-para">The IC division I ran was actually the star of the downturn. Benefiting from high technology (MSI), low costs (Houston new fab), and custom products, we continued to earn substantial profits quarter after quarter while the rest of the industry wailed. This was the fruit of our strategic success and the execution of our high-morale team.</p>
<p class="en-para">While the IC division did not need to cut headcount, our colleagues in germanium, silicon, and other component divisions were devastated — they had expanded too rapidly in prior years! Shepherd and Shep therefore decided: all semiconductor divisions would cut headcount uniformly by roughly 10%. The uniform cut meant the transistor divisions would still be overstaffed, while the IC division would be understaffed. Shepherd and Shep's simple remedy was to transfer surplus staff from the transistor divisions to the IC division. This was, of course, nothing more than an arithmetic, on-paper remedy. The real problem: the IC division was losing experienced IC people while gaining staff with no IC experience — a farce! But for the sake of "uniform action" and team spirit, we did it anyway.</p>
<p class="en-para">The scale of layoffs could be calculated by finance — but who to cut? The question generated heated debate in Shep's managerial meetings. Many managers advocated using performance ratings — the lowest-rated employees would be cut. On this point, I took a firm stand. TI had no unions and wanted none, so the layoff standard had to be fair and credible. Using performance ratings would not be fair — because each employee's rating was given by their own boss. Every boss had different rating standards; even the same boss would rate the same employee differently at different times. Basing layoffs on performance ratings was asking for trouble.</p>
<p class="en-para">The only unquestionably fair standard was seniority. Last in, first out — across all levels. Of course, this produced another farce: new hires who had been told just months earlier how much the company needed them were suddenly told to leave. The company suddenly no longer needed them. Fundamentally, this could only be blamed on senior management (business unit GMs and above) — they had hired too fast!</p>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Last In, First Out (LIFO)**
**中文解释**：后进先出裁员法，指以年资为裁员标准——年资最浅的先被裁；在公平性上最无争议，但也可能导致「裁了新员工、留下低效老员工」的反效果。
**商业造句**: *The LIFO layoff standard was fairest on paper but pernicious in practice — it penalized the new hires who had been the most carefully selected.*

2. **Uniform cut vs. selective cut**
**中文解释**：一致裁员 vs. 选择性裁员，指所有部门按相同比例裁 vs. 按业绩裁；张忠谋认为前者会造成「不需要裁的部门裁了、需要裁的部门裁不够」的荒诞。
**商业造句">The uniform cut was solidarity theater — a way of spreading pain across divisions while pretending that everyone shared equally in the sacrifice.*

3. **Performance rating subjectivity**
**中文解释**：考绩主观性，指因评分标准不一而导致考绩结果不可靠；在裁员决策中，以主观考绩为准最常见但也最容易引发劳资纠纷。
**商业造句">Using performance ratings to choose who gets laid off is like using a reading from a broken thermometer to decide if someone has a fever — the instrument itself is the problem.*

### 修辞赏析

<span class="rhetoric-note">
<span class="zh">「简直是一个闹剧！但为了『一致行动』团队精神，我们还是这样做了。」——张忠谋用「闹剧」这个词，道出了「一刀切」组织决策中最常见的荒诞性。他知道这不合理，但他也知道：在某些时刻，「公平感」比「效率」更重要。这是他在管理实践中最具代表性的两难选择——不是「对与错」的两难，而是「公平与效率」的两难。他选择了公平（年资标准），同时也为此付出了效率的代价。</span>
<span class="en">"A farce! But for the sake of 'uniform action' and team spirit, we did it anyway." — Chang uses the word "farce" to name the absurdity most common in "one-size-fits-all" organizational decisions. He knew it was irrational, but he also knew: at certain moments, "fairness" matters more than "efficiency." This is the most representative dilemma in his management practice — not a dilemma of "right versus wrong," but of "fairness versus efficiency." He chose fairness (seniority standard) and paid the efficiency price accordingly.</span>
</span>
