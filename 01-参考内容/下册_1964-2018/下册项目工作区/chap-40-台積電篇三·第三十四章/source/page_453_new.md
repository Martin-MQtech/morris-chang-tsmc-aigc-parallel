<p class="en-para">**Duties of the President**
(1) Lead and operate the company under the board's strategic guidance, across strategy, operations, marketing, and finance. (2) Report to the board of directors, not to the chairman. (3) The entire company reports to the president.</p>
<p class="en-para">To emphasize that the dual-leadership system was neither a 'chairman system' nor a 'president system,' I deliberately placed the two roles on equal footing in rank and compensation, and stressed that the chairmanship was a full-time position. I even suggested that the chairman should 'chair' or 'attend' the meetings on which I had relied heavily during my tenure as chairman.</p>
<p class="en-para">On July 2, 2017, I convened Tim and Morris, showed them the three documents above, and sought their consent. Both were deeply excited and agreed without hesitation.</p>
<p class="en-para">August 7–8, 2017, were the board's third-quarter meeting dates. On the evening of August 6, I had my regular dinner with the independent directors. Because I needed to discuss the succession plan I had drafted, I specifically submitted it in writing at 4 p.m. before dinner. The discussion was quite vigorous—besides the five independent directors, attendees included representatives of the Development Fund, Fan-Che Tseng, Tim Liu, and Morris Wei. I also visited the Development Fund in person on September 28 to explain the plan to Mei-ling Chen and received her agreement.</p>
<p class="en-para">On September 28, 2017, I formally presented the board with three documents: the 'Succession Plan,' 'Duties of the Chairman,' and 'Duties of the President.' After minor textual revisions, the documents were approved unanimously by all directors present. I also announced the director nominations for the next term (June 2018 through May 2021): among the ten directors, besides myself preparing to retire and one independent director retiring for health reasons, the remaining eight were all nominated for re-election.</p>
<p class="en-para">In other words, the June 2018 succession plan had, by September 2017, already received the approval of both the current and incoming boards, and had been enthusiastically accepted by the two successors, Tim Liu and Morris Wei.</p>
