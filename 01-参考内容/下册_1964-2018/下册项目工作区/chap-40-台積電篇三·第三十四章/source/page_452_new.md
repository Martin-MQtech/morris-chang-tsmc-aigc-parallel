<p class="en-para">Many Western companies adopt a 'dual-leadership' system. The president and CEO is the company's operational leader; the chairman leads the board of directors, supervises the president, and because important company decisions require board approval, the chairman also becomes the final gatekeeper of corporate decisions. If the chairman and president can cooperate closely, each placing the company's interests above all and setting personal interests aside, the dual-leadership system should be an excellent arrangement.</p>
<p class="en-para">In TSMC's special circumstances in 2017, the two co-CEOs had been in place for four years, and with the chairman about to retire, the viable succession system was恐怕 nothing other than dual leadership; otherwise one of the two would have to leave the company. That was something I was unwilling to see.</p>
<p class="en-para">Moreover, the two co-CEOs had seemed to cooperate quite well over the past four years, which gave me considerable confidence that they could continue to work together for TSMC's future under the dual-leadership system.</p>
<p class="en-para">Before deciding to propose my succession plan to the board, the only person I consulted was Vice Chairman Fan-Che Tseng. He fully agreed with my thinking.</p>
<p class="en-para">(1) MC (the initials of my English name) would complete this term as chairman and director, and retire completely from the company at the conclusion of the June 2018 shareholders' meeting.</p>
<p class="en-para">(2) The new board elected at the 2018 shareholders' meeting would select the company's 'dual leaders': Tim Liu as chairman, Morris Wei as president.</p>
<p class="en-para">(3) The chairman and president would hold equal rank and compensation (including salary and dividends).</p>
<p class="en-para">**Duties of the Chairman**
(1) Lead the board of directors. (2) Serve as the company's highest representative to society and government. (3) Act as the final gatekeeper of company decisions. (4) Hold a full-time position.</p>
<p class="en-para">I suggested that the chairman should continue to chair the 'capital investment' meetings, attend the 'pricing meetings,' 'Marketing Week,' and earnings calls, and maintain seamless two-way communication with the president.</p>
