<p class="en-para">TSMC was founded in 1987, and every ten years since then we have held a celebration. For the twentieth and thirtieth anniversaries, we invited important global customers, suppliers, and partner organizations to share in the joy.</p>
<p class="en-para">For the tenth anniversary in 1997, TSMC was already quite prosperous but still a mid-scale company. We invited a domestic symphony orchestra to perform Beethoven's Ninth Symphony (with choir). In 2007 and 2017, TSMC had become a large-scale company, and we invited internationally renowned orchestras to perform Beethoven's Ninth Symphony (with choir). I love this symphony because it is both beautiful and majestic, and requires a full symphony orchestra of over eighty players and a choir of over ninety to deliver a fine performance. Ending each decade celebration with this work was a fitting expression of TSMC's ambition.</p>
<p class="en-para">The 2017 thirtieth-anniversary celebration was already scheduled for October 23. Since we had finalized the succession plan for the following year in September, I decided to announce it publicly in early October, before the anniversary. This would allow partners to become better acquainted with my successor earlier, and avoid any possible complications arising from an announcement after the celebration.</p>
<p class="en-para">On the afternoon of October 2, at 2 p.m., I first announced the succession plan, the duties of the chairman, and the duties of the president to TSMC's internal staff. At 4:30 p.m. I held a press conference to make the announcement publicly. On October 5 and 6, I gave interviews to seven domestic and international media outlets, including Japan's Nikkei and Bloomberg News.</p>
