### 时间轴

| 年份 | 职务 | Position |
|---|---|---|
| 1958 | 加入德州仪器公司，任锗电晶体良率工程师 | Joined TI as a germanium transistor yield engineer |
| 1961 | 锗部经理，开创锗电晶体大量生产 | Manager, Germanium Division — pioneered mass production of germanium transistors |
| 1964 | 获斯坦福博士学位，返回德仪 | Earned Stanford Ph.D., returned to TI |
| 1964 | 升任锗部总经理 | Promoted to General Manager, Germanium Division |
| 1966 | 转任矽部总经理 | Transferred as General Manager, Silicon Division |
| 1967 | 升任积体电路部总经理，同年晋任德仪副总裁 | Named GM of Integrated Circuit Division; promoted to TI Vice President the same year |
| 1972 | 升任集团副总裁暨半导体集团总经理 | Promoted to Group VP and General Manager of the Semiconductor Group |
| 1978 | 转任消费者产品集团总经理 | Transferred as General Manager, Consumer Products Group |
| 1980 | 离开消费者产品集团 | Left Consumer Products Group |
| 1983 | 自德仪辞职，结束二十五年生涯 | Resigned from TI, concluding a twenty-five-year career |

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">The timeline above traces Morris Chang's ascent from a fresh Ph.D. joining TI in 1958 to his resignation in 1983. In those twenty-five years, he rose from an entry-level engineer to Group Vice President — the highest position a Chinese-American had reached in a major American corporation at the time. The trajectory was not linear: he moved from germanium to silicon, from silicon to integrated circuits, from ICs to the entire semiconductor group, and finally to consumer products. Each transfer was either a promotion or a lateral move that broadened his scope. The story of those twenty-five years — the ambition, the triumphs, the frustrations, and the eventual resignation — unfolds in the chapters that follow.</p>
</div>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Yield engineer**
**中文解释**：良率工程师，负责提升芯片制造合格率的技术人员；在半导体制造业中，良率直接决定利润。
**商业造句**：*A yield engineer's work is invisible when things go right and blamed when they go wrong — but a 1% yield improvement can translate to millions in profit.*

2. **Mass production**
**中文解释**：大量生产，指将实验室技术转化为可规模化制造的产品；是半导体商业化的关键一步。
**商业造句**：*Moving from prototype to mass production in semiconductors requires not just engineering but an entire supply chain ecosystem.*

3. **Group Vice President**
**中文解释**：集团副总裁，指大型企业中负责一个完整业务集团的高层管理者，通常直接向总裁或执行长汇报。
**商业造句**：*Reaching Group VP at a Fortune 500 company typically requires not only functional excellence but also political acumen and cross-divisional credibility.*

4. **Lateral move**
**中文解释**：平调，指在同一层级上转换职能或业务领域；在职业生涯中，平调常被用来拓宽管理者的经验广度。
**商业造句**：*Her lateral move from sales to operations was initially seen as a demotion — but it gave her the cross-functional credibility needed to become CEO.*

5. **Trajectory**
**中文解释**：轨迹，指职业或事业的发展路径；在传记写作中，trajectory 常用来描述一个人从起点到终点的整体走向。
**商业造句**：*The founder's career trajectory — from academic researcher to venture capitalist to policy advisor — defies conventional categorization.*

### 背景知识延伸

**德仪（TI）的黄金年代**：一九五八至一九八三年，正是德州仪器从一家地质物理公司转型为全球半导体龙头的关键时期。TI 在此期间发明了集成电路（Jack Kilby，1958）、推出了第一批商用矽电晶体、开发了第一台手持计算器（1967），并一度成为全球最大的半导体公司。张忠谋的二十五年，恰好覆盖了 TI 最辉煌的上升期。

**「锗→矽」的技术迭代**：张忠谋在德仪的第一个重要成就，是解决锗电晶体的大量生产问题。但就在他升任锗部总经理前后，产业正快速从锗转向矽。矽的天然氧化物（SiO₂）可作为优质绝缘材料，且矽的能带间隙更大、热稳定性更好。张忠谋敏锐地推动部门从锗转型矽，这一决策奠定了他后来在积体电路领域的管理地位。

**华人职业天花板**：张忠谋在 1972 年升任集团副总裁时，是美国大型企业中职位最高的华人。此后十年，他始终未能再进一步——离「总裁」仅一步之遥，却始终无法跨越。这段经历成为他 1983 年辞职的重要伏笔，也是理解他后来接受台湾邀请、创办台积电的关键背景。
