## 员工分红、认股

美国企业也有分红，但对绝大部分员工而言，分红仅是固定薪资的一小部分。台湾企业不同，假使景气好，企业盈余高，员工分红可以是固定薪资的一大部分，甚至比固定薪资更高。

一九八零年代中期，联华电子（联电）已有相当丰盛的员工现金分红；一九八七年起，联电开始以股票面值计算的股票分红，更让其他公司员工啧啧称羡。台积电尚未成立时，曾繁城就先几乎以工会领袖的姿态"照会"我：成立台积电后，每年都要有员工分红。

但是成立后我们每月都亏损，当然没有理由分红。一九八八年七月（成立后十七个月），台积电第一次做到当月收支平衡。其实，一个月的不亏损也不是一般公司分红的依据；但鉴于成立前曾繁城的"照会"，以及我"尽早分红"的诺言，我决定于当月结账后就发相当于半个月的奖金。全体同仁皆大欢喜。

八八年底结账，当年营业净利为一亿三千余万元，几乎把八七年的亏损都赚回来了。我们又发了一次奖金，大家更皆大欢喜。

除奖金外，八八年十一月第一次现金增资，我们开始员工认股。此后每次现金增资（八九年十月、九零年六月及十一月、九一年十一月、九二年十一月），员工同时认股。直至最后一次增资，员工已拥有全公司八．五%股权。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">American companies also have bonuses, but for the vast majority of employees, bonuses are only a small part of fixed salary. Taiwanese companies are different. When the economy is good and company profits are high, employee bonuses can be a large portion of fixed salary, or even exceed it.</p>
<p class="en-para">By the mid-1980s, United Microelectronics Corporation (UMC) already had quite generous cash bonuses for employees. From 1987 onward, UMC began issuing stock bonuses calculated at face value, which made employees of other companies green with envy. Before TSMC was even formally established, Zeng Fan-cheng had already "informed" me in the posture of a union leader: after TSMC's founding, there must be employee bonuses every year.</p>
<p class="en-para">But after founding, we lost money every month—naturally there was no reason for bonuses. In July 1988 (seventeen months after founding), TSMC achieved monthly break-even for the first time. In truth, one month of no loss was not the basis for bonuses at a normal company; but in view of Zeng Fan-cheng's pre-founding "informing" and my promise to "bonus as early as possible," I decided to issue a bonus equivalent to half a month's salary after that month's books closed. Everyone was overjoyed.</p>
<p class="en-para">At year-end 1988 closing, net operating profit for the year was over NT$130 million, almost earning back all of 1987's losses. We issued another bonus and everyone was even happier.</p>
<p class="en-para">Besides bonuses, in November 1988 during the first cash capital increase, we began employee stock subscriptions. Thereafter, at each cash capital increase (October 1989, June and November 1990, November 1991, November 1992), employees simultaneously subscribed for stock. By the last capital increase, employees owned 8.5% of the company's total equity.</p>
</div>

## 飞利浦反对员工认股

在我对董事会提出员工认股时，公司已转亏为盈。飞利浦对台积电的看法，也自怀疑、保守转为乐观，所以他们反对员工认股。他们的理由是：（一）此举稀释投资协议内飞利浦股权比例自27.6%至25%左右；（二）飞利浦母公司没有员工认股的先例。

理由（二）当然不成理由——飞利浦不让员工认股，难道台积电也不可以？关于理由（一），在我坚持员工认股下飞利浦的台湾总经理还特地写信给开发基金，说飞利浦被稀释的股权，要在实施股票承购权时补回。经过我和开发基金讨论后，此点开发基金暂时默认。毕竟，飞利浦的股票承购权是下一场战争，我另有战略，此时不必与飞利浦起冲突。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">When I proposed employee stock options to the board, the company had already turned profitable. Philips' attitude toward TSMC had also shifted from skeptical and conservative to optimistic, and precisely because of this they opposed employee stock options. Their reasons were: (1) this would dilute Philips' equity stake under the investment agreement from 27.6% to about 25%; (2) Philips parent company had no precedent for employee stock options.</p>
<p class="en-para">Reason (2) was of course not a valid argument—just because Philips didn't allow employee stock options, should TSMC also not be allowed? Regarding reason (1), under my insistence on employee stock options, the Philips Taiwan general manager even wrote to the Development Fund, saying that Philips' diluted equity would be made up when the stock option was exercised. After discussions between me and the Development Fund, the Development Fund tacitly accepted this for the moment. After all, Philips' stock option was the next battle; I had a separate strategy and saw no need to create a conflict with Philips at this time.</p>
</div>

## 台塑退出

八九年间接听闻台塑已卖出它持有的全部台积电股票，大为震惊。台积是台积电第三大股东（5%，仅次于开发基金及飞利浦），也是募资时俞国华认为"必要"的投资者。怎么在投资还不到三年，公司已开始赚钱时就卖出？况且，无论在卖出前或卖出后，王永庆或他的董事代表王文洋，对我一句交代话都没有。

震惊之余打电话给王文洋，他只冷淡地证实台塑已卖出消息。当时对台湾尚相当天真的我，还在电话上请他继续担任台积电董事，使得我们可以继续从台湾"经营之神"受益。文洋君似乎有点惊讶，但他只冷淡地说："不做了。"事后我才知道台湾法律规定，如一个董事转让他代表的一半（或一半以上）股权，他必须辞董事职。文洋君在电话上没有告诉我这点，大约也是因为他认为我的无知，实在已到了超出"孺子不可教"地步吧？

后来听说台塑把台积电股票卖给了一个证券经纪商，此经纪商又转卖给许多散户。与王文洋通电话后，我已失去继续追逐此事的兴趣。后来台积电多了许多散户，倒是事实。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">In 1989, I indirectly heard that Formosa Plastics had sold all its TSMC shares. I was shocked. Formosa Plastics was TSMC's third-largest shareholder (5%, behind only the Development Fund and Philips). It was also the investor that Premier Yu Kuo-hua considered "necessary" during fundraising. Why sell when the company had only been invested for less than three years and had begun to be profitable? Moreover, neither Wang Yung-ching nor his board representative Wang Wen-yang said a single word of explanation to me, either before or after the sale.</p>
<p class="en-para">Shocked, I called Wang Wen-yang. He only coldly confirmed the news that Formosa Plastics had sold. I, who was still quite naive about Taiwan at the time, even asked him over the phone to continue serving as a TSMC director so we could continue benefiting from Taiwan's "Management God." Wen-yang seemed a bit surprised but only coldly said: "I won't do it anymore." I learned afterward that Taiwanese law stipulates that if a director transfers half (or more) of the shares he represents, he must resign his directorship. Wen-yang didn't tell me this over the phone, probably because he thought my ignorance truly had reached a point beyond "unteachable."</p>
<p class="en-para">Later I heard that Formosa Plastics had sold the TSMC shares to a securities broker, who then resold them to many retail investors. After the phone call with Wang Wen-yang, I lost interest in pursuing the matter further. That TSMC later gained many retail shareholders, however, is a fact.</p>
</div>
