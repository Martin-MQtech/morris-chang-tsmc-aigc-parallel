## 开关美、欧市场，美国行销经理易人

台积电开张第一年（一九八七年）的营收，几乎全部来自台湾，但戴克斯以及他雇的美国行销经理匹列切致力开关美国市场，次年即开始见效果。八八年来自美国的营收占全部营收16.3%，八九年更升高至55%。好几个大型美国半导体公司，如英特尔、德仪、摩托罗拉都赫然在客户之列。我"开关美、欧市场"的第一策略，可说已有成功的开始。

戴克斯于八八年在压力下辞职，几个月后他出任Signetics总经理；几个月后，他也把匹列切带去。如果飞利浦存心要破坏台积电的开关美国市场计划，也不能比这样做来得更彻底吧？幸而我二十几年前的德仪旧同事"高价路克"（John Luke）忽然出现，而且毛遂自荐，要当台积电的美国行销经理。

一九六零年代，我在德仪初任业务单位总经理时，路克是德仪众多"区域行销经理"之一；只是大部分行销经理都销"商品"。路克却销"客制品"，我那时已领会供应商在"客制品"上的议价权比在"商品"上高，而路克也完全同意，所以我们合作相当愉快。那时他告诉我在客户群中他有"高价路克"绰号，而且颇自傲这绰号。

现在，台积电作为"专业晶圆代工"者，产品都是"客制品"，"高价路克"又出现了。经过一、两次话旧及面试，我高兴地雇用电。路克后来在台积电做到一九九八年。他充分参与台积电的"咆哮九零年代"。

<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">TSMC's revenue in its first year of operation (1987) came almost entirely from Taiwan. But Dexter and the American marketing manager he hired, Pletcher, worked aggressively to open up the U.S. market, and results were already visible the following year. In 1988, U.S.-origin revenue accounted for 16.3% of total revenue; in 1989, it rose to 55%. Several major American semiconductor companies—Intel, TI, Motorola—were all prominently among our clients. My first strategy of "opening up U.S. and European markets" could be said to have had a successful start.</p>
<p class="en-para">Dexter resigned under pressure in 1988. A few months later he became Signetics general manager; a few months after that, he took Pletcher with him. If Philips had wanted to sabotage TSMC's U.S. market opening plan, they could hardly have done it more thoroughly than this! Fortunately, a former TI colleague from over twenty years earlier, "High-Price Luke" (John Luke), suddenly appeared and recommended himself for the position of TSMC's American marketing manager.</p>
<p class="en-para">In the 1960s, when I had just become general manager of a business unit at TI, Luke was one of TI's many "regional marketing managers." But most marketing managers sold "commodities." Luke sold "custom products." I had already appreciated that a supplier's bargaining power on "custom products" was higher than on "commodities," and Luke completely agreed, so we cooperated quite pleasantly. At that time he told me he had the nickname "High-Price Luke" among his customer base, and he was quite proud of it.</p>
<p class="en-para">Now, as a "dedicated foundry," TSMC's products were all "custom products," and "High-Price Luke" appeared again. After one or two conversations reminiscing and an interview, I happily hired him. Luke stayed at TSMC until 1998. He was fully involved in TSMC's "Roaring '90s."</p>
</div>

## 欧洲行销与麦克

一九八八年，我也雇了一位以前德仪的老下属，麦克（Siegfried Mack），请他做台积电欧洲行销经理。欧洲市场虽不如美国市场大，但也颇可观，在九零年后，台积电的欧洲营收达到全部营收的8%以上。我尤其享受我与麦克九零年一月的客户之旅。我们驱车自法兰克福至弗莱堡（Freiburg），又至Marin，再去日内瓦，一共三天。麦克是道地德国人，所以我们一路吃、住、行完全德国式。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">In 1988, I also hired a former TI subordinate, Siegfried Mack, to be TSMC's European marketing manager. The European market, though not as large as the U.S. market, was also quite substantial. After 1990, TSMC's European revenue reached over 8% of total revenue. I especially enjoyed a customer trip I took with Mack in January 1990. We drove from Frankfurt to Freiburg, then to Marin, then to Geneva, over three days. Mack was German through and through, so our eating, accommodation, and travel along the way were thoroughly German-style.</p>
