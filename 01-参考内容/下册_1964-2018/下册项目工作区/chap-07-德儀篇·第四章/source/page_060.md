# 德仪篇·第四章｜一帆风顺

在「矽部」总经理职位上，我继续学习半导体经营。「矽部」的客户只有部分与「锗部」重叠，所以我认识了不少新客户，尤其在国防工业领域上。「矽部」产品不少用在武器上，客户非常重视产品的品质和可靠度，价格倒在其次。经过德仪代我申请，我也获得了美国政府的 Secret Clearance（授权阅读机密文件）。后来我担任德仪全球半导体总经理，我的 Secret Clearance 更进阶为 Top Secret Clearance（授权阅读最高机密文件）。

德仪是第一个开发「矽晶体管」的公司，在五〇年代领先同业，但六〇年代中期，快捷半导体（Fairchild Semiconductor）已是一个厉害的竞争者。在我任「矽部」总经理一年半内的研发案中，最重要的是把消费者产品（例如电视、收音机的晶体管）改为塑胶包装。塑胶包装的製造成本较原来的金属包装为低，减低的成本就可以减低售价，进而扩展市场；但是问题是塑胶包装的可靠性。我们的挑战是又要减低成本，又要符合高的可靠性规格。我在「矽部」成立了一个新的「塑胶包装处」，任命了一位年轻、富有进取心的比利时籍工程师为处长。这个案子做得很成功，帮助了「矽部」的市占率、营收及获利。这位比利时籍处长也在两年后（一九六七年）成为德仪的助理副总裁。

<p class="en-para">In the Silicon Division GM role, I continued learning semiconductor operations. The Silicon Division's customers overlapped only partially with the Germanium Division's, so I got to know many new customers, particularly in the defense industry. Many Silicon Division products went into weapons systems, where customers valued quality and reliability above all — price was secondary. Through TI's sponsorship, I obtained a U.S. government Secret Clearance (authorization to access classified documents). Later, when I became GM of Worldwide Semiconductors, my clearance was upgraded to Top Secret Clearance.</p>
<p class="en-para">TI was the first company to develop silicon transistors, leading the industry in the 1950s. But by the mid-1960s, Fairchild Semiconductor had become a formidable competitor. Among the R&D projects during my eighteen months as GM of the Silicon Division, the most important was converting consumer products (such as transistors for televisions and radios) to plastic packaging. Plastic packaging cost less to manufacture than the original metal packaging — the savings could be passed on as lower prices to expand the market. The challenge was plastic packaging's reliability. Our challenge was to reduce costs while still meeting high reliability specifications. I established a new "Plastic Packaging Department" within the Silicon Division and appointed a young, aggressive Belgian-born engineer as its director. The project was a great success, boosting the Silicon Division's market share, revenue, and profit. This Belgian director later became a TI Assistant Vice President in 1967.</p>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Plastic packaging conversion**
**中文解释**：塑胶包装转型，指将半导体晶体管从金属封装改为塑胶封装；这是一九六〇年代中期降低成本、扩展消费市场的关键创新。
**商业造句">The plastic packaging conversion cut unit costs by 40% and opened the consumer electronics market — but only if the reliability specifications could be met.*

2. **Fairchild Semiconductor (快捷半导体)**
**中文解释**：快捷半导体，又称「费尔柴尔德半导体」，一九五七年由「叛徒八人组」（Traitorous Eight）从萧克利半导体实验室出走后创立；它是矽谷的母体，也是德仪在矽晶体管领域最强大的竞争对手。
**商业造句">Fairchild was both the birthplace of Silicon Valley and TI's most dangerous competitor in silicon transistors — the outsider that made the insider nervous.*

3. **Secret Clearance / Top Secret Clearance**
**中文解释**：机密许可 / 最高机密许可，指美国政府颁发给需要接触机密信息的人员的安全许可；在半导体产业中，从事国防相关项目的高管通常需要此类许可。
**商业造句">The DoD contract required not only a proven technology but also a management team with active Top Secret Clearances — a barrier that excluded most foreign-born executives.*

### 修辞赏析

<span class="rhetoric-note">
<span class="zh">「我们的挑战是又要减低成本，又要符合高的可靠性规格。」——这句话道出了所有制造业创新的核心矛盾：成本与质量的平衡。张忠谋没有把它包装成什么「双赢策略」，而是坦然承认这是一个「挑战」。这种诚实的叙事风格，在喜欢「包装」的商业传记中极为罕见。他随后给出了一个简洁的解决方案：成立专门部门、任命有进取心的年轻工程师。没有豪言壮语，只有一步一步的落实。这种「去戏剧化」的管理叙事，正是张忠谋自传中最为珍贵的特质。</span>
<span class="en">"Our challenge was to reduce costs while still meeting high reliability specifications." — This sentence captures the core tension of all manufacturing innovation: the balance between cost and quality. Chang does not package it as some "win-win strategy"; he honestly acknowledges it as a "challenge." This honest narrative style is rare in business biographies that love to "spin." He then lays out a concise solution: establish a dedicated department, appoint an aggressive young engineer. No grand rhetoric. Just step-by-step execution. This "de-dramatized" management narrative is among the most precious qualities of Chang's autobiography.</span>
</span>

### 背景知识延伸

**加拿大封裝廠與國際擴張**：張忠謀在自傳中記載了另一段在加拿大設立封裝測試廠的故事。那是一九五〇年代，英國提高從美國輸入的半導體關稅，但從加拿大輸入可以免稅。德儀於是在加拿大首都渥太華附近的城市赫爾（Hull）設立封裝廠。張親自在華氏十度（約攝氏零下十二度）的嚴寒中视察廠址——「至今難忘卻」。他挑了一位剛從哈佛商學院畢業不久的年輕人做赫爾廠的第一任廠長——後來也成为了副總裁。這座加拿大廠是德儀國際化的起點。此後張忠謀在日本（一九六八年封裝測試廠、一九七一年晶圓廠）、新加坡（一九六八年）、馬來西亞（一九七二年）建立了五、六個境外工廠，為德儀構建了全球製造網路的早期雛形。
