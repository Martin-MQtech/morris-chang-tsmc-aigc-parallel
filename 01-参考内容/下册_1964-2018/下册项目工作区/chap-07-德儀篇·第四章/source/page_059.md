# 德仪篇·第四章｜一帆风顺

「锗部」总经理只做了一年，但留下辉煌的业绩。营收、获利、市占率一年中有显著的增加，而且这业绩的背景，是「锗」正在很快地被「矽」取代、「锗」市场已开始萎缩的时期。一九六五年夏——也就是我任「锗部」总经理一年后——我被调任为「矽晶体管部」（简称「矽部」）总经理。

任「矽部」总经理——

当时德仪「矽部」无论营收、员工人数、获利都较「锗部」低；但是，「锗部」的营收在我做总经理之前或之后都只有微幅成长或甚至萎缩，而「矽部」营收却每年以一五%至二〇%的速度成长。所以「矽部」总经理的职阶比「锗部」高，对我来说，调任「矽部」是升级。

<p class="en-para">I was GM of the Germanium Division for only one year, but I left behind a record of brilliant performance. Revenue, profits, and market share all saw significant increases in that single year — and this against the backdrop that germanium was rapidly being replaced by silicon, and the germanium market had already begun to shrink. In the summer of 1965 — just one year after becoming GM of Germanium — I was transferred to become GM of the Silicon Transistor Division (简称 Silicon Division).</p>
<p class="en-para">Becoming GM of the Silicon Division —</p>
<p class="en-para">At the time, TI's Silicon Division was smaller than the Germanium Division in revenue, headcount, and profit. But the Germanium Division's revenue had seen only marginal growth or even contraction before and after my tenure, while the Silicon Division's revenue was growing at 15% to 20% per year. So the Silicon Division GM position was a higher rank than the Germanium Division GM. For me, the transfer was a promotion.</p>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Market share growth against industry decline**
**中文解释**：在市占率下滑的产业中实现增长，指在「夕阳产业」中做到逆势增长；这种业绩比在朝阳产业中增长更具含金量。
**商业造句">Growing market share in a declining industry — as Chang did with germanium transistors in 1965 — is a rarer and more impressive feat than riding a growth wave.*

2. **Marginal growth**
**中文解释**：微幅成长，指增长率极低，通常在百分之三以下；在成熟或衰退市场中，微幅成长往往是常态。
**商业造句">The Germanium Division's marginal growth was not a failure of management — it was the inevitable result of silicon replacing germanium as the industry's dominant material.*

### 修辞赏析

<span class="rhetoric-note">
<span class="zh">「锗部总经理只做了一年，但留下辉煌的业绩。」——张忠谋用「只做了一年」的时间限定词，给「辉煌的业绩」增加了一个意外的维度。在大多数人的认知中，总经理至少需要两三年才能做出成绩。张忠谋不仅只用了一年，而且是在一个正在萎缩的「夕阳产业」中做到逆势增长。这个对比是本章「一帆风顺」主题的核心张力：看似平坦的晋升之路，处处暗藏「在衰退中增长」的逆势挑战。</span>
<span class="en">"I was GM of the Germanium Division for only one year, but I left behind a record of brilliant performance." — Chang uses the time qualifier "only one year" to add an unexpected dimension to "brilliant performance." Most people assume a GM needs at least two or three years to deliver results. Chang not only did it in one year — he did it in a declining "sunset industry." This contrast is the central tension of the chapter's title "Smooth Sailing": a seemingly smooth path, every turn hiding the counter-trend challenge of "growing in decline."</span>
</span>

### 背景知识延伸

**从锗到矽的产业转型**：一九六〇年代中期，半导体产业正处于从锗（Germanium）向矽（Silicon）转型的关键节点。锗是最早的半导体材料，但矽在高温稳定性、成本和氧化层品質上具有决定性优势。德仪的锗晶体管部门虽然是当时公司营收最高的部门，但所有人都看到了矽的未来。张忠谋被调到「矽部」掌管一个成长型而非成熟型事业体——这是一个明显的人才培养信号。
