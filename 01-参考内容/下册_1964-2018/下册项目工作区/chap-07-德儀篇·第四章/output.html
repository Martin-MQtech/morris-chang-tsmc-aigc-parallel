德仪篇·第四章｜一帆风顺

「锗部」总经理只做了一年，但留下辉煌的业绩。营收、获利、市占率一年中有显着的增加，而且这业绩的背景，是「锗」正在很快地被「矽」取代、「锗」市场已开始萎缩的时期。一九六五年夏——也就是我任「锗部」总经理一年后——我被调任为「矽晶体管部」（简称「矽部」）总经理。

任「矽部」总经理——当时德仪「矽部」无论营收、员工人数、获利都较「锗部」低；但是，「锗部」的营收在我做总经理之前或之后都只有微幅成长或甚至萎缩，而「矽部」营收却每年以一五%至二〇%的速度成长。所以「矽部」总经理的职阶比「锗部」高，对我来说，调任「矽部」是升级。

ReadShift 双语翻译

I was GM of the Germanium Division for only one year, but I left behind a record of brilliant performance. Revenue, profits, and market share all saw significant increases in that single year — and this against the backdrop that germanium was rapidly being replaced by silicon, and the germanium market had already begun to shrink. In the summer of 1965 — just one year after becoming GM of Germanium — I was transferred to become GM of the Silicon Transistor Division (简称 Silicon Division).

Becoming GM of the Silicon Division —

At the time, TI's Silicon Division was smaller than the Germanium Division in revenue, headcount, and profit. But the Germanium Division's revenue had seen only marginal growth or even contraction before and after my tenure, while the Silicon Division's revenue was growing at 15% to 20% per year. So the Silicon Division GM position was a higher rank than the Germanium Division GM. For me, the transfer was a promotion.

Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇
Market share growth against industry decline—在市占率下滑的产业中实现增长，指在「夕阳产业」中做到逆势增长；这种业绩比在朝阳产业中增长更具含金量。
Marginal growth—微幅成长，指增长率极低，通常在百分之三以下；在成熟或衰退市场中，微幅成长往往是常态。
修辞赏析 · Language & Logic Appreciation

「锗部总经理只做了一年，但留下辉煌的业绩。」——张忠谋用「只做了一年」的时间限定词，给「辉煌的业绩」增加了一个意外的维度。在大多数人的认知中，总经理至少需要两三年才能做出成绩。张忠谋不仅只用了一年，而且是在一个正在萎缩的「夕阳产业」中做到逆势增长。这个对比是本章「一帆风顺」主题的核心张力：看似平坦的晋升之路，处处暗藏「在衰退中增长」的逆势挑战。 "I was GM of the Germanium Division for only one year, but I left behind a record of brilliant performance." — Chang uses the time qualifier "only one year" to add an unexpected dimension to "brilliant performance." Most people assume a GM needs at least two or three years to deliver results. Chang not only did it in one year — he did it in a declining "sunset industry." This contrast is the central tension of the chapter's title "Smooth Sailing": a seemingly smooth path, every turn hiding the counter-trend challenge of "growing in decline."

背景知识延伸 · Beyond the Text

从锗到矽的产业转型：一九六〇年代中期，半导体产业正处于从锗（Germanium）向矽（Silicon）转型的关键节点。锗是最早的半导体材料，但矽在高温稳定性、成本和氧化层品质上具有决定性优势。德仪的锗晶体管部门虽然是当时公司营收最高的部门，但所有人都看到了矽的未来。张忠谋被调到「矽部」掌管一个成长型而非成熟型事业体——这是一个明显的人才培养信号。

德仪篇·第四章｜一帆风顺

在「矽部」总经理职位上，我继续学习半导体经营。「矽部」的客户只有部分与「锗部」重叠，所以我认识了不少新客户，尤其在国防工业领域上。「矽部」产品不少用在武器上，客户非常重视产品的品质和可靠度，价格倒在其次。经过德仪代我申请，我也获得了美国政府的 Secret Clearance（授权阅读机密文件）。后来我担任德仪全球半导体总经理，我的 Secret Clearance 更进阶为 Top Secret Clearance（授权阅读最高机密文件）。

德仪是第一个开发「矽晶体管」的公司，在五〇年代领先同业，但六〇年代中期，快捷半导体（Fairchild Semiconductor）已是一个厉害的竞争者。在我任「矽部」总经理一年半内的研发案中，最重要的是把消费者产品（例如电视、收音机的晶体管）改为塑胶包装。塑胶包装的制造成本较原来的金属包装为低，减低的成本就可以减低售价，进而扩展市场；但是问题是塑胶包装的可靠性。我们的挑战是又要减低成本，又要符合高的可靠性规格。我在「矽部」成立了一个新的「塑胶包装处」，任命了一位年轻、富有进取心的比利时籍工程师为处长。这个案子做得很成功，帮助了「矽部」的市占率、营收及获利。这位比利时籍处长也在两年后（一九六七年）成为德仪的助理副总裁。

ReadShift 双语翻译

In the Silicon Division GM role, I continued learning semiconductor operations. The Silicon Division's customers overlapped only partially with the Germanium Division's, so I got to know many new customers, particularly in the defense industry. Many Silicon Division products went into weapons systems, where customers valued quality and reliability above all — price was secondary. Through TI's sponsorship, I obtained a U.S. government Secret Clearance (authorization to access classified documents). Later, when I became GM of Worldwide Semiconductors, my clearance was upgraded to Top Secret Clearance.

TI was the first company to develop silicon transistors, leading the industry in the 1950s. But by the mid-1960s, Fairchild Semiconductor had become a formidable competitor. Among the R&D projects during my eighteen months as GM of the Silicon Division, the most important was converting consumer products (such as transistors for televisions and radios) to plastic packaging. Plastic packaging cost less to manufacture than the original metal packaging — the savings could be passed on as lower prices to expand the market. The challenge was plastic packaging's reliability. Our challenge was to reduce costs while still meeting high reliability specifications. I established a new "Plastic Packaging Department" within the Silicon Division and appointed a young, aggressive Belgian-born engineer as its director. The project was a great success, boosting the Silicon Division's market share, revenue, and profit. This Belgian director later became a TI Assistant Vice President in 1967.

Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇
Plastic packaging conversion—塑胶包装转型，指将半导体晶体管从金属封装改为塑胶封装；这是一九六〇年代中期降低成本、扩展消费市场的关键创新。
Fairchild Semiconductor (快捷半导体)—快捷半导体，又称「费尔柴尔德半导体」，一九五七年由「叛徒八人组」（Traitorous Eight）从萧克利半导体实验室出走后创立；它是矽谷的母体，也是德仪在矽晶体管领域最强大的竞争对手。
Secret Clearance / Top Secret Clearance—机密许可 / 最高机密许可，指美国政府颁发给需要接触机密信息的人员的安全许可；在半导体产业中，从事国防相关项目的高管通常需要此类许可。
修辞赏析 · Language & Logic Appreciation

「我们的挑战是又要减低成本，又要符合高的可靠性规格。」——这句话道出了所有制造业创新的核心矛盾：成本与质量的平衡。张忠谋没有把它包装成什幺「双赢策略」，而是坦然承认这是一个「挑战」。这种诚实的叙事风格，在喜欢「包装」的商业传记中极为罕见。他随后给出了一个简洁的解决方案：成立专门部门、任命有进取心的年轻工程师。没有豪言壮语，只有一步一步的落实。这种「去戏剧化」的管理叙事，正是张忠谋自传中最为珍贵的特质。 "Our challenge was to reduce costs while still meeting high reliability specifications." — This sentence captures the core tension of all manufacturing innovation: the balance between cost and quality. Chang does not package it as some "win-win strategy"; he honestly acknowledges it as a "challenge." This honest narrative style is rare in business biographies that love to "spin." He then lays out a concise solution: establish a dedicated department, appoint an aggressive young engineer. No grand rhetoric. Just step-by-step execution. This "de-dramatized" management narrative is among the most precious qualities of Chang's autobiography.

背景知识延伸 · Beyond the Text

加拿大封装厂与国际扩张：张忠谋在自传中记载了另一段在加拿大设立封装测试厂的故事。那是一九五〇年代，英国提高从美国输入的半导体关税，但从加拿大输入可以免税。德仪于是在加拿大首都渥太华附近的城市赫尔（Hull）设立封装厂。张亲自在华氏十度（约摄氏零下十二度）的严寒中视察厂址——「至今难忘却」。他挑了一位刚从哈佛商学院毕业不久的年轻人做赫尔厂的第一任厂长——后来也成为了副总裁。这座加拿大厂是德仪国际化的起点。此后张忠谋在日本（一九六八年封装测试厂、一九七一年晶圆厂）、新加坡（一九六八年）、马来西亚（一九七二年）建立了五、六个境外工厂，为德仪构建了全球制造网路的早期雏形。

德仪篇·第四章｜一帆风顺

任「集成电路部」总经理——一九六〇和七〇年代德仪最重要的「业务单位」，无疑是「集成电路」。德仪与快捷半导体共同发明了集成电路。十年后，集成电路已是所有半导体公司的重要业务，而且这项业务继续急速成长。在一九六六年，德仪的「集成电路部」总经理虽然还没有副总裁头衔，但这职位已被视为公司最重要的职位之一。

一九六六年，谁是德仪集成电路总经理？原来他就是我在本书第二章已提到——我初进德仪时下属一位工程师。此人名为史厥特（Strat），甚聪明，有强烈的企图心及进取心，又善于操纵他人，是一个不折不扣的「公司政客」。他与我几乎同时加入德仪，起先是我下属两位工程师之一；一年后我升了一级，我也升了他一级；后来我去史丹佛两年半，在那两年半内，史厥特足足升了三级！我自史丹佛回来，他已是「矽部」总经理，在我做「锗部」总经理时，他又调升为「集成电路部」总经理。

在一九五〇、六〇年代——也就是半导体的早期——德仪的确有过许多「猎富武士」（Soldiers of Fortune）。这些「武士」们不少拥有高学位、高能力，而且强烈的企图心。可惜他们的企图心并不是要与大家共同建立大企业，而只是为自己发一笔财。当他们在德仪赚了一票钱，或发现发不了财，就立刻去别处找机会了。我从史丹佛回德仪时，就发现以前许多高职位的人已走了。

ReadShift 双语翻译

Becoming GM of the Integrated Circuit Division —

The most important "business unit" at TI in the 1960s and 1970s was unquestionably the "Integrated Circuit Division." TI and Fairchild Semiconductor had jointly invented the integrated circuit. A decade later, the IC had become a critical business for every semiconductor company, and it continued to grow at breakneck speed. In 1966, although the TI Integrated Circuit Division GM did not yet carry the VP title, the position was already regarded as one of the most important in the company.

Who was TI's Integrated Circuit GM in 1966? It was the same engineer I mentioned in Chapter 2 — someone who had been my subordinate when I first joined TI. His name was Strat. Extremely clever, fiercely ambitious and aggressive, and skilled at manipulating others — a corporate politician through and through. He had joined TI at almost the same time I had. He started as one of two engineers under me. A year later when I was promoted one level, I promoted him one level too. Then I left for Stanford for two and a half years. In those two and a half years, Strat was promoted three full levels! When I returned from Stanford, he was already GM of the Silicon Division. While I was GM of the Germanium Division, he was transferred again — this time to GM of the Integrated Circuit Division.

In the 1950s and 1960s — the early days of semiconductors — TI indeed had many "Soldiers of Fortune." Many of these "warriors" held advanced degrees, had high ability, and possessed fierce ambition. Unfortunately, their ambition was not to build a great enterprise together with others — it was simply to make a fortune for themselves. When they had made their killing at TI, or discovered they could not get rich there, they immediately went elsewhere for opportunities. When I returned to TI from Stanford, I found that many of the people who had held high positions were gone.

Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇
Soldiers of Fortune (猎富武士)—猎富武士，指以个人财富积累为主要目标的职业经理人；在矽谷早期，这类人物极为常见——他们频繁跳槽、创业、上市、套现，构成了矽谷生态的「游牧民族」。
Corporate politician—公司政客，指善于在组织内部运用政治手段（而非绩效）获取晋升的人；在大型企业中，「公司政客」与「业务高手」的并存是一种常见但危险的组织现象。
Breakneck speed—急速、极快速度；在半导体产业叙事中，这个词常用来描写技术迭代或市场扩张的节奏。
修辞赏析 · Language & Logic Appreciation

「史厥特」这个名字在张忠谋的笔下是一个精心塑造的反面角色。张忠谋用了三个平行的形容词来描述他：「甚聪明、有强烈的企图心及进取心、又善于操纵他人」——每个词单独看都是褒义词，但组合在一起，却构成了一个令人不寒而栗的「高效自私人」画像。张忠谋的高明之处在于：他没有把史厥特写成坏人，而是把他写成了一类人——「猎富武士」的代表。这种不带恶意的精准刻画，比任何道德批判都更有力。 "Strat" is a carefully crafted foil in Chang's narrative. Chang uses three parallel adjectives: "extremely clever, fiercely ambitious and aggressive, and skilled at manipulating others." Each word alone is positive, but together they compose a chilling portrait of an "efficient selfish person." Chang's brilliance is this: he does not write Strat as a villain. He writes him as a type — the representative of the "Soldiers of Fortune." This precise characterization without malice carries more force than any moral condemnation could.

背景知识延伸 · Beyond the Text

「猎富武士」时代：一九五〇至六〇年代的半导体产业，「猎富武士」是一种普遍现象。这些人聪明、有能力、有野心——但他们的野心不是建立一个伟大的企业，而是为自己赚一笔钱。一旦赚到（或发现赚不到），他们就走人。张忠谋在自传中说：「我从史丹佛回德仪时，就发现以前许多高职位的人已走了。」这种高流动性是矽谷早期生态的标志性特征——它既是创新的催化剂（人才不断流入新公司），也是组织稳定性的毒药（没有人愿意为长期目标做承诺）。张忠谋自己也将在十年后成为另一家公司的「创始人」——但他不是「猎富武士」，而是一个「建设者」。这个区别，或许就是他与其他人的根本分际。

德仪篇·第四章｜一帆风顺

史厥特恐怕是德仪最后的「猎富武士」。一九六六年秋，他的期限也到了。德仪集成电路的竞争者（如摩托罗拉、快捷等）都成长得很好，而德仪集成电路业务却萎靡不振。对这个原始发明集成电路的公司，「是可忍，孰不可忍」。于是，道森决定撤换史厥特，把这个重要职位交给我。这是一九六六年十一月，我三十五岁的事。

当时「集成电路部」的责任，只限于「双极」，并不包括几年后就会开始取代双极的 MOS 的研发。德仪低估了 MOS 的潜力。在 MOS 开始、也是最关键的时期，没有放足够资源在 MOS 研发上，也没有找对人领导研发。这是德仪的大失足——容我在第六章再谈。

一九六六年圣诞节及新年，父母亲来达拉斯与我们一起过节。那时女儿刚七岁，正是可爱的年龄。我们树立了圣诞树，挂了不少彩灯，树下放了不少礼物。我还在起居室的火炉生了火。一家五人，温馨地围炉过节过年。虽然父亲对我在年底仍要到公司赶出货不无微词，但他也知道我刚得到一个重要职位，必须要有好的表现，所以也没有太批评。在我一生为事业忙碌的过程中，一九六六年底的十天与父母亲的围炉团聚，是我常常怀念的时刻。

ReadShift 双语翻译

Strat was probably the last of TI's "Soldiers of Fortune." In the fall of 1966, his time was up. TI's IC competitors — Motorola, Fairchild, and others — were all growing strongly, while TI's IC business languished. For the company that had originally invented the integrated circuit, "this was more than could be endured." Dawson therefore decided to replace Strat and hand this critical position to me. It was November 1966. I was thirty-five years old.

At that time, the Integrated Circuit Division's responsibilities were limited to "bipolar" technology and did not include MOS R&D — which would begin to replace bipolar a few years later. TI underestimated MOS's potential. During MOS's earliest and most critical period, TI did not invest sufficiently in MOS R&D nor put the right person in charge of it. This was a major misstep for TI — I will discuss it further in Chapter 6.

During the Christmas and New Year holidays of 1966, my parents came to Dallas to celebrate with us. My daughter was just seven years old — a lovely age. We set up a Christmas tree, hung strings of colorful lights, and piled gifts beneath it. I lit a fire in the living room fireplace. The five of us, gathered warmly around the fire for the holidays. Although Father grumbled a little that I still had to rush to the company at year-end to push shipments, he understood I had just taken on an important position and needed to deliver strong results, so he did not criticize too much. Amid a lifetime of dedication to my career, those ten days of warmth with my parents at the end of 1966 remains one of my most cherished memories.

Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇
This was more than could be endured—是可忍孰不可忍，指已经到达了无法继续容忍的极限；在组织管理中，这种心态往往是重大人事变动或战略转向的前奏。
MOS (Metal-Oxide-Semiconductor)—金属氧化物半导体，指一种晶体管技术架构；MOS 技术在六〇年代仍在早期阶段，但将在七〇至八〇年代主导半导体产业。
Year-end push—年底赶出货，指财年末为达成销售业绩而进行的集中出货；在制造业中，这种行为是常见的「业绩管理」手段，但也可能造成客户关系和员工士气的长期损害。
修辞赏析 · Language & Logic Appreciation

张忠谋在自传中插入了一段罕见的家庭叙事——一九六六年底的圣诞节团聚。他说：「在我一生为事业忙碌的过程中，一九六六年底的十天与父母亲的围炉团聚，是我常常怀念的时刻。」这段温情叙事出现在「史厥特被撤换、我接任集成电路总经理」的权力交接之后，形成了一种独特的叙事节奏：从战场到家园，从权力到温情。它告诉读者：即便是在「一帆风顺」的晋升途中，家庭始终是他最重要的锚点。 Chang inserts a rare domestic narrative — the 1966 year-end Christmas reunion. "Amid a lifetime of dedication to my career, those ten days of warmth with my parents at the end of 1966 remains one of my most cherished memories." This tender passage appears immediately after the power transition ("Strat removed, Chang promoted"), creating a distinctive narrative rhythm: from battlefield to home, from power to warmth. It tells the reader: even amid the "smooth sailing" of promotions, family has always been his most important anchor.

背景知识延伸 · Beyond the Text
