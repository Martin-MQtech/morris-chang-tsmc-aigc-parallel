台积电篇三·第三十二章｜老骥伏枥，志在千里

二○○九年六月，在「假淘汰、真裁员」风波平息后，我重新兼任台积电CEO。那一年我七十八岁。许多人问我：为什幺还要回来？我的回答是：台积电正面临关键时刻，四十纳米良率问题未解、客户关系紧张、内部士气低落——这些都需要有人出来承担。

「老骥伏枥，志在千里」——这句曹操的诗，正是我当时心境的最佳写照。

ReadShift 双语翻译

In June 2009, after the 'fake elimination, real layoffs' controversy had subsided, I resumed the CEO role at TSMC. I was seventy-eight years old. Many people asked me why I had come back. My answer was that TSMC was facing a critical moment: the forty-nanometer yield problem remained unsolved, customer relations were strained, and internal morale was low—all of which required someone to step up.

'The old steed in the stable still dreams of galloping a thousand li'—that line from Cao Cao's poetry was the perfect expression of my state of mind at the time.

解决四十纳米良率问题（二○○九年）

就在我重返CEO职前的几个月，台积电的最新节点技术——四十纳米——已开始量产，但并不顺利：良率过低，且有品质问题。我似乎又回到了一九六八年的德仪（见第六章），但四十一年前的经验也给了二○○九年的我可用的教训。

二○○九年的我已远离生产技术的细节，所以绝不一九六八年我的上司彪希所为——「帮忙」远过于「帮忙」。我把信心寄托在生产团队主管秦永沛副总上，从不参加他的技术会议，只是随时都抽出时间听取他主动给我的报告。

我还做了一件「公关」事。在我重返CEO职的二○○九年六月时，台积电的「四十纳米生产问题」已广为投资人所知。二○○九年七月底，在我返任CEO后的第一次「法人说明会」上，财务长做了每季的例行报告后，我站起来宣布：

「大家都很关心四十纳米的生产问题，我们也很努力要解决这问题。现在我想请负责营运的资深副总刘德音博士报告生产线现状——但他在生产线工作太忙，不能亲自来此，所以我们以网络直播联机方式，请他做此报告。」接着，会场的屏幕上出现德音穿着白色实验室长袍，站在洁净室门前，开始做他的专业性报告。我看一百多听众的脸色：又惊异又感动。我想：这是一个吉兆。

果然，几个月后，四十纳米良率问题解决，一切回归正常。我对刘德音、秦永沛以及他们生产团队的信心没有白费。

ReadShift 双语翻译

Several months before I resumed the CEO post, TSMC's newest node technology—forty nanometers—had begun mass production, but not smoothly: yield was too low, and there were quality issues. I seemed to have returned to TI in 1968 (see Chapter 6), but the experience from forty-one years earlier also provided lessons I could apply in 2009.

In 2009, I was far removed from the details of production technology, so I would never repeat what my boss Bush did in 1968—'helping' far beyond what was helpful. I placed my confidence in Vice President Yong-pei Chin, who led the production team, and never attended his technical meetings; I simply made time whenever he主动 brought me reports.

I also did something of a 'public relations' nature. By June 2009, when I resumed the CEO role, TSMC's 'forty-nanometer production problem' was well known to investors. In late July 2009, at the first investor conference after my return, after the CFO had delivered the quarterly report, I stood up and announced:

'Everyone is concerned about the forty-nanometer production problem, and we are working hard to resolve it. I would now like to ask Dr. Aric Liu, senior vice president in charge of operations, to report on the production line status—but he is too busy on the production floor to come here in person, so we will connect via live video.'

Next, Dr. Liu appeared on the screen at the venue, wearing a white lab coat and standing before the cleanroom door, beginning his technical report. I watched the expressions of the more than one hundred listeners: a mixture of surprise and being moved. I thought: this was a favorable omen.

As it turned out, a few months later, the forty-nanometer yield problem was resolved and everything returned to normal. My confidence in Aric Liu, Yong-pei Chin, and their production team was not misplaced.

解决与辉达的悬案（二○○九年七月）

辉达是台积电四十纳米节点技术的主要伙伴及客户。台积电在四十纳米初期的生产问题使辉达遭受了严重损失。辉达在我返任CEO一年前就开始与台积电争议，但一直没有结论。

我返任CEO后的头几天，几乎全部时间都在电话上告诉重要客户我返任的消息。在与辉达CEO黄仁勋（Jensen Huang）通话时，他也特别提醒我们两家公司之间的悬案，并要求尽速解决。

此后三星期，我花了几十小时与公司内有关同仁深入探讨此案。二○○九年七月初，我认为我已清楚了解此事并且有解决方案，我电邮Jensen：「我下星期来硅谷，拟于七月十五日下午六点半到你家吃沙拉和披萨。」那时我和Jensen已是十几年的老友，也常去他家吃沙拉和披萨。但这次他接到我的电邮着急了——我们两家公司间有这幺严重的争论，怎幺我只管吃沙拉和披萨？他回电邮说：「那幺，我们什幺时候谈正事？」我立刻回复：「六点半到你家，先和你全家吃披萨，八点整我和你到你的书房谈生意。」七月十五日我准时到Jensen家，与他全家有一个简单而愉快的披萨晚餐。八点整，我对Jensen说：「我们去你书房吧。」进书房后我们关起门来开始严肃的谈话。我提出我与台积电同仁研究了三星期的和解建议——上亿美元的赔偿金，并且强调：这建议是我们唯一的建议，它的有效期是四十八小时，如你在四十八小时内仍不同意，我们只得找仲裁人了。

Jensen在两天内同意了。直到现在辉达仍是台积电最大客户之一，而且Jensen与他夫人Lori仍是我与Sophie的好友。

ReadShift 双语翻译

NVIDIA was TSMC's primary partner and customer for the forty-nanometer node. TSMC's early production problems at forty nanometers caused NVIDIA significant losses. NVIDIA had begun disputing with TSMC a year before my return as CEO, but no resolution had been reached.

In the first few days after resuming the CEO role, I spent almost all my time on the phone informing key customers of my return. When I spoke with NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang, he specifically called attention to the outstanding dispute between our two companies and urged a swift resolution.

For the next three weeks, I spent dozens of hours in deep discussion with relevant colleagues within the company. In early July 2009, I felt I understood the matter clearly and had a solution. I emailed Jensen: 'I will come to Silicon Valley next week and plan to join your family for salad and pizza at 6:30 p.m. on July 15.'

By then Jensen and I had been friends for over a decade, and I had often dined at his home on salad and pizza. But this time he was alarmed by my email—given how serious the dispute was between our companies, why was I only thinking about salad and pizza? He emailed back: 'Then when are we going to discuss business?'

I replied immediately: 'I'll come to your home at six-thirty, have pizza with your family first, and at eight o'clock sharp, you and I will go to your study to discuss business.'

On July 15 I arrived on time at Jensen's home and had a simple and pleasant pizza dinner with his family. At eight o'clock sharp, I said to Jensen, 'Let's go to your study.' Once inside, we closed the door and began a serious conversation. I presented a settlement recommendation that I and my TSMC colleagues had studied for three weeks—over one hundred million dollars in compensation—and emphasized that this was our only proposal, valid for forty-eight hours; if he did not agree within forty-eight hours, we would have no choice but to seek arbitration.

Jensen agreed within two days. To this day, NVIDIA remains one of TSMC's largest customers, and Jensen and his wife Lori remain friends of Sophie and me.

调整降价速度（二○一○年及以后）

经验和「学习曲线」理论都告诉我们：同一节点技术晶圆的单位成本会随累积产量而下降。再加上「抢单」竞争激烈，半导体的降价已成惯例。

只是二○○五至二○○九年蔡力行任CEO期间，台积电降价的幅度超过了成本降低的幅度，以致毛利率降低。在这期间，管理团队又提出了一个营收大幅增加、但利润不增加、无法接受的五年计划。我只得把这五年计划原封退还，要求管理团队再提。

二○○九年六月我回任CEO后的首要工作之一就是降低降价速度，使得我们的毛利率能逐步上升。通常台积电每年的价格在前一年底就和客户商定，所以我在二○○九年六月中已无能为力，我只能致力于二○一○年的价格。经过半年我自己、总部「订价」幕僚单位以及全球营销组织的努力，使得二○一○年的降幅缩小到二○○九年降幅的三分之二；后一年的降幅再进一步降低到二○○九年降幅的一半。

随着售价比较稳定，「减低成本」运动又每时每刻都在推行，我们的毛利率也有显着的进步。二○○九至二○一八年都在四十五％至五十％间。五年内毛利率有五个百分点的进步！

多位「派遣人力」为「正职同仁」（二○一○年）

在同一地区内，如果雇主候用的人员有「同工不同酬」现象，这个雇主是自找麻烦——小则士气不振，大则工会干涉、群体抗议。

我在返任CEO后发现台积电约有两万两千位「正职员工」，却有三千两百位「派遣人力」。其中约五百位「派遣人力」是属于一些不同于一般「正职员工」的专业（保全、医护、团膳等），但绝大部分约两千七百位「派遣人力」的原因是为了降低成本。

在二○○九年底发现这问题后，我找了五位与此问题有关的副总成立「专案小组」，要求他们拟出办法以「正职员工」取代「派遣人力」。二○一○年三月我们对全体同仁公布此办法，并在六月至九月执行。办法是在原本两千七百多位工作与既有「正职员工」相似的「派遣人力」中，遴选两千四百位新任「正职同仁」。未经遴选的三百多位，台积电也经中介公司为他们找到新工作，或领取我们提供的「关怀金」离去。

此「替代案」还有一个我未预料但也不惊讶的后果——两千四百位自「派遣人力」改为「正职同仁」的生产力，竟与原来两千七百位「派遣人力」的生产力一样。我们不需要多添人。

若对的事，常有好报。

ReadShift 双语翻译

Both experience and the 'learning curve' theory tell us that the unit cost of wafers at the same node technology declines as cumulative output increases. Coupled with fierce competition for orders, price reductions in semiconductors had become customary.

However, during Ming-Lieh Tsai's tenure as CEO from 2005 to 2009, TSMC's price cuts exceeded the rate of cost reduction, causing gross margins to decline. During this period, the management team also proposed an unacceptable five-year plan that would significantly increase revenue but not profit. I had no choice but to return the plan unopened and ask the team to resubmit.

One of my first tasks after resuming the CEO role in June 2009 was to slow the pace of price cuts so that our gross margins could gradually recover. Because TSMC's prices for the coming year were typically agreed upon with customers at the end of the preceding year, I could do nothing for 2009 by mid-June; I could only focus on 2010 pricing. After six months of effort by myself, the headquarters pricing staff, and the global marketing organization, the price decline for 2010 was reduced to two-thirds of the 2009 decline; the following year's decline was reduced further to half of the 2009 decline.

As selling prices stabilized and the campaign to reduce costs continued around the clock, our gross margin also improved significantly. From 2009 to 2018, it remained between forty-five and fifty percent—a five percentage point improvement over five years!

Within the same workplace, if employees doing the same work receive different pay, the employer is inviting trouble—on the small scale, demoralization; on the large scale, union interference and collective protest.

After resuming the CEO role, I discovered that TSMC had approximately 22,000 'regular employees' but 3,200 'dispatched workers.' Of these, about 500 dispatched workers were in specialized roles different from regular employees (security, medical, catering, etc.), but the vast majority—approximately 2,700—were dispatched workers hired primarily to reduce costs.

After discovering this issue at the end of 2009, I assembled five vice presidents associated with this matter into a 'special project team' and asked them to devise a plan to replace dispatched workers with regular employees. We announced this plan to all employees in March 2010 and implemented it from June to September. From the original 2,700+ dispatched workers whose jobs were similar to existing regular employees, we selected 2,400 to become new 'regular colleagues.' For the remaining 300+ not selected, TSMC also helped them find new jobs through intermediary companies or provided them with 'care payments' to leave.

This 'substitution plan' had one consequence I had not anticipated but was not surprised by—the productivity of the 2,400 who were converted from dispatched workers to regular employees was the same as that of the original 2,700 dispatched workers. We did not need to add any headcount.

合并先进技术事业组织及主流技术事业组织（二○○九年十月）

在蔡力行任CEO期间，他成立了先进技术事业组织及主流技术事业组织。这两个单位的主力都是生产线（在台积电，我们亦称生产为「营运」），先进组织负责十二吋厂，主流组织负责六吋及八吋厂。

力行的事业组织其实也是一九九六年布鲁克主意的重演（见第二十四章）。一九九六年我否决了布鲁克的主意，十几年后没有否决力行。我想是因为布鲁克只是「总经理」不是CEO，而力行是CEO，应该给CEO更多空间。

但现在我回来做CEO，这个错误也应该改掉了。所以在处理最急迫问题（调整降价速度、解决与辉达的悬案）后，我就把先进组织和主流组织的业务发展单位抽出来，再把这两个组织合并为全公司的营运组织。

接着，合并两个抽出来的业务发展单位，并且赋予它新的使命——台积电的Marketing Department。

欧美大公司通常把行销（Sales）和Marketing放在一起。台湾企业界了解行销，但常忽视Marketing，甚至连好的译名都没有。我查英汉辞典，它对Marketing的译名是：市场学。其实Marketing岂止是一门学问！对以「客制」产品为主的台积电，它是每天都要执行的专业。

既然Marketing没有好的译名，我们就称它为Business Development吧。这名字译成中文容易，就是「业务发展」。

业务发展组织的使命是：

（一）了解并预测世界晶圆代工市场。

（二）了解台积电前三十大客户（约占台积电总营收的八十％）。

（三）了解台积电自己的研发及制造能力，因而配合客户的新产品要求，提高台积电的市占率。

（四）因为要做到（一）、（二）、（三），所以要常与客户接触。

我对CEO（包括自己）的期许是：把外面的世界带进公司里面，动员公司的人力、财力资源来应付外来的挑战及机会。其实，业务发展组织的负责人就是一个小型的CEO。

ReadShift 双语翻译

During Ming-Lieh Tsai's tenure as CEO, he established an Advanced Technology Business Organization and a Mainstream Technology Business Organization. Both units were centered on production lines (at TSMC, we also call production 'operations'); the Advanced unit was responsible for twelve-inch fabs, and the Mainstream unit for six-inch and eight-inch fabs.

Tsai's business organization was essentially a replay of Brook's idea from 1996 (see Chapter 24). In 1996 I had rejected Brook's proposal, but more than a decade later I did not reject Tsai's. I suppose it was because Brook was only a 'general manager,' not CEO, whereas Tsai was CEO, and a CEO should be given more latitude.

But now that I had returned as CEO, this mistake也应该 be corrected. So after addressing the most urgent issues (adjusting the pace of price cuts and resolving the outstanding dispute with NVIDIA), I pulled out the business development units from both the Advanced and Mainstream organizations and merged the two into a single company-wide operations organization.

Next, I merged the two extracted business development units and endowed them with a new mission—TSMC's Marketing Department.

Large companies in Europe and the United States typically group Sales and Marketing together. The Taiwanese business community understands 'sales' but often neglects 'marketing,' to the point that there is not even a good translation. I looked up marketing in an English-Chinese dictionary, and the translation given was 'market studies.' But marketing is far more than an academic discipline! For TSMC, which is primarily a custom-product company, it is a professional function practiced every day.

Since marketing had no good Chinese translation, we called it Business Development. Translating that name into Chinese was easy: 'business development.'

The mission of the business development organization was:

(1) Understand and forecast the global foundry market.

(2) Understand TSMC's top thirty customers (accounting for approximately eighty percent of TSMC's total revenue).

(3) Understand TSMC's own research and development and manufacturing capabilities, thereby aligning with customers' new product requirements to increase TSMC's market share.

(4) To accomplish (1), (2), and (3), it was necessary to maintain frequent contact with customers.

My expectation for a CEO (including myself) was to bring the outside world into the company and mobilize the company's human and financial resources to meet external challenges and opportunities. In essence, the head of the business development organization was a small CEO.

任命魏哲家与刘德音

蔡力行已把公司里最被看好的两位经理人——刘德音和魏哲家——分别担任先进技术组织和主流技术组织的负责人。现在我把这两个组织合并了，又把业务发展单位抽出来成立新组织（约六、七十人），我怎幺善待德音和哲家？

这两位过去都有相当丰富的研发及营运经验，但都缺乏营销、市场及客户接触经验。新的业务发展组织会是一个新的学习机会和经验。我先把业务发展组织的职位征询两位中较资深的刘德音，但他拒绝了：「我现在（先进技术事业组织）就有一万多名员工，怎幺要我接受只有六、七十名员工的位置？」事实上，以员工数目衡量主管职位的重要性是一个错误的迷思。高层接触机会也许是更好的衡量。

接着我征询魏哲家，他倒欣然接受业务发展组织的职位。那幺就当然定了。当然，刘德音也得到了他所要的营运组织职位。

召回蒋尚义（二○○九年九月）

蒋尚义在一九九七年加入台积电，是第一个我赋予「技术领先」使命的研发主管（见第二十七章）。但是他在蔡力行任CEO期内选择退休。我认为他是不可多得的研发主管人才，所以在我回任CEO后，请他到我的新竹办公室共进牛肉面午餐，并要求他回台积电重任研发主管一职。尚义欣然答应，并于二○○九年十月初——亦即我回任CEO后不到四个月——重任研发资深副总。

ReadShift 双语翻译

Tsai had already placed the company's two most promising managers—Aric Liu and CC Wei—as heads of the Advanced Technology and Mainstream Technology organizations, respectively. Now that I had merged these two organizations and extracted the business development unit to form a new organization (about 60 to 70 people), how should I treat Liu and Wei fairly?

Both had considerable experience in research and development and operations, but neither had experience in marketing, market analysis, or customer engagement. The new business development organization would be a new learning opportunity. I first approached the more senior of the two, Aric Liu, about the position, but he declined: 'I already manage over ten thousand employees in the Advanced Technology Business Organization. How can you ask me to take a position with only 60 or 70 employees?'

In fact, measuring the importance of a managerial position by the number of subordinates is a misconception. Opportunities for senior-level engagement might be a better measure.

I then approached CC Wei, who readily accepted the position. So it was settled. Of course, Aric Liu also received the operations organization position he wanted.

Shang-yi Chiang joined TSMC in 1997 and was the first research and development executive I entrusted with the mission of 'technology leadership' (see Chapter 27). But he chose to retire during Tsai's CEO tenure. I considered him an exceptionally rare R&D executive talent, so after I resumed the CEO role, I invited him to my Hsinchu office for beef noodle soup lunch and asked him to return to TSMC as head of R&D. Chiang gladly accepted and, in early October 2009—less than four months after my return—resumed his role as senior vice president of R&D.

确定营收八％为研发费用（二○一○年）

蒋尚义回任后，台积电的研发经费已相当充足，研发同仁也没有埋怨经费不足。但我想：应该把我们对研发承诺数量化。

在那「滚滚新世纪」的头十年，我常常追忆我在德仪任全球半导体集团总经理时，想把德仪的半导体研发经费自营收的四.七％增加至五.五％，而累被拒绝的痛苦（见第九章）。在二○○九年回顾台积电的研发经费占营收比例，自一九九七年至二○○九年一直在五％至六％范围内。我想：还有增加的空间！就随手把「营收的八％」作为我们研发经费占营收的目标。
