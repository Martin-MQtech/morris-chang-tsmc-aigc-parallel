> **背景知识延伸｜莎士比亚与半导体策略**
>
> 张忠谋引用莎士比亚《凯撒大帝》中「There is a tide in the affairs of men」这一段来描述他加大投资的决定。这一引用既是文学修辞，也透露出张忠谋的决策哲学：识别产业「高潮」并全力押注。这与他在德仪时代倡导的「直线投资」理念一脉相承。
>
> <div class="rebook-translation">
> <span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
> <p class="en-para">Chang quoted Shakespeare's line from Julius Caesar—"There is a tide in the affairs of men"—to describe his decision to increase investment. The allusion is literary, yes, but it also reveals Chang's decision-making philosophy: identify the industry's tidal peak and commit fully. This instinct was consistent with the "straight-line investment" principle he had championed during his TI years.</p>

> **修辞赏析｜「老骥伏枥」的叙事力量**
>
> 本章标题「老骥伏枥，志在千里」取自曹操《龟虽寿》。这七个字同时承载了三重含义：一是张忠谋以七十多岁之龄重返CEO岗位的自况；二是对台积电这家已有二十多年历史的公司仍然充满雄心壮志的象征；三是预告了此后八年台积电在二十八纳米、智能手机市场的大手笔投资和最终取得的辉煌成就。
>
> <div class="rebook-translation">
> <span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
> <p class="en-para">The chapter title, "The old steed in the stable still dreams of galloping a thousand miles," comes from Cao Cao's "Though the Tortoise Live Long." These seven characters carry three meanings at once: Chang's own self-portrait as a man in his seventies returning to the CEO role; a symbol of the ambition still burning in a company more than two decades old; and a foreshadowing of the massive investments TSMC would make over the next eight years—in twenty-eight-nanometer technology and the smartphone market—and the brilliant achievements that followed.</p>
> </div>
