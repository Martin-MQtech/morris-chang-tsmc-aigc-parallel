# 台积电篇三·第三十二章｜老骥伏枥，志在千里

二○○九年六月，在「假淘汰、真裁员」风波平息后，我重新兼任台积电CEO。那一年我七十八岁。许多人问我：为什么还要回来？我的回答是：台积电正面临关键时刻，四十纳米良率问题未解、客户关系紧张、内部士气低落——这些都需要有人出来承担。

「老骥伏枥，志在千里」——这句曹操的诗，正是我当时心境的最佳写照。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">In June 2009, after the 'fake elimination, real layoffs' controversy had subsided, I resumed the CEO role at TSMC. I was seventy-eight years old. Many people asked me why I had come back. My answer was that TSMC was facing a critical moment: the forty-nanometer yield problem remained unsolved, customer relations were strained, and internal morale was low—all of which required someone to step up.</p>
<p class="en-para">'The old steed in the stable still dreams of galloping a thousand li'—that line from Cao Cao's poetry was the perfect expression of my state of mind at the time.</p>
