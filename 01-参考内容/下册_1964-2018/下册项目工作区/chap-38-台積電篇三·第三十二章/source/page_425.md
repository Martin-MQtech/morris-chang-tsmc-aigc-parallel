## 解决与辉达的悬案（二○○九年七月）

辉达是台积电四十纳米节点技术的主要伙伴及客户。台积电在四十纳米初期的生产问题使辉达遭受了严重损失。辉达在我返任CEO一年前就开始与台积电争议，但一直没有结论。

我返任CEO后的头几天，几乎全部时间都在电话上告诉重要客户我返任的消息。在与辉达CEO黄仁勋（Jensen Huang）通话时，他也特别提醒我们两家公司之间的悬案，并要求尽速解决。

此后三星期，我花了几十小时与公司内有关同仁深入探讨此案。二○○九年七月初，我认为我已清楚了解此事并且有解决方案，我电邮Jensen：「我下星期来硅谷，拟于七月十五日下午六点半到你家吃沙拉和披萨。」

那时我和Jensen已是十几年的老友，也常去他家吃沙拉和披萨。但这次他接到我的电邮著急了——我们两家公司间有这么严重的争论，怎么我只管吃沙拉和披萨？他回电邮说：「那么，我们什么时候谈正事？」

我立刻回复：「六点半到你家，先和你全家吃披萨，八点整我和你到你的书房谈生意。」

七月十五日我准时到Jensen家，与他全家有一个简单而愉快的披萨晚餐。八点整，我对Jensen说：「我们去你书房吧。」进书房后我们关起门来开始严肃的谈话。我提出我与台积电同仁研究了三星期的和解建议——上亿美元的赔偿金，并且强调：这建议是我们唯一的建议，它的有效期是四十八小时，如你在四十八小时内仍不同意，我们只得找仲裁人了。

Jensen在两天内同意了。直到现在辉达仍是台积电最大客户之一，而且Jensen与他夫人Lori仍是我与Sophie的好友。

<p class="en-para">NVIDIA was TSMC's primary partner and customer for the forty-nanometer node. TSMC's early production problems at forty nanometers caused NVIDIA significant losses. NVIDIA had begun disputing with TSMC a year before my return as CEO, but no resolution had been reached.</p>
<p class="en-para">In the first few days after resuming the CEO role, I spent almost all my time on the phone informing key customers of my return. When I spoke with NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang, he specifically called attention to the outstanding dispute between our two companies and urged a swift resolution.</p>
<p class="en-para">For the next three weeks, I spent dozens of hours in deep discussion with relevant colleagues within the company. In early July 2009, I felt I understood the matter clearly and had a solution. I emailed Jensen: 'I will come to Silicon Valley next week and plan to join your family for salad and pizza at 6:30 p.m. on July 15.'</p>
<p class="en-para">By then Jensen and I had been friends for over a decade, and I had often dined at his home on salad and pizza. But this time he was alarmed by my email—given how serious the dispute was between our companies, why was I only thinking about salad and pizza? He emailed back: 'Then when are we going to discuss business?'</p>
<p class="en-para">I replied immediately: 'I'll come to your home at six-thirty, have pizza with your family first, and at eight o'clock sharp, you and I will go to your study to discuss business.'</p>
<p class="en-para">On July 15 I arrived on time at Jensen's home and had a simple and pleasant pizza dinner with his family. At eight o'clock sharp, I said to Jensen, 'Let's go to your study.' Once inside, we closed the door and began a serious conversation. I presented a settlement recommendation that I and my TSMC colleagues had studied for three weeks—over one hundred million dollars in compensation—and emphasized that this was our only proposal, valid for forty-eight hours; if he did not agree within forty-eight hours, we would have no choice but to seek arbitration.</p>
<p class="en-para">Jensen agreed within two days. To this day, NVIDIA remains one of TSMC's largest customers, and Jensen and his wife Lori remain friends of Sophie and me.</p>
