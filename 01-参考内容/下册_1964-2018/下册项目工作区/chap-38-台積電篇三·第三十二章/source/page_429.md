## 确定营收八％为研发费用（二○一○年）

蒋尚义回任后，台积电的研发经费已相当充足，研发同仁也没有埋怨经费不足。但我想：应该把我们对研发承诺数量化。

在那「滚滚新世纪」的头十年，我常常追忆我在德仪任全球半导体集团总经理时，想把德仪的半导体研发经费自营收的四.七％增加至五.五％，而累被拒绝的痛苦（见第九章）。在二○○九年回顾台积电的研发经费占营收比例，自一九九七年至二○○九年一直在五％至六％范围内。我想：还有增加的空间！就随手把「营收的八％」作为我们研发经费占营收的目标。

果然，二○一一年以后，几乎每年研发经费都是当年营收的八％左右。随着营收的成长，研发经费也达到了二○二三年的五十六亿美元（新台币一千八百亿元），相当可观的数字，已超过麻省理工学院整年的总经费！当然，我们只做制程的研发，如果与客户伙伴的设计研发经费一并算起来，又有哪家半导体IDM公司的研发经费可以与我们相比？

<p class="en-para">After Shang-yi Chiang returned, TSMC's R&amp;D funding was already quite adequate, and R&amp;D colleagues had no complaints about insufficient funding. But I thought: we should quantify our commitment to R&amp;D.</p>
<p class="en-para">In the first decade of the 'surging new century,' I often recalled the frustration I had experienced at TI when I tried to increase TI's semiconductor R&amp;D spending from 4.7 percent to 5.5 percent of revenue, only to be repeatedly rejected (see Chapter 9). In 2009, reviewing TSMC's R&amp;D spending as a percentage of revenue, I found that from 1997 to 2009 it had remained in the five to six percent range. I thought: there is room to increase! So I casually set the target at 'eight percent of revenue.'</p>
<p class="en-para">As it turned out, from 2011 onward, R&amp;D spending was approximately eight percent of revenue almost every year. With revenue growth, R&amp;D spending reached 5.6 billion dollars (180 billion New Taiwan dollars) in 2023—a considerable figure, exceeding the entire annual budget of MIT! Of course, we only conduct process R&amp;D. If we add the design R&amp;D spending of our customer partners, which semiconductor IDM company's R&amp;D spending could compare with ours?</p>
