# 出版者的话｜全球化与地缘政治交织下的「三赢」｜高希均

出版者的的话

全球化与地缘政治交织下的「三赢」——张忠谋、护国神山、台湾贡献

高希均

半导体功臣张忠谋的一生（一九三一—），是一场二十一世纪人人可以置身其中的科技与智慧盛宴。

《晶片战争》作者克里斯·米勒（Chris Miller）曾在二〇二三年三月十六日与张忠谋对谈时表示：「如果只选一个人代表半导体，那就是张忠谋。他的人生故事是整个半导体产业的缩影。」

《华尔街日报》专栏作家科恩（Ben Cohen）于二〇二四年三月二十九日刊文指出：「张忠谋专业晶圆代工的创新商业模式改变了产业结构，是推动全球最重要技术发展的重要人物。」

这些赞词的根源，实质上证实了二〇〇七年张忠谋在上海发表的演讲。他提出了要想成为世界级的企业，就要做到九个条件。

回想十七年前十一月二十三日，时任台积电董事长的张忠谋，在当时两岸直航未通前，一清早从台北出发，香港转机，傍晚才赶到，刚好赶上大会颁赠他的「终身成就奖」。

<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">The life of Morris Chang (1931–), the man who did so much for the semiconductor industry, has been a feast of technology and intelligence that everyone in the 21st century could immerse themselves in.</p>
<p class="en-para">Chris Miller, author of <em>Chip War</em>, said in a conversation with Chang on March 16, 2023: "If you had to choose just one person to represent the semiconductor industry, it would be Morris Chang. His life story is the entire semiconductor industry in miniature."</p>
<p class="en-para">Ben Cohen, columnist for <em>The Wall Street Journal</em>, wrote on March 29, 2024 that "Morris Chang's innovative business model of pure-play foundry changed the industrial structure and positioned him as a pivotal figure in driving the world's most critical technology forward."</p>
<p class="en-para">The roots of such praise point, in substantive terms, to a speech Morris Chang delivered in Shanghai in 2007. He proposed nine conditions for building a "world-class" enterprise.</p>
<p class="en-para">Think back to November 23, seventeen years ago — Morris Chang, then Chairman of TSMC, before direct cross-strait flights were available, departed from Taipei early in the morning, connected through Hong Kong, and arrived only in the evening, just in time to receive a Lifetime Achievement Award at the ceremony.</p>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Pure-play foundry**
**中文解释**：专业晶圆代工，指专门为其他公司制造芯片、自身不做芯片设计的商业模式。台积电是这一模式的开创者。
**商业造句**：*The pure-play foundry model allows fabless design companies to focus on innovation without owning a single fabrication plant.*

2. **Industry in miniature**
**中文解释**：产业的缩影，指一个人物或事件能够折射出整个行业的发展轨迹与时代特征。
**商业造句**：*Steve Jobs' career is the personal computer industry in miniature — from garage startup to global empire.*

3. **Pivotal figure**
**中文解释**：关键人物，指在产业或历史转折点上具有决定性作用的个体。
**商业造句**：*Sharing-economy platforms depend on one pivotal figure — the platform architect who designs the trust mechanism.*

4. **Lifetime Achievement Award**
**中文解释**：终身成就奖，授予在某领域作出持久且卓越贡献的人物，通常代表官方或行业的最高认可。
**商业造句**：*Receiving the Lifetime Achievement Award was less a capstone than a starting signal for her next venture.*

5. **World-class enterprise**
**中文解释**：世界级企业，指在创新能力、盈利水平、全球影响力和持续成长方面均达到国际顶尖标准的公司。
**商业造句**：*Becoming a world-class enterprise demands more than scale — it requires defensible intellectual property and enduring client trust.*

6. **Cross-strait**
**中文解释**：两岸的，特指台湾海峡两岸（中国大陆与台湾）之间的关系语境；在商业叙事中常用于描述地缘政治对产业的影响。
**商业造句**：*Cross-strait supply chain integration has historically been a determining factor for Taiwan's semiconductor competitiveness.*

### 修辞赏析

<span class="rhetoric-note">
<span class="zh">高希均的叙事起手：「半导体功臣张忠谋的一生，是一场二十一世纪人人可以置身其中的科技与智慧盛宴。」用「盛宴」二字将个人传记升华为公共盛宴——既是知识的，也是体验的。这种写法让一个出版人的推荐序突破了「序」的边界，变成了一篇独立的文化宣言。</span>
<span class="en">Gao Xijun's opening narrative move: using the word "feast" (盛宴) to elevate a personal biography into a public event — intellectual and experiential. This rhetorical choice transforms a publisher's note into something closer to a cultural manifesto, breaking the conventions of what a preface is supposed to be.</span>
</span>

<span class="rhetoric-note">
<span class="zh">引语的节奏控制也很讲究：先用米勒的「缩影」定位人物，再用科恩的「推动者」赋予动感，最后落到「九条铁律」这个具体锚点。三步递进，从宏观定义到微观方法，读者被不知不觉引入正题——这正是高希均作为出版人四十年的功力。</span>
<span class="en">The pacing of the quotations is equally deliberate: first Miller's "in miniature" to locate the figure, then Cohen's "driving force" to impart momentum, finally arriving at the concrete anchor of "Nine Iron Rules." Three steps, from macro-definition to micro-method — an old publisher's trick for drawing readers into the main narrative without their realizing it.</span>
</span>

### 背景知识延伸

**《晶片战争》（Chip War）**：克里斯·米勒（Chris Miller）著，2022 年出版，获《金融时报》2022 年度商业图书奖。该书梳理半导体产业从德州到硅谷、从日本到韩国、再到台湾的地缘政治变迁。书中将张忠谋定位为「改写产业规则」的核心人物，认为没有台积电就没有今日的全球芯片供应链格局。

**「九条铁律」的由来**：二〇〇七年十一月，张忠谋在上海获颁「终身成就奖」后，以「打造世界级企业」为题发表演讲，提出九个条件。后来这九条被外界称为「张忠谋手订的台积电九条铁律」。详见本书第二十一章〈台积电的经营理念及九〇年代的策略〉。
