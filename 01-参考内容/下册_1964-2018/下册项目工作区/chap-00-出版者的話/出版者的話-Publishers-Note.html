出版者的话｜全球化与地缘政治交织下的「三赢」｜高希均

出版者的的话全球化与地缘政治交织下的「三赢」——张忠谋、护国神山、台湾贡献高希均半导体功臣张忠谋的一生（一九三一—），是一场二十一世纪人人可以置身其中的科技与智慧盛宴。

《晶片战争》作者克里斯·米勒（Chris Miller）曾在二〇二三年三月十六日与张忠谋对谈时表示：「如果只选一个人代表半导体，那就是张忠谋。他的人生故事是整个半导体产业的缩影。」《华尔街日报》专栏作家科恩（Ben Cohen）于二〇二四年三月二十九日刊文指出：「张忠谋专业晶圆代工的创新商业模式改变了产业结构，是推动全球最重要技术发展的重要人物。」这些赞词的根源，实质上证实了二〇〇七年张忠谋在上海发表的演讲。他提出了要想成为世界级的企业，就要做到九个条件。

回想十七年前十一月二十三日，时任台积电董事长的张忠谋，在当时两岸直航未通前，一清早从台北出发，香港转机，傍晚才赶到，刚好赶上大会颁赠他的「终身成就奖」。

修辞赏析 · Language & Logic Appreciation

高希均的叙事起手：「半导体功臣张忠谋的一生，是一场二十一世纪人人可以置身其中的科技与智慧盛宴。」用「盛宴」二字将个人传记升华为公共盛宴——既是知识的，也是体验的。这种写法让一个出版人的推荐序突破了「序」的边界，变成了一篇独立的文化宣言。 Gao Xijun's opening narrative move: using the word "feast" (盛宴) to elevate a personal biography into a public event — intellectual and experiential. This rhetorical choice transforms a publisher's note into something closer to a cultural manifesto, breaking the conventions of what a preface is supposed to be.

引语的节奏控制也很讲究：先用米勒的「缩影」定位人物，再用科恩的「推动者」赋予动感，最后落到「九条铁律」这个具体锚点。三步递进，从宏观定义到微观方法，读者被不知不觉引入正题——这正是高希均作为出版人四十年的功力。 The pacing of the quotations is equally deliberate: first Miller's "in miniature" to locate the figure, then Cohen's "driving force" to impart momentum, finally arriving at the concrete anchor of "Nine Iron Rules." Three steps, from macro-definition to micro-method — an old publisher's trick for drawing readers into the main narrative without their realizing it.

修辞赏析 · Language & Logic Appreciation

「禁不住有了泪影与共鸣」是典型的高希均笔法：用「泪影」而非「流泪」，克制中传递出深沉的情感。这种「写情而不溺于情」的分寸感，既是文人出版人的标志，也让这段叙事在推荐序中脱颖而出。 "Could not hold back the tears of resonance" is classic Gao Xijun: "tears" (泪影) rather than "weeping," conveying deep emotion with restraint. This calibrated sentimentality — writing about emotion without drowning in it — is the hallmark of a literate publisher, and it sets this narrative apart from a conventional endorsement.

九条铁律的数字罗列打破前文的散文节奏，制造出类似PPT幻灯片的「视觉切换」。这种从感性叙事突然切入理性清单的手法，是高希均用来模拟张忠谋本人思维模式的方式——正如张忠谋既是感性的上海文人，也是理性的德州工程师。 The numbered list of Nine Iron Rules disrupts the preceding prose rhythm, creating a slide-like visual shift. This abrupt move from感性 narrative into a理性 list is Gao Xijun's way of mirroring Chang's own cognitive mode — a man who is simultaneously the lyrical Shanghai intellectual and the analytical Texas engineer.

Born in a war-torn China, Morris Chang had changed schools many times and lived in six cities before the age of eighteen, personally experiencing the Japanese bombing of Guangzhou and Chongqing. Then in 1948, with his family, he left Shanghai for Hong Kong, stayed briefly, and emigrated to the United States. The hardships of his youth made him appreciate his parents' devotion to their only son — they had arranged the best possible schools for him: Nankai Middle School in Chongqing and Nanyang Model Middle School in Shanghai. His brilliance taught him early that everything in life would depend on himself, making him all the more treasure his chance to study in America.

The following year he entered MIT, earning his master's degree in 1953 and acquiring the skills to build a life in the United States. Later, sponsored by Texas Instruments, he went to Stanford and earned his Ph.D. in 1964. Equipped with credentials from America's three most prestigious institutions, he returned to TI.

Morris Chang: An Autobiography, Volume I, 1931–1964 tells of his youth, the youth of the semiconductor industry, and one large era's arc from desolation to prosperity. It shows a literary youth adrift in war choosing between the arts and engineering; a determined young man forged by the hard realities of the American workplace; a man of formidable skill who could not afford to hesitate when opportunity called. The first half of his life — under new-world skies that were mostly sunny but not without their storms — had already revealed the figure of a man who knew when to advance and when to retreat.

Now published, Morris Chang: An Autobiography, Volume II, 1964–2018, in his own words: the chairman's work grew so enjoyable that he simply could not let go.

Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇
War-torn—饱受战争蹂躏的，形容一个国家或地区长期处于战争创伤之中；在传记写作中常用于解释人物的早期驱动力。
Desolation to prosperity—从苍凉到繁盛，形容一个人、组织或国家从极端困顿走向高度繁荣的轨迹。
Forge—锻造，指通过艰苦经历塑造性格或能力；在组织行为学中常与「developed through adversity」搭配使用。

Credentials—学历凭证，指证明个人能力的正式资质（学位、证书等）；在职业发展中作为「信号机制」，帮助招聘方降低信息不对称。
修辞赏析 · Language & Logic Appreciation

对比结构贯穿本页：「苍凉→繁盛」「文科↔工科」「晴天↔风雨」——三组反义词编织出一段紧张的命运叙事。高希均用这种紧凑的对仗，把近百年的家族史和个人成长史压缩进两段文字，是标准的「出版人写法」：信息密度极高，留给读者回味。 Antithesis structures the page: "desolation → prosperity," "arts ↔ engineering," "sunny skies ↔ storms" — three antonym pairs weaving a tense narrative of fate. With these tightly wound parallel structures, Gao Xijun compresses a century of family history and personal growth into two short passages. It is the publisher's signature move: maximum information density, leaving space for the reader's reflection.

「欲罢不能」四字收束本段，既是张忠谋对自己董事长生涯的坦诚自白，也为下册漫长的叙事埋下了最关键的「钩子」：一个本可随时退休的人，为什么选择做到八十七岁？读者带着这个问题翻开第十四章。 The four characters 欲罢不能 ("simply could not let go") close the passage — both Chang's candid assessment of his chairman years and the book's most crucial narrative hook: a man who could have retired at any point chose to stay until eighty-seven. The reader turns to Chapter 14 carrying that question.

This rhetorical device of borrowing another's voice is a classic biographical technique — the reader feels they are "hearing about" rather than "being told," which deepens trustworthiness.
