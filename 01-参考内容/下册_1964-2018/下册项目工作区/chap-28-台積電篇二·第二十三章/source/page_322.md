## 世界先进的起源

第二位中了记忆体诱惑的是当时工研院副院长史钦泰。钦泰是我在规画台积电时亲密度仅次于胡定华的同事，但在工研院衍生公司这课题上，他的看法与我很不一样。我认为衍生公司，人要移过去；他却不放人，认为工研院的半导体计画不只衍生一个公司，而是要衍生一个产业。

一个妈妈，要生许多孩子，而且这许多孩子要各有特色；不然，他们会互相竞争、互相残杀。这样的妈妈，工研院能做吗？

假使有人问我，我的答复会是斩钉截铁的"不能"。但是，我在八八年十二月已被调任为无权、不领薪、也没有什么事的工研院董事长，钦泰已不必问我。大概他知道如果问我，我会反对，所以也不问。

另一方面，工研院的"美洲技术顾问团"在八九竭力推荐记忆体为工研院"下一个半导体计画"。于是，以记忆体为载具的"次微米计画"就在九零年上路。这计画有一特点：自始就预备衍生一家记忆体公司。所以，它的"实验室"就盖在新竹科学园区，实际上是一座厂房——以便"衍生"新公司后立刻可成为工厂。

我在九零年才陆续知道"次微米计画"的内容，立刻就认为这是一个野心极大的计画。它的目的是五年后（一九九五年）衍生一个记忆体公司；既然要衍生一个公司，这公司当然要有竞争力，那么，从制程技术面来说，"次微米计画"必须紧紧跟随"摩尔定律"——这是它的前身（VLSI计画）在十年内都没做到的事；除制程技术外，记忆体公司还需要记忆体设计技术。工研院一直就没有好好发展设计技术，而且发展的只有简单的逻辑设计，几乎从未涉猎记忆体设计。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">The second person to fall to the lure of memory was then-ITRI Vice President Shi Qin-tai (Shih Ching-tai). Qin-tai was my closest colleague in planning TSMC after Hu Ding-hua. But on the topic of ITRI spin-offs, his view was quite different from mine. I believed that when spinning off companies, people should move over. He didn't want to release people; he believed ITRI's semiconductor program should spin off not just one company but an entire industry.</p>
<p class="en-para">One mother giving birth to many children—and these many children each having distinctive characteristics, otherwise they would compete with each other and kill each other. Could ITRI be that mother?</p>
<p class="en-para">Had anyone asked me, my answer would have been a resounding "no." But in December 1988, I had been reassigned as ITRI chairman with no power, no salary, and nothing to do, so Qin-tai no longer needed to ask me. He probably knew I would oppose it, so he didn't bother asking.</p>
<p class="en-para">On another front, ITRI's "Americas Technical Advisory Committee" vigorously pushed memory as ITRI's "next semiconductor program" in 1989. Thus, the memory-vehicle "Submicron Program" was launched in 1990. This program had one characteristic: from the start, it was designed to spin off a memory company. So its "laboratory" was built in Hsinchu Science Park—actually a fab building—so that after the new company was spun off, it could immediately become a fab.</p>
<p class="en-para">I didn't learn the details of the "Submicron Program" until 1990. I immediately saw this as an extremely ambitious program. Its goal was to spin off a memory company five years later (1995). Since a company was to be spun off, it would of course need competitiveness. From a process technology standpoint, the "Submicron Program" had to keep pace with "Moore's Law"—something its predecessor (the VLSI Program) had not accomplished in ten years. Beyond process technology, a memory company also needed memory design technology. ITRI had never properly developed design technology, and what it had developed was only simple logic design—it had barely touched memory design.</p>
</div>

## 李健熙的早餐

一九八九年一月，我刚卸任工研院院长职；施振荣董事长已告诉我他想自德仪购买记忆体技术、成立德碁记忆体公司的大致构想；但"次微米计画"还在史钦泰副院长心中酝酿，我尚不知情。此时我忽然接到韩国三星电子公司台北办事处的电话邀请：董事长李健熙正在台湾，想邀请我早餐。

当时大部分美国半导体公司已退出记忆体业，日本公司仍占据大部分记忆体市场，但因为《广场协议》和自，日币在一九五至八八年的三年内升值了一倍有余，所以日本记忆体公司已渐渐不支；相反地，韩国三星公司渐露锋芒。

我为了好奇而赴约——毕竟台积电不做记忆体，为什么李健熙要请我吃早餐？

我同时也邀施振荣和史钦泰一同前往。邀施，是因为我也知道他正在想办记忆体公司；邀史，是因为他是我成立台积电的重要助手，也是工研院研发重心的代表人物。见面后发现，原来这次李健熙的角色是"说客"。他认为记忆体业需要很大资本、很多人才。台湾出不起这资本、聚不到这人才；所以，还是死了进入记忆体业的心吧！为了要让我们见识三星已投入的资本和人才，他邀请我们前往韩国访问三星。

我事后才知道，李健熙在邀我早餐时，已知道施董事长要办德碁（李的情报可能来自德仪管道）。至于为什么用我做媒介？大概他从我在德仪的经历以及之后在台积电创立"专业晶圆代工"商业模式，知道我是一个"内行的半导体经营者"。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">In January 1989, I had just stepped down as ITRI president. Chairman Shi Zhengrong had already told me his general vision of buying memory technology from TI to form a Tepro memory company. But the "Submicron Program" was still brewing in Vice President Shi Qin-tai's mind, and I was unaware of it. Then I suddenly received a phone call from the Taipei office of Korea's Samsung Electronics: Chairman Lee Kun-hee was in Taiwan and wanted to invite me to breakfast.</p>
<p class="en-para">At that time, most American semiconductor companies had exited the memory business. Japanese companies still dominated most of the memory market, but because of the Plaza Accord, the Japanese yen had more than doubled in value against the USD between 1985 and 1988, so Japanese memory companies were gradually losing ground. Conversely, Korea's Samsung was emerging.</p>
<p class="en-para">I attended out of curiosity—after all, TSMC didn't do memory; why would Lee Kun-hee want to buy me breakfast?</p>
<p class="en-para">I also invited both Shi Zhengrong and Shi Qin-tai to come along. I invited Shi Zhengrong because I also knew he was thinking of starting a memory company. I invited Shi Qin-tai because he was an important aide in establishing TSMC and a representative figure of ITRI's R&amp;D center of gravity. At the meeting, it turned out that Lee Kun-hee's role this time was as a "lobbyist." He believed the memory industry required huge capital and much talent. Taiwan couldn't afford the capital or gather the talent—so give up the idea of entering the memory industry! To show us the capital and talent Samsung had already invested, he invited us to visit Samsung in Korea.</p>
<p class="en-para">I learned afterward that Lee Kun-hee already knew about Chairman Shi's plan to establish Tepro when he invited me to breakfast (Lee's intelligence may have come through TI channels). As for why he used me as an intermediary—probably from my experience at TI and afterward in creating the "dedicated foundry" business model at TSMC, he knew I was a "knowledgeable semiconductor operator."</p>
</div>

## 访问三星

总之，我和史、施接受了李健熙的邀请访问三星，也在两个半月后（一九八九年四月十三至十五日）实践了我们的访问。在韩国受到了三星诚恳的款待。十三日晚上到达首尔，住进三星开办的旅馆；十四日参观三星记忆体工厂。在此之前，我已看过不少记忆体工厂——德仪日本、德仪美国、还有好几个日本记忆体公司。的确，与我看过德日、德仪美的工厂相比，三星的工厂毫不逊色。三星的工程师和经理人相当熟谙他们的工作，也都相当诚恳回答我们的问题。

十五日上午，李健熙在一个布置华丽的大会客室接见我们。也有几位三星经理人员陪同。我们人见，在我脑海中一瞬即逝的印象是：有点像古代中国边疆酋长进贡时拜见中国皇帝。

李健熙问我们：怎么样，你们看到了吧？记忆体需要很大资本、很多人才。我已不记得我们的详细答复，总之是谢谢他，也说了些很佩服三星成就之类的话。当天下午我们就登机回台北。

这次访问，丝毫没有改变施董事长、史副院长对记忆体的看法。施董事长的德碁计画木已成舟，他在与李健熙早餐（一月）及访问三星（四月）中间，已在二月对经济部长报告要成立德碁。看了三星后，他还是继续积极进行德碁的开办。

史副院长在与李早餐前，已开始在心中酝酿以记忆体为载具、以五年内衍生记忆体公司为目标的"次微米计画"。访问三星后，他更积极对经济部倡议"次微米计画"。终于在一年多后，一九九零年七月，此计画正式开始。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">In any case, Shi, Shi, and I accepted Lee Kun-hee's invitation to visit Samsung, and two and a half months later (April 13-15, 1989), we fulfilled our visit. We received warm hospitality from Samsung in Korea. On the evening of the 13th we arrived in Seoul and checked into a Samsung-operated hotel. On the 14th we toured Samsung's memory fab. Before this, I had seen quite a few memory fabs—TI Japan, TI U.S., and several Japanese memory companies. Indeed, compared to the TI Japanese, TI U.S. fabs I had seen, Samsung's fab was in no way inferior. Samsung's engineers and managers were highly knowledgeable about their work and answered our questions quite frankly.</p>
<p class="en-para">On the morning of the 15th, Lee Kun-hee received us in a magnificently decorated boardroom. Several Samsung managers also accompanied him. A fleeting impression in my mind was: somewhat like a frontier tribal chief paying tribute to the Chinese emperor in ancient times.</p>
<p class="en-para">Lee Kun-hee asked us: Well, did you see? Memory requires huge capital and lots of talent. I don't remember our exact replies—in any case, we thanked him, and said things like how much we admired Samsung's achievements. That afternoon we boarded a plane back to Taipei.</p>
<p class="en-para">This visit didn't change Chairman Shi's or Vice President Shi's views of memory at all. Chairman Shi's Tepro plan was already a done deal—between the breakfast with Lee Kun-hee (January) and the Samsung visit (April), he had already reported to the Economic Minister in February about establishing Tepro. After seeing Samsung, he continued actively pursuing Tepro's founding.</p>
<p class="en-para">Vice President Shi, before the breakfast with Lee, had already begun brewing in his mind the "Submicron Program" with memory as its vehicle and the goal of spinning off a memory company within five years. After visiting Samsung, he became more active in advocating for the "Submicron Program" to the Ministry of Economic Affairs. Finally, over a year later, in July 1990, the program officially began.</p>
</div>
