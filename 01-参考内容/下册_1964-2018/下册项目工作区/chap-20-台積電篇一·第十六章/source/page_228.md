## 公文接力赛与正式批准

我还记得我把初稿给李政委看时，李政委频频摇头。他先告诉我要采取的格式，等我再次写稿后又仔细帮我修改字句，又告诉我以工研院院长身份，不应直接写签呈给俞院长，而应签呈给经济部部长李达海。我忙了几天后，于十一月二十七日签呈李达海部长，重点如下：

（一）共需资本两亿零两百万美元，七〇%投资（Equity），三〇%贷款。

（二）投资（Equity）部分一亿四千五百万美元。股份分配计划如下：政府七千万美元（四八．三%）；其他国内或国外投资三千五百万美元（二四．一%）；飞利浦投资可能性甚高。

（三）今日建立TSMC之原因，至少有四。

（四）总经理人选应为已在国际VLSI工业有相当管理地位、熟识最新制造技术、且能与国外市场建立密切关系者。大约需一美国人。需在美国招雇人员约十人左右。中下级干部人员可在台湾招雇，电子所可移转约一百名工程人员，应足够初步需要。

李达海部长接到我的签呈后又等了一个月，直至十二月三十日才签呈俞院长，除了摘述我对他的签呈外，他加了：「拟请钧院核定后指定有关首长成立筹划指导小组协调筹划及诱导资金必需租税措施，并请工研院张院长为执行秘书兼办筹建工作。」

此后行政院的行动就快了。（一九八六年）一月四日，行政院秘书长王章清函经建会：请经建会「提前审议」台积电；一月八日经建会就开会，主席为主委赵耀东，副主席为主委王昭明，我记得的出席委员有财政部部长钱纯、经济部部长李达海、交通部部长连战、中央银行总裁张继正。我又报告了一次新创的「专业代工」模式，以及我十一月二十七日签呈李部长的重点。报告后赵主委征求各委员意见，最令我难忘的是张继正总裁与我的对白：

张总裁：「你凭什么与美、日、韩公司竞争？」

我：「我们专业做晶圆代工，我凭好的服务竞争。」

张总裁哈哈了一声：「哈，台湾公司要凭商誉竞争，倒是第一次听闻。」

我那时虽听懂这是讥讽性的评语，但不太懂：难道台湾公司从不讲商誉？幸而没有别的委员借张总裁之题发挥。张总裁的话也像风一样，过了就没有了。

次日，赵主委就发文行政院秘书长：原则同意进行本案，并同意成立筹划指导小组，并请工研院张院长忠谋为执行秘书，兼办筹建工作。

一月十六日，行政院院会决定接受经建会的结论。一月二十八日，俞院长行文经济部等五部会及工研院，指派赵耀东主委、李达海部长为筹划指导小组的共同召集人；钱纯财政部部长、陈履安主委及我为小组成员，我也是执行秘书。这就是所谓「五人小组」。

促成台积电最出力的李政委，却不在「五人小组」内。李政委对我说，他自己要求俞院长不在小组内；又说，他功成身退了一九八五年九月十日我对俞院长简报、首次倡议台积电，到次年一月二十八日俞院长指定「筹划指导」五人小组，可说是筹备台积电的第一幕；第二幕即将展开。

<p class="en-para"><b>The Official Document Relay and Formal Approval</b></p>
<p class="en-para">I still remember showing my first draft to Minister Li — he shook his head repeatedly. He first instructed me on the proper format. After I rewrote it, he carefully helped me polish the wording. He also told me that as ITRI President, I should not submit a memorandum directly to Premier Yu, but to Minister of Economic Affairs Li Da-hai. After several days of work, I submitted my memorandum to Minister Li on November 27, with the following key points:</p>
<p class="en-para">(1) Total capital required: $202 million USD — 70% equity, 30% debt.</p>
<p class="en-para">(2) Equity portion: $145 million. Proposed share allocation: Government $70 million (48.3%); other domestic or foreign investors $35 million (24.1%); Philips investment highly probable.</p>
<p class="en-para">(3) At least four reasons to establish TSMC today.</p>
<p class="en-para">(4) The General Manager should be someone with significant management standing in the international VLSI industry, familiar with the latest manufacturing technology, and capable of building close relationships with foreign markets. Likely an American. Need to recruit about 10 people in the U.S. Mid- and lower-level staff can be recruited in Taiwan; the Electronics Institute can transfer about 100 engineering personnel, sufficient for initial needs.</p>
<p class="en-para">Minister Li Da-hai received my memorandum and waited a full month before forwarding it to Premier Yu on December 30. In addition to summarizing my memorandum, he added: "I respectfully request that the Yuan, after approving, designate relevant agency heads to form a Preparatory Steering Group to coordinate planning and necessary tax incentive measures, and ask ITRI President Chang to serve as Executive Secretary and handle the preparatory work."</p>
<p class="en-para">After this, the Executive Yuan moved quickly. On January 4, 1986, Secretary-General Wang Zhangqing wrote to the CEPD asking it to "expedite review" of TSMC. On January 8, the CEPD convened, chaired by Chairman Zhao Yaodong with Vice Chair Wang Zhaoming. I recall attending committee members including Minister of Finance Qian Chun, Minister of Economic Affairs Li Da-hai, Minister of Transportation Lian Zhan, and CBC Governor Zhang Jizheng. I presented the innovative "dedicated foundry" model once more, along with the key points from my November 27 memorandum. After the presentation, Chairman Zhao solicited opinions. The exchange I remember most was with Governor Zhang:</p>
<p class="en-para">Governor Zhang: "What gives you the right to compete with American, Japanese, and Korean companies?"</p>
<p class="en-para">Me: "We specialize in foundry. I compete on excellent service."</p>
<p class="en-para">Governor Zhang laughed: "Ha! A Taiwanese company competing on reputation — that's the first I've heard of such a thing."</p>
<p class="en-para">Though I understood this was a sarcastic remark, I didn't quite grasp it: Don't Taiwanese companies care about reputation? Fortunately, no other committee members took the bait. Governor Zhang's words passed like the wind — gone as soon as spoken.</p>
<p class="en-para">The next day, Chairman Zhao issued a letter to the Secretary-General: In principle, agree to proceed; agree to form a Preparatory Steering Group; and ask ITRI President Morris Chang to serve as Executive Secretary handling preparatory work.</p>
<p class="en-para">On January 16, the Executive Yuan plenary adopted the CEPD's conclusion. On January 28, Premier Yu issued a directive to five ministries and ITRI, appointing Chairman Zhao Yaodong and Minister Li Da-hai as co-conveners of the Preparatory Steering Group; Minister of Finance Qian Chun, Minister Chen Lian, and myself as group members, with me also serving as Executive Secretary. This was the so-called "Five-Person Committee."</p>
<p class="en-para">Minister Li — the person who had done the most to advance TSMC — was not on the Five-Person Committee. Minister Li told me he had asked Premier Yu to exclude him, saying he had "retired after success." From my September 10, 1985 briefing to Premier Yu proposing TSMC, to Premier Yu's January 28, 1986 designation of the Five-Person Committee — this was Act One of TSMC's founding. Act Two was about to begin.</p>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Preparatory Steering Group (筹划指导小组)**
**中文解释**：行政院为筹备台积电而设的跨部会协调机构；五人小组是台积电得以在官僚体系中快速推进的关键制度设计。
**商业造句**：*The Preparatory Steering Group was the institutional vehicle that cut through bureaucratic inertia — giving TSMC the cross-ministerial authority it needed to move at unprecedented speed.*

2. **"Competing on reputation" (凭商誉竞争)**
**中文解释**：张继正总裁对张忠谋「凭服务竞争」的讥讽——台湾公司凭商誉竞争是第一次听闻；这句话反映了当时台湾产业的国际形象。
**商业造句**：*Governor Zhang's sarcastic "competing on reputation" remark captured the prevailing skepticism — yet within two decades, TSMC would become Taiwan's most respected global brand.*

3. **"Retired after success" (功成身退)**
**中文解释**：李国鼎主动要求不进入五人小组，自谓「功成身退」；这是中国传统政治智慧的体现——在最成功时退出，避免日后承担责任。
**商业造句**：*Li Guoding's decision to "retire after success" was a classic move in Chinese political culture — stepping away at the moment of triumph to preserve his legacy and avoid future entanglement.*
