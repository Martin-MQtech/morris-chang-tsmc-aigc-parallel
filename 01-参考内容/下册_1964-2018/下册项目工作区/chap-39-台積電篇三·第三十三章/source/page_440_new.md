<p class="en-para">I asked one question: 'Is it twenty-eight nanometer?'</p>
<p class="en-para">Jeff answered that it was twenty nanometer, with mass production planned one year later. I began to calculate in my head. Twenty nanometer was merely a half-node; pursuing it would be a detour, because we were already trial-producing twenty-half nanometer, and the true next node was sixteen nanometer.</p>
<p class="en-para">Jeff requested a meeting with TSMC's senior management the following day. On the morning of November 10, after arriving at the office, I convened TSMC's senior team in preparation for the two-thirty meeting with Jeff. During the session Jeff asked Apple's design, technology, and procurement personnel to come to TSMC as soon as possible to begin discussing the cooperation.</p>
<p class="en-para">Although Thanksgiving fell in late November and was followed by Christmas and the New Year—the two-month period when Americans most favored holidays—he still intended to begin close communications with TSMC's staff after Thanksgiving and before Christmas.</p>
<p class="en-para">In the end, on December 15 and 16, key personnel from Apple and TSMC held two days of intensive discussions in Hsinchu. Thus began the Apple-TSMC cooperative relationship.</p>
<p class="en-para">For me, Apple's coming to our door also brought an additional comfort: the business development organization that I had asked Morris Wei to establish the previous year (see Chapter 32) was now proving enormously valuable. They played a major role in this new customer relationship.</p>
