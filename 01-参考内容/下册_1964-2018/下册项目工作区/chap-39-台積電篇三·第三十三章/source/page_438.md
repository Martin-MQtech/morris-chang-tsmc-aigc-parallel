## 苹果的背景

苹果自一九七六年成立后，一直就是一个传奇性的科技公司。一九八○年它的股票在美国上市，苹果市值立刻比历史悠久、规模巨大的福特汽车公司还高。记得苹果上市后不久，我正访问台湾，对李国鼎政务委员提起苹果与福市的市值比较，李政委频频摇头，并説：「怎么有这样的事？」我回答：「这就是现代科技公司的神妙。」

一九八五年，苹果创办人贾伯斯（Steve Jobs）因公司政治被迫辞职。九○年代苹果一度家道中落。一九九七年贾伯斯返任，苹果又复兴，尤其在二○○七年推出智慧型手机，惊动世界，几乎人手一支，也改变了千千万万人的生活习惯。后来苹果的智慧型手机及个人电脑产品，市值曾达三兆美元，高居世界前两名。

智慧型手机是一个相当「半导体密集」的产品。所以台积电自二○○七年后就以苹果为潜在的大客户。只是苹果除了以创新成名外，也是一个非常保密的公司。那时我们只听到它已开始设计自用的积体电路，而且找韩国的三星电子代工。三星本来就是世界最大的记忆体公司（见第二十三章），一定也是苹果的存储器供应商；所以苹果找它代工逻辑产品也不稀奇。

只是，苹果打开智慧型手机市场后不久，雄心勃勃的三星会长李健熙忽然宣布三星也要做智慧型手机了。三星既是苹果的主要供应商，也变成苹果的主要竞争者。

假使我是贾伯斯，我一定会有「是可忍，孰不可忍」的感觉。

<p class="en-para">Since its founding in 1976, Apple had always been a legendary technology company. When its stock went public in the United States in 1980, Apple's market capitalization immediately exceeded that of Ford Motor Company, a firm with a long history and massive scale. I remember that shortly after Apple's listing, I was visiting Taiwan and mentioned the comparison of Apple and Ford's market values to Vice Premier Kuo-Ting Li. Premier Li shook his head repeatedly and said, 'How can such a thing exist?' I replied, 'That is the marvel of modern technology companies.'</p>
<p class="en-para">In 1985, Apple's founder Steve Jobs was forced to resign due to corporate politics. During the 1990s, Apple fell on difficult times. Jobs returned in 1997, and Apple revived, especially with the 2007 launch of the smartphone, which stunned the world. Nearly everyone carried one, and it changed the daily habits of hundreds of millions of people. Later, Apple's smartphone and personal computer products at one point reached a market capitalization of three trillion dollars, ranking among the top two in the world.</p>
<p class="en-para">Smartphones are products that are quite 'semiconductor-intensive.' So after 2007, TSMC regarded Apple as a potential major customer. But Apple, known for its innovation, was also an exceptionally secretive company. At that time, we only heard that it had begun designing its own integrated circuits and was having them fabricated by Samsung Electronics of Korea. Samsung was already the world's largest memory company (see Chapter 23) and certainly also Apple's memory supplier; so it was not surprising that Apple turned to it for logic product fabrication as well.</p>
<p class="en-para">But soon after Apple opened the smartphone market, the ambitious Samsung chairman Kun-Hee Lee suddenly announced that Samsung would also make smartphones. Samsung, already Apple's primary supplier, had become Apple's primary competitor.</p>
<p class="en-para">If I were Steve Jobs, I would certainly have felt that 'if this can be tolerated, nothing cannot be tolerated.'</p>
