# 台积电篇三·第三十三章｜苹果来敲门

二○一○年的一个秋日夜晚，苹果公司的营运长杰夫·威廉斯（Jeff Williams）突然成了我家的不速之客——由我太太Sophie的表弟郭台铭（鸿海集团创办人）带领，亲自到我家拜访。此举掀开了苹果与台积电合作关系的序幕，也成为台积电进军智慧型手机芯片市场的里程碑。

然而，从「苹果来敲们」到真正拿到订单，中间经历了两年的艰辛谈判、财务规划和技术挑战。苹果不仅要求台积电承诺庞大的产能，更在台积电与三星之间做了一番取舍。这段故事，既有商业博弈的紧张，也有两家公司价值观碰撞后的契合。

<p class="en-para">On an autumn evening in 2010, Jeff Williams, Apple's operations chief, suddenly became an unexpected guest at my home—accompanied by my wife Sophie's cousin, Terry Gou (founder of the Foxconn Group), who personally visited our home. This visit opened the prelude to the Apple–TSMC partnership and became a milestone in TSMC's entry into the smartphone chip market.</p>
<p class="en-para">However, from 'Apple knocking at our door' to actually receiving orders, there was a two-year journey of arduous negotiations, financial planning, and technical challenges. Apple not only demanded that TSMC commit to massive capacity but also made TSMC choose between it and Samsung. This story contained both the tension of business negotiation and the alignment of values between the two companies.</p>
