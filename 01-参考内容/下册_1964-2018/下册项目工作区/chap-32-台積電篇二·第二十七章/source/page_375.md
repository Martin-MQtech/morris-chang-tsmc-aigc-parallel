## 招募蒋尚义：技术领先世界的志愿

九七年四月，布鲁克辞职，我自兼总经理。除了与重要客户持续强化伙伴关系外，我也想好好地在研发上干一番——因为我认为研发人才的素质及勤奋敬业的精神应该是台湾的一个优势。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">In April 1997, Brook resigned, and I assumed the role of general manager. In addition to continuing to strengthen partnerships with key clients, I also wanted to make significant strides in R&D—for I believed that the quality of R&D talent and their diligent, dedicated spirit should be Taiwan's advantage.</p>
</div>

我接兼总经理后，曾繁城几乎立刻推荐了一位研发长给我。我在台湾面试蒋尚义，他有不错的学术背景及工作履历：台大电机系学士、普林斯顿硕士、史丹佛博士，先在德仪，后来到惠普做研发工作。我与蒋尚义谈了三小时，谈得很投机，当场就邀请他加入台积电做我们的研发副总。那时我已有「技术领先世界半导体业」的念头，但我想还是不要第一次见面就跟他谈这个——会把他吓坏了。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Almost immediately after I became general manager, Zeng Fan-cheng recommended a chief technology officer to me. I interviewed Chiang Shang-yi in Taiwan. He had an impressive academic background and work履历: a B.S. in electrical engineering from NTU, an M.S. from Princeton, and a Ph.D. from Stanford. He had worked at TI and later at HP in R&D. I talked with Chiang Shang-yi for three hours, and we hit it off immediately. I invited him on the spot to join TSMC as our vice president of R&D. At that time, I already harbored the notion of "technology leadership in the global semiconductor industry," but I thought it better not to discuss it on our first meeting—I would have frightened him.</p>
</div>

蒋尚义在九七年七月七日报到。我想：现在要讲了。新的研发副总应该知道我们的长期研发目标。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Chiang Shang-yi reported for duty on July 7, 1997. I thought: now is the time to speak. The new VP of R&D should know our long-term R&D objectives.</p>
</div>

几年后的《商业周刊》相当生动地描述了这个场面：
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Years later, Commercial Weekly described the scene vividly:</p>
</div>

> 报到后，蒋尚义第一次与台积电董事长张忠谋开会，张忠谋开门见山，要求他必须将台积电研发水准拉到全球领先地位。
>
> 蒋尚义初来乍到，当时台积电的技术世代距世界领先厂有两年之遥，看着研发部门仅一百二十人，一年经费不到一亿美元，他想：「如果能做到第二排的第一个，就很不错了。」于是婉转地回答张忠谋：「技术领先者在研发技术的投资，必须是当老二者的三倍。」
>
> 董事长马上听出我话里的意思，训了我一顿——说力行（蔡力行）之前的台积电研发副总就不会像蒋尚义这样——「就是知道台积电要做technology leader（技术领先者）。」蒋尚义回忆当年，笑着说：「大概觉得我很没出息。」

「技术领先全世界」的目标，一直到二○一八年七奈米量产才实现。但这个目标的建立，产生了一个副产品：它巩固了我们「技术自主」的决心。时机也正对，因为诱惑又要来了。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">The goal of "technology leadership worldwide" was not realized until the mass production of seven-nanometer nodes in 2018. But establishing this goal produced a side benefit: it consolidated our resolve for "technological self-sufficiency." And the timing was right, for another temptation was about to arrive.</p>
</div>
