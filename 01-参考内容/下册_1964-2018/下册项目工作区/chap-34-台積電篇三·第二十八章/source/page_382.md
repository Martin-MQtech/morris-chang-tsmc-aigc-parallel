# 台积电篇三·第二十八章｜结婚

## 结婚

我在一九八一年底与前妻分离。此后二十年，在职业上历经德仪、通用、工研院、台积电；在住处的迁移上历经达拉斯、纽约、台湾；在年龄上，已自中年迈人晚年；在心情上，可谓饱经风霜。本来已无再婚意愿，但是在台湾我遇到了张淑芬（Sophie）。她的活泼、好动、乐观，忽然使我感觉又年轻起来。于是我们就决定于二○○一年一月二十三日结婚。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">I separated from my first wife at the end of 1981. Over the following twenty years, I experienced career transitions across TI, General Instrument, ITRI, and TSMC; residential moves from Dallas to New York to Taiwan; and a passage from middle age into late life. Emotionally, I had weathered many storms. I had no intention of marrying again—until I met Chang Shu-fen (Sophie) in Taiwan. Her vivacity, restlessness, and optimism suddenly made me feel young again. We decided to marry on January 23, 2001.</p>
</div>

我们两人都是第二次婚姻，「重作冯妇」，已无第一次婚姻的「人生才刚开始」的新鲜感觉。当然也不想惊动很多朋友，所以选择在美国加州圣荷西市政府的小礼拜堂举行仪式。当晚在硅谷一个高级中国餐馆开了三桌宴席，与住在加州的近亲同庆。Sophie的父母都参加了。我的父亲已逝世，母亲九十一岁，已在失智初期，而且远在纽约，不能参加。直至两个月后，Sophie和我去纽约，母亲才见到新媳妇。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">We were both entering our second marriage—without the fresh sensation of "life just beginning" that accompanies a first wedding. Of course, we did not wish to inconvenience many friends, so we chose to hold the ceremony in the small chapel of the San Jose city hall in California. That evening, we hosted three tables of banquet at a fine Chinese restaurant in Silicon Valley, celebrating with close relatives living in California. Sophie's parents were both present. My father had passed away; my mother, ninety-one and in the early stages of dementia, lived far away in New York and could not attend. It was not until two months later, when Sophie and I visited New York, that my mother finally met her new daughter-in-law.</p>
</div>

在我们决定结婚日期时，我不知道一场暴风雨即将侵袭台积电——二○○一年第一季的订单，竟比前一季度的订单少了四分之一！而且看起来，这场暴风雨正要开始，没有停止的迹象。待得我们知道了，我和Sophie决定不改变结婚日期，但延迟蜜月。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">When we set the wedding date, I had no idea that a storm was about to descend upon TSMC—the first-quarter 2001 orders turned out to be a full quarter lower than the preceding quarter. And it appeared that this storm was only beginning, with no sign of abating. Once we learned of this, Sophie and I decided not to change the wedding date, but to postpone the honeymoon.</p>
</div>

新婚后的第一天，我一早起床，到圣荷西机场赶搭早上七时的飞机飞奥斯汀，当天下午去达拉斯，次日又飞波士顿，第三天飞回圣荷西，晚上与客户晚餐。三天内飞了近万里，访问了五个大客户。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">On the first day after our wedding, I rose early, rushed to San Jose Airport, and caught a 7 a.m. flight to Austin. That afternoon I traveled to Dallas, the next day to Boston, and on the third day flew back to San Jose for dinner with a client. In three days, I had flown nearly ten thousand miles and visited five key customers.</p>
</div>

一月二十七日，星期六，婚后第四天，再飞万里回台北。星期日清晨到达，星期一早上去新竹上班。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">On January 27, Saturday—the fourth day after our wedding—I flew another ten thousand miles back to Taipei. I arrived Sunday morning and went to work in Hsinchu on Monday.</p>
</div>

Sophie谅解，但也惊异了——原来这是她要分享的我的生活。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Sophie understood, but was also astonished—at this was the life she had chosen to share.</p>
</div>
