## 谈判重点七——营业计划

我与胡定华、史钦泰等工研院同仁，花了不少时间准备台積電十年详细营业计划。克列斯曼仔细地看了，认为我们的售价太乐观。飞利浦很可能是我们未来客户，他做此言，我认为大有自私意味在，但我无意与他做冗长辩论。我们稍事调整计划内的售价，但仍呈示不错的获利及投资回收。详细的营业计划附录在"协议纲要"里。

在这个计划里，我们前五年的营收年复合成长率为六四%，营业利益率除了在第一年以外，可以由单位数成长到一九九二零年的二五%左右。而实际上，台積電前五年的营收年复合成长率为一三零%，营业利益率除了第一年以及产业不景气的一九九零年以外，都是双位数。

"营业计划"的假设是我们只有一厂及二厂，所以从九二年起，二厂成熟后，营收就不再成长。"真实"的数字代表我们后来继续建三厂、四厂。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Along with ITRI colleagues Hu Ding-hua and Shi Qin-tai, I spent considerable time preparing TSMC's detailed ten-year business plan. Krigsman reviewed it carefully and considered our pricing too optimistic. Philips was likely to be our future customer; in making this comment, I believed there was considerable selfish motivation, but I had no intention of engaging in a lengthy debate with him. We made slight adjustments to the pricing in the plan, but it still showed solid profitability and investment recovery. The detailed business plan was appended to the "Heads of Agreement."</p>
<p class="en-para">In this plan, our projected compound annual revenue growth rate for the first five years was 64%, and operating margin—except for the first year—could grow from single digits to about 25% by 1992. In reality, TSMC's compound annual revenue growth rate for the first five years was 130%, and operating margins—except for the first year and the industry downturn year of 1990—were all double digits.</p>
<p class="en-para">The "business plan" assumed we would only have Fab 1 and Fab 2; thus from 1992 onward, once Fab 2 matured, revenue would stop growing. The "actual" figures represent our later decision to continue building Fab 3 and Fab 4.</p>

## "投资协议纲要"与"技术合作协议"

七个月的谈判，有时顺利，有时痛苦，但我一直抱着"非谈成不可"的态度。如果谈不成，飞利浦不投资，我们就办不成台積電，工研院的IC计划也会走到山穷水尽地步。抱着这样"可忍则忍"的心怀，终于谈成了与飞利浦的"投资协议纲要"。"五人小组"同意后，我与飞利浦总裁在一九八六年六月签署。

有了飞利浦的投资承诺，我们满足了俞院长的台湾募资条件，政府也就展开了台湾募资。几个月后，股款凑足（一亿四千五百万美元，相当于新台币五十五亿元），全体投资人在十月签署"股东协议书"。这"股东协议书"完全保存了"投资协议纲要"的要点。

也就是说，七个月的谈判造就了台積電，也决定了台積電头六年的经营模式。一直到一九九二年十月，台積電决定申请上市，原始股东在"股东协议书"的私自约定不可能在上市时成立。而且原始股东（包括行政院开发基金及飞利浦）嗅到了上市后股票增值的诱惑，才决定解除原来的"股东协议"。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">The seven-month negotiation was sometimes smooth, sometimes painful, but I always maintained an attitude of "this must succeed." If negotiations failed and Philips didn't invest, we couldn't establish TSMC, and ITRI's IC program would reach the end of its road. With this "endure what can be endured" mindset, we finally concluded the "Investment Agreement Heads" with Philips. After approval by the "Five-Person Committee," I signed it with the Philips president in June 1986.</p>
<p class="en-para">With Philips' investment commitment in hand, we satisfied Premier Yu's condition for Taiwan fundraising, and the government launched its Taiwan fundraising campaign. Months later, the full capital was raised (US$145 million, equivalent to NT$5.5 billion), and all investors signed the "Shareholders Agreement" in October. This "Shareholders Agreement" fully preserved the key points of the "Investment Agreement Heads."</p>
<p class="en-para">In other words, seven months of negotiation created TSMC and determined TSMC's business model for its first six years. It wasn't until October 1992, when TSMC decided to apply for listing, that the private arrangements in the "Shareholders Agreement" could no longer be sustained upon public listing. Moreover, the original shareholders (including the Executive Yuan Development Fund and Philips) caught the scent of stock appreciation after listing and agreed to terminate the original "Shareholders Agreement."</p>
</div>
