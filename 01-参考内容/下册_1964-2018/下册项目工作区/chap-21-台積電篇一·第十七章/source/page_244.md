## 谈判重点五——人事权

人事权在没有异议下达成协议：

董事会十人：政府指派四人，飞利浦指派三人，其余股东指派两人，总经理兼董事一人。

如政府持股在二五%以上，政府指定董事长。最大股东提名总经理，由董事会决定。

总经理为公司行政首长（CEO）。

第二大股东提名财务副总。

监察人三名。政府及飞利浦各指定一人，其他股东指定一人。

总经理并列，而且总经理是行政首长，这些规定都属于标准欧美公司体制——也是飞利浦及我习惯的体制。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Personnel authority was agreed upon without dissent:</p>
<p class="en-para">The board would consist of ten members: four appointed by the government, three appointed by Philips, two appointed by other shareholders, and one serving as both general manager and director.</p>
<p class="en-para">If the government held more than 25% of shares, the government would appoint the chairman. The largest shareholder would nominate the general manager, subject to board approval.</p>
<p class="en-para">The general manager would be the company's chief executive (CEO).</p>
<p class="en-para">The second largest shareholder would nominate the financial vice president.</p>
<p class="en-para">Three supervisors: one each appointed by the government and Philips, and one by other shareholders.</p>
<p class="en-para">That the general manager would also be a board member, and that the general manager would be the chief executive—these provisions were all standard European and American corporate governance—the system both Philips and I were accustomed to.</p>
</div>

## 谈判重点六——飞利浦享受股票承购权

开启飞利浦谈判不久，赵耀东主委就对我说："他们（飞利浦）一定会要求股票承购权。"赵主委富有与国外公司合资经验，他的话也让我有心理准备。果然，在一切都已大致谈定，双方准备签署"协议纲要"时，飞利浦突然提出股票承购权，建议如下：

（一）最早一九八九年九月三十日，飞利浦可提出承购要求，增加飞利浦持股至五一%，"政府股东"及"其他股东"必须转让股票给飞利浦；如"其他股东"不愿，则"政府股东"必须转让给飞利浦。

（二）转让价格依下列两条件，孰者为高而定：（A）转让时之公司资产净值。（B）原始投资价，加银行利率。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Early in the Philips negotiations, Committee Chairman Zhao Yaodong said to me: "They (Philips) will definitely demand a stock option." Chairman Zhao had extensive experience in joint ventures with foreign companies, and his words gave me mental preparation. Sure enough, when everything had been largely negotiated and both parties were preparing to sign the "Heads of Agreement," Philips suddenly raised the stock option issue with the following proposal:</p>
<p class="en-para">(1) Beginning September 30, 1989 at the earliest, Philips could exercise an option to increase its stake to 51%, and "government shareholders" and "other shareholders" would be obligated to transfer shares to Philips; if "other shareholders" refused, then "government shareholders" must transfer to Philips.</p>
<p class="en-para">(2) The transfer price would be determined by whichever of the following two conditions was higher: (A) the company's net asset value at the time of transfer, or (B) the original investment price plus bank interest.</p>
</div>

## 反建议与张忠谋的坚持

我接受了（一），但不接受（二）。我对飞利浦的反建议是：

（二）转让价格三条件，等量考虑：（A）转让时之公司资产净值。（B）转让时之公司获利能力及获利预测。（C）在美国证券市场上市之美国半导体公司之本益比。

我的反建议使飞利浦惊愕了。时间已是一九八六年五月二十七日。与飞利浦总裁已订六月九日来台湾签署"协议纲要"。几乎一切别的都已谈定，飞利浦谈判团也不想为了这件可能不会发生的事（如台積電不成功，飞利浦不会要增加持股），阻碍他们以台湾作为主要生产基地的第一步。

匆匆折冲后，最后的转让价格条件是：（A）转让时之公司资产净值。（B）转让时之公司获利能力及获利预测。（C）在美国证券市场上市之美国半导体公司之市价／净值比。

最后的协议与我的反建议相差不远。当时（C）市价／净值比大约二．五倍到十倍，所以最后协议比飞利浦的原建议对开发基金有利得多。飞利浦一向以谈判能力著称，但在这件事上，我们赢了。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">I accepted (1) but rejected (2). My counter-proposal to Philips was:</p>
<p class="en-para">(2) Transfer price determined by three factors given equal consideration: (A) the company's net asset value at transfer. (B) The company's earning power and profit forecast at transfer. (C) The price-to-earnings ratio of American semiconductor companies listed on U.S. stock exchanges.</p>
<p class="en-para">My counter-proposal stunned Philips. It was May 27, 1986. The Philips president had already scheduled a visit to Taiwan on June 9 to sign the "Heads of Agreement." Almost everything else had been settled, and the Philips negotiation team did not want to let this potentially moot point (if TSMC failed, Philips wouldn't want to increase its stake) block their first step in making Taiwan a primary production base.</p>
<p class="en-para">After hasty negotiations, the final transfer price conditions were: (A) the company's net asset value at transfer. (B) The company's earning power and profit forecast at transfer. (C) The price-to-book ratio of American semiconductor companies listed on U.S. stock exchanges.</p>
<p class="en-para">The final agreement was not far from my counter-proposal. At that time, (C) the price-to-book ratio was approximately 2.5x to 10x, so the final agreement was far more favorable to the Development Fund than Philips' original proposal. Philips had always been renowned for its negotiation skills, but on this matter, we won.</p>
</div>
