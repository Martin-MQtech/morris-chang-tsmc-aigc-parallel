## 二访王永庆

这次访王仍有饭吃，但气氛与上次完全不同。我单独赴宴，王神态肃杀，席中没有"三娘"，只有两位台塑人员。当我问他投资意愿，他只说："仍有兴趣"，但令我难忘的却是他的另一句话："你为什么找政府办这件事？你早来找我，也许我们已办成了。这种代工……"

令我更难忘的是他的补充："你早来找我，我们甚至可以出你月薪一万美元。"

月薪一万美元，这就是王董事长对我的慷慨评价！王董事长不知道，我也没有理由告诉他——因为告诉他会使他更生气——几个月后我就会开出对台積電第一个美国人总经理的聘书："年薪三十万美元。第一年如达到营运计划，加花红十五万美元。"

后来，王董事长承诺投资五%。当然，关键是俞院长，不是我的两次访问。

八月，李政委对我说：还缺几亿新台币，但决定不再恳求民股，缺额由中央投资（国民党投资机构）补足。

同月，民间股东签"认购书"，纲要如下：荷兰飞利浦出资二七．六%，行政院开发基金出资四八%，其他民间投资人出资二四．一%。

募资大功告成。十二月召开发起人会议，选举第一任董监，选举我为董事长。发起人会议后登记公司。

<p class="en-para">This second visit still included dinner, but the atmosphere was completely different from the last time. I attended alone. Wang's demeanor was stern. "Third Madame" was not present at the table; only two Formosa Plastics personnel accompanied him. When I asked about his investment intent, he merely said: "Still interested." But what I found even more unforgettable was another thing he said: "Why did you go to the government for this? If you had come to me earlier, perhaps we would have already done it. This foundry model..."</p>
<p class="en-para">Even more unforgettable was his addendum: "If you had come to me earlier, we might even have offered you a monthly salary of $10,000."</p>
<p class="en-para">A monthly salary of $10,000—this was Chairman Wang's generous appraisal of me! Chairman Wang didn't know, and I had no reason to tell him—because telling him would make him even angrier—that a few months later I would extend an offer to TSMC's first American general manager: "Annual salary $300,000. If the business plan is met in the first year, add a $150,000 bonus."</p>
<p class="en-para">Later, Chairman Wang committed to investing 5%. Of course, the decisive factor was Premier Yu, not my two visits.</p>
<p class="en-para">In August, Commissioner Li told me: we still lacked several hundred million NT dollars, but had decided to stop pleading with private investors—the shortfall would be made up by Central Investment (the KMT's investment arm).</p>
<p class="en-para">In the same month, private shareholders signed "subscription documents," with the following outline: Philips Netherlands contributed 27.6%, the Executive Yuan Development Fund contributed 48%, and other private investors contributed 24.1%.</p>
<p class="en-para">Fundraising was successfully completed. In December, the founders' meeting was convened; the first board of directors and supervisors were elected. I was elected chairman. After the founders' meeting, the company was registered.</p>
