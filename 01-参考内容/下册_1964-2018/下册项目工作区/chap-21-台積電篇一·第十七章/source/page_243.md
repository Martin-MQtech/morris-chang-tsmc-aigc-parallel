## 谈判重点三——技术

首晤克列斯曼（飞利浦首席谈判员）时，他就要求台積電"完全以飞利浦技术为基础"，我立刻拒绝。我从德仪时期就知道，技术是一个半导体公司的灵魂。怎么能在公司还未组成时，就先出卖他的灵魂？接着，克列斯曼要求飞利浦以技术做股，我也拒绝了。我们要的全额现金投资（李政委还认为我要得太少，因为他预计在台湾民间募资会有困难），任何折扣都会使台湾民间募资更困难。

但是我们的确也要他的技术协助。在一九八六年一月初，工研院的技术团队（副院长胡定华、电子所所长史钦泰、副所长章青驹、组长曾繁城）去恩荷芬领略飞利浦的技术。他们回来的报告是："可以帮助我们自己的开发。"

更重要的是：飞利浦与十几家大型半导体公司交换授权；假使飞利浦拥有二五%以上台積電股权，台積電也在飞利浦的保护伞下。也就是说：台積電不会受到这十几家大公司专利权的干扰。

技术开发的帮助，以及专利权的"保护伞"，都要钱买。飞利浦也毫不客气，要求高价。经过不少折冲，我们（"五人小组"）同意在先进技术（一微米及更先进）营收内，台積電付予飞利浦三分之一权利金。工研院已开发的技术（二微米及以上），台積電不必付权利金。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">At my first meeting with Krigsman (Philips' chief negotiator), he demanded that TSMC be "entirely based on Philips technology." I immediately refused. Since my TI days, I had known that technology is the soul of a semiconductor company. How could we sell the soul of a company before it was even formed? Next, Krigsman demanded that Philips contribute technology as equity. I also refused. We wanted full cash investment (Commissioner Li even thought I was asking for too little, because he anticipated difficulties raising funds from Taiwanese private investors)—any discount would make Taiwanese private fundraising even more difficult.</p>
<p class="en-para">But we did need their technical assistance. In early January 1986, an ITRI technology team (Vice President Hu Ding-hua, Electronics Research Institute Director Shi Qin-tai, Deputy Director Zhang Qingju, and Section Chief Tseng Fan-cheng) went to Eindhoven to survey Philips' technology. Their report upon return: "Can assist our own development."</p>
<p class="en-para">More importantly: Philips had cross-licensing agreements with over a dozen major semiconductor companies. If Philips owned more than 25% of TSMC's equity, TSMC would also be under Philips' protective umbrella. In other words: TSMC would not be harassed by the patent rights of these dozen-plus major companies.</p>
<p class="en-para">Help in technology development, and the patent "umbrella," both cost money. Philips, without courtesy, demanded a high price. After considerable negotiations, we (the "Five-Person Committee") agreed that TSMC would pay Philips a one-third royalty on revenue from advanced technology (one micron and below). For technology already developed by ITRI (two microns and above), TSMC would pay no royalty.</p>
</div>

## 谈判重点四——独立性

一旦避免了"以飞利浦技术"做基础，我就要强调台積電的独立性。我不要台積電成为飞利浦的子公司，政府也不要台積電成为"国营"或"国有"公司。所以在"协议纲要"上我们明白说明：

台積電是一个独立事业，她的成长和获利的优先考量，与独立的美国IT C公司一样。

与独立美国ITC公司相比拟，是刚自美国来台湾的我决定的。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Having avoided being "based on Philips technology," I then emphasized TSMC's independence. I did not want TSMC to become a Philips subsidiary, and the government did not want TSMC to become a "state-run" or "state-owned" enterprise. So in the "Heads of Agreement" we clearly stated:</p>
<p class="en-para">TSMC is an independent enterprise. Its priority considerations for growth and profitability are the same as those of an independent American ITC company.</p>
<p class="en-para">The comparison to an independent American ITC company was my own decision, as someone who had just come to Taiwan from the United States.</p>
