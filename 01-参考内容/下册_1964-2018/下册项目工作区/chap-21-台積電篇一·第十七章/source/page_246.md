## 技术合作合约几乎翻脸

除了"投资协议纲要"，飞利浦要求根据"纲要"内关于飞利浦技术的约定，双方订立"技术合作合约"。为了这个合约，我几乎与飞利浦闹翻。

原因是这样的：飞利浦建议，因为他们富有建IC厂经验，所以台積電的IC厂也由他们设计。看到这个建议，我觉得这已违反台積電的独立原则，我从"可忍则忍"转为"是可忍，孰不可忍"。与飞利浦初步争论无效后，我对"五人小组"报告：也许决裂点已到。赵耀东（素有"铁头"之称）说："决裂就决裂，自己办亦可。"但李达海主张还是请李国鼎周旋一下，也许可以解套。会后我就与李政委商量，他决定写信给飞利浦总裁，要求飞利浦撤回替我们设计IC厂要求。这封信果然有效。几天后飞利浦不再提此要求。我们也就在一九八六年的十二月与飞利浦签署"技术合作合约"。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">In addition to the "Investment Agreement Heads," Philips requested that both parties enter into a "Technology Cooperation Agreement" based on the technology-related provisions in the "Heads." Over this agreement, I nearly had a falling out with Philips.</p>
<p class="en-para">It happened like this: Philips proposed that because they had extensive experience building IC fabs, TSMC's IC fab should also be designed by them. Upon seeing this proposal, I felt it violated TSMC's principle of independence. I shifted from "endure what can be endured" to "this cannot be endured." After initial arguments with Philips proved futile, I reported to the "Five-Person Committee": perhaps the breaking point had arrived. Zhao Yaodong (known as the "Iron Head") said: "Break then break—we can do it ourselves." But Li Dahai advocated having Li Guomiao mediate, perhaps finding a way out. After the meeting I discussed it with Commissioner Li, who decided to write to the Philips president requesting that Philips withdraw its demand to design our IC factory. The letter proved effective. A few days later, Philips no longer raised this demand. We then signed the "Technology Cooperation Agreement" with Philips in December 1986.</p>
</div>

## 台湾募资

一九八六年五月，我对"五人小组"报告：与飞利浦的谈判已大致底定。"五人小组"随即致函台湾区电工器材工业同业公会理事长龚孝勇以及九家公司（台塑、华夏、中美和、声宝、宏碁电脑、神通电脑、大同、新光纺织、台湾聚合）的董事长，告诉他们台積電总股本金额，以及政府及飞利浦的参与金额。主要目的是邀请他们参与投资。

没有一个人打电话给我。"五人小组"也只要求我访问台塑王永庆。至于联成石化，访问相当顺利。苗君曾在英特尔公司担任微处理器设计工程师多年，对IC非常内行。他的投资兴趣是真实的时，苗的父亲苗育秀先生亦在座，而且很明显地是最后决策者。他也相当赞成投资。后来联成石化（亦为苗育秀先生主持）成为台積電原始股东之一。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">In May 1986, I reported to the "Five-Person Committee": negotiations with Philips were substantially finalized. The "Five-Person Committee" immediately wrote to Gong Xiaoyong, chairman of the Taiwan Electrical and Electronic Manufacturers' Association, and the chairs of nine major companies (Formosa Plastics, Huaxia, Union Chemical, Tatung, Acer, Mitac, Tatung, Shin Kong Textile, Taiwan Polymer), informing them of TSMC's total capital amount and the participation amounts from the government and Philips. The primary purpose was to invite them to participate in the investment.</p>
<p class="en-para">Not a single person called me. The "Five-Person Committee" only asked me to visit Formosa Plastics' Wang Yung-ching. As for United Petrochemical (Lien Cheng), the visit went quite smoothly. Mr. Miao (Feng-chiang) had worked at Intel for many years as a microprocessor design engineer and was very knowledgeable about ICs. His investment interest was genuine. When I visited, his father Miao Yuxiu was also present and clearly the final decision-maker. He too was quite supportive. Later, United Petrochemical (also chaired by Miao Yuxiu) became one of TSMC's original shareholders.</p>
</div>

## 台塑王永庆

台塑王永庆董事长却是另一个故事。胡定华曾与王董事长合作过，所以我请他去约见。王董事长表示要请我们吃饭，并邀我们携眷。当时我无眷，但胡、史带着他们的夫人一起去了。

晚宴地点在台塑顶楼，也是王董事长私寓。宴席上王坐主位，夫人李宝珠在旁相陪，王也邀了五、六位台塑人员。胡、史及我有备而来，我们准备答复王董事长任何有关台積電的问题。

席中觥筹交错，宾主尽欢。主菜是"台塑牛排"，吃后王董事长问我喜不喜欢，我当然说喜欢，王董事长就即刻命他家人次日送几块到我家来。

但是，关于台積電的讨论却出奇地少。胡、史和我都已半醉，我们还以为王已决定投资，所以也不多问关于台積電的事。

实情恰恰相反，王董事长已决定不投资。这顿饭只是对我们的努力的回馈而已。几星期后，李达海部长电话王，问他投资意愿，王说他不投资。李部长急了，请俞院长帮忙。俞院长自己打电话给王，王才勉强同意。嘱咐我再访王（"上次你没做好，这次做得好一点"）。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Formosa Plastics chairman Wang Yung-ching was another story. Hu Ding-hua had worked with Chairman Wang before, so I asked him to arrange a meeting. Chairman Wang expressed that he would treat us to dinner and invited us to bring our spouses. I had no spouse at the time, but Hu and Shi brought their wives.</p>
<p class="en-para">The dinner was held at the top floor of Formosa Plastics—also Chairman Wang's private residence. At the dinner, Wang sat in the host's seat, with his wife Li Baozhu accompanying him, and Wang had also invited five or six Formosa Plastics personnel. Hu, Shi, and I came prepared to answer any questions Chairman Wang might have about TSMC.</p>
<p class="en-para">Amidst the toasts and cheerful conversation, the main course was "Formosa Plastics steak." After eating it, Chairman Wang asked me whether I liked it. Of course I said I liked it, and Chairman Wang immediately ordered his family to deliver several pieces to my home the next day.</p>
<p class="en-para">Yet discussion about TSMC was remarkably scarce. Hu, Shi, and I were all half-drunk, and we assumed Wang had already decided to invest, so we didn't press the matter of TSMC further.</p>
<p class="en-para">The reality was precisely the opposite: Chairman Wang had already decided not to invest. This dinner was merely a gesture reciprocating our efforts. Weeks later, Minister Li Dahai called Wang to ask about his investment intent; Wang said he would not invest. Minister Li panicked and asked Premier Yu for help. Premier Yu personally called Wang, and Wang reluctantly agreed. He instructed me to visit Wang again ("You didn't do well last time—do better this time").</p>
</div>
