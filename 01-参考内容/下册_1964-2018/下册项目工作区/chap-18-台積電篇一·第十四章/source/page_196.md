## 徐贤修「三顾茅庐」（一九八五年，五十四岁）

徐贤修先生曾任美国普渡大学（Purdue University）数学教授，在美国退休后，历任台湾清华大学校长、国科会主委等职；在国科会任内，他建议设立新竹科学工业园区，为当时行政院院长蒋经国采纳。

我在一九八一及八二年来访台湾时，曾拜访国科会主委徐贤修。一九八五年，徐已卸任国科会，接任工研院董事长。工研院院长方贤齐因年龄及健康关系有退休意，徐正在找一个新的院长。

根据徐贤修后来告诉我，他的朋友萨支唐教授在报纸上看到我辞职通用器材公司的消息，就打电话给他推荐我（后来李政委告诉我科技顾问赛驰 Frederick Seitz、RAT Bob Evans 亦在报纸上看到我辞职消息后对李推荐我，但李因为工研院不属他管，所以没有主动找我）。

四月八日，我从通用器材辞职后不到十天，徐贤修就打电话到我的通用器材办公室（我与通用约定辞职后仍可继续用办公室数月），说要和我谈工研院院长事，并约定四月二十八日见面。徐先生很喜欢用中文典故，后来在我工研院院长就职典礼上，他曾说为请我曾「三顾茅庐」；其实他只来过两次，而且我住在川普大厦楼上，与当时地产巨富、后来美国总统的川普上下为邻，我的住所绝对不能说是「茅庐」。

四月二十八日，我与徐贤修谈了两、三小时，我答应五月先到台湾工研院「看看」。五月十四日行政院科技顾问艾凡斯也来纽约看我。艾凡斯是旧识，他任IBM副总裁时，我在德仪，他是我的客户。后来他离开IBM，做创业投资，现来纽约劝我接受工研院职。他说我若做工研院院长，实质上会是「台湾科技长」。当时我不知道他说得对不对，后来做了工研院院长后，才知道艾凡斯此话荒谬之极。台湾没有「科技长」，即使有，也不会是工研院院长。

<p class="en-para"><b>Hsu Hsiu-hsien's "Three Visits to the Thatched Cottage" (1985, age 54)</b></p>
<p class="en-para">Mr. Hsu Hsiu-hsien had served as a mathematics professor at Purdue University in the United States. After retiring from the U.S., he held successive positions as President of Tsing Hua University in Taiwan and Minister of the National Science Council. During his NSC tenure, he proposed the establishment of the Hsinchu Science Park, which was adopted by then-Premier Chiang Ching-kuo.</p>
<p class="en-para">During my 1981 and 1982 visits to Taiwan, I had called on Minister Hsu. By 1985, Hsu had left the NSC and become Chairman of ITRI. ITRI President Fang Xianqi wished to retire due to age and health, and Hsu was searching for a new president.</p>
<p class="en-para">According to what Hsu later told me, his professor friend Sah Chi-tang had seen news of my resignation from General Instruments in the newspaper and called to recommend me. (Minister Li later told me that technology advisor Frederick Seitz and RAT Bob Evans had also seen the news and recommended me to Li, but since ITRI was not under Li's purview, Li did not take the initiative to contact me.)</p>
<p class="en-para">On April 8 — less than ten days after my GI resignation — Hsu called my GI office (GI had agreed I could keep using the office for several months after resigning) to discuss the ITRI presidency, scheduling a meeting for April 28. Mr. Hsu was fond of Chinese literary allusions. At my ITRI inauguration, he would later say he had paid "three visits to the thatched cottage" to recruit me. In truth, he only came twice, and I lived in the Trump Tower, a high-rise luxury building — my residence could hardly be called a "thatched cottage."</p>
<p class="en-para">On April 28, Hsu and I talked for two to three hours. I agreed to visit ITRI in May first to "take a look." On May 14, Executive Yuan technology advisor Bob Evans also came to New York to see me. Evans was an old acquaintance — when he was IBM Vice President and I was at TI, he had been my client. After leaving IBM for venture capital, he now came to New York to persuade me to accept the ITRI post. He said that if I became ITRI President, I would effectively be Taiwan's "Technology Czar." I didn't know then whether he was right. Only after becoming ITRI President did I realize how absurd Evans' statement was. Taiwan had no "Technology Czar," and even if it did, it wouldn't be the ITRI President.</p>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **"Three visits to the thatched cottage" (三顾茅庐)**
**中文解释**：三国典故，刘备三次亲往草庐请诸葛亮出山；徐贤修以此自比，但张忠谋幽默指出——他只来了两次，而且张的住所是川普大厦，绝非「茅庐」。
**商业造句**：*Hsu's self-deprecating "three visits" allusion was a charming touch in the recruitment story — though Chang's Trump Tower apartment made the "thatched cottage" comparison delightfully incongruous.*

2. **Hsinchu Science Park (新竹科学工业园区)**
**中文解释**：台湾第一个科学工业园区，成立于一九八〇年；徐贤修在国科会任内推动设立，后来成为全球半导体产业的重要聚落。
**商业造句**：*The Hsinchu Science Park became Taiwan's answer to Silicon Valley — a concentrated ecosystem where semiconductor companies could share talent, infrastructure, and knowledge spillovers.*

3. **Technology Czar (科技长)**
**中文解释**：对一国科技政策最高主管的俗称；艾凡斯称张忠谋做工研院院长会成为「台湾科技长」，但张认为此言荒谬——工研院院长并无政策制定权。
**商业造句**：*Evans' "Technology Czar" label was a well-meaning exaggeration — in reality, the ITRI President's influence came not from formal authority but from convening power and technical credibility.*
