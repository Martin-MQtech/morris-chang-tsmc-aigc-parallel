## 德仪新加坡与台湾设厂

一九六八年德仪终于决定去东南亚设厂，德仪总裁夏伯特先选了香港，但我推荐台湾，总裁也接受了。他与我一起到台北见经济部长李国鼎，想不到遇到困难，只好暂时搁置在台湾建厂计划。

一年后（一九六九年），台湾设厂的困难解决，德仪就来台湾建厂，但那时德仪的海外厂已不归我管；直到一九七二年，我当上了半导体集团总经理，全球所有德仪半导体单位，包括海外厂，都在我属下。此后直到一九七七年，我大约每年都来台湾视察一次，总是与东京、吉隆坡、新加坡同一旅程，每次在台湾逗留一两天，匆匆来、匆匆走，停留的地方通常只有机场、旅馆，位在中和的德仪台湾厂。

夏伯特与我转程去新加坡，几乎立刻与新加坡政府谈妥，就把原本要到台湾设厂的计划，包括在达拉斯待命出发的厂长及干部一并移到新加坡。

<p class="en-para"><b>TI's Singapore and Taiwan Factory Decisions</b></p>
<p class="en-para">In 1968, TI finally decided to build a factory in Southeast Asia. TI President Shepherd initially chose Hong Kong, but I recommended Taiwan, and the President accepted. He and I traveled together to Taipei to meet Minister of Economic Affairs Li Guoding. Unexpectedly, we encountered difficulties and had to shelve the Taiwan factory plan temporarily.</p>
<p class="en-para">A year later (1969), the difficulties for building in Taiwan were resolved, and TI came to build a factory in Taiwan. However, by then TI's overseas factories were no longer under my management. It was not until 1972, when I became General Manager of the Semiconductor Group, that all TI semiconductor units worldwide — including overseas factories — came under my authority. From then until 1977, I visited Taiwan about once a year, always on the same trip as Tokyo, Kuala Lumpur, and Singapore. Each time I stayed only a day or two, coming and going in a hurry, typically visiting only the airport, the hotel, and the TI Taiwan factory in Zhonghe.</p>
<p class="en-para">Shepherd and I diverted to Singapore and almost immediately reached an agreement with the Singapore government. We moved the entire plan originally intended for Taiwan — including the factory manager and staff already on standby in Dallas — to Singapore.</p>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Semiconductor Group General Manager (半导体集团总经理)**
**中文解释**：德州仪器公司半导体事业群的最高负责人，管辖全球所有半导体设计、制造、销售单位；这是张忠谋在德仪职业生涯的最高职位。
**商业造句**：*As Semiconductor Group General Manager, Chang had global P&L responsibility for TI's entire semiconductor operations, from R&D to fabs to sales.*

2. **P&L responsibility (损益责任)**
**中文解释**：Profit and Loss responsibility，指管理者对业务单位的收入、成本及利润负责；拥有完整P&L责任的总经理是真正意义上的业务一把手。
**商业造句**：*True General Manager authority comes only with full P&L responsibility — the power to make trade-offs between revenue growth and cost control.*
