## 未成的赴台与一九八五年徐贤修的「三顾茅庐」

一九八二年，我在德仪已到「最后的呐喊」阶段（本书第十一章）。假使我的整个德仪生涯也如我在哈佛的一年，是一个「可带走的盛宴」，那么到了一九八二年，这「盛宴」也已到了杯盘狼藉，主人连连打呵欠，只有我身为客人却还在高谈阔论的阶段。盛宴将终，我的离开德仪，仅是迟早问题；但是迟或早却需要拿捏，因为我还有未到期的「股票承购权」；我还要倚靠这些「股票承购权」赚来的钱，过我未来也许几十年的低薪，或无薪的生活。

当然孙院长答应我在待遇上「不会让我吃亏」，但这话能维持多久？我在德仪的薪水比台湾工研院院长高得太多，虽然孙院长有诚意，但他不会永远当行政院院长，若换了一个新的行政院院长，还会不会延续这个承诺？

有一个办法，假如德仪答应，我可在德仪「留职停薪」；那么，我可在台湾工作，在德仪虽不领薪，但未到期的「股票承购权」可保留；等到「承购权」到期，我把它实现了，才正式自德仪辞职。但这办法需要德仪同意。

回到达拉斯后，我对彪希请示在德仪「留职停薪」。我很坦白地告诉他我想去台湾工作，但希望保留股票承购权。彪希说去问夏伯特，一星期后彪希回话：「不准！我们要留你。」

我在五月四日致函孙院长，并将副本寄给李政委及方院长，坦白告诉我的处境：我想来台湾工作，但放弃股票承购权的损失实在太大（我甚至告诉他可能损失的金额），而「留职停薪」的请求又被拒绝。

孙院长及李政委在一个多月后各自回信，两人都表示他们的失望。李政委另提了一个办法：我每年台湾几次，每次一、两星期。

鉴于上次李政委要求「借用」我时夏伯特的不高兴，以及我对夏伯特和彪希性格的了解，我不认为他们会答应李政委的折衷办法，所以也没对他们提。

一九八三年十月三十一日，股票承购权也等到了，我辞职德仪，首先想到的就是台湾。辞职次日就电话李政委，告诉他我已是「自由之身」；意外地，电话后却没有下文。几天内，已有好几个猎头者与我接触，所以我也不再向台湾追踪。为什么这次李政委冷淡，对我至今是一个谜。但一九八五年，徐贤修找我后，李政委又热烈劝我做工研院院长，我接任工研院院长后，他又热烈要我成立台积电。

只是，一九八五年主动到纽约找我当工研院院长的不是我熟识的李国鼎，而是我比较不熟识的徐贤修。

<p class="en-para"><b>The Failed Taiwan Move and Hsu Hsiu-hsien's "Three Visits" in 1985</b></p>
<p class="en-para">In 1982, I was already at the "last hurrah" phase of my TI career (Chapter 11). If my whole TI career had been, like my year at Harvard, a "portable feast," then by 1982 that feast had reached the stage of dirty dishes and scattered crumbs, with the host repeatedly yawning — only I, the guest, was still holding forth. The feast was ending; my departure from TI was only a matter of time. But timing mattered: I still had unvested stock options, and I would need the money from those options to sustain what might be decades of low-income or no-income life ahead.</p>
<p class="en-para">Of course Premier Sun had promised I "wouldn't suffer a loss" on compensation — but how long would that promise hold? My TI salary was vastly higher than an ITRI President's could ever be. Though Premier Sun was sincere, he wouldn't be Premier forever. Would a new Premier honor the commitment?</p>
<p class="en-para">There was one solution — if TI agreed: a leave of absence. I could work in Taiwan while technically remaining at TI, drawing no salary but retaining my unvested stock options until they vested, at which point I would exercise them and formally resign. But this required TI's consent.</p>
<p class="en-para">After returning to Dallas, I asked Barr about a TI leave of absence. I told him frankly that I wanted to work in Taiwan but hoped to retain my stock options. Barr said he would ask Shepherd. A week later, Barr replied: "No! We want to keep you."</p>
<p class="en-para">On May 4, I wrote to Premier Sun, copying Minister Li and President Fang, laying out my predicament honestly: I wanted to come to Taiwan, but forfeiting my stock options would mean an enormous financial loss (I even told him the likely amount), and my leave-of-absence request had been denied.</p>
<p class="en-para">Premier Sun and Minister Li each replied over a month later, both expressing disappointment. Minister Li proposed an alternative: I could visit Taiwan a few times a year, one to two weeks each time.</p>
<p class="en-para">Given how unhappy Shepherd had been last time Minister Li requested "borrowing" me, and knowing Shepherd and Barr's temperaments well, I did not believe they would agree to Minister Li's compromise, so I didn't raise it with them.</p>
<p class="en-para">On October 31, 1983, my stock options having vested, I resigned from TI. The first thing that came to mind was Taiwan. The day after resigning, I called Minister Li to tell him I was a "free man." Surprisingly, nothing followed the call. Within days, several headhunters had already approached me, so I stopped pursuing Taiwan. Why Minister Li was cold on this occasion remains a mystery to me to this day. But in 1985, after Hsu Hsiu-hsien approached me, Minister Li once again warmly urged me to become ITRI President. And after I took that post, he passionately urged me to found TSMC.</p>
<p class="en-para">It's just that the one who came to New York in 1985 to recruit me as ITRI President was not the familiar K.T. Li, but the less familiar Hsu Hsiu-hsien.</p>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Portable feast (可带走的盛宴)**
**出自**：张忠谋将自己在哈佛的一年比作海明威的「可带走的盛宴」；此处反用——德仪的盛宴将终，已不可带走。
**商业造句**：*Chang's literary reference to a "portable feast" captured the bittersweet realization that the best years of his TI career could not be carried forward — they were ending in a dusty dining room.*

2. **Stock options / 股票承购权**
**中文解释**：允许持有人在特定期间内以预定价格购买公司股票的权利；这是美国科技公司高管薪酬的关键组成部分，也是张忠谋离开德仪时必须精算的财务因素。
**商业造句**：*Chang's meticulous calculation of unvested stock options versus the Taiwan opportunity was a classic executive career decision — weighing known wealth against unknown impact.*

3. **Leave of absence (留职停薪)**
**中文解释**：员工暂时离开职位但保留雇佣关系的安排；德仪拒绝张忠谋的留职停薪请求，等于切断了他在台美之间的退路。
**商业造句**：*TI's refusal of Chang's leave-of-absence request was the final signal that the company would not accommodate his Taiwan ambitions — forcing an irreversible choice.*
