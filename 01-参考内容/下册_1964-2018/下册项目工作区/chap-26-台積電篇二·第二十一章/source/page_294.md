# 台积电篇二·第二十一章｜台积电的经营理念及九零年代策略

## 经营理念

大约在一九九四、九五年之际，台积电营收已以年复合成长率60-70%气势成长了几年，员工及重要主管人数也急剧地增加，我已不能常常与所有重要主管做理念性讨论。我想：应该把我们基本的价值观，也就是台积电的经营理念，写出来。虽然写理念的过程只有几天，但理念的形成却是几十年家教及学校教育加上职业及社会经验的结果。一共写了十条如下：

**台湾积体电路制造股份有限公司企业经营理念**

**（一）坚持高度职业道德**

这个理念代表公司的品格，是我们最基本也是最重要的理念，也是执行业务时必须遵守的法则。所谓高度职业道德是：第一、我们说真话。第二、我们不夸张、不作秀。第三、对客户我们不轻易承诺，一旦做出承诺，必定不计代价，全力以赴。第四、对同业我们在合法范围内全力竞争时，也尊重同业的智财权。不恶意中伤。第五、对供应商我们以客观、清廉、公正的态度进行挑选及合作。

在公司内部，我们绝不容许贪污；不容许在公司内有派系或小圈圈产生；也不容许"公司政治"（Company Politics）的形成。至于我们用人的首要条件是品格与才能，绝不是"关系"。

<p class="en-para">## Business Philosophy</p>
<p class="en-para">Around 1994-95, TSMC's revenue had been growing at a compound annual rate of 60-70% for several years. The number of employees and key managers had also increased sharply. I could no longer frequently engage in philosophical discussions with all key managers. I thought: we should write down our core values—TSMC's business philosophy. Although the process of writing the philosophy took only a few days, the philosophy itself was the result of decades of family upbringing and schooling plus professional and social experience. I wrote ten principles:</p>
<p class="en-para">**TSMC Business Philosophy**</p>
<p class="en-para">**(1) Uphold the Highest Professional Ethics**</p>
<p class="en-para">This principle represents the company's character. It is our most fundamental and most important philosophy, and a code we must follow when conducting business. By highest professional ethics we mean: First, we tell the truth. Second, we do not exaggerate or put on airs. Third, we do not make promises to customers lightly; once a promise is made, we must spare no cost and give our all. Fourth, while competing aggressively within legal bounds, we also respect competitors' intellectual property. We do not maliciously disparage. Fifth, we select and collaborate with suppliers based on objectivity, integrity, and fairness.</p>
<p class="en-para">Within the company, we absolutely do not tolerate corruption; we do not tolerate factions or cliques within the company; nor do we tolerate the formation of "company politics." As for hiring, the primary criteria are character and competence, definitely not "connections."</p>
</div>

**（二）专注于"专业积体电路制造服务"本业**

我们的本业是"专业积体电路制造服务"，这个领域发展迅速，只要我们能集中力量，积极从事本业的钻研，发展空间必定无可限量。因此，我们要心无旁骛、全力以赴，在"专业积体电路制造服务"本业中谋求最大的成就。

**（三）放眼世界市场，国际化经营**

我们的目标是全球市场，而不局限于东南亚或任何地区。集成电路是一个跨越国界的产业，全球各主要业者无不将目标延伸到世界各地。而我们最强的竞争者也来自国外，如果我们不能够把眼光放远在世界市场中建立竞争力，我们在国内终究也将无法生存。

我们的根在台湾，但要在全世界主要市场建立基地，是国际化经营的意义。而为配合国际多元发展的文化需求，在人才募集方面，我们则不论国籍，唯才是用。

**（四）注意长期策略，追求永续经营**

我们确信"人无远虑，必有近忧"的道理，只要我们能做好长期策略规划，并认真地执行，短期较为急就章式的冲刺便会大大减少。因此，除了每年我们都要为未来五年做一个长期策略规划外，在我们的日常工作中，也应有相当高的程度落实于长期的成效与回收。

<p class="en-para">**(2) Focus on Our Core Business: "Dedicated IC Manufacturing Services"**</p>
<p class="en-para">Our core business is "dedicated IC manufacturing services." This field is developing rapidly. As long as we concentrate our energy and actively pursue excellence in our core business, the development potential is boundless. Therefore, we must be single-minded and give our all to achieving the greatest success in our core business of "dedicated IC manufacturing services."</p>
<p class="en-para">**(3) Look to World Markets, Operate Internationally**</p>
<p class="en-para">Our target is the global market, not limited to Southeast Asia or any single region. Semiconductors are a borderless industry; every major global player has extended its reach to every corner of the world. And our strongest competitors also come from abroad. If we are not able to broaden our vision and establish competitiveness in the world market, we will ultimately be unable to survive domestically either.</p>
<p class="en-para">Our roots are in Taiwan, but establishing footholds in every major world market is the meaning of international operations. To support international and diverse cultural needs, in terms of talent recruitment we are nationality-blind—we hire solely on merit.</p>
<p class="en-para">**(4) Pay Attention to Long-Term Strategy, Pursue Sustainable Operation**</p>
<p class="en-para">We firmly believe in the principle that "if a person does not have long-term considerations, there will be near-term worries." As long as we can do long-term strategy planning well and execute it diligently, short-term hastily-executed sprints will be greatly reduced. Therefore, in addition to the five-year long-term strategy plan we do every year, in our daily work there should also be a high degree of focus on long-term results and returns.</p>
