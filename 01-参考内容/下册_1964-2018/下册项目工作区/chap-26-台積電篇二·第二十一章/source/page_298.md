## 策略VII——企业文化

我们的企业文化表述于十大经营理念中，其内涵和本策略彼此一致且相互依赖。十大经营理念和本策略应相辅相成。

## 理想的量化成果

同仁们将此视为成功执行此策略应有的成果，而不是目标本身：成为全球最大的积体电路制造服务公司，营收领先第二大晶圆制造公司比例至少为二比一。（3）二零一零年之前，营收达美金百亿元。

## 策略的总结

这策略的重点是：运用台积电的核心优势——工程技术及制造能力，而使它成为一个服务公司。以"优质服务"为导向，以"为客户创造最大整体利益"为卖点，毫不犹豫地要求"价差"。而且，"10%价差绝非上限"。这样大胆的策略，不但在台湾制造业没听闻过（不记得张继正总裁在台积电募资时的评语吗？"台湾公司要凭商誉竞争，倒是第一次听闻。"见第十六章），即使在全世界也很少见。

但是，这策略出奇地成功了，不但在九零年代成功，而且一直到我退休，还是继续成功。当然我们在一九九九年，就加了"技术领先"一项。起初也是"耳提面命"，后来正式成为我们"商标"的一部分："技术领先！制造优越！客户信任！"

"技术领先"的果实在二零一一年开始出现（二十八纳米），到二零一八年，也就是我退休的那年，台积电的尖端技术（七纳米）已到了独家领先世界、供不应求的地步。

虽然"技术领先"是今日台积电的重要支柱，但我从未忘记，台积电的基础是"服务导向"打下来的。

<p class="en-para">Our corporate culture is captured in the Ten Business Philosophies, whose content and this strategy are consistent with and dependent on each other. The Ten Philosophies and this strategy should complement each other.</p>
<p class="en-para">### Ideal Quantitative Results: The Outcome, Not the Goal</p>
<p class="en-para">Colleagues should view these as the fruits that should result from successful execution of this strategy, not as goals per se: Become the world's largest IC manufacturing services company, with revenue lead over the second-largest foundry at least 2:1. (3) Achieve revenue of $10 billion USD by 2010.</p>
<p class="en-para">### Summary of Strategy</p>
<p class="en-para">The essence of this strategy is: leverage TSMC's core strengths—engineering technology and manufacturing capability—to transform it into a service company. Oriented around "superior service," with "creating the greatest total benefit for customers" as the selling point, unapologetically demanding a "price premium." And "10% premium is by no means the upper limit." Such a bold strategy had not been heard of in Taiwan's manufacturing industry (don't you remember President Zhang Jizheng's comment during TSMC's fundraising? "A Taiwanese company competing on reputation—that's the first I've heard of it." See Chapter 16). Even globally, such a strategy was rare.</p>
<p class="en-para">But this strategy succeeded remarkably—not only in the '90s, but continued to be successful all the way through my retirement. Of course, in 1999 we added "technology leadership." At first, it too was transmitted by "personal briefings," and later it formally became part of our "trademark": "Technology Leadership! Manufacturing Excellence! Customer Trust!"</p>
<p class="en-para">The fruits of "technology leadership" began to appear in 2011 (28nm), and by 2018—the year I retired—TSMC's cutting-edge technology (7nm) had reached a stage of exclusive world dominance, with supply falling short of demand.</p>
<p class="en-para">Although "technology leadership" is an important pillar of today's TSMC, I have never forgotten: TSMC's foundation was laid down by being "service-oriented."</p>
