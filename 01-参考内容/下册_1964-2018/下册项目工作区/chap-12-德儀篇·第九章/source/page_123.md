## 彪希的怪诞与夏伯特的茫然

电子表市场崩溃，整个德仪营收倒退一大部分。夏伯特和彪希对消费者产品的前景开始焦虑，但他们距离「壮士断腕」、放弃消费者事业还远。同时，消费者事业的亏损，就要从公司中获利最高的半导体集团身上弥补。

一九七六、七七年是我任半导体集团总经理的最后两年。在这两年中，我几乎每月都被要求增加营收和获利，以弥补消费者产品的亏损。研发经费只占营收4.7%——这在同业中非常低的研发对营收比例。我想增加到5.5%，但通通被彪希驳回；可以「早交」的货我们就「早交」，也不管这对公司有害，因为客户付款不会因「早交货」而提早，反而他知道我们急了，所以下次议价时他会更硬；行销费用也要减，一切费用都要降到最低，一切人事都要缩到最小。

这一切都是为短期绩效，对德仪的长期健康，绝对有害。

为什么夏伯特和彪希要我这样做？为什么我也跟着他们走了两年？说穿了，夏伯特拼命要半导体弥补消费者产品集团的亏损是为了他好强的个性，不肯承认他自己从海格底接手的最大决定是一个大错误；彪希忠诚地执行了夏伯特的命令，是因为他要保持他是夏伯特继承者的地位；我为什么遵从了他们的命令，做我不喜欢做的事两年之久？因为过去十八年德仪对我太好了！教我怎么做总经理，怎么从基础建立企业，让我实现了我的「美国梦」——如今忽然有一百八十度的转变，我想：「这是暂时的吧？很快地，我们该又会回到过去理性、快乐的德仪吧？」

<p class="en-para"><b>Shep's Absurdity and Shepherd's Bewilderment</b></p>
<p class="en-para">The electronic watch market collapsed, and TI's total revenue retreated significantly. Shepherd and Shep began to worry about the prospects for consumer products, but they were still far from "cutting off the arm" — abandoning the consumer business. Meanwhile, the consumer business's losses had to be compensated by the company's most profitable unit: the Semiconductor Group.</p>
<p class="en-para">1976 and 1977 were my last two years as Semiconductor Group GM. During those two years, I was asked almost monthly to increase revenue and profits to offset consumer products' losses. R&D spending was only 4.7% of revenue — a very low R&D-to-revenue ratio versus the industry. I wanted to increase it to 5.5%, but every proposal was rejected by Shep. Shipments that could be "shipped early" we shipped early, even though this harmed the company — because customers wouldn't pay earlier just because we shipped early, and knowing we only that we were desperate, they'd be even tougher in the next price negotiation. Marketing expenses had to be cut. All costs had to be minimized. All staffing had to be trimmed to the bone.</p>
<p class="en-para">All of this was for short-term performance, and absolutely harmful to TI's long-term health.</p>
<p class="en-para">Why did Shepherd and Shep want me to do this? Why did I follow them for two years? Truth be told: Shepherd fought to have semiconductors offset the Consumer Products Group's losses because of his stubborn pride — he refused to acknowledge that the biggest decision he'd made after Haggerty was a colossal mistake. Shep loyally executed Shepherd's orders because he wanted to preserve his position as Shepherd's heir apparent. Why did I comply with their orders for two years, doing things I disliked? Because TI had been so good to me for the past eighteen years! It had taught me how to be a GM, how to build a business from the ground up, and enabled me to achieve my "American Dream." Now, suddenly, a 180-degree reversal — I thought: "This is temporary, right? Soon enough, we'll return to the old rational, joyful TI, won't we?"</p>

### 修辞赏析

<span class="rhetoric-note">
<span class="zh">「这是暂时的吧？很快地，我们该又会回到过去理性、快乐的德仪吧？」——这是张忠谋自传中为数不多的、直接展现内心自我欺骗的句子。他以一个问句的形式，暴露了一个理性主义者在面对非理性组织时的心理防御机制。他知道这不是暂时的，但他仍选择相信这是暂时的。这种「知其不可而信之」的心态，恰恰是组织惯性最深层的人性根源。</span>
<span class="en">"This is temporary, right? Soon enough, we'll return to the old rational, joyful TI, won't we?" This is one of the rare moments in Chang's autobiography where he directly exposes his own self-deception. In the form of a question, he reveals the psychological defense mechanism of a rationalist confronting an irrational organization. He knew it wasn't temporary, yet he chose to believe it was. This "believing despite knowing" mentality is precisely the deepest human root of organizational inertia.</span>
</span>
