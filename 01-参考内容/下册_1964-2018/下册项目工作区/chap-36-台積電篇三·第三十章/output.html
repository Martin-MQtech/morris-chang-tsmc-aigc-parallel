台积电篇三·第三十章｜建构理想的董事会

我心目中理想的董事会

理论上，一个公司的最高权力机构是股东大会，但股东大会通常一年只开一次，所以把权力交给它选出来的董事会。在英美制度下，董事会选任有能者为总裁（CEO），主理公司业务；也选出董事领导董事会，监督总裁，扮演公司重要决定最后把关者的角色。董事会既然是公司最高权力单位，每一位董对股东、对员工、对社会都负有相当责任。董事应以公司经营的长期方向和策略为己任。

ReadShift 双语翻译

In theory, the highest authority of a company is the shareholders' meeting, but since shareholders' meetings are typically held only once a year, power is delegated to the board of directors elected by them. Under the Anglo-American system, the board appoints a capable president (CEO) to manage company business and also elects directors to lead the board, oversee the CEO, and serve as the ultimate gatekeepers of important company decisions. Since the board is the company's highest governing body, each director bears considerable responsibility to shareholders, employees, and society. Directors should take as their duty the company's long-term direction and strategy.

但大部分台湾的董事会并不如此。大部分董事会都被大股东控制，董事人选自然倾向大股东能控制的一类。开会议程都由大股东或大股东所派的董事长决定。每次开会行礼如仪，绝少讨论，更没有真正的集思广益。

ReadShift 双语翻译

But most Taiwanese boards do not operate this way. Most boards are controlled by major shareholders, and director candidates naturally tend to be those whom major shareholders can control. Meeting agendas are determined by major shareholders or the chairman they appoint. Meetings proceed with ritualistic formality, with little discussion and certainly no genuine deliberation.

我在创办台积电前已有不少董事会经验。在德仪，我虽不是董事，但因主持最重要的业务单位，多年来经常列席董事会。后来任通用器材以及亚德诺半导体（ADI）的董事，又以天使投资人身份任好几个初创公司的董事。在台湾我也当过远东水泥的董事，后来，我又当了美国高盛银行总公司的董事。

ReadShift 双语翻译

I had considerable board experience before founding TSMC. At TI, although I was not a director, I frequently attended board meetings for many years because I headed the most important business unit. Later, I served as a director of General Instrument and Analog Devices (ADI), and as an angel investor, I served on the boards of several startups. In Taiwan, I was also a director of Far East Cement, and later, I became a director of Goldman Sachs' global headquarters.

有了这些经验，我在创办台积电时对董事会的运作已有相当成熟的意见。这些意见大约可以摘要如下：

理想董事会的五项原则

（一）台积电董事的资历应与半导体有关。「隔行如隔山」，找一个不懂半导体业的人来做董事，他（她）的贡献可能很少。

ReadShift 双语翻译

(1) TSMC directors should have qualifications relevant to semiconductors. "Each trade is a world apart"—finding someone who does not understand the semiconductor industry to serve as a director would likely yield little contribution.

（二）董事的社会声望应与我不相上下，或比我高。如此，才可免除被人认为被我控制的顾虑。

ReadShift 双语翻译

(2) Directors' social prestige should be comparable to or higher than mine. Only then can concerns about being perceived as controlled by me be dispelled.

（三）董事会人数应少于十人。人数太多时，各方意见常不易整合，相互之间的顾虑较多，更不能够开心胸表达意见。

ReadShift 双语翻译

(3) The board should have fewer than ten members. When the number is too large, diverse opinions are often difficult to integrate, mutual concerns are numerous, and it becomes harder to open one's heart and express opinions freely.

（四）独立董事（独董）——独立于经理阶层及大股东——应占董事会的多数。经理阶层平日就在经营公司业务，董事会应代表更高一层的监督、策略和治理。大股东（持股百分之五以上者）占董事会席次的比例，应少于其持股比例。

ReadShift 双语翻译

(4) Independent directors—independent of both management and major shareholders—should constitute a majority of the board. Management already runs the company day to day; the board should represent a higher level of oversight, strategy, and governance. The proportion of board seats held by major shareholders (those holding five percent or more) should be less than their shareholding percentage.

（五）董事会先设两个委员会——审计委员会及薪酬委员会。这两个委员会都以所有独立董事为成员，资深独董担任主席。审计委员会还雇用了一位顾问——前飞利浦半导体部门的财务长，曾以飞利浦法人代表身份当过台积电的董事。审计委员会每次开会前三天就到新竹，先花两天时间查账，然后在审委开会时报告查账结果。台积电也有全职内部稽核人员常驻新竹，平日对我报告，审委开会时对委员们报告。

ReadShift 双语翻译

(5) The board should first establish two committees—an audit committee and a compensation committee. Both committees should consist entirely of independent directors, with a senior independent director serving as chair. The audit committee also engaged a consultant—the former chief financial officer of Philips' semiconductor division, who had previously served as a TSMC director in Philips' representative capacity. Three days before each audit committee meeting, the consultant travels to Hsinchu, spends two days examining the accounts, and then reports the findings at the committee meeting. TSMC also has full-time internal auditors常驻 in Hsinchu, who report to me on a daily basis and to the committee members when the audit committee meets.

薪酬委员会参考经理阶层的建议，决定一般调薪幅度，也逐一决定高层人员的分红。每次开会，我总以董事长暨CEO身份列席，逐一报告每位资深副总及副总的表现和潜力，建议每人的当季分红。我报告后即自动离席，让委员们闭门讨论我的分红。

陆续增加独立董事

飞利浦最初持有台积电近七成股份，董事会十席中占七席。直至二○○○年，飞利浦持股降至约二成，空出一席董事席位，我才有机会提名一席董事——立刻提名施振荣。施振荣是台湾电脑界巨子，也办过半导体事业（德碁）。

Philips initially held nearly 70% of TSMC's shares, occupying seven of ten board seats. It was not until 2000, when Philips' holdings dropped to roughly 20% and a board seat became vacant, that I had the opportunity to nominate one director—I immediately nominated Schindler. Schindler is a titan of Taiwan's computer industry and had also founded a semiconductor business (Tatung).

再等两年，到了二○○二年，开发基金持股已降至9.7%，三席监察人也空出一席，再加上公司章程可设十名董事，那时只有八人。所以我又有机会提名两位董事、一位监察人。

Two more years passed, and by 2002, the Development Fund's holdings had dropped to 9.7%, creating a vacancy among the three supervisors. Additionally, the company charter allowed for ten directors, but only eight sat at the time. So I had another opportunity to nominate two directors and one supervisor.

二○○一年底，我在法国巴黎参加世界杯桥牌比赛，每天早上看欧洲版《华尔街日报》。有一天忽然看到彼得·邦菲（Peter Bonfield）即将自英国电信公司退休的消息。邦菲是英国人，也是我在德仪的旧属，与我有「共患难」之谊（见本书第十章）。他在德仪兵荒马乱时期离开德仪，回到英国后事业顺利，做到英国电信的总裁，还被英国女皇封为爵士。

At the end of 2001, I was in Paris, France, competing in the World Bridge Championship, reading the European edition of The Wall Street Journal each morning. One day I suddenly saw news that Peter Bonfield was about to retire from British Telecom. Bonfield is British and was also my former subordinate at TI, with whom I shared "hardship during difficult times" (see Chapter 10). He left TI during its chaotic period, returned to the UK where his career prospered, rising to president of British Telecom, and was knighted by the British Queen.

邦菲完全符合我理想中台积电董事的条件。见到他即将退休新闻的当天，我就从巴黎打电话给他，问他愿否担任台积电董事。当时他很高兴接到我的电话，但还未决定退休后做什幺，要我等他正式退休后再找他。几个月后，我从台湾再打电话给他，他欣然答应任台积电董事。

Bonfield perfectly matched my ideal criteria for a TSMC director. On the very day I saw the news of his impending retirement, I called him from Paris and asked whether he would serve on TSMC's board. He was delighted to receive my call but had not yet decided what to do after retirement, asking me to contact him again after his formal retirement. A few months later, I called him from Taiwan, and he gladly accepted the position of TSMC director.

二○○二年我还提名了另一位董事：雷斯特·梭罗（Lester Thurow）。他是MIT史隆管理学院教授及前院长，也是ADI的董事。我在当ADI董事时认识他，后来又在台湾时代基金会与他更为熟悉。梭罗是管理学「大师」级人物，而且因长期担任ADI董事，对公司治理有丰富经验。

ReadShift 双语翻译

In 2002, I also nominated another director: Lester Thurow. He was a professor and former dean of MIT's Sloan School of Management, and also a director of ADI. I had met him when I served on the ADI board, and later became more familiar with him through the Taiwan Times Foundation. Thurow was a "master" figure in management studies, and through his long service on the ADI board, he had rich experience in corporate governance.

二○○二年我提名的监察人是迈克·波特（Michael Porter）。波特是哈佛商学院教授，竞争学大师，在竞争策略领域著作等身。

ReadShift 双语翻译

In 2002, the supervisor I nominated was Michael Porter. Porter is a professor at Harvard Business School, a master of competition studies, and has authored numerous works in the field of competitive strategy.

**关于波特的小故事**

ReadShift 双语翻译

一九九九年四月，波特应《天下杂志》之约来台湾演说，我也去听了。波特的演说一向很精彩，这次也不例外。但他讲英语，而且讲的内容对没有管理学理论背景的听众而言稍嫌生硬，听众的反应不很热烈。波特似乎也察觉了。演讲后《天下杂志》还安排了我和另外两位台湾企业家与波特对谈。波特更显得没精打采，似乎已认定台湾是管理学蛮荒之地，他在这里完全浪费时间。对谈一结束，他就拔脚想走。

ReadShift 双语翻译

In April 1999, Porter came to Taiwan at the invitation of *Global Management* magazine, and I attended his lecture as well. Porter's lectures are always excellent, and this was no exception. But he spoke in English, and the content was somewhat stiff for an audience without a background in management theory; the audience's response was not very enthusiastic. Porter also seemed to notice this. After the lecture, *Global Management* arranged a dialogue between Porter, myself, and two other Taiwanese entrepreneurs. Porter appeared even more listless, as if he had already concluded that Taiwan was a managerial wilderness and that he was wasting his time here. As soon as the dialogue ended, he was ready to leave.

我喊：「请留步。」他转过头来，我问：「您认识布鲁斯·汉德森（Bruce Henderson）吗？」

ReadShift 双语翻译

I called out, "Please stay." He turned around, and I asked, "Do you know Bruce Henderson?"

波特的眼睛突然亮了——居然在此「管理学蛮荒之地」，有人问他认不认识竞争学的鼻祖、学习曲线的创始者、波士顿顾问集团（BCG）的创办人。波特转过身来，以惊异的口气反问我：「你知道他吗？」

ReadShift 双语翻译

Porter's eyes suddenly lit up—to have someone ask him in this "managerial wilderness" whether he knew the founder of competitive strategy, the creator of the learning curve, and the founder of the Boston Consulting Group (BCG). Porter turned around and asked me in an astonished tone, "Do you know him?"

我不但知道汉德森，他还是我「竞争学」的启蒙者。我一直运用他所发明的「学习曲线」（见本书第三章）。我与波特有了十五分钟愉快的谈话。

ReadShift 双语翻译

Not only did I know Henderson, but he was also my启蒙 in "competition studies." I have continuously applied the "learning curve" he invented (see Chapter 3 of this book). I had a pleasant fifteen-minute conversation with Porter.

他回到波士顿后还派两位助理到新竹花了两星期时间，为撰写台积电「个案研究」而搜集资料。在个案首次在哈佛商学院授课时，波特邀请我去哈佛现身说法。学生在那堂课前已预读「个案研究」。在两个小时的时间中，第一个小时由波特讲授台积电个案，休息十分钟后，第二个小时由学生对我提问。波特的讲授很精彩，学生提问的水准也很高，当然我也不是省油的灯。总之，那天我度过了愉快的两个小时。

After returning to Boston, he still dispatched two assistants to Hsinchu for two weeks to gather materials for writing a TSMC "case study." When the case was first taught at Harvard Business School, Porter invited me to come to Harvard to share my experience. Students had pre-read the "case study" before the class. In the two-hour session, the first hour was spent by Porter lecturing on the TSMC case; after a ten-minute break, the second hour consisted of students asking me questions. Porter's lecture was excellent, and the students' questions were of high caliber. Naturally, I was no pushover either. In short, I spent a pleasant two hours that day.
