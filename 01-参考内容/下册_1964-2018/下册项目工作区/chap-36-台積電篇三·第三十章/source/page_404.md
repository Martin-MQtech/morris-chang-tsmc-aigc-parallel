## 董事会对台积电的贡献（续）

在我任董事长的三十一年内，有二十七年（一九八七至二○○五年，及二○○九至二○一八年）我也担任CEO。从外面看起来，台积电一帆风顺；事实上，我们也度过、经历不少危机，抓住许多机会，避免了许多陷阱。当然我是责无旁贷的掌舵者，但是董事会对我的支持及建言，着实功不可没。

>
> **Building the Ideal Board of Directors**
>
> Theoretically, the highest authority in a corporation rests with the shareholders' meeting. But since this convene only once annually, power is delegated to the board of directors it elects. Under the Anglo-American system, the board selects a capable CEO to manage the company's business while also providing oversight and governance. The board is the highest governing authority, and each director bears considerable responsibility to shareholders, employees, and society at large. Directors should regard the company's long-term direction and strategy as their personal charge.
>
> Yet most Taiwanese boards do not operate this way. Most are controlled by the dominant shareholder, and board candidates naturally tend to be those amenable to the controlling interest. Agendas are predetermined by the controlling shareholder or the chairman they appointed. Meetings proceed perfunctorily, with little debate and no genuine exchange of ideas.
>
> Before founding TSMC, I had accumulated substantial board experience. At TI, though not a director, I regularly attended board meetings for years as head of the company's most critical business unit. Later I served on the boards of General Instrument and Analog Devices, and as an angel investor on several startup boards. My ideal board criteria were: semiconductor-relevant experience; personal prestige comparable to or exceeding mine; fewer than ten members; independent directors constituting a majority; and two standing committees (audit and compensation) composed entirely of independents.

> **背景知识延伸｜《天下杂志》与台湾管理学界**
>
> 张忠谋在《天下杂志》活动中以「布鲁斯·汉德森」这一个冷知识「hold住」了迈克·波特。这一段小插曲不仅是知识分子之间的有趣邂逅，更折射出台湾在全球管理学版图中的微妙位置——既是快速工业化的新经济体，又常被西方理论家视为「管理学蛮荒」。波特后来为台积电撰写了哈佛案例，也成为台湾管理学界与西方对话的一个缩影。
>

> **修辞赏析｜「管理学蛮荒之地」的反转**
>
> 张忠谋在叙述波特对台反应「不很热烈」时的沮丧感之后，立刻抛出「布鲁斯·汉德森」这一冷知识，让波特「眼睛突然亮了」。这一段叙事的精妙之处在于：叙事者先抑后扬，先用「管理学蛮荒之地」这一自嘲稳住波特的傲慢，再用「学习曲线」创始人这一共同语言桥接两个看似不相干的世界。最终波特甚至派了两位助理来新竹搜集资料——这是对「蛮荒之地」最有力的反讽。
>

> <span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
> <p class="en-para">After conveying his frustration at Porter's lukewarm response to Taiwan, Chang immediately produced the cold fact of Bruce Henderson—and Porter's eyes lit up. The craft of this passage lies in its restraint: the narrator first suppresses, then elevates. By self-deprecatingly accepting the label of "management wilderness," Chang steadied Porter's condescension; by invoking the founder of the learning curve, he built a bridge between two worlds that seemed unrelated. Porter's eventual decision to send two assistants to Hsinchu to gather material proved the most pointed irony of all.</p>
