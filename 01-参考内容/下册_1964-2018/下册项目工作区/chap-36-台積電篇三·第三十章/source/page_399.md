## 巴瑞特的婉拒

这么多年聘台积电董事的过程中，我只被婉拒一次。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Throughout all these years of recruiting TSMC directors, I was politely declined only once.</p>
</div>

二○○八年底，我想在二○○九増一名独立董事。恰巧遇见当时英特尔总裁欧德宁（Paul Otellini）。当时台积电与英特尔互认对方为友好的同业，况且英特尔还是我们的客户。我问欧德宁有没有适当的人可以推荐。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">At the end of 2008, I wanted to add one independent director in 2009. By chance, I met with Paul Otellini, then CEO of Intel. At that time, TSMC and Intel recognized each other as friendly peers, and moreover, Intel was our客户. I asked Otellini whether he could recommend a suitable person.</p>
</div>

欧德宁的回答使我吃惊：「你不妨试试巴瑞特。」巴瑞特当过英特尔总裁，是当时英特尔的董事长。我与他为熟识——为成立台积电募资时，我曾找过英特尔，巴瑞特当时代表英特尔拒绝投资（见本书第十七章）。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Otellini's answer surprised me: "You might want to try Barrett." Barrett had been Intel's president and was then Intel's chairman. I was acquainted with him—for when raising capital to found TSMC, I had approached Intel, and Barrett had represented Intel in declining the investment (see Chapter 17).</p>
</div>

我说：「他不是你们的董事长吗？」欧德宁说：「是。但是根据英特尔的退休年龄章程，他就要退休了。」停了一下后，欧德宁又说：「我可以先为你试探巴瑞特一下。」我立刻说：「请！」
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">I said, "Isn't he your chairman?" Otellini replied, "Yes. But according to Intel's retirement age policy, he is about to retire." After a pause, Otellini added, "I can probe Barrett's interest for you first." I immediately said, "Please!"</p>
</div>

过了几个星期，欧德宁果然告诉我巴瑞特有意。我就打电话给巴瑞特，他回答有兴趣，但一切得等他正式退休后再说。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">A few weeks later, Otellini indeed informed me that Barrett was interested. I then called Barrett, and he responded that he was interested, but everything depended on waiting until his formal retirement.</p>
</div>

但是过了几天，我就接到他的电邮：「英理士（Morris），我非常尊敬您，也非常尊敬台积电。但是，我一生事业几乎都在英特尔，而我相信，将来总有一天，英特尔与台积电会互相竞争。那时，假使我任台积电独董，我又如何自处？所以，我想还是现在婉谢你的邀请。」
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">But a few days later, I received his email: "Morris, I deeply respect you and also deeply respect TSMC. However, my entire career has been almost entirely at Intel, and I believe that someday, Intel and TSMC will compete against each other. At that time, if I were serving as an independent director of TSMC, how would I place myself? Therefore, I think it is best to politely decline your invitation now."</p>
</div>
