## 理想董事会的五项原则

（一）台积电董事的资历应与半导体有关。「隔行如隔山」，找一个不懂半导体业的人来做董事，他（她）的贡献可能很少。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">(1) TSMC directors should have qualifications relevant to semiconductors. "Each trade is a world apart"—finding someone who does not understand the semiconductor industry to serve as a director would likely yield little contribution.</p>
</div>

（二）董事的社会声望应与我不相上下，或比我高。如此，才可免除被人认为被我控制的顾虑。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">(2) Directors' social prestige should be comparable to or higher than mine. Only then can concerns about being perceived as controlled by me be dispelled.</p>
</div>

（三）董事会人数应少于十人。人数太多时，各方意见常不易整合，相互之间的顾虑较多，更不能够开心胸表达意见。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">(3) The board should have fewer than ten members. When the number is too large, diverse opinions are often difficult to integrate, mutual concerns are numerous, and it becomes harder to open one's heart and express opinions freely.</p>
</div>

（四）独立董事（独董）——独立于经理阶层及大股东——应占董事会的多数。经理阶层平日就在经营公司业务，董事会应代表更高一层的监督、策略和治理。大股东（持股百分之五以上者）占董事会席次的比例，应少于其持股比例。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">(4) Independent directors—independent of both management and major shareholders—should constitute a majority of the board. Management already runs the company day to day; the board should represent a higher level of oversight, strategy, and governance. The proportion of board seats held by major shareholders (those holding five percent or more) should be less than their shareholding percentage.</p>
</div>

（五）董事会先设两个委员会——审计委员会及薪酬委员会。这两个委员会都以所有独立董事为成员，资深独董担任主席。审计委员会还雇用了一位顾问——前飞利浦半导体部门的财务长，曾以飞利浦法人代表身份当过台积电的董事。审计委员会每次开会前三天就到新竹，先花两天时间查账，然后在审委开会时报告查账结果。台积电也有全职内部稽核人员常驻新竹，平日对我报告，审委开会时对委员们报告。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">(5) The board should first establish two committees—an audit committee and a compensation committee. Both committees should consist entirely of independent directors, with a senior independent director serving as chair. The audit committee also engaged a consultant—the former chief financial officer of Philips' semiconductor division, who had previously served as a TSMC director in Philips' representative capacity. Three days before each audit committee meeting, the consultant travels to Hsinchu, spends two days examining the accounts, and then reports the findings at the committee meeting. TSMC also has full-time internal auditors常驻 in Hsinchu, who report to me on a daily basis and to the committee members when the audit committee meets.</p>
</div>

薪酬委员会参考经理阶层的建议，决定一般调薪幅度，也逐一决定高层人员的分红。每次开会，我总以董事长暨CEO身份列席，逐一报告每位资深副总及副总的表现和潜力，建议每人的当季分红。我报告后即自动离席，让委员们闭门讨论我的分红。
