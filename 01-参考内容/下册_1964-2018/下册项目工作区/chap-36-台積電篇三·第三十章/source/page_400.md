## 董事会角色及运作

台积电董事长（有异于总裁或CEO）最重要的工作就是领导董事会，这是我的看法及做法。董事会不是我的橡皮图章，也不是我的敌人。我需要他们的支持，但我也要听他们的意见。如果他们的意见不同于我的，我要关起门来与他们协调，而不是公开在正式董事会议中争论。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">The most important work of the TSMC chairman (distinct from president or CEO) is to lead the board—that is my view and practice. The board is not my rubber stamp, nor is it my enemy. I need their support, but I also need to hear their opinions. If their opinions differ from mine, I coordinate with them behind closed doors, rather than arguing publicly in formal board meetings.</p>
</div>

董事会的两个委员会（审计及薪酬）都应该非常独立。在我任董事长期间内，我从未干涉它们的运作。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Both board committees (audit and compensation) should be highly independent. During my tenure as chairman, I never interfered with their operations.</p>
</div>
