# 德仪篇·第二章｜工程师一跃为总经理

「新案」的成功——

如前述，「新案」在我接任「锗部」总经理时，已达到可以量产的地步。我接任总经理后，立刻与 IBM 锁定产品规格及 IBM 需要的数量，并议定了价格。我在刚加入德仪、为 IBM 生产 NPN 扩散型晶体管时代，已与 IBM 建立了相当不错的互信关系，所以「新案」的洽谈非常顺利。在我接任两、三个月后，我就对吕斯和半导体副总裁道森报告：我们可以接受 IBM「新案」的订单了。对德仪，「新案」会带来每月约一百万美元营收、五十万毛利、四十五万营业利益。

我的报告使道森和吕斯大喜过望。当场道森就说：「一百万新营收、四十五万新营业利益！哇，这样的生意你赶快多做！」

<p class="en-para">The success of the New Project —</p>
<p class="en-para">As mentioned earlier, when I became GM of the Germanium Division, the New Project was already ready for mass production. After taking over, I immediately worked with IBM to lock down the product specifications, the volumes IBM required, and the pricing. In my early days at TI, when I manufactured NPN diffused transistors for IBM, I had built a solid relationship of mutual trust with IBM. So the New Project negotiations went very smoothly. Two or three months after taking over, I reported to Lewis and semiconductor VP Dawson: we could accept IBM's New Project order. For TI, the New Project would bring roughly $1 million in monthly revenue, $500,000 in gross profit, and $450,000 in operating profit.</p>
<p class="en-para">My report made Dawson and Lewis overjoyed. Dawson said on the spot: "$1 million in new revenue and $450,000 in new operating profit! Wow — do more of this kind of business, fast!"</p>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Overjoyed**
**中文解释**：大喜过望，指超出预期的喜悦；在商业叙事中，这个词通常暗示报告者的实际表现远超标。
**商业造句">The board was overjoyed when the CFO reported Q3 earnings — not because the numbers were good, but because they were good against a guidance that had been set in the worst quarter.*

2. **Do more of this kind of business, fast**
**中文解释**：赶快多做这类生意，指管理层对高利润业务的即时扩张指令；在运营层面，这种指令往往意味着资源优先级的重新分配。
**商业造句">After the first enterprise contract closed, the CEO told the sales team: "Do more of this kind of business, fast — before the incumbents figure out what we're doing."*

3. **Lock down specifications**
**中文解释**：锁定规格，指与客户确定最终的技术参数和采购条件；在半导体产业中，「锁定规格」往往是量产前的最后一道关卡。
**商业造句">The customer locked down specifications in September — any change after that point would trigger a new qualification cycle costing six months and $2 million.*

### 修辞赏析

<span class="rhetoric-note">
<span class="zh">「一百万新营收、四十五万新营业利益！哇，这样的生意你赶快多做！」——道森的这句话，是管理层对下属最直接的「货币式赞美」。不是「做得好」，不是「我为你骄傲」，而是「这样的生意赶快多做」——因为在商业中，利润是最好的鼓掌。张忠谋特意记录了这句话，因为它不仅验证了他的「新案」策略，也验证了一个更宏观的判断：在快速成长的半导体产业中，抓住一个关键客户的关键需求，就能创造一个全新的利润中心。</span>
<span class="en">"$1 million in new revenue and $450,000 in new operating profit! Wow — do more of this kind of business, fast!" — Dawson's line is the most direct form of "monetary praise" a manager can offer. Not "well done," not "I'm proud of you," but "do more of this business, fast" — because in business, profit is the best applause. Chang records this line deliberately, because it validates not only his strategy but a broader judgment: in a rapidly growing semiconductor industry, capturing one key customer's critical need can create an entirely new profit center.</span>
</span>

### 背景知识延伸

**「新案」对德仪全公司的意义**：一九六四年德仪全公司每月营收约两千七百万美元，每月税后净利约一百四十万美元。「新案」带来的每月四十五万美元新营业利益，相当于全公司月净利的约三十二个百分点——这是一个了不起的贡献。一个三十二岁的中国人，用不到两个月的技术突破和两三个月的商业谈判，为全公司贡献了将近三分之一的月净利增量。这个成绩，是张忠谋在德仪此后十几年持续升迁的最坚实基础。
