## 两个策略，一个价值

今日（二零二零年）的台積電以尖端技术（七纳米、五纳米、三纳米）见称。因为台積電领先世界的尖端半导体技术，才成为我所称的"地缘政治策略家的兵家必争之地"。最近有友人问我：

"听说你们创始时的策略是市场营销，而不是达到尖端技术。我以为我一定听错了。"

他没有听错。创办时最大问题是没有客户。一厂开始时的产能是少少的每月一千片（六吋晶圆），第一年整年都没填满。创始时我们就准备建二厂，二厂初期的预计产能是每月一万两千片，从每月几百片跳到每月一万两千片，不但需要想象力，还需要策略以及说服力，才能说服董事会拨款给我们。

所以我们的第一策略是：开关美国，其次欧洲市场。美、欧市场是当时全世界最大的半导体市场。台積電核心团队只熟悉台湾市场。与美、欧市场比，台湾市场真是微乎其微。谁能领导核心团队开关美、欧市场？我认为自己是最佳人选，但在台積電成立时，我任工研院院长才一年多，正火热地要改革工研院，绝无时间或精力再兼一个创新、开关的任务。替代方案就是：雇一个富有半导体经营能力的美国人做总经理。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Today (2020) TSMC is renowned for its cutting-edge technology (7nm, 5nm, 3nm). It is precisely because of TSMC's world-leading advanced semiconductor technology that it has become what I call "a place that geopolitical strategists fight over." Recently a friend asked me:</p>
<p class="en-para">"I heard that your founding strategy was marketing, not achieving cutting-edge technology. I thought I must have misheard."</p>
<p class="en-para">He had not misheard. The biggest problem at founding was having no customers. Fab 1 started with a modest capacity of a thousand wafers per month (6-inch), and wasn't fully utilized for the entire first year. At founding, we were already planning to build Fab 2, whose initial projected capacity was twelve thousand wafers per month. Jumping from a few hundred per month to twelve thousand per month required not only imagination but also strategy and persuasiveness to convince the board to appropriate funds to us.</p>
<p class="en-para">So our first strategy was: open up the U.S. market, then the European market. The U.S. and European markets were the largest semiconductor markets in the world at that time. TSMC's core team was only familiar with the Taiwanese market. Compared to the U.S. and European markets, the Taiwanese market was truly negligible. Who could lead the core team to open up the U.S. and European markets? I considered myself the best candidate, but when TSMC was founded, I had been ITRI president for only a little over a year and was passionately reforming ITRI. I absolutely had no time or energy to also take on the pioneering, market-opening mission. The alternative was: hire an American with semiconductor management capability to be general manager.</p>
</div>

## 第二策略与价值观

第二策略是：加强核心团队已有的高良率能力。高良率是我们创台时的长处，也是我们选择"专业晶圆代工"的原因，但核心团队的高良率建立在当时台湾市场需求的廉价品上（例如电子表IC）。现在我们要开关高、中档美、欧市场。他们对于品质、可靠度、制程监控（Process Control）、制程改变控制（Change Control）等等，要比台湾市场讲究得多。我有相当信心我们核心团队既有的高良率建立在扎实工程基础上，现在要更上一层。

除了这两个策略外，我从我做"客制品"的经历体验到：千金难买客户对你的信任。现在，台積電只做"客制品"。我们必须一开始就赢得客户的信任。不轻易答应，但答应的，一定要不计代价做对。对供应商、对社会，甚至对竞争者，也要诚信以对。不但对客户要诚信，我不相信一个公司能做一个人又是另一面。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">The second strategy was: strengthen the core team's existing high-yield capability. High yield was our strength at founding, and the reason we chose the "dedicated foundry" model. But the core team's high yield had been built on low-cost products for the Taiwanese market (e.g., electronic watch ICs). Now we were going to open up the high-end and mid-range U.S. and European markets. Their demands regarding quality, reliability, process control, change control, and so on were far more rigorous than those of the Taiwanese market. I had considerable confidence that our core team's existing high yield was built on solid engineering foundations, and now needed to go one level higher.</p>
<p class="en-para">Beyond these two strategies, I had learned from my own experience doing "custom products" as TI: customer trust is a thousand pieces of gold cannot buy. Now, TSMC did only "custom products." We had to win customers' trust from the very beginning. Don't promise easily, but once promised, deliver at all costs. To suppliers, to society, even to competitors—conduct with integrity. Not only to customers must we be honest; I don't believe a company can act one way to people and another way otherwise.</p>
</div>

## 商誉竞争

当年我对经建会报告成立台積電，并且说台積電要以"好的服务"竞争时，中央银行总裁曾经哈哈了一声："台湾公司要凭商誉竞争，倒是第一次听闻。"现在，我们真的要做了。

起始台積電的两个策略，后来有增加，最重要的增加就是十二年后（一九九九年）的"技术领先"，也随市场和竞争环境而有所改变，但起始的价值观——诚信，却一直没变过。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Back when I reported to the Council for Economic Planning and Development on establishing TSMC, and said that TSMC would compete on "good service," the Central Bank governor laughed out loud: "A Taiwanese company competing on reputation—that's the first I've heard of it." Now, we were really going to do it.</p>
<p class="en-para">TSMC's initial two strategies were later augmented—the most important addition being "technology leadership" twelve years later (1999)—and they evolved with the market and competitive environment. But the founding value—integrity—never changed.</p>
</div>
