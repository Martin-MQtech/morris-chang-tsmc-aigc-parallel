## 戴克斯——第一任总经理

一九八六年秋，台積電募资有谱，我就开始物色美国人总经理。我接触了三、四位我熟悉的德仪前下属，现在已在别的半导体公司任要职。竟无一人有兴趣，连来台湾看一看的兴趣都没有。

就在那时，戴克斯和我接触了。我认识戴克斯，但不熟。一九八五年春，我们曾一起参加过一次美国半导体业协会理事会。那时我代表通用器材；他代表GE半导体公司。一年半后，我已在台湾，即将组成台積電；他仍在GE，但即将失业。他即将失业的原因是：GE总裁威尔许决定关闭GE半导体公司。

威尔许是一九八一九九一年至二零零一年的GE总裁，也是一个风云人物，一度被誉为"二十世纪后半最佳企业总裁"。GE是一个许多事业的集合体。在威尔许接任总裁初期，他检讨了每一事业，对一个在它行业营收排名不高的事业（例如GE半导体公司在半导体行业的营收排名也许是第十几名或第二十几名），威尔许只有一个命令："五年内做到第一或第二名；否则，关门！"对戴克斯而言，要他做到半导体业第一或第二等于要他爬喜马拉雅山，所以结论是关门。拖到一九八六年秋，威尔许的五年时限亦将到临，戴克斯亦将失业，所以戴对台積電总经理这机会大有兴趣。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">In the fall of 1986, with TSMC's fundraising looking promising, I began scouting for an American general manager. I approached three or four former TI subordinates whom I knew well, who were now holding senior positions at other semiconductor companies. Not a single one was interested—they weren't even interested in coming to Taiwan to take a look.</p>
<p class="en-para">Just then, Douglas Dexter made contact with me. I knew Dexter, but not well. In the spring of 1985, we had both attended a meeting of the U.S. Semiconductor Industry Association board of directors. At that time I represented General Electric (G.E.) equipment; he represented GE's semiconductor division. A year and a half later, I was in Taiwan, about to form TSMC; he was still at GE, but about to lose his job. The reason he was about to be unemployed: GE president Jack Welch had decided to shut down GE's semiconductor business.</p>
<p class="en-para">Welch was GE's president from 1981 to 2001, a legendary figure once hailed as "the best corporate president of the second half of the twentieth century." GE was a collection of many businesses. Early in Welch's tenure as president, he reviewed each business. For a business whose revenue ranking in its industry was not high (e.g., GE Semiconductor's ranking in the semiconductor industry was perhaps fifteenth or twentieth), Welch had only one command: "Become first or second within five years—otherwise, shut it down!" For Dexter, asking him to make GE Semiconductor first or second in the industry was like asking him to climb Mt. Everest, so the conclusion was to shut it down. This dragged on until the fall of 1986, when Welch's five-year deadline was approaching and Dexter was about to lose his job. So Dexter was very interested in the opportunity to be TSMC's general manager.</p>
</div>

## 飞利浦的审核

我在一九八六年十月亲访戴克斯于他居住地——北卡罗来纳州图兰市，相谈甚投契。戴很认同"专业晶圆代工"商业模式以及开关美欧市场策略；戴也在年底来台湾与"五人小组"五人小组相会。"五人小组"没有什么意见，只是戴和我都觉得很奇怪，在晚餐席上"五人小组"除了在介绍时与戴寒暄几句外，只互相中文谈笑，而没有对戴发一言或问一句话。

根据当时的"股东协议"，总经理由最大股东（开发基金）提名，董事会通过后任命。飞利浦在董事会十席内占三席，所以我也请飞利浦在台湾的两位董事与戴克斯面谈，两位都很支持。我以为我已做得很周到了，想不到一星期后我就接到第三位飞利浦董事（克列斯曼）的电话，他数落了我一顿，警告我"必须小心"（Careful）维护飞利浦权益，而且坚持面试戴克斯。我只得嘱戴于一九八七年一月去荷兰见克列斯曼。见戴面后，克列斯曼倒很爽快同意。戴克斯也在二月初来到台湾报到。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">In October 1986, I personally visited Dexter at his home—Raleigh, North Carolina. We hit it off well. Dexter strongly identified with the "dedicated foundry" business model and the strategy to open up U.S. and European markets. Dexter also came to Taiwan at the end of the year to meet with the "Five-Person Committee." The committee had no objections, but both Dexter and I thought it was strange: at the dinner, apart from exchanging a few pleasantries during introductions, the committee members only chatted with each other in Chinese and didn't speak a word to Dexter or ask him a single question.</p>
<p class="en-para">Under the then-existing "Shareholders Agreement," the general manager was nominated by the largest shareholder (the Development Fund) and appointed upon board approval. Philips held three of the board's ten seats, so I also arranged for Philips' two directors in Taiwan to interview Dexter. Both were very supportive. I thought I had been quite thorough, but barely a week later I received a call from a third Philips director (Krigsman), who lectured me, warning me to "be careful" to protect Philips' rights and insisting on interviewing Dexter himself. I had no choice but to ask Dexter to go to the Netherlands in January 1987 to meet Krigsman. After meeting Dexter, Krigsman readily approved. Dexter came to Taiwan to report for duty in early February.</p>
</div>
