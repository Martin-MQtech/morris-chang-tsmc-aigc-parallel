## 美国ADR上市

台积电在台湾上市的证券商是大华证券，但在正式上市时，原来对我做建议的张孝威总经理已离职，由宋学仁接替。台积电上市后宋也离开大华而加入美商高盛银行（Goldman Sachs）。宋学仁虽然在大华只待了短时期，但在这短时期内刚好碰到台积电在台湾上市。他很看好的前景，况且他初入高盛，还不是"Partner"，正想快快立功升级为"合伙人"。如能说服台积电在美国上市，将是不小的功劳。

有此背景，宋学仁在我们已通过台湾上市、而尚未承销之际，就开始游说我以ADR（American Depositary Receipt）方式在美国上市。对此我起初很冷淡；我刚经历在台湾上市，已对上市前的准备工作感觉疲倦；况且，在美国上市又有什么好处？

后来的几个月，终于打动了我。他的理由是：飞利浦与开发基金都想出脱台积电股票，而台湾股票市场交易量低；如飞利浦或开发基金大量释股，将遭遇"愈卖愈贱"的恶运。相对的，美国市场远较活泼。所以，在美国上市可视为让飞利浦与开发基金卖股票的一个好渠道。

在飞利浦同意降低承购权至40%，并废止"投资协议"时，我已认定飞利浦已放弃原先要控制及经营台积电的意向，而转为要赚钱。但是，假如他们因为卖股不易，仍长期继续保持相当高的股权，那他们的股权仍会像"达摩克利斯之剑"一般，悬挂在我头上。何不给他们一条快速售股的捷径？

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Grand Cathay Securities was the underwriter for TSMC's Taiwan listing, but by the time of the formal listing, general manager Zhang Xiaowei, who had originally advised me, had left and was replaced by Song Xueren. After TSMC's listing, Song also left Grand Cathay to join the American investment bank Goldman Sachs. Although Song Xueren was only at Grand Cathay for a brief period, it happened to coincide with TSMC's Taiwan listing. He was very bullish on TSMC's prospects. Moreover, having just joined Goldman Sachs, he was not yet a "Partner" and was eager to achieve merit and get promoted to "partner" quickly. Persuading TSMC to list in the U.S. would be no small accomplishment.</p>
<p class="en-para">Against this background, while we had already passed Taiwan listing but not yet been underwritten, Song Xueren began lobbying me to list in the U.S. via ADR (American Depositary Receipt). I was initially quite cool to this. I had just gone through the Taiwan IPO and felt exhausted by the preparatory work; moreover, what was the benefit of listing in the U.S.?</p>
<p class="en-para">In the following months, he finally moved me. His reasoning was: both the Development Fund and Philips wanted to sell TSMC stock, but the Taiwan stock market had low trading volume. If飞利浦 or the Development Fund sold large quantities, they would face the nightmare of "the more you sell, the cheaper it gets." The U.S. market was far more active. Therefore, listing in the U.S. could be seen as a good channel for飞利浦 and the Development Fund to sell their shares.</p>
<p class="en-para">By the time Philips agreed to reduce its subscription rights to 40% and annul the "Investment Agreement," I had already concluded that Philips had abandoned its original intention to control and operate TSMC and switched to wanting to make money. But if they continued to hold a rather high equity stake long-term because selling shares was difficult, their equity would still hang over my head like the "Sword of Damocles." Why not give them a fast track to sell shares?</p>
</div>

## 高盛长久合作

我对"在美国上市"的看法转为积极后，就与宋学仁说我要与高盛高层建立直接关系；我心里想的不只是这次在美国上市，而是在长久的未来，如果台积能如我那时已期许地成功，就的确需要一家关系密切的世界级投资银行，而高盛恰可充当这角色。宋学仁很配合，把高盛关键人物，包括最高层，都介绍给我。此后几十年，高盛几乎是台积电的唯一投资银行，而且把这工作做得很好。

台积电ADR于一九九七年十月八日在纽约证券交易所以代号TSM上市。台积电每一单位ADR代表台湾的五股。ADR交易远较台股交易活泼。以目前情形来说，ADR大约占全部股权20%，平均每天交易量为一千万ADR，相当于五千万台股；台股占全部股权80%，平均每天交易量只是三千万股；所以ADR交易之活泼度，是台股的六倍多！

我的主要目的——让飞利浦尽快释股，以消灭挂在经营者头上的"达摩克利斯之剑"——也逐渐实现。ADR上市前，飞利浦尚拥有31%股权；八年后（二零零五年）已减至16.4%。二零零八年八月全部出清。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">After my view of "listing in the U.S." turned positive, I told Song Xueren that I wanted to establish direct relationships with Goldman Sachs senior management. What I had in mind was not just this U.S. listing, but for the long-term future: if TSMC could succeed as I already envisioned at that time, we would indeed need a close relationship with a world-class investment bank—and Goldman Sachs could precisely play that role. Song Xueren was very cooperative and introduced me to Goldman Sachs key figures, including the highest levels. For decades thereafter, Goldman Sachs has been virtually TSMC's sole investment banking partner, and they did the job very well.</p>
<p class="en-para">TSMC's ADR was listed on the New York Stock Exchange on October 8, 1997 under the ticker TSM. Each TSMC ADR represents five shares traded in Taiwan. ADR trading is far more active than Taiwan share trading. As of now, ADRs account for about 20% of total equity, with average daily trading volume of 10 million ADRs (equivalent to 50 million Taiwan shares). Taiwan shares account for 80% of total equity, with average daily trading volume of only 30 million shares. So the activity level of ADR trading is more than six times that of Taiwan shares!</p>
<p class="en-para">My primary goal—allowing飞利浦 to quickly release shares, to eliminate the "Sword of Damocles" hanging over management—was gradually achieved. Before the ADR listing,飞利浦 still owned 31% equity; eight years later (2005), it had reduced to 16.4%. In August 2008, it fully exited.</p>
</div>

## 投资回收与反思

飞利浦在台积电投资上赚了多少钱？我们估计了一下：它的投资是三十亿元，总回收（包括股利及出售股票获利）是四千零三十八亿元——二十一年内一百三十五倍回收！非常好的回收！但是他们原来要经营台积电，要把台积电拥为己有的野心却落空了。他们本以为台积电只是一个台湾性、台湾级的公司，而飞利浦却是世界级的公司。他们在台积电成立四、五年后才发现：这家公司会比他们自己更"世界级"。

开发基金呢？他们一直遵守了"不干涉经营"的原则，但他们卖台积电股票的力道，比飞利浦还大。他们在飞利浦执行承购权后，仍拥有30.8%台积电，但在之后的六年内，就在台湾市场卖得只剩17.4%。ADR上市后，他们更是痛快地卖，卖到二零零五年九月，只剩6.4%。

开发基金赚了多少钱？它的投资是二十二亿元。出售股票收入及截至二零二二年一月底股利收入为两千七百六十七亿元。现在尚握有的6.4%，如以目前股价（约六百三十元）计，约值一兆零四百一十八亿元。也就是说，如果现在出清台积电股票，他们的总投资回收约六百倍。

当然，真正的回收还不能用金钱算。今日台积电的技术和产品，已渗入世界几十亿人民的工作和生活，以及几十万公司、组织的作业中。如果台积电忽然消失，世界几十亿人民的生活肯定会受影响。当年李国鼎、俞国华要成立台积电，为的是要发展台湾半导体工业，至于投资回收，我想他们的目标有限——只要小赚就不对了。

我必须承认，没有政府的投资和协助，我办不起来台积电。在一片"市场经济"呼声中，有些事情还是要由政府来做的。

<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">How much did Philips make on its TSMC investment? We estimated: its investment was NT$3 billion, total recovery (including dividends and stock sale profits) was NT$403.8 billion—a 135x return in 21 years! An excellent return! But their ambition to operate TSMC and claim it as their own was defeated. They originally thought TSMC would be a Taiwan-level company, while Philips was a world-class company. Only four or five years after TSMC's founding did they realize: this company would be even more "world-class" than they were themselves.</p>
<p class="en-para">The Development Fund? They consistently adhered to the principle of "non-interference in operations," but their selling of TSMC stock was even more aggressive than Philips. After Philips executed its subscription rights, the Development Fund still held 30.8% of TSMC, but within the next six years they sold down to 17.4% on the Taiwan market. After the ADR listing, they sold even more vigorously, down to 6.4% by September 2005.</p>
<p class="en-para">How much did the Development Fund earn? Its investment was NT$2.2 billion. Stock sale proceeds plus dividend income through end of January 2022 totaled NT$276.7 billion. The 6.4% still held, at the current stock price (about NT$630), would be valued at approximately NT$1.0418 trillion. In other words, if they liquidated their TSMC stock now, their total investment return would be about 600x.</p>
<p class="en-para">Of course, the true return cannot even be measured in money. Today TSMC's technology and products have permeated the work and lives of billions of people around the world, as well as the operations of hundreds of thousands of companies and organizations. If TSMC suddenly disappeared, the lives of billions of people would certainly be affected. Back then Li Guoding and Yu Kuo-hua wanted to establish TSMC to develop Taiwan's semiconductor industry. As for investment return, I think their goals were limited—just a small profit would have been fine.</p>
<p class="en-para">I must admit: without the government's investment and assistance, I could not have started TSMC. Amidst the chorus calls for "market economy," there are still things that need to be done by the government.</p>
</div>
