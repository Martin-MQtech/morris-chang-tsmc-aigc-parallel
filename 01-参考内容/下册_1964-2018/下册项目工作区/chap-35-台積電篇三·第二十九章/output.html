张忠谋

台积电篇三·第二十九章｜自「咆哮」至「绚烂」

九十年代的台积电，是「咆哮」的年代——产能供不应求，营收与获利翻倍增长。然而进入新世纪，一场不期而至的暴风雨骤然降临。二○○一年的订单暴跌，毛利率腰斩，台积电面临成立以来最严峻的考验。但也正是这场危机，催生了张忠谋的「直线投资策略」和「不景气经营法则」，为后来的「绚烂」奠定了基础。

二○○一至二○○三年的不景气

九十年代是台积电的「咆哮」年代，公元二○○○年台积电营收及获利更有惊人的成长。全年营收自九九年的七百三十一亿元成长2.27倍至一千六百六十二亿元；营业净利自九九年的两百四十八亿元成长2.47倍至六百一十三亿元。当年七月，我们完成德碁及世大的购并，当晚有一个庆祝派对。天上似乎没有一片乌云。

ReadShift 双语翻译

The 1990s were TSMC's "roaring" decade, and in the year 2000, TSMC's revenue and profitability achieved astonishing growth. Full-year revenue grew 2.27 times from NT$73.1 billion in 1999 to NT$166.2 billion; operating profit grew 2.47 times from NT$24.8 billion to NT$61.3 billion. In July of that year, we completed the acquisitions of Tatung and SECO, and there was a celebration party that evening. There did not seem to be a single cloud in the sky.

虽然七月没有乌云，但八、九月就有了，而且很快密集。台积电从接订单到交货大约需四、五个月，所以二○○一年第一季的营收，我在二○○○年十一月底就大约知道——很可能会比二○○○年第四季低二成五。这是台积电成立后最大的挑战。

Although there were no clouds in July, they appeared in August and September—and quickly thickened. TSMC requires approximately four to five months from order receipt to delivery, so by late November 2000 I already had a reasonable estimate of first-quarter 2001 revenue—it would likely be twenty-five percent lower than the fourth quarter of 2000. This was the greatest challenge TSMC had faced since its founding.

在此不景气期间，我实施了我在德仪已领悟到但无权完全实施的「不景气时期经营法则」。要点有三项：

ReadShift 双语翻译

During this recession, I implemented the "recession management principles" I had learned at TI but had never had the authority to fully apply. There were three key points:

（一）全力与客户及供应商维持伙伴关系。不景气导致需求的突然萎缩，使得一切都乱了——本来吵着要货的客户端，忽然要砍掉一半订单。另一方面，我们本来要求供应商供应的材料或设备，现在也要减少了。而我的立场是：半导体业长期一定会成长，目前的不景气是短期的、暂时的，我们要做好准备，蓄势待发。伙伴关系的意义是：我们与客户及供应商互相体谅对方的难处，做合情合理的协调；同患难，才能共同享受长国的利益。

ReadShift 双语翻译

(1) Exhaust every effort to maintain partner relationships with clients and suppliers. A recession causes a sudden contraction in demand, throwing everything into disarray—clients who had been clamoring for products suddenly slash their orders by half. On the other hand, the materials and equipment we had been requesting from suppliers now needed to be reduced. My position was: the semiconductor industry will grow in the long run; the current recession is short-term and temporary. We must prepare ourselves and wait for the right moment. The meaning of partnership is: we and our clients and suppliers mutually understand each other's difficulties, make reasonable and equitable adjustments, and endure hard times together so that we may jointly enjoy the benefits of long-term growth.

（二）不裁员。我一直认为过去日本企业的「终身雇用」习惯，对员工向心力很有帮助。况且，假使裁员后一年内又要雇用新人，裁员在经济上也不划算——因为裁员费大约是半年的薪资，而训练新员工又差不多是半年薪资。事实上，二○○一年底员工人数只略低于二○○○年底，因为我们没有完全填补自动流失人员。但二○○二年底员工人数已超出二○○○年底，二○○三年底更高。

ReadShift 双语翻译

(2) No layoffs. I have always believed that the Japanese corporate custom of "lifetime employment" was highly beneficial for employee loyalty. Moreover, if a company had to rehire within a year after layoffs, it was economically irrational—because severance pay was approximately half a year's salary, and training new employees cost roughly another half year's salary. In fact, the headcount at the end of 2001 was only slightly lower than at the end of 2000, because we did not fully replace voluntary attrition. But by the end of 2002, headcount had exceeded the end-of-2000 level, and by the end of 2003 it was even higher.

（三）不但不削减研发经费，反而急増加。台积电研发费用自二○○○年五十一亿元，增至二○○一年的一百零六亿元、二○○二年的一百一十七亿元。不景气的时期别人不投资，是最好吸引人才的时机。

ReadShift 双语翻译

(3) Not only did we not reduce R&D spending, we aggressively increased it. TSMC's R&D expenses rose from NT$5.1 billion in 2000 to NT$10.6 billion in 2001 and NT$11.7 billion in 2002. During a recession, when others are not investing, is the best time to attract talent.

半导体业的周期性

其实半导体业的周期性，至少对我而言不应是惊奇。自一九六四年我在德仪开始担任半导体业务单位总经理，至二○○○年，半导体业已经过一九六七、一九七○至七一、一九七五、一九八五、一九九○、一九九六至九八年——七次不景气。头三次我在德仪处理；第四次我仍在德仪但已不在其位，故不谋其政；第五次我在通用器材应对。一九九○年那次，台积电刚成立不久，二厂也才加入生产，我们不太感觉到不景气，只觉得二厂起步慢了些。到了一九九六至九八年的不景气，台积电处在「咆哮」地成长阶段，产能供不应求，只觉得客户要求的产能压力稍缓，并不感受到任何「不景气」。

ReadShift 双语翻译

Actually, the cyclicality of the semiconductor industry should not have been surprising—at least not to me. From when I began serving as general manager of a semiconductor business unit at TI in 1964 through 2000, the industry had experienced seven recessions: 1967, 1970–71, 1975, 1985, 1990, and 1996–98. I handled the first three at TI; the fourth I was still at TI but no longer in a position of authority, so I did not involve myself; the fifth I addressed at General Instrument. The 1990 recession came shortly after TSMC's founding, when Fab 2 had just begun production—we hardly felt the recession, only that Fab 2's start-up was somewhat slow. By the 1996–98 recession, TSMC was in the midst of its "roaring" growth phase, with capacity in short supply. We only felt that the pressure from clients demanding capacity had eased slightly; we did not feel any "recession" at all.

但是二○○一年台积电已有相当规模，我粗略估计当年我们供应全球逻辑IC一成五左右。我们的规模已使我们不能幸免于整个行业的不景气。毛利率也从二○○○年的四六％跌至二○○一年的二七％、二○○二年的三二％、二○○三年的三七％。直至二○○四年，毛利率才回复到四五％。

「直线」投资理念的由来

建产能需要很长的时间。在七○、八○年代，如果要从建厂房开始，大约需要三年；即使有了厂房，从建洁净室开始，大约也要两年。时至今日，制程更为复杂，设备更为精密，投资更为巨大，新人员的训练需要更长时间，所以建产能的时间比七○、八○年代还须增加至少一年。

ReadShift 双语翻译

Building capacity requires a considerable amount of time. In the 1970s and 1980s, if one started from constructing a fabrication plant, it took approximately three years; even with a building in place, starting from cleanroom construction required roughly two years. To the present day, processes are more complex, equipment more precise, investments far larger, and training for new personnel takes longer—so the time required to build capacity has increased by at least one year beyond the 1970s and 1980s.

既然建产能需时三、四年，业者投资心态就成为供需平衡与否的重要因素。通常当需求强劲时，业者猛建产能，几年后产能过剩；一旦需求趋缓，业者一面杀价抢订单，一面缩手不建产能；再过几年就会产能不足。

ReadShift 双语翻译

Since building capacity takes three to four years, the investment mindset of industry participants becomes a crucial factor in supply-demand balance. Typically, when demand is strong, companies build capacity aggressively; a few years later, overcapacity results. Once demand slows, companies engage in price-cutting to抢 orders while simultaneously pulling back on capacity investment; a few years later, capacity shortage follows.

我在七○年代在德仪时就倡议「直线建产能」。在一九七四年的德仪全公司策略会议中，我建议每年以「滚动十季计划」为根据，增加一定数量的产能——也就是：景气好时不猛建，景气坏时不缩手。我的建议在策略会议中颇受赞赏，但到真正执行时，我的上面两层上司（公司的COO及CEO），以及他们上面的董事会，还是把我的计划折扣到我都不认识的地步。

ReadShift 双语翻译

I advocated "straight-line capacity building" as early as the 1970s at TI. At TI's 1974 company-wide strategy meeting, I proposed increasing a fixed amount of capacity each year based on a "rolling ten-quarter plan"—that is, not building aggressively during booms, and not pulling back during downturns. My proposal was well-received at the strategy meeting, but when it came to actual execution, my two layers of superiors (the company's COO and CEO), as well as the board above them, still discounted my plan to the point where it was unrecognizable.

在台积电，我终于可以实行我的「直线」投资理念了。

ReadShift 双语翻译

At TSMC, I was finally able to put my "straight-line" investment philosophy into practice.

长期成长率变缓

二○○一至二○○三年的不景气虽然只延续约三年，但它带来了一个长期的变化：半导体业的长期成长率显着变慢了。

ReadShift 双语翻译

Although the 2001–2003 recession lasted only about three years, it brought about a lasting change: the semiconductor industry's long-term growth rate slowed significantly.

读者可能还记得我在第九章的叙述：我每年都参加十月在华盛顿召开的「国际电子元件会议」（IEDM），有时还与别的会友一起出去喝酒、吃饭、在深秋夜晚唱歌回旅馆。那时我们都年轻，三十岁左右，自以为已进入了一个前途无限的行业，自己也变成前途无限。年轻人在他们的憧憬和希望里，享受无穷的乐趣。

ReadShift 双语翻译

Readers may recall my account in Chapter 9: I attended the International Electron Devices Meeting (IEDM) in Washington every October, and sometimes went out drinking, dining, and singing back to the hotel with fellow attendees in the deep-autumn nights. We were all young then, around thirty years old, believing we had entered an industry with limitless prospects, and that our own futures were equally boundless. Young people, in their visions and hopes, enjoy infinite pleasure.

今日白发苍苍，回想六十余年前的年轻兴奋，不免有「时不我与」的伤感。当年对「半导体这个前途无限的行业」的期许倒是真的兑现了。自一九六○至二○○○年这四十年中，半导体业虽有好几次景气起伏，但这四十年的平均「年成长率」，却是高得惊人的一七％。

ReadShift 双语翻译

Today, with white hair, looking back on the youthful excitement of over sixty years ago, I cannot help but feel a touch of "time waits for no one." The expectation back then—that "semiconductors were an industry with limitless prospects"—was indeed realized. From 1960 to 2000, despite several cycles of boom and bust, the semiconductor industry's average annual growth rate over those forty years was an astonishingly high 17 percent.

二○○○年以后呢？成长率明显转缓。二○○○至二○○一年的平均年成长率是四％。二○○○至二○一八年的平均年成长率是五％。为什幺？我想这是因为摩尔定律渐渐老了——虽然光在台积电就有几千位工程师努力地每两年把晶体管在芯片上的密度加倍，但这样做的成本愈来愈高。也就是说，摩尔定律的经济效益愈来愈差，所以芯片市场需求的成长也转缓了。

ReadShift 双语翻译

And after 2000? The growth rate slowed markedly. The average annual growth rate from 2000 to 2001 was 4 percent. The average annual growth rate from 2000 to 2018 was 5 percent. Why? I believe this is because Moore's Law is gradually aging—even though TSMC alone employs thousands of engineers working diligently to double transistor density every two years, the cost of doing so is rising ever higher. In other words, the economic returns of Moore's Law are diminishing, so the growth of chip market demand has also slowed.

随着半导体市场年成长率自一六％转缓至四至五％，台积电的平均年成长率也自九○年代的五二％转缓至二○○○年至二○一八年的十一％。十一％年成长率是母市场（半导体市场）的一倍多。长久维持，台积电才能在世界半导体业杰出。后来发展的确证明这点。但我必须强调，使我们有能力维持母市场一倍多年成长率的原动力还是我们的基本价值：技术领先、制造优越、客户信任。

ReadShift 双语翻译

As the semiconductor market's annual growth rate slowed from 16 percent to 4–5 percent, TSMC's average annual growth rate also slowed from 52 percent in the 1990s to 11 percent from 2000 to 2018. An 11 percent annual growth rate is more than double that of the parent market (the semiconductor market). Only by sustaining this over the long term could TSMC excel in the global semiconductor industry—something subsequent developments indeed confirmed. But I must emphasize that the driving force enabling us to maintain growth rates double that of the parent market remains our core values: technology leadership, manufacturing excellence, and customer trust.
