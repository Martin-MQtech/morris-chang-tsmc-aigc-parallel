## 二○○一至二○○三年的不景气

九十年代是台积电的「咆哮」年代，公元二○○○年台积电营收及获利更有惊人的成长。全年营收自九九年的七百三十一亿元成长2.27倍至一千六百六十二亿元；营业净利自九九年的两百四十八亿元成长2.47倍至六百一十三亿元。当年七月，我们完成德碁及世大的购并，当晚有一个庆祝派对。天上似乎没有一片乌云。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">The 1990s were TSMC's "roaring" decade, and in the year 2000, TSMC's revenue and profitability achieved astonishing growth. Full-year revenue grew 2.27 times from NT$73.1 billion in 1999 to NT$166.2 billion; operating profit grew 2.47 times from NT$24.8 billion to NT$61.3 billion. In July of that year, we completed the acquisitions of Tatung and SECO, and there was a celebration party that evening. There did not seem to be a single cloud in the sky.</p>
</div>

虽然七月没有乌云，但八、九月就有了，而且很快密集。台积电从接订单到交货大约需四、五个月，所以二○○一年第一季的营收，我在二○○○年十一月底就大约知道——很可能会比二○○○年第四季低二成五。这是台积电成立后最大的挑战。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Although there were no clouds in July, they appeared in August and September—and quickly thickened. TSMC requires approximately four to five months from order receipt to delivery, so by late November 2000 I already had a reasonable estimate of first-quarter 2001 revenue—it would likely be twenty-five percent lower than the fourth quarter of 2000. This was the greatest challenge TSMC had faced since its founding.</p>
</div>

在此不景气期间，我实施了我在德仪已领悟到但无权完全实施的「不景气时期经营法则」。要点有三项：
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">During this recession, I implemented the "recession management principles" I had learned at TI but had never had the authority to fully apply. There were three key points:</p>
</div>

（一）全力与客户及供应商维持伙伴关系。不景气导致需求的突然萎缩，使得一切都乱了——本来吵着要货的客户端，忽然要砍掉一半订单。另一方面，我们本来要求供应商供应的材料或设备，现在也要减少了。而我的立场是：半导体业长期一定会成长，目前的不景气是短期的、暂时的，我们要做好准备，蓄势待发。伙伴关系的意义是：我们与客户及供应商互相体谅对方的难处，做合情合理的协调；同患难，才能共同享受长国的利益。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">(1) Exhaust every effort to maintain partner relationships with clients and suppliers. A recession causes a sudden contraction in demand, throwing everything into disarray—clients who had been clamoring for products suddenly slash their orders by half. On the other hand, the materials and equipment we had been requesting from suppliers now needed to be reduced. My position was: the semiconductor industry will grow in the long run; the current recession is short-term and temporary. We must prepare ourselves and wait for the right moment. The meaning of partnership is: we and our clients and suppliers mutually understand each other's difficulties, make reasonable and equitable adjustments, and endure hard times together so that we may jointly enjoy the benefits of long-term growth.</p>
</div>

（二）不裁员。我一直认为过去日本企业的「终身雇用」习惯，对员工向心力很有帮助。况且，假使裁员后一年内又要雇用新人，裁员在经济上也不划算——因为裁员费大约是半年的薪资，而训练新员工又差不多是半年薪资。事实上，二○○一年底员工人数只略低于二○○○年底，因为我们没有完全填补自动流失人员。但二○○二年底员工人数已超出二○○○年底，二○○三年底更高。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">(2) No layoffs. I have always believed that the Japanese corporate custom of "lifetime employment" was highly beneficial for employee loyalty. Moreover, if a company had to rehire within a year after layoffs, it was economically irrational—because severance pay was approximately half a year's salary, and training new employees cost roughly another half year's salary. In fact, the headcount at the end of 2001 was only slightly lower than at the end of 2000, because we did not fully replace voluntary attrition. But by the end of 2002, headcount had exceeded the end-of-2000 level, and by the end of 2003 it was even higher.</p>
</div>

（三）不但不削减研发经费，反而急増加。台积电研发费用自二○○○年五十一亿元，增至二○○一年的一百零六亿元、二○○二年的一百一十七亿元。不景气的时期别人不投资，是最好吸引人才的时机。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">(3) Not only did we not reduce R&D spending, we aggressively increased it. TSMC's R&D expenses rose from NT$5.1 billion in 2000 to NT$10.6 billion in 2001 and NT$11.7 billion in 2002. During a recession, when others are not investing, is the best time to attract talent.</p>
</div>
