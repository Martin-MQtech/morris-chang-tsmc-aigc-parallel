## 长期成长率变缓

二○○一至二○○三年的不景气虽然只延续约三年，但它带来了一个长期的变化：半导体业的长期成长率显著变慢了。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Although the 2001–2003 recession lasted only about three years, it brought about a lasting change: the semiconductor industry's long-term growth rate slowed significantly.</p>
</div>

读者可能还记得我在第九章的叙述：我每年都参加十月在华盛顿召开的「国际电子元件会议」（IEDM），有时还与别的会友一起出去喝酒、吃饭、在深秋夜晚唱歌回旅馆。那时我们都年轻，三十岁左右，自以为已进入了一个前途无限的行业，自己也变成前途无限。年轻人在他们的憧憬和希望里，享受无穷的乐趣。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Readers may recall my account in Chapter 9: I attended the International Electron Devices Meeting (IEDM) in Washington every October, and sometimes went out drinking, dining, and singing back to the hotel with fellow attendees in the deep-autumn nights. We were all young then, around thirty years old, believing we had entered an industry with limitless prospects, and that our own futures were equally boundless. Young people, in their visions and hopes, enjoy infinite pleasure.</p>
</div>

今日白发苍苍，回想六十余年前的年轻兴奋，不免有「时不我与」的伤感。当年对「半导体这个前途无限的行业」的期许倒是真的兑现了。自一九六○至二○○○年这四十年中，半导体业虽有好几次景气起伏，但这四十年的平均「年成长率」，却是高得惊人的一七％。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">Today, with white hair, looking back on the youthful excitement of over sixty years ago, I cannot help but feel a touch of "time waits for no one." The expectation back then—that "semiconductors were an industry with limitless prospects"—was indeed realized. From 1960 to 2000, despite several cycles of boom and bust, the semiconductor industry's average annual growth rate over those forty years was an astonishingly high 17 percent.</p>
</div>

二○○○年以后呢？成长率明显转缓。二○○○至二○○一年的平均年成长率是四％。二○○○至二○一八年的平均年成长率是五％。为什么？我想这是因为摩尔定律渐渐老了——虽然光在台积电就有几千位工程师努力地每两年把晶体管在芯片上的密度加倍，但这样做的成本愈来愈高。也就是说，摩尔定律的经济效益愈来愈差，所以芯片市场需求的成长也转缓了。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">And after 2000? The growth rate slowed markedly. The average annual growth rate from 2000 to 2001 was 4 percent. The average annual growth rate from 2000 to 2018 was 5 percent. Why? I believe this is because Moore's Law is gradually aging—even though TSMC alone employs thousands of engineers working diligently to double transistor density every two years, the cost of doing so is rising ever higher. In other words, the economic returns of Moore's Law are diminishing, so the growth of chip market demand has also slowed.</p>
</div>

随着半导体市场年成长率自一六％转缓至四至五％，台积电的平均年成长率也自九○年代的五二％转缓至二○○○年至二○一八年的十一％。十一％年成长率是母市场（半导体市场）的一倍多。长久维持，台积电才能在世界半导体业杰出。后来发展的确证明这点。但我必须强调，使我们有能力维持母市场一倍多年成长率的原动力还是我们的基本价值：技术领先、制造优越、客户信任。
<div class="rebook-translation">
<span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
<p class="en-para">As the semiconductor market's annual growth rate slowed from 16 percent to 4–5 percent, TSMC's average annual growth rate also slowed from 52 percent in the 1990s to 11 percent from 2000 to 2018. An 11 percent annual growth rate is more than double that of the parent market (the semiconductor market). Only by sustaining this over the long term could TSMC excel in the global semiconductor industry—something subsequent developments indeed confirmed. But I must emphasize that the driving force enabling us to maintain growth rates double that of the parent market remains our core values: technology leadership, manufacturing excellence, and customer trust.</p>
</div>
