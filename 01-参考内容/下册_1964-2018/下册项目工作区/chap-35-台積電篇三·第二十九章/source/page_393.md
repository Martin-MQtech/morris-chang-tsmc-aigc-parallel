> **背景知识延伸｜「直线投资」的现实意义**
>
> 张忠谋的「直线投资」策略后来被无数商学院案例研究引用。在半导体这个高度周期性的产业中，多数企业的投资行为是「景气时猛扩、低迷时猛缩」，结果总是出现「产能过剩→产能不足」的恶性循环。台积电的「直线投资」反其道而行之——在别人不敢投资时继续投，等到景气恢复时恰好迎来产能释放。
>
> <div class="rebook-translation">
> <span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
> <p class="en-para">Chang's "straight-line" investment strategy was later cited in innumerable business school case studies. In the highly cyclical semiconductor industry, most firms expanded aggressively in upturns and retrenched sharply in downturns, producing the familiar vicious cycle of overcapacity followed by shortage. TSMC's straight-line approach inverted this logic: it kept investing when others dared not, so that capacity came online precisely when the cycle turned.</p>

> **修辞赏析｜「咆哮」与「绚烂」**
>
> 本章标题「自咆哮至绚烂」用两个反差强烈的意象概括了台积电在九十年代初到新世纪的历程：「咆哮」代表九十年代那种带有野性张力的快速扩张，「绚烂」则暗示新世纪台积电绽放的成熟光芒。途中经历暴风雨（不景气），更显「绚烂」的珍贵。
>
> <div class="rebook-translation">
> <span class="rebook-translation__label">ReadShift 双语翻译</span>
> <p class="en-para">The chapter title, "From Roar to Radiance," frames TSMC's journey from the early 1990s to the new century through two sharply contrasting images. "Roar" captures the untamed, high-velocity expansion of the nineties; "radiance" suggests the mature brilliance TSMC would radiate in the new century. The storms encountered along the way—downturns that tested the company—only made that radiance more precious.</p>
