## 品质蓝皮书及巡回视察

在我任「品质总监」的第一年，除了开始大规模「瀑布式训练」外，我又开始了「品质蓝皮书」和巡回视察。

什么是「品质蓝皮书」？先解释德仪的「蓝皮书」。「蓝皮书」就是一个业务单位的盈亏表，每月出版。每一业务单位，从基层一直到全公司，都有它的「蓝皮书」。夏伯特和彪希的「蓝皮书」厚厚一本，除了全公司的盈亏表外，公司百来个业务单位的盈亏表都在他们的「蓝皮书」内。

现在，我要求每一业务单位的「品质经理」编辑他单位的「品质蓝皮书」。品质蓝皮书的数据是「品质成本」——品质成本观念来自正统的品质理论：完美品质等于无瑕疵或无错误；任何瑕疵或错误都代表成本。品质蓝皮书就是这些品质成本的报导。

每一业务单位的「品质经理」都非常倡导我的到来。平常在他们同僚间（研发经理、工程经理、生产经理等等），品质经理往往不受重视，现在，我变成他们的「虚线」上司，他们有我为他们撑腰了。他们对编辑品质蓝皮书充满热忱，也热心地看管他们单位的瀑布式训练。

我也开始巡回视察。在八〇年代，德仪工厂已遍布欧、美、洲。在我任品质总监的三十四个月内，我到过所有工厂地点——有的每季一次，有的半年一次，有的年一次。在这三十四个月内，我超过一半时日都在旅行！

<p class="en-para"><b>Quality Blue Book and巡回视察</b></p>
<p class="en-para">In my first year as "Quality Director," in addition to launching the massive cascade training, I also introduced the "Quality Blue Book" and巡回视察.</p>
<p class="en-para">What was the "Quality Blue Book"? First, let me explain TI's "Blue Book." The "Blue Book" was a profit-and-loss statement for each business unit, published monthly. Every business unit — from the lowest-level shop floor to the entire company — had its own Blue Book. Shepherd and Shep's Blue Book was a thick volume containing not only the companywide P&L but also the P&L statements for the company's hundred-odd business units.</p>
<p class="en-para">Now I required each business unit's "Quality Manager" to compile a "Quality Blue Book" for their unit. The Quality Blue Book reported "quality cost" data — the quality cost concept coming from orthodox quality theory: perfect quality equals zero defects or zero errors; every defect or error represents a cost. The Quality Blue Book was the reporting vehicle for these quality costs.</p>
<p class="en-para">Every business unit's Quality Manager enthusiastically welcomed my initiative. Among their peers (R&D managers, engineering managers, production managers), quality managers had often been undervalued; now I became their "dotted-line" boss, and they had me backing them. They threw themselves into editing their units' Quality Blue Books with zeal and also diligently monitored their units' cascade training.</p>
<p class="en-para">I also began touring the factories. In the 1980s, TI factories spanned Europe, America, and Asia. During my thirty-four months as Quality Director, I visited all factory locations — some quarterly, some semi-annually, some annually. In those thirty-four months, I spent more than half my time traveling!</p>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Quality cost (品质成本)**
**中文解释**：企业因品质失败而产生的所有成本，包括内部失败成本（报废、返工）、外部失败成本（退货、赔偿）和预防/鉴定成本；「品质成本」的概念由裘兰和克罗斯比系统化——它让「品质」从一个主观的「好东西」变成了一个可量化的财务指标。
**商业造句">The quality cost concept — making the cost of poor quality visible on a P&L statement — transformed quality from a subjective virtue into a quantifiable financial metric that managers could optimize.*

2. **Dotted-line authority (虚线职权)**
**中文解释**：指组织中跨正式指挥链的报告关系——「实线」是直接主管，「虚线」是业务指导关系；张忠谋以「品质总监」身份成为各业务单位品质经理的「虚线」上司，这种安排赋予了幕僚对专业职能线的横向影响力。
**商业造句">Chang's "dotted-line" authority over unit quality managers was a classic staff-power arrangement — it gave a cross-functional leader lateral influence over specialists without disrupting the formal chain of command.*
