# 台积电篇一·第十五章｜工研院院长（一九八五－一九八八）

## 工研院的背景

工研院成立于一九七三年，是当时经济部长孙运璿先生的创造物。孙部长有工程背景，是近几十年来台湾政界中最重视科技发展的政要。一九七三年时，孙部长认为政府应该设立一个工业技术研究机构，但公教人员的薪资不足以吸引能干的研究人员，所以他花了不少时间精力，说服立法院通过「工业技术研究院设置条例」，以财团法人形式成立了「工研院」。

「工业技术研究院设置条例」等于是工研院的「宪法」，我现把它纲要如下：

为加速发展工业技术，特设工业技术研究院。本院为财团法人。

本院主管机关为经济部。

本院置董事十一至十五人，其中一人为董事长，均由行政院院长遴选。

工研院既是经济部，所以工研院等于是政府机构。政府机构的工作有保障。所以研院员工既有公职的工作保障，又有财团法人优于公职人员的待遇。

<p class="en-para"><b>The ITRI Background</b></p>
<p class="en-para">ITRI was founded in 1973, the creation of then-Minister of Economic Affairs Sun Yun-suan. Minister Sun had an engineering background and was, in recent decades, the political figure in Taiwan who most valued science and technology development. In 1973, Minister Sun believed the government should establish an industrial technology research institution, but civil service salaries were not sufficient to attract capable research staff. So he spent considerable time and energy persuading the Legislative Yuan to pass the "ITRI Establishment Act," creating ITRI in the form of a foundation.</p>
<p class="en-para">The "ITRI Establishment Act" is essentially ITRI's "constitution." I outline its key provisions:</p>
<p class="en-para"><i>To accelerate the development of industrial technology, the Industrial Technology Research Institute is hereby established. This Institute is a juridical foundation.</i></p>
<p class="en-para"><i>The competent authority of this Institute is the Ministry of Economic Affairs.</i></p>
<p class="en-para"><i>This Institute shall have eleven to fifteen directors, one of whom shall be the Chairman, all selected by the Premier.</i></p>
<p class="en-para">ITRI being under the Ministry of Economic Affairs made it effectively a government agency. Government jobs had employment security. So ITRI employees enjoyed both the job security of civil servants and the compensation advantages of a foundation over regular government employees.</p>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **ITRI (Industrial Technology Research Institute，工业技术研究院)**
**中文解释**：台湾最重要的应用研究机构，成立于一九七十三年；工研院是台湾半导体、光电、机械等关键产业的技术摇篮。
**商业造句**：*ITRI served as Taiwan's technology incubator — spinning out companies and capabilities that would form the backbone of the island's high-tech economy.*

2. **Juridical foundation (财团法人)**
**中文解释**：以财产为基础、经法院登记成立的法人组织；工研院以财团法人形式运作，享有薪资弹性但主管机关仍为经济部。
**商业造句**：*The juridical foundation structure gave ITRI the hiring flexibility of a private entity while maintaining its status as a government-funded institution.*

3. **"Constitution" of ITRI (工研院的「宪法」)**
**中文解释**：指工研院设置条例，明定了工研院的使命、定位与治理架构；张忠谋以此条例的「加速发展工业技术」为改革依据。
**商业造句**：*Chang's reading of the ITRI Act — that ITRI existed to serve industry — became the textual foundation for his controversial reform agenda.*
