## 接任第三任院长

我在一九八五年八月十八日自美飞抵台北，当时工研院徐贤修董事长在机场接我。他告诉我当他对工研院董事会报告我已被行政院院长遴聘为院长时，陈履安董事曾强力反对。陈的反对理由是：「台湾人才很多，为什么要在美国找人？」陈当时任国科会主委，并非工研院的「主管机关」，而且徐强调「别的董事都很支持你」，所以虽然我视陈的反对为不祥预兆，但也没太在意。

八月二十日院长交接典礼，我自前任方贤齐院长接过印鉴，正式成为工研院院长。在就职致词中，我说：「工研院为工业界之一员；如何与工业界配合，将是我主要工作。」

接任后的几个星期，为了了解工研院的业务，我听每个所的简报（当时工研院有五个所、一个中心）。

一开始在工研院听简报，就发现这是很累的事。简报者似乎认为这是一出他被命令演出的戏。他写剧本、导演、主演；他用心准备、排演、最后演出；他期待的是我的鼓励和喝彩。

我却认为这是他对我的沟通，我要了解他在做什么？有没有困难？我能不能解决他的困难？更重要的，我要了解他为什么做他在做的？做成了对台湾工业界有没有用？

我在德仪和通用无论听或做简报已有二十多年经验。我维持我的一贯风格：听就打断简报发问，因为问题不能累积，如果一开始不打断发问，以后等于白听；而且，在简报结束后，我问几个重要但在简报中没有提到的问题，例如：市场多大？哪家公司有兴趣？怎么移转研究成果等等。

在德仪和通用，一般简报者（包括我自己）都很能应付这类问题；但是一九八五年的工研院，我的作风竟然造成大大的震撼。当时我不知道，打断简报发问被视为「不尊重简报者」；问一些简报中没有提到、甚至简报者想都没想过的问题更被视为不近人情。其实我问这些问题时绝无要简报者出糗的意思；虽然我知道他大概没想过，但我希望我的发问也许可以启发他的思想。后来看，领情的同仁虽然我自己以为只是坦率，但我在听简报时「不尊重」同仁的恶名，在我上任的头几个月就成立了，也是我后来改革失败原因之一。

<p class="en-para"><b>Assuming the Third Presidency</b></p>
<p class="en-para">On August 18, 1985, I flew from the U.S. to Taipei. ITRI Chairman Hsu Hsiu-hsien met me at the airport. He told me that when he reported to the ITRI Board that I had been appointed by the Premier, Director Chen Lian had strongly objected. Chen's objection: "Taiwan has plenty of talent — why look in America?" Chen was then Minister of the National Science Council, not ITRI's "competent authority," and Hsu emphasized that "all the other directors strongly support you." So although I took Chen's opposition as an ill omen, I didn't dwell on it.</p>
<p class="en-para">On August 20, at the presidential transition ceremony, I received the official seal from my predecessor, President Fang Xianqi, formally becoming ITRI President. In my inaugural address, I said: "ITRI is a member of the industrial community; how to collaborate with industry will be my primary task."</p>
<p class="en-para">In the first few weeks after taking office, to understand ITRI's operations, I listened to briefings from each institute (ITRI then had five institutes and one center).</p>
<p class="en-para">From the very first briefings, I found the experience exhausting. The briefers seemed to treat the session as a play they had been ordered to perform. They wrote the script, directed it, and starred in it. They prepared diligently, rehearsed, and finally performed. My encouragement and applause were what they expected.</p>
<p class="en-para">I, however, saw this as their communication to me. I wanted to understand: What are they doing? Do they have difficulties? Can I solve them? More importantly, I wanted to understand why they were doing what they were doing, and whether it would be useful to Taiwan's industry.</p>
<p class="en-para">At TI and GI, I had over twenty years of experience both giving and receiving briefings. I maintained my consistent style: interrupt with questions during the briefing, because questions shouldn't accumulate — if you don't interrupt early, the rest is wasted. Moreover, after the briefing, I asked several important questions that hadn't been addressed: How big is the market? Which companies are interested? How will the research results be transferred?</p>
<p class="en-para">At TI and GI, most briefers (myself included) could handle such questions easily. But at ITRI in 1985, my approach caused an enormous shock. I didn't know then that interrupting a briefing with questions was seen as "disrespecting the briefer." Asking questions that hadn't been addressed — or that the briefer had never even considered — was seen as unreasonable. In truth, I had absolutely no intention of embarrassing the briefer. Though I knew they probably hadn't thought about these questions, I hoped my inquiries might stimulate their thinking. In hindsight, few colleagues appreciated my intent. My reputation for "disrespecting" colleagues during briefings was established within my first few months — and became one of the reasons my reforms later failed.</p>

### Cheat Sheet · 商业语汇

1. **Briefing culture clash (简报文化冲突)**
**中文解释**：张忠谋在美企养成的「打断发问」风格，与工研院「尊重简报者」的文化产生激烈冲突；这是跨文化管理失败的典型案例。
**商业造句**：*Chang's briefing style — honed in the aggressive Q&A culture of American semiconductor companies — collided head-on with ITRI's more deferential norms, creating a trust deficit that would haunt his reform agenda.*

2. **"ITRI is a member of the industrial community" (工研院为工业界之一员)**
**中文解释**：张忠谋就职演说的核心命题——工研院不应是闭门研究的学术机构，而应服务于工业界；这句话成为他三年改革的指导思想。
**商业造句**：*Chang's inaugural declaration that ITRI belonged to industry — not to itself — was a radical reframing of the institution's identity and purpose.*

3. **Technology transfer (技术移转)**
**中文解释**：将研究成果从研究机构转移至产业界的过程；张忠谋认为最有效的移转方式不是授权，而是「把人一起移转」——衍生公司。
**商业造句**：*Chang's insight that the most effective technology transfer involved moving people — not just patents — anticipated the spin-off model that would define Silicon Valley's relationship with Stanford.*
